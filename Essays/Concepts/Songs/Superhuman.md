## Strong Read

### 1. The marching Protector: the guard gone collective [W]

The isolated trauma of the album's earlier tracks is converted into a mechanized front: the vulnerable solo voice gives way to a roaring "we," and the cadence — "Left, right, left, it goes, we sing" — standardizes movement into a rigid binary that locks the tender self inside a moving tank. This is `CONCEPT-TheProtector` in its armoring mode — the vault's Protector definition: *adaptive psychic-defense architecture; "if humiliation is inevitable, control the humiliation yourself"; anticipatory shame-neutralization; precursor to [[Sir Lucius]].*

**The Protector's third mode [W].** Against [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Loser]] and [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Good Day]], Superhuman completes a trilogy of the *same* faceless formation in three modes: Loser is **shame-mode** (dissolve into "we're all losers" so shame can't land); Good Day is **mood-mode** (seal the vibe so disturbance can't land); Superhuman is **martial/collective-mode** (armor up as an army). One wound-born operator, three faces, all **pre-Lucius** — before it acquires a name and a mask. [W] The escalation tell: the march *needs* the inadequacy — if you were already enough, there'd be nothing to fight. The defense goes collective rather than resolving. *(The "savior archetype becomes unstable when the wound heals" mechanism the parallel draft cited is **not** in the live vault — the [W] reasoning here stands on the lyric + the Ontology Protector definition, not on that quote.)*

### 2. The horizonless loop and the counterfeit "we" [W]

"Ain't got no ceiling up on the roof" reads as freedom but scans as inflation: not ascent but exit from any structured frame into a boundless grind. Because the army "don't stop never," it locks into permanent motion — a [[The Circle|closed loop]] mistaking the horizontal endurance of a tour-grind for vertical breakthrough.

[W] And the collective *looks* relational while **erasing the individual** — "left, right," interchangeable units. So the solidarity is counterfeit: a closed loop simulating connection while deleting the person. That is exactly why **"Ain't gonna be no face in the crowd"** is loaded — the song's own protest against the flattening its "we" enacts. (Cf. the flattening/[[The Aquarium|totalized-system]] register the vault associates with the Algorithm/Circus.)

### 3. The bridge: the exhaustion of the "I" [W]

The bridge drops the march to expose the fracture: "All we are, all we are is human" acknowledges the baseline — but the response is not surrender, it's a grinding assertion of will, "But I'll try, try, try, try to be…" The mind's machinery forcing an artificial ascent.

**Why it can't hold [W].** This collides with Roy's own stated thesis. He opens *Subject to Flooding* with Loser so listeners feel *"we're all humans underneath… good enough just as they are… it's us, we, not me, and we are all Losers"* (none — Atwood Magazine, 2018; cf. [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Loser]]). Loser says *enough*; Superhuman says *become more*. The bridge glimpses the Loser truth and recoils — because at the level of the wound, *human* registers as insufficiency. (See §4.)

### 4. Perfectionism: the wound the song performs [W → grounded anchor]

Superhuman's "try, try, try to be more" *is* the perfectionism Roy names outright (above). The fuller Briana exchange opens the mechanism: "the perfect child all the time" is a **persona** — a constructed, approval-seeking self over the real one — and the people-pleasing gloss chains it forward:

> perfectionism (the wound) → the perfect-child persona (the mask that buries the authentic child — [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy]]) → people-pleasing (managing perception to be approved) → the Protector / [[Sir Lucius]] lineage (the constructed self that hides the genuine one).

Superhuman is the **collective** form of the perfect-child compulsion — the army as mass persona, performing more-than-human adequacy because *merely human* feels, at the wound, like not enough. **This part is grounded in Roy's own words** (Briana episode), independent of any private material.

### 5. The Leo labour: the lion not yet slain [W]

Roy works inside Bailey's *The Labours of Hercules* (grounded by Roy naming the book on his IG Live — see [[The Labors of Hercules]]). The Leo labour (Bailey's **Labour V**) is the *slaying* of the Nemean lion — but the "slaying" reads as an esoteric blind for **mastery-through-integration, not annihilation**. In Bailey's text the lion is the *coordinated, devastating personality* (mind, emotion, vitality fused into one strong, wild force, *"devastating the countryside"*); Hercules meets it **with his bare hands** — *"slew the lion with his bare hands"* / *"With my own hands I strangled… the lion"* — then *"killed the lion and **stripped its skin**, shewing it to the people"* (none — Bailey, *Labours of Hercules*, Labour V; verified). Per the Lucis Trust reading ("In the Tracks of Hercules," Leo), he then **wears that skin as his mantle, carrying it through every later labour and shedding it only at Capricorn** (transfiguration). [W] He doesn't destroy the personality's power — he slays its *independence* and **wears it**: the conquered lion becomes the disciple's vesture.

**This *is* the Protector arc [W].** The wound-born armor — Loser / Good Day / Superhuman — is not eliminated when the shadow is integrated; it is **worn, repurposed, subordinated to the soul. The lion's skin is the Protector, integrated.** Superhuman is **the lion not yet slain**: the coordinated personality marching as one ("left, right, left"), mistaking its own untamed force for the goal — *"try to be superhuman"* is the ego trying to become *more lion*, before the turn that would master and wear it.

**The cave's two mouths [W, esoteric].** Bailey gives the cave **two openings** and ties the labour to the **pituitary** (both verified in the Leo chapter); the Lucis reading develops this as the "cave in the head," the two openings as the gland's two lobes — the disciple sealing the one toward the dark and entering by the one toward the light, so the **soul, not the mind, governs the personality**. The cave is the Heart/Mind subordination rendered as architecture. *(Verification note: the finer phrasings the source draft quoted — "cutting out the root of evil… without external help," "the real self shines through the personality," "conquered his own monsters," a Uranus/mind clause — I could **not** confirm verbatim (the Lucis site blocks fetch; they are not in the* Labours *book, where "Uranus" appears only in a later sign). They are **paraphrased above, not quoted** — confirm against the Lucis "Leo" essay before promoting to quotation.)*

**The wrong vector — over, not through [W].** Bailey: *"the path of liberation leads **through** the midst of the world of illusion."* Superhuman wants to go *over* — "ain't got no ceiling," transcend by force — rather than down-and-through by integration. That mismatch is why the bridge's "human" can't hold.

[W] Roy returns to the half-vs-whole figure years later: the 2026 IG post *"I reached for my Father's hand / He said you're half the man that you know"* (none — [[Sources/Quotes/Roy IG Post - Half the Man (Father)]]) voices incompleteness-until-integration in the Father's mouth — **cross-era thematic continuity** with Superhuman's "try to be more," not a same-source claim. (The "ascend only in the light / rejected as only half a human" elaboration is interpretive, not the post's text.)

## Resonance

### Kabbalistic: Geburah severed from Chesed — and the Strength-card correction

Superhuman reads as **Geburah** (Severity, Mars, martial contraction) severed from **Chesed** (Mercy, Expansion): combat, debt-paying, endless labor. Untempered, Geburah closes the psyche into a militarized state that skips **Tiferet** (the heart — [[Roy]]'s seat), thickening armor instead of opening in love. The **Strength** card renders the correction: Geburah *tempered* by Chesed — the lion mastered through calm, not force. Superhuman is the untempered pole. (Resonance note: the taming figure is traditionally feminine — a possible route to the Mary / Shekinah / "la conquistadora" feminine-conqueror thread; flag as resonance, not asserted.)

### Theosophical / Bailey: glamour and the spectacle

Bailey's "the glamour of the world blinds humanity to reality" frames Superhuman's spectacle — "Let's give 'em something to see," "Raise up your voices, screaming it loud." The army performing superhuman-ness *manufactures glamour* in Bailey's sense: the illusion that blinds. Not merely inside the Circus — producing the glamour the Circus runs on.

### Gurdjieffian: the mechanical "we"

"Left, right, left, it goes" is the sound of human machines under external stimulus — collective sleep, the "many I's" under command. The "we" believes it has will but is reacting to friction; "But I'll try…" is the sleeping man's illusion that he can *do* through ordinary exertion. (Framework correspondence.)

### Gnostic: the demiurgic grid

Reads as the anthem of those fully integrated into the archonic system — internal suffering converted to public spectacle, survival renamed a "superhuman" spiritual state. (Resonance.)

### Linguistic: Left/Right as the consonantal march [W]

"Left, right, left" scans as the **consonantal** cadence of the project's angle/breath grammar — structure without vowel/breath; "all we are is human" is the vowel almost cracking through before the grind reasserts. (Resonance with the [[Duality|angle/angel]] axis.)
