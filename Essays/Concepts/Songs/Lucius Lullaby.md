## Context

Written at Roy's absolute bottom. Age 35, broke, couch-surfing, back in his parents' house. Recorded into a MacBook microphone in his childhood bedroom, in desperation. This is not a polished studio product — it's a transmission from the floor.

---

## Who's Speaking

**[[Sir Lucius]]** is the primary voice. This is Lucius singing *to* Roy — but the crucial insight is that Lucius is genuinely trying to wake Roy up. The lullaby form is subverted: this is not a song to put someone to sleep. It's a song to pull someone out of collapse.

Roy may speak within the song, but the dominant presence is Lucius. The "deal" between them originates here — Lucius pulled Roy from the bottom, and whatever the cost of that deal, it may have been worth it.

---

## Position in the Arc

This is the **origin point**. The moment of maximum descent — [[Malkhut]] in the Kabbalistic framework, the lowest sphere, the kingdom of matter, the place where spirit has fully fallen into the material world.

Everything in the Jagwar Twin mythology flows forward from this moment. The arc from Lucius Lullaby toward [[SOL]] (the Song of Love) is the arc of the entire project.

---

## Kabbalistic Mapping

- **Sphere:** [[Malkhut]] → [[Yesod]] threshold
- Lucius, mapped to [[Yesod]] (the foundation, the sphere of the unconscious, dreams, and reflection), reaches *down* into Malkhut to pull Roy upward
- The lullaby is the bridge between the two lowest spheres — the moment the unconscious makes contact with someone who has bottomed out in material reality
- This is the beginning of ascent on the [[Tree of Life]], though Roy doesn't know it yet

- Written at 35, at rock bottom 
- Mary appears twice, face hidden both times
- Lucius pulled Roy from the bottom; "the deal may have been worth it"

**Interpretive (well-supported):**
- Lucius is genuinely trying to wake Roy up, not seduce or trap him
- The lullaby form is deliberately subverted
- Malkhut → Yesod mapping as the initiating movement on the Tree
- The childhood bedroom as the site of original wounding creates a deliberate geographic echo with Ballerina Boy

- What exactly is the deal? What did Lucius get in return?
- Why is Mary's face hidden? Is she not yet integrated, not yet earned, or protecting herself?
