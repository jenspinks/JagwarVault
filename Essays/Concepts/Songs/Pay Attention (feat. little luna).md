## Grounded
- **Attention is a speaking character**, voiced by Jagwar Twin — the song opens *"Hello, Attention / I'd like to introduce you to someone,"* then *Attention* delivers the pitch in first person.
- **An act of introduction opens the song** — someone presents/introduces Attention to a "someone" (the mark). *(That this introducer is **little luna**'s voice is a `[W]` by-ear attribution — the lyric sheet assigns no vocals; see §5.)*
- **Carnival-barker register:** *"Step right up to my Door… But I can show you what's in Store."*
- **Names a price and minimizes it:** *"There's a little price you pay"*; later *"Just a small price and a rendezvous."* (The bargain includes a **rendezvous** — a meeting/tryst, not only a fee.)
- **The desires enumerated:** *"You want the money? / You want the car? / You want the big BIG house? … Everybody"* — the wants asserted as universal.
- **The chorus puns payment on awareness:** *"Everybody's gotta pay me / You gotta pay Attention"* — the currency you spend *is* the resource being harvested.
- **Cosmological flattery (V2):** *"They say Stars are Born from Light / And you're so much more than you Mind"* — light/origin imagery, and a **mind/consciousness** line (you are *more than your mind*).
- **The bridge expands the narrator's scale:** *"May I have all your ears to the back of the room"* → claims omnipresent perception *"I got eyes all around my head"* → offers to control the view *"you can see anything when you see my view"* → and claims position behind history/media: *"I've been behind the Empires that rise / What they televise is just my disguise."*
- **Closing naming ritual:** *"What's my name? / Attention!"* (×3, escalating), then the chorus, then a final *"MaMa."*

## Strong Read

### 1. Attention is the hidden god of the modern world [W]
A conversation with a *force*, not a person. It tempts by **offering**, never threatening — money, status, security, recognition. The cost stays understated precisely because the payment *is* awareness. The chorus pun ("pay me" / "pay Attention") states the thesis outright: the currency spent and the resource extracted are one substance.

### 2. It co-opts the language of light to make the bargain holy [W]
*"They say Stars are Born from Light"* puts cosmological/sacred vocabulary in the tempter's mouth; *"you're so much more than you Mind"* flatters the listener with a soul-beyond-mind promise. This is the **counterfeit-light** move — a lower, acquisitive power borrowing a higher source's idiom — structurally the error the vault attributes to [[Sir Lucius]] (mistaking the channel for the source). Ties to the light/*ohr* framework and to [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Soul Is A Star|Soul Is A Star]] (soul/star/light). *(Leap, flagged: the lines could be plain flattery; the *ohr* correspondence is the reader's frame, not stated intent.)*

### 3. The bridge reveals the true scale of the narrator [W]
The intimate pitch widens to a room, then to *"behind the Empires that rise,"* with television as *"my disguise."* Empires rise and fall; Attention remains. The narrator is reframed from a psychological pressure into a **trans-historical constant** that kingdoms, institutions, and media all compete to capture.

### 4. Attention is an early prototype of the Sir Lucius *function* (not the figure) [W]
Lucius doesn't appear, but Attention carries his operating signature — now grounded in the text: **persuasion not force** (*"I can show you," "I'll get it for you"*); **leverage on existing desire**; **intimate knowledge of the want**; **concealment behind visible structures** (*"behind the Empires," "televise is just my disguise"*); proprietary authority. The song works the territory [[Sir Lucius]] later personifies. The function, not the character.

### 5. little luna as procurer + desire-namer — a two-role negotiation [W]
Read as dialogue: **Attention offers; the featured voice delivers the mark and voices the wanting that makes the offer bite** — the intro's *"I'd like to introduce you to someone"* (procurement/intermediary) and the *"You want the money…"* pre-chorus (embodied desire). Narrows the draft's five-option question to these two compatible roles. **Caveat:** the lyric source assigns no vocals — that little luna sings these specific lines is a **by-ear `[W]`** attribution; confirm against the recording before treating the vocal assignment as grounded.

### 6. The song enacts its own bargain — the form collects the toll [W]
The closing *"What's my name? / (Attention)"* makes **answering** the entity's name an act of paying attention — the very payment the song describes. The work performs its content: to engage the question is to pay. A closed loop (the demand creates the surrender it asks for), rhyming with the recursive [[The Circle|Circle]]/[[Welcome to the Circus|Circus]] architecture — same structure, awareness pulled *outward*. *(Defensible from the call-and-response form.)*

### 7. Temptation through consent — the JT pattern, distilled [W]
Attention compels no one; the listener is invited and **assents**. The danger is not external domination but the willing surrender of agency for comfort, validation, status. The bargain works because the mark already wants what's offered. The catalogue's purest statement of this move.

## Resonance
- **Mammon / idolatry** — money/status/visibility as objects of devotion; *"pay attention"* as ritual offering, *"what's my name?"* as demand for invocation. Framework correspondence, not intent.
- **The Demiurge** — lower powers holding consciousness captive by directing awareness *outward*: *"see anything when you see my view," "eyes all around."* Attention redirects perception rather than creating truth. Framework correspondence.
- **Hermes / trickster** — messenger, advertiser, dealer, intermediary; promises access while staying invisible behind the message.
- **The Ringmaster** — *"Step right up"* is barker language; orchestrates spectacle while hidden behind it. Textual anticipation of [[Welcome to the Circus]].
- **The marketplace of souls** — payment/desire/exchange vocabulary echoes bargain-for-power myths; transaction internal, structure identical (power by bargain, not conquest). The *"rendezvous"* sharpens the deal/tryst register.
- **[~] The naming demand** — *"What's my name?"* as power-through-naming resonates with the vault's name-as-power material (El Roy / El Roi throat motif). Pointer only.
