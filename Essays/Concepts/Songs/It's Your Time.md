> **One-line reading:** *It's Your Time* is the earnest twin of *Online*. Where *Online* satirizes the "we come from space" cosmology and deflates it into the surveillance grid, *It's Your Time* states the same structure straight: the soul came from elsewhere, forgot on arrival, and must find its way back. Disguised as a motivational anthem, it's really a song about **remembrance** — the journey is not toward acquisition but toward recollection. And at its center sits the catalogue's clearest cosmological statement of the integration thesis: *a man is like an in-between, animating Heaven and Hell.*

---

## Grounded

- **Descent-and-forgetting, stated plainly.** *"One you get to Earth you forget where you come from / gotta find your Way back"* — the song's repeated spine. The movement is **return**, not advance; the destination is forgotten, not new.
- **The in-between couplet.** *"A man is like an in-between / Animating Heaven and Hell."*
- **The Fool.** *"Cause I ain't nothing but a Fool"* (opens and recurs); *"with all the things you've ever seen / you still can hardly spell."*
- **The Eye and the Well.** *"Within you got the Eye to see / All the stars under your Belt / And in your heart there is a Well / You can follow where it leads."*
- **The mutating identity line** (appears twice, changed):
  - V1: *"If you don't know who you are to **be** / You still don't know you **fell**."*
  - V2: *"If you don't know Who You Are to **'me'** / You still don't know your **Self**."*
- **Pronouns in scare-quotes.** *"As far as '**I**' can tell"*; *"who you are to '**me**'"*; *"your **Self**"* (capital S).
- **Plain-imperative chorus.** *"It's your Time / All my life I've been waiting for this moment"*; *"Learn to fly / Don't give up / When it's tough / You got just one life to live."*
- **Context (public, Spotify panel):** *33*-era related videos are *welcome to the circus* and *the circle — lucius* (both *Jagwar Twin & sir lucius*); artist bio reads *"There's two sides to every story."*

## Strong Read

### 1. The earnest twin of *Online* — the album holds both versions of "from above" [W]

*Online* (track 1) and *It's Your Time* (track 2) are **back-to-back**, running the same cosmology in opposite registers. *Online* invokes "we come from space" through a crowd of gurus and **deflates** it — *"but we're stuck / just like a bug inside your database."* *It's Your Time* takes the identical premise **sincerely**, no shrug: *"once you get to Earth you forget where you come from / gotta find your way back."* The split runs *through the record itself*. Read as a pair: *Online* is **external capture** (the system knows you — "they got my face"); *It's Your Time* is **internal amnesia** (you don't know yourself — "you forget where you come from"). The machine remembers everything about you; you remember nothing about you. *(The adjacency is grounded, tracks 1→2; the "deliberate pair" intent is the [W] reading.)*

### 2. "A man is like an in-between / animating Heaven and Hell" — the integration keystone [W]

The catalogue's clearest cosmological statement of the integration thesis. Man is the **in-between** — the middle of the Tree (Tiferet), hanging between the pillars — and crucially he *animates both* Heaven and Hell. Not chooses Heaven over Hell, not slays the lower: **animates the whole polarity.** This is the [[Jacob & Esau|Jacob/Esau]] "one blessing, two halves" rendered as ontology — the work isn't to eliminate the shadow (Lucius / Esau / Hell) but to be the living middle that holds and vivifies both poles. "Animating" *is* [[The Thread|the Thread]] — feeling as the animating principle. The human vehicle is the channel between registers, and its orientation determines which energy it projects into the material mirror.

### 3. Anamnesis — the journey as recollection, not acquisition [W]

The structure is **descent → forgetting → remembrance → return**: the soul comes from elsewhere, forgets on arrival in matter, must "find its way back." The obstacle isn't failure or evil — it's *amnesia*. This reframes the motivational surface: "it's your time" sounds like *now is your moment to become something*, but the verses say *now is your moment to remember something.* The Eye already exists, the Well already exists, "all the stars" are already "under your Belt," the path already exists — **the only thing missing is memory.** (The descent-and-return shape proper to Neoplatonic/Gnostic cosmology — see Resonance — and the "from above" the catalogue keeps circling: a fall into forgetting, not an invasion from outside.)

### 4. "I ain't nothing but a Fool" — Key 0 and "I'm no thing" [W]

Two readings converge on one line. The **Fool** (Tarot Key 0) is the beginning of the journey — the unconditioned traveler who leaves home, learns by experience, takes the leap into matter; "ignorant of worldly logic" (*"you still can hardly spell"*) yet the pure vessel. *And* — "I ain't **no-thing**" is the same **anti-categorization / ego-emptiness** disposition grounded elsewhere (*"I'm no thing,"* [[Roy on Authenticity & Masks]]): the Fool is precisely the one who knows he is no-thing, uncategorized, empty enough to be filled. The line is the disposition and the tarot key at once.

### 5. The heart-Well and the Eye — the Thread, internalized [W]

*"In your heart there is a Well / you can follow where it leads"* is a [[The Thread|Thread]] statement: the heart as a Well (depth, source, living water) with a current you *follow* — heart-as-navigator, Ariadne's line, feeling as the orienting faculty. Pointedly **in the heart**, not the mind, not achievements, not reputation. Paired with *"within you got the Eye to see / all the stars under your Belt"*: the cosmos internalized, heaven brought inside, the inner Eye opened. Both name an *interior* infrastructure — the opposite vector to *Online*'s outward gaze at the consuming external web.

### 6. The constructed self vs. the Self — the mutating line [W]

The scare-quotes are on purpose — *"as far as 'I' can tell," "who you are to 'me'"* — the provisional ego flagged as provisional, pointed past toward *"your Self"* (capital S, the Jungian totality). The doubled line carries the whole arc in miniature:
- *"who you are to **be**"* → *"you still don't know you **fell**"* (purpose/being, and the **Fall** that obscured it)
- *"Who You Are to **'me'**"* → *"you still don't know your **Self**"* (relational/social identity, and the **Self** beneath it)

Knowing your role to others isn't knowing yourself; not knowing your purpose isn't yet knowing that you fell. The line teaches by mutating — same frame, deeper each pass. *(Both renderings verified in the stable lyric source — the variation is the device, not a transcription artifact.)*

### 7. The two "Time" songs — panic and answer [W]

Against [[Sources/Lyrics/Precious Time|Precious Time]] (awakening *too late*; time as panic, the drowning clock), *It's Your Time* is **kairos** — the seized moment, *"all my life I've been waiting for this."* Same noun, opposite charge: *Precious Time* is the terror of the wasted hour; *It's Your Time* is the arrival of the right one.

## Resonance

- **Neoplatonic / Gnostic descent (anamnesis).** *"You came here… you forget where you come from… find your way back"* is the soul's descent into matter, the draught of Lethe (forgetting), the return by recollection — Plotinus's fall-and-ascent; the Gnostic spark that forgets its origin in the dense copy and must remember. *"You still don't know you fell"* names the Fall directly.
- **Tarot — the Fool, Key 0.** The unmanifested spirit before it steps into matter; the traveler who "can hardly spell" yet is the unconditioned vessel. The journey's beginning — fitting for a song about remembering the origin.
- **Hermetic Qabalah — the middle pillar.** *"A man is like an in-between"* tracks the central column; the Eye (perception above) and the Well (Tiferet, the heart-sphere) instruct the seeker to abandon lateral strategy and descend into the heart — opposite to the strategic mind-pole personified by Lucius.
