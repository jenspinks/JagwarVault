> **One-line reading:** a creation myth — a feminine maker holding a world on a string makes the **Jagwars** and teaches them to **sing**; kings persuade them they cannot; she hides the song *inside* them and withdraws; empires cage and sell — until a **wise one** finds the old cave and **remembers she once held the string**, and the circle begins to grow again *from the inside of the inside.* The album's closing word.

## Grounded
- **A third-person myth:** *"Long ago, there was a Jagwar with a world tied on a string / And she made other Jagwars, then taught them to sing."*
- **Immanence then withdrawal:** *"She would come down to the empires of the Earth, Wind, and the Sea / And she'd walk with the Jagwars."*
- **Loss by persuasion, in cycles:** *"Many cycles of the story came and went with their kings / And the new kings told Jagwars they never could sing."*
- **Concealment-within, then retreat:** *"So the maker of the Jagwars hid the song inside their wings / On the inside of the inside, then left them to sing."*
- **Degradation into capture/commerce:** *"the Earth changed, and the sky raged… the empires built cages and sold everything."*
- **Recovery by remembrance:** *"'Til a wise one found a small cave where the Jagwars used to sing / And the wise one remembered she once held the string."*
- **The doctrine-refrain (the album's final words, to fade):** *"The circle will grow / From the inside of the inside… But the inside of the inside must be where you go."*
- Both maker and wise one are **"she."**

## Strong Read

### 1. The foundational Jagwar myth — and the namesake [W]
The catalogue's origin story: where the **Jagwars** came from, what was lost, how, and how it returns. The creatures are *Jagwars* — **the artist's own name**; "**Jagwar Twin**" reads as a self-identification with the made-and-divided creatures of this song. The maker who *makes others* is the same gesture as the One becoming two in [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Another Way II Heaven|Another Way II Heaven]] / the board cosmogony ([[The Pattern]]). As closer, it's the record's resolving word.

> **Earliest documented naming (2019) — the doctrine predates this song.** *The Circle* (this *33* closer, 2022) is the **fully-narrated, mythologized** statement of a doctrine Roy had already been speaking aloud **three years earlier.** In the *Subject to Flooding* press cycle he names it directly, attached to **[[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Move To You]]**: *"there is no beginning and no end — it's just a circle,"* felt live when the crowd sings it back (none — Digital Journal **2019-09-23**, [[Sources/Transcripts/Digital-Journal-2019-Move-To-You-Loser]]). So the keystone was **laid in the debut era, on its most vulnerable song** — which gives the "*33* grew from *Subject to Flooding*'s flood-rich soil" thesis a literal receipt (see [[Subject to Flooding (album)]]). `[G]` the 2019 naming · `[W]` this song as its mythic elaboration.

### 2. The gift is song, not power [W]
The maker gives neither wealth, dominion, nor knowledge — she teaches them to **sing**. Song = essence (authentic expression, identity, participation in creation). The tragedy isn't losing a possession — it's being convinced the essence was never theirs.

### 3. The loss is belief, not theft [W]
*"The new kings told Jagwars they never could sing."* The song is never taken — the Jagwars are **persuaded** it never existed. Authority gained by convincing them something inherent is absent. **This is the exact mechanism the attention/spectacle tracks dramatize** ([[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Pay Attention (feat. little luna)|Pay Attention]] / [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/I Like to Party|I Like to Party]]) — here named at the mythic root.

### 4. "Hid the song inside their wings" — the governing paradox [W]
The maker preserves the song **within** the Jagwars, *"on the inside of the inside."* What's sought is already present; the journey is **remembrance, not acquisition** — the catalogue's central paradox stated as plot. *(This line is the **public source** of the "song hidden inside its wings" phrasing — citable per [[_System/Boundaries]].)*

### 5. The string is the Thread [W]
*"A world tied on a string"* (the maker) and *"she once held the string"* (the wise one) are the same filament — the connection to the source, Ariadne's thread back through the maze of cages and empires. Remembering = re-grasping the string. **This song is where [[The Thread]] originates** — *world-on-a-string* and *song-in-the-wings* both born here.

### 6. A descent-and-withdrawal cosmology [W]
The maker *"comes down" and "walks with"* the Jagwars (immanence), then *"hid the song inside… and left them to sing"* (withdrawal). Emanation → concealment-and-retreat — the same shape as [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Another Way II Heaven|Another Way II Heaven]], structurally parallel to **tzimtzum** (the maker contracts, leaving the divine hidden within creation). The inward-hiding is *why* "the inside of the inside" is the only route back.

### 7. Inside-of-the-inside reverses the attention vector [W]
Where the album's attention-force pulls awareness **outward** (and [[Welcome to the Circus]] runs the same recursion outward), here the circle *"will grow / from the inside of the inside"* — outward growth **sourced from the deepest interior.** Same recursive shape, opposite direction. The repeated instruction is a manifesto: **go inward, not further out.** As closer, this is the album's **mythic cure for its own attention-disease.**

### 8. Remembrance over conquest; the wise one as Sophia [W]
The resolution defeats no enemy and seizes no kingdom — *"the wise one remembered."* Memory placed above victory. That the rememberer is **"the wise one" and feminine** maps her onto **Sophia / Shekinah** (wisdom recovering the thread), paired with the feminine maker: source and remembering both feminine. *(The "she" is grounded; the Sophia/Shekinah ID is the read.)*

### 9. The circle grows by spreading remembrance [W]
A circle expanding from a center — the plot tracks it (hidden → remembered → sung → grown). Growth is **organic and by awakening**, not coercion; the forgetting came *"in cycles… with their kings,"* and the remembering reverses the cycle into a growing circle.

### 10. The Wise One's two faces — the source, and the template ("all of us") [W / ~]
The Wise One is not one *or* the other of two readings — the song's own doctrine makes her **both**, joined at the act of remembrance:
- **The cosmic / source face `[W]`:** she *"remembered she once held the string"* — so she **was** the Maker; seeker and maker are two phases of one feminine being (the loop). That face is the divine-feminine source — **Sophia / Shekinah** (and [[Brain/Characters/Mary|Mary]]'s register; §8).
- **The immanent / collective face `[W/~]` (Jen's read):** because the song was hidden *"inside their wings"* — inside **every** Jagwar — the Wise One is also the **template of remembrance that any of us can instantiate.** "Jagwar Twin" *is* self-identification with the made creatures; we are the Jagwars who were told we couldn't sing. So the Wise One = **us, remembering** — and the **exemplars who completed the circle** (*a Jesus, a Bowie, a Prince*: the ones who remembered the song and sang it back). This is the **Christ-as-exemplar** move — not a god above us but the *pattern* of awakening any spark can follow (theosis; "the kingdom within"; cf. the album title **33** = Christ's age `[~]`). **Roy grounds this publicly** (X, 2026; [[Sources/Quotes/Roy X Posts - June 2026 (aphorisms)]]): *"Jesus is my North Star," "I'm here to serve Jesus"* (Christ-as-guide); *"Every person has it inside of them. The trick is getting it inside out"* + *"If it's happening for me it's happening for everyone"* (the song-inside-everyone / the collective); *"Meet David Bowie"* (the exemplar). The "all of us / Christ-as-exemplar" face is now Roy-anchored, not only inferred.
- **Why both at once (the Gnostic hinge):** in this cosmology the spark inside each person — the song in the wings — *remembered*, **reunites with the source.** So a personal awakening and reunion with the cosmic Maker are the **same event**, which is exactly why the Wise One can be the divine feminine **and** "all of us": to remember you once held the string *is* to rediscover your identity with the One who held it. Source and seeker meet in **anamnesis**. → `CONCEPT-Malkhut` (the spark within); the vessel/"download" doctrine ([[The Upload]] / [[The Pattern]]).

> **Source discipline (do not over-collapse; do not over-ground):** the three names stay a **family resemblance**, not an equation — *Marian vessel / Gnostic Sophia / Kabbalistic Shekinah* — and the Wise One's defining act (**remembrance**) is **more Sophia/Shekinah than conventional Mary** (CLAUDE.md §register-collapse). *"Inside of the Inside"* is a **NotebookLM artifact** (an AI Audio-Overview "podcast" + grounded notes) — a *source-grounded, source-citing finding-aid* (**valuable** for tracing to primaries), **but `citable: false` as output** (never cite the NotebookLM synthesis itself). **Handling:** trace each claim to the primary it cites and ground on that primary **iff public/citable**; the AI hosts *interpret/extrapolate* the uploads, so conversational lines like *"blue jeans / California corner / ABC"* are **AI synthesis — not Roy's intent — unless traced to an actual uploaded source**; and **check the upload set for private material** (if the vault / Secret Doc / private calls were uploaded, its citations can be circular or non-citable, per [[_System/Boundaries]]). Gemini's error was treating the AI-host interpretation as **authorial**. The *"Themes, Parallels" doc* is likewise a finding-aid. The readings above stand on the **public lyric** + existing canon.

## Resonance
- **The music of creation (Ainulindalë)** — creation taught/sustained as *song* resembles Tolkien's world sung into being. Framework correspondence.
- **Divine spark / kingdom within** — the song concealed within the Jagwars = the essential truth hidden in the individual, uncovered not acquired (`CONCEPT-Malkhut`).
- **Eden remembered / the Cave** — a fall into forgetfulness and recovery of an original relation; the *"small cave where the Jagwars used to sing"* (and a Plato's-cave *inversion* — here the cave holds the truth, not the shadows).
- **Tzimtzum / the Great Work** — withdrawal leaving the divine hidden within (Lurianic); caged fragmentation → remembered reintegration (tikkun).
- **[~] Joni's "Circle Game"** — *"round and round… captive on the carousel of time… in the circle game"* (cf.
