# Good Time (1:08)

## Album / Position
Standalone single, released **2023-09-29** (MV 2023-10-11), Big Loud, 2:36. `[G]` Lyric **Spotify-verified** (2026-06-10). **Writers:** Roy English, Lucky West, **Elijah Noll**, **Josh Dun**; **producers** Roy English / Lucky West / Elijah Noll ([[Sources/Lyrics/Good Time (1-08)]]). *(The credits confirm the TikTok's session detail — **Lucky West + Elijah Noll** are the studio collaborators Roy names; **Josh Dun** = Twenty One Pilots, also on Great Time to Be Human + Happy Face per the 2023 feed.)* The **2023 face-paint video** (full makeup + knight armor + *"I'm a mastermind"*) is the catalogue's earliest assembled-Lucius iconography — three years before the 2026 co-credit. → [[Sir Lucius]].

## The grounded floor — Roy's own dream-origin `[G]`

Roy narrates the song's origin publicly (TikTok @jagwartwin, [[Sources/Transcripts/Roy TikTok - Good Time (1-08) dream story|the dream-story video]]): a vivid dream (he keeps a dream journal, *"every dream comes true"*) — a party, a text that his ride is here, Drake in a white minivan giving fame-advice and saying *"it's your turn to drive,"* then Roy alone on the highway **as a rabbit**, *"looking for something, but I didn't know what I was looking for."* Then:

> *"This other character pops into my passenger's seat and he's a rabbit, but he's got like clown makeup on, and he says, **I'm Sir Lucius**. And he's like, **I'm here to help you.** … **He had all the answers.** … But **I had a weird feeling in my heart** when Sir Lucius would speak — like he was **telling the truth, but he was manipulating me in some way**, and I couldn't explain why, because he could **factually** identify all the things he was saying."*

The dream ends in a crash — *"white semi truck with a lightning bolt comes, boom"* — and Roy wakes at **1:08**, the title. The crash then **manifests** in waking traffic that day (a white semi-truck rear-ends him). `[G]` that Roy publicly tells it this way.

## Strong reads

**1. The song *is* the dream, and the lyric proves it.** Two lines are near-verbatim dream-callbacks: *"Just a rabbit in a road rage"* (Roy-the-rabbit, the highway, the crash) and *"I don't know what I'm looking for"* (the dream's exact phrase). The party-frame (*"step right up, get on the floor"*) is the dream's opening party. The song doesn't *describe* the dream from outside — it's sung **from inside** it. `[W]` (grounded on the TikTok).

**2. Sir Lucius's voice is in the verses — the manipulator-guide.** Verse 2 *is* the passenger-seat pitch: *"You're a star, you know you are / Stick with me, I'll take you far."* Flattery + the offer to lead — the dream-Lucius who *"had all the answers"* and *"knew which way to go."* And the pre-chorus is his self-designation: *"I'm a mastermind / I don't need a witness."* The figure who appears in the 2023 video in full face paint is **singing the seductive-guide verses** of the song that narrates his own arrival. → [[Sir Lucius]]. `[W]`

**3. The cleanest grounded statement of the truth-that-deceives — and heart over mind.** Roy's own words: Lucius *"was telling the truth, but manipulating me… he could factually identify all the things,"* yet *"I had a weird feeling in my heart."* This grounds, in Roy's voice and **dated to 2023**, the dynamic the vault elsewhere anchors to the 2024 *"the truth can be deceiving 🍏"* post — and it grounds **heart-knows-over-mind**: the heart registered the manipulation the mind couldn't argue against. It also grounds the **three-register Lucius** ([[Sir Lucius]] §The nature of Lucius): *"here to help"* (protective) **and** *"manipulating"* (shadow) held in the same figure. `[G]` on the words · `[W]` on the mapping.

**4. "Step right up" = the proto-Circus barker.** *"Step right up, get on the floor"* is the carnival-barker register that hardens into [[Welcome to the Circus]] (*"Step right up, shut your mouth"*). Good Time is the **bright** version of the same call — the Circus before it shows its teeth; the party that is already the trap. `[W]`

**5. "I don't need a witness" — anti-witnessing.** The mastermind who refuses to be *seen* (vs. [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]'s witnessing-that-restores) — the figure who operates without accountability to a witnessing gaze. Pointed, given the El Roi *"God who sees me"* axis Lucius later wears on his throat. `[W]`/`[~]`

**6. The lightning as truth/revelation.** *"Truth is on the mountaintop where man can pray to lightning"* welds the dream's **lightning-bolt semi-truck** (the crash that ends the dream and then comes true) to **truth at the summit** — the Mountain motif. The crash isn't only catastrophe; it's the dream's revelatory full-stop. *"Youth is not a fountain, it's a hand grenade in hiding"* keeps danger under the bright surface. `[W]`/`[~]`
