## 0. Board corroboration — Roy wrote this cosmogony twice [GROUNDED]
The lyric is **near-identical to the standalone cosmogonic poem on Roy's "I'm 33 this year" board** ([[Sources/Artifacts/Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)]]; hub: [[The Pattern]]): *"I spoke the words 'One become Two'… A Darkness and a Light… when we crashed… A Sun was born inside… Next there were 4 — Process & Time… Split into 5,6,7,8,9… 'I' was still I… see The Great Design."* So the creation-myth reading is **not a reader's projection** — it is **Roy's own intentional cosmology, composed twice** (as this song and as a board text), and it sits inside the board's larger system (Kybalion, Tree of Life, the 10-stage emanation list). The song adds one thing the board poem lacks: the **captor ad-lib** *"(you'll never get away)"* (§8). *(The cosmogony-as-Roy's-own-framing is grounded; specific Kabbalah label-mappings below stay `[W]`.)*

On the board, the lyric block is **color-coded to the board's ten-station field** — the structure elsewhere rendered as tone, color, traits, animal/Tree address, body/return address, soul-plane, and emanation ([[The Pattern]]). `[G]` the coloring and printed indices; `[W]` that the lyric is performing the full conversion described here. The newly legible animal/body family matters because it shows that Roy counts a station from opposite ends: Swan 1→Rabbit 10 runs toward the World, while Feet 1→Crown 10 runs bodily return. That makes the reading in §6 below (*the count is a clock; the 1-through-11 procession maps descent and return*) something closer to **Roy's own annotation, not only the reader's Kabbalah frame** — the song is, in the board's own hand, **the emanation field narrated.** The lyric's line *"From One and through Eleven"* is textual; reading eleven specifically as ten Sefirot plus hidden Da'at remains resonance. The human-shaped Ark's **11 sections** and the bestiary's **ten numbered animals plus one unnumbered vulture** add a strong board-internal convergence, not a solved equation.

The board is titled **"I'm 33 this year"** and self-dates to **2021** — one year before *33* released (birthdate-gematria: `5+22+1+9+8+8` = Roy's 5/22/1988 birthdate; title + digits together give 1988+33 = 2021). Since it carries this song's finished lyric plus [[Soul Is A Star]]'s, the board functions at its origin as ***33*'s concept-document** — the making-of map whose Kybalion/Tree-of-Life/emanation scaffolding *is* the album's conceptual architecture, later kept growing into a running cosmology board. `[G]` the board's contents and self-dating; `[W]` the "concept-document" framing.

## Grounded
- **Solitude that can't self-know:** *"Back long ago / I was just me / But who was 'I' to Know?"* — motive is **sight:** *"I wanted to see myself / And where I could go."*
- **Creation = a speech-act of division:** *"I spoke the words / 'One, become Two' / Then 'I' was in reverse,"* after which *"'I' could see my Self / And 'I' became 'You'."*
- **Dyad + union:** *"'You' and 'I' could dance forever / A Darkness and a Light"*; *"When we crashed and came together / A Sun was born inside."*
- **The count:** 1 → 2 → (no 3) → *"Next there was 4 / 'Process' and 'Time'"* → *"Split into Five (5) / Six / Seven / Eight / But when it came to Nine… / I had to remind myself / That 'I' was still 'I'."*
- **A Door at 4:** *"I opened up a Door / And I just released myself / To cover the sky."*
- **Promised reunion:** *"All of 'Us' could dance forever / In every Space and Time… One day we'll get back together / And see The Great Design."*
- **Heaven as perspective (refrain):** *"I see it different every Time / Another way to Heaven."*
- **Number-range two ways:** *"From One and through Eleven"* / *"From 1:00 and through 11:00"*; the title-line shifts *"to"* → *"II."*
- **Captor ad-lib:** *"(You'll never get away)"* (at the Door) and the close *"Don't you ever try to get away."*

## Strong Read

### 1. A creation myth driven by the need for self-sight [W → grounded as Roy's myth, per §0]
The primordial problem is **epistemic, not material**: a lone "I" cannot know itself (*"who was 'I' to Know?"*). Creation is initiated to *see* — the world emerges from **self-reflection**, not warfare or necessity. Division is the precondition of self-knowledge.

### 2. The title encodes its argument: II = two = the way [W]
The chorus moves *"another way **to** Heaven"* → *"another way **II** Heaven."* The "II" is the Roman two — *"One, become Two."* The path *to* heaven is the path *of the two*: **multiplicity is itself the route.** Not decorative — the thesis.

### 3. The mirror is built into the cosmogony [W]
*"One, become Two"* yields *"'I' was in reverse"* — a **reversed self / reflection** — before *"I became You."* The catalogue's pervasive mirror motif **originates here**: the "You" is the "I" reversed. (Cf. the board's ENSO / "mirror" material and Joni's "a mirror… the listener sees themself" — the mirror is the project's master device.)

### 4. Darkness, Light, and the Sun of their collision — coniunctio [W]
The first pair is *"A Darkness and a Light"* that *"dance"*; their crash births *"A Sun… inside."* The dyad the whole project runs on (heart/mind, **Roy/Lucius**) is placed **at the origin**, and the **union of opposites generates a new luminous center**. Alchemically the coniunctio birthing the Sun/Self; cosmologically a star ignites from collision. Either way, light here is *produced by* the meeting of dark and light, not opposed to dark. (Ties to the Sun/Son thread — [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Soul Is A Star|Soul Is A Star]].)

### 5. Nine as the point of self-forgetting — Yesod [W]
At nine the narrator must *"remind myself / That 'I' was still 'I'."* In the vault's Tree-of-Life frame the ninth Sefirah is **Yesod** — the channel/foundation where the self most risks **mistaking the channel for the source** (the Lucius error). The lyric's lone moment of threatened self-loss landing exactly at nine is strikingly coherent with that frame. *(`[W]` — the song never says "Yesod"; the mapping is the reader's. **But** Kabbalah/Tree-of-Life is demonstrably in Roy's toolkit — it's on the board — so the *frame* is licensed even if this specific 9=Yesod label is interpretive.)*

### 6. The count is a clock; the missing twelve is the return [W]
The second chorus renders the numbers as **hours:** *"From 1:00 and through 11:00."* A clock has twelve; the song stops at eleven. The unreached twelve — which loops back to one — is the promised reunion (*"get back together"*). Emanation runs 1→11; the return (12→1) is deferred to *"The Great Design."* (1-through-11 also reads as the ten Sefirot **plus Da'at**, the hidden eleventh — Resonance.)

### 7. Heaven is perception, not destination [W]
*"I see it different every Time / Another way to Heaven."* The song never *arrives*; it **sees differently.** Each perspective generated by the dividing One is "another way to heaven" — multiplicity itself is the set of routes.

### 8. The myth's captor underside [W]
*"(You'll never get away)"* and the closing *"Don't you ever try to get away"* undercut the benign self-knowledge reading. The One that divided itself also **forbids escape** — a **closed system**, multiplicity held *inside* the design rather than freely dispersing. Links to the [[Sir Lucius|Lucius]] closed-loop / inescapability material and complicates any purely redemptive reading. *(Lines grounded; "closed system" is the read. **Note:** this ad-lib is in the song but absent from the board poem — the captor framing is song-specific.)*

### 9. The metaphysical charter of the wider mythology [W → grounded per §0]
Mirrors, doubles, twins, loops, fragmented identities, reunification — the recurring JT vocabulary is given its **generating logic** here: unity divided itself to know itself, and everything after is a variation on that act. As Roy's solo-written piece *and* a board text, it functions as the project's cosmological charter.
