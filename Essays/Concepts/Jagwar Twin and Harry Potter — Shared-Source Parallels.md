# Jagwar Twin and Harry Potter — Shared-Source Parallels (interpretive map)

> **Governing thesis.** Jagwar Twin's **only documented direct Harry Potter reference is [[Brain/Songs/Singles & EPs/dumbledore|"dumbledore"]]** (title + "DARK ARTS VERSION" + the wizard/"freeing" framing). The deeper resonances between the two bodies of work are **real but are convergence, not influence** — both draw on the **same Western esoteric source-traditions** (alchemy, the double/shadow, the mirror, awakening/anamnesis, names-as-word-magic, death-rebirth). **Route every parallel through a shared *tradition* node, never "Potter → JT" directly** — to do otherwise makes a co-participant in a tradition look like a *source* (the same error the vault forbids with Joni). Sir Lucius ↔ Lucius Malfoy is an **etymological resonance, not a sourced derivation.**

Potter-side facts and scholarship live in the context node [[Harry Potter — Esoteric Architecture (reference)]] (Rowling-confirmed items kept separate from Granger/fandom interpretation). This page is the disciplined cross-map.

**Lucius etymology (kept clean).** Sir Lucius and Lucius Malfoy share the Latin **light-name field** (*lux*; Lucifer-as-light-bringer adjacent). In Potter this creates irony around an aristocratic villain; in JT it suits the brilliant intellect/shadow who brings a *dangerous* kind of light. **This is an etymological resonance only — no derivation from Malfoy is documented** (and Malfoy is the *weakest* strand of the Lucius name-field; the stronger lineage is the Lucifer/*lux* one, handled on [[Sir Lucius]]).
