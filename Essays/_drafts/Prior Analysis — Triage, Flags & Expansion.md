## 🆕 Part 3 — New / underdeveloped (expand)

### A. The Circle is bracketed by two feminine figures — and the "string" is the Thread `[W]`
This is the strongest new structural observation, and it's grounded in *The Circle* itself. The myth **opens** with a primordial jaguar — **female** — who has the world tied on a string and teaches the jaguars to sing. It **closes** with a Wise One — **she** — who finds the cave where they used to sing and remembers she once held the string. Beginning and end rhyme, and both are feminine.

The decoder is Code's canon that **the Thread = feeling**, given by the feminine source and held by the hero. Lay the two together and the whole cosmogony resolves into one image: the **feminine holds the string/thread (feeling/the song); the "new kings" cut it (suppress the song, sever feeling); recovery = re-membering the feminine thread.** The kings are the masculine system-of-control (cages, commodification, "you can't sing") — i.e. mind divorced from heart at civilizational scale, the same severance Lucius is at personal scale. So the feminine isn't a side character in this project; she's the **missing structural half** — the held thread of feeling that the entire arc is about losing and recovering. That reframes Mary/Shekinah, the Wise One, and the primordial Jaguar as **one feminine function seen at three points in the cycle**: origin (the one who tied the world on the string and taught the song), exile (the hidden source within darkness — Shekinah-in-exile), and return (the Wise One who re-members). `[W]`, grounded in *The Circle* + Code's Thread canon.

### B. The Wise One: remembrance, not a guru — and maybe the listener `[W]`
Two prior readings look like they conflict; they don't. Doc 11: the Wise One is **not a separate character** — she's the awakened Jagwar / recovered self / *future state of the listener*; the emphasis is **remembrance, not acquisition** (Gnostic *anamnesis* — recovering what was always there). Doc 9: the Wise One occupies **Mary's archetypal territory** — the feminine rememberer, carrier of the hidden song. Synthesis: in this system **remembrance is feminine-coded** (caves, mirrors, intuition, the held thread, "creation from chaos = the feminine principle" — Bohnes). So the awakened self recovers the song *by recovering the feminine faculty* (feeling / witnessing) that the kings suppressed. **The Wise One = whoever re-members the feminine thread** — which can be the listener, Roy, or the feminine principle itself, because at the moment of recovery they're the same act. Hard guard: **not "the Wise One is Mary"** (no evidence) — archetypal *overlap* only, and not a fourth figure to slot into the roster (that's the tetramorph mistake again).

### C. "There's only one song" — the load-bearing public line `[G]`
The Hall of Mirrors FAQ line — paraphrasing: *the world needs music but not more songs; there's only one song, and he's still learning to write it* — is the citable spine that ties the whole mythology together. It connects **The Circle** (the primordial, suppressed song), **SOL / "Soul Is A Star"** (the soul *as* the star/song), the **Hidden Song "inside the wings,"** and the Joni "fifty-year transmission" thesis. Read this way, the entire catalogue is **successive attempts to re-transmit the one lost song** — which is exactly what the Wise One's remembrance (§B) and the feminine thread (§A) are *for*. This is a `[G]` public anchor and probably belongs on the `[[The Circle]]` / `[[SOL]]` pages as the connective tissue.

### D. The Jaguar's *song* precedes the Jaguar's *sight* `[W]` — a note for the Jaguar stub
Current canon defines the Jaguar as the **seeing-field** (Roy's etymology: "sees into all living beings"). But *The Circle*'s primordial Jaguar's defining act is **teaching the song**. So the Jaguar-origin is **voice as much as vision** — the field that both *sees into* and *sings*. Reconcile rather than choose: the primordial faculty is a single capacity with two faces (seeing-into-beings / sounding-the-world), and **the "hidden song inside the wings" is that field's lost voice.** Worth a line in the pending `[[The Jaguar]]` stub so the seeing-field isn't read as *only* surveillance/witnessing — it's also the original singer. (Ties cleanly to §C: the one song is the Jaguar's song.)
