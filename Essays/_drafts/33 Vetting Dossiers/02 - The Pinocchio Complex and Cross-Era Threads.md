# Dossier 02 — The Pinocchio Complex & Cross-Era Threads

**What to vet.** The pass's headline (D1) plus three threads that track a single element across the catalogue (D7–D9). For D1, judge whether "inherited myth-template" over-claims; for the threads, the usual test — real organizing pattern, or "same word/number recurs"?

## D1 — The Pinocchio complex (the headline) `[G anchors · W synthesis]`
Three things the vault holds separately, and one board-cell that fuses them:
1. **The wound-phrase:** *"you're not a real boy"* (×4, BALLERINA BOY, `[G]`).
2. **The string:** *"a world tied on a string"* / *"the wise one remembered she once held the string"* (The Circle, `[G]`) — the Thread / connection to the maker.
3. **The realness-blessing:** the motherly outro *"you are one of a kind, God's design… let your light shine"* (BALLERINA BOY, `[G]`).

**And on Roy's own film-board (`[G]`): *Pinocchio*.** The synthesis `[W]`: Pinocchio is the single myth that contains all three — a **made boy** told he is **not a real boy**; a **puppet** defined by the **strings** tying him to his maker (whose famous song *"I've Got No Strings"* sells cutting them as freedom); a **feminine intercessor** (the Blue Fairy) whose blessing makes him **real**. Run through the catalogue's grammar:
- *"I've got no strings"* = **Lucius's pitch** — stringlessness sold as freedom, which in the Circle's cosmology is **severance from the maker** (the kings cutting the Jagwars' song). The puppet who cuts the string doesn't become real; he becomes a performer in someone else's show (Stromboli's caged stage = the **Circus**, beat for beat).
- The Circle's resolution — *remembering you once **held** the string* — is the **anti-Pinocchio-song**: realness from re-membering the connection, not stringlessness.
- The Blue Fairy's blessing = the **Mary-function** (the faceless/feminine voice that confers being: *"you are one of a kind, God's design"* ↔ *"you're a real boy now"*).
- **Pleasure Island** (boys who indulge become **donkeys** and are *sold*) = *Bananas*' id-permission arc, species swapped.

The claim is **not** "Ballerina Boy is about Pinocchio" — it's that **Pinocchio is the inherited myth-template the wound-language, the string-cosmology, and the blessing-function all instantiate**, and **Roy curated the film onto his own board.** `[G]` the board cell + three lyric anchors · `[W]` the template-synthesis · `[~]` the song-level "I've Got No Strings"/Pleasure Island links.
- **Falsifier:** Roy sourcing the film-list cell differently; or the wound-phrase being purely biographical (it is *both* — the girls said it, and the mythic template coexists). **Vet:** does "inherited myth-template" earn `[W]`, or is it a clever retrofit of a film on a long list?

## D7 — The John 8:32 decay arc `[W]` (one scripture, three eras, downhill)
- **It's Your Time (2022):** *"the **Truth will set you free** / But the Truth is hard to tell"* `[G]` — held sincerely, with honest difficulty.
- **Bad Feeling (2023):** *"the **truth is a lie** that nobody can tell"* `[G]` — the paradox stage.
- **Lucius Lullaby (2026):** *"the **truth will set you free** but nothing's, nothing's what it seems"* `[G]` — quoted and **bent by the serpent-voice.**
The vault tracks the 2023 and 2026 instances separately; **the 2022 starting point connects to neither.** Drawn as one line: the same verse **degrades era by era — held → paradoxed → weaponized.** The catalogue's clearest measure of the Lucius arc in *scriptural handling.*
- **Vet:** three uses of a very common Bible verse — is the degradation arc real, or three independent uses retrofitted into a trajectory?

## D8 — The Barker's origin `[W]` ("Step right up" begins in Attention's own mouth)
Census `[G]` ×3: **Pay Attention (2022)** *"**Step right up** to my Door / there's a little price you pay"* → **Good Time (2023)** *"Step right up, get on the floor"* → **WTTC (2026)** *"Step right up, shut your mouth."* The GT→WTTC link is drawn; the **three-point chain with its origin in the deity's own voice** is not. The origin matters: in 2022 the barker is **Attention itself**, naming its **price** up front — and the call gets cheaper as it descends: *price* (2022) → *party* (2023) → *silence* (2026). *(PA's closer "may I have all your ears…" = the crowd-turning oration, `[~]`.)*
- **Vet:** is "Step right up" distinctive enough to be a deliberate chain (it's a generic carnival call), and does the price→party→silence descent hold?

## D9 — 33⅓ rpm: the title as the speed of a spinning circle `[~]`
Roy's stated title-meaning is his age (so all title-numerology is demoted to resonance). Within that: **33⅓ rpm is the speed of the vinyl LP** — an album titled *33* is named (in this register) after **a circle that gives back a song when it spins.** And: **a record plays outside-in** — the stylus spirals toward the *innermost* groove. *33* ends on **"The Circle"** repeating *"the **inside of the inside** must be where you go"* — which, on an LP, is **literally where the needle is during that song.** The closer describes the listener's own stylus completing the album; a record is *recurrence with a center* (the spindle) = the Circle/Loop hinge in the object.
- **Vet:** `[~]` by design (Roy says the title is his age). Is the LP/inside-the-inside enactment a genuine deep cut or cute coincidence? It's the one resonance the album's own closing line *enacts* — does that earn it a footnote, or nothing?
