## D2 — Joni "Down to You": re-open the coincidence verdict `[?]→W candidate`
A prior dossier closed the shared title *"Down to You"* as coincidental — *but reached that via the **fame** lens, without the lyric side-by-side it admitted was pending.* Two problems:
1. **The phrase, not just the title.** Roy's hook is *"**it all comes down to you**, comes down to you"* (`[G]` stable lyric). Joni's *"Down to You"* (Court and Spark, 1974) — by report — **resolves on the same phrase** (*"…it all comes down to you"*). **⚠ THE GATE: pull Joni's official lyric (jonimitchell.com) and confirm verbatim before any claim.** If it confirms, this is a **verbatim hook-phrase match with a co-credited writer on the same album** (Joni is credited on *33*'s "Soul Is A Star") — the Woodstock pattern repeating two tracks later.
2. **The wrong lens.** Joni's song is a **duality** text (*"you're a kind person, you're a cruel person too"*) resolved by *what comes down to you* — siblings with Roy's *"nothing ever changes 'til it changes when you be it / you are the one."* And the JT **vertical rotation**: Roy takes the idiom's horizontal sense (responsibility lands on you) and aims it **down** as descent (*"heaven in the sky to the earth below… it all comes down to you"*).
- **Vet:** **pull the Joni lyric** — does the phrase match verbatim? If yes → propose `[W]` ("33's second Joni dialogue") and correct the coincidence note. If no → it stays coincidence. *(This is the single highest-payoff lookup in the packet.)*
