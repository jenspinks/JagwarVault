# ChatGPT Session Archive — Jagwar Twin Fan Site

> Archive note: This is a chronological working archive of the Jagwar Twin Fan Site conversation. ## Standing editorial decisions

- Make the public site approachable for someone new to the Jagwar Twin world: core story first, then clear tempting doors into deeper material.
- The public Field Guide and source vault have different jobs. The Field Guide is a front door; this vault can preserve research, handoffs, drafts, and longer speculative work.
- The public editorial goal is faithful interpretation, not a legal brief. Center the central character/arc meanings, hedge lightly when needed, and place weakly defended or high-complexity material deeper.
- Roy’s direct feedback guides the page architecture and central symbols, but private feedback is not presented as a public citation.
- Public page order: visible material, songs and public statements, strong readings, older maps. Do not mix private DMs with public proof.
- The new-user page should be titled So You Found Jagwar Twin, not a flat Start Here page.
- The voice should be warm, witty, lightly sarcastic, and welcoming. The project’s absurdity is part of its authorship, not an embarrassment to edit out.

## Core working map

Lucius: green apple, crow or Corvus, cigarettes, truth and light divorced from heart, intellect, El Roi on throat; a Hod or Mercury link may be useful as a deeper lens.

Roy: dove named Cygnus, El Roy, sword, heart; a Netzach or Venus link may be useful as a deeper lens.

Mary: carries wound and blessing in beginner-facing language. The Sophia, Malkuth, or other older-map material belongs below the public front door.

The public framing should never say Roy’s own keys or Roy told us this privately. It can say basic symbol map or core character symbols while letting the feedback control what the pages center.

## Dove / Cygnus

Cygnus conventionally means swan, not dove. This should be held as a productive mismatch.

Dove is the heart descending. Cygnus is the song becoming a map.

Dove gives spirit, gentleness, descent, blessing. Cygnus adds swan, constellation, song, transformation, and celestial orientation.

Paired with Lucius’s crow/Corvus:

- Crow: watching, calculating, surviving, moving among ruins.
- Cygnus: singing, orienting, transforming, becoming a path through the sky.

Do not gender Cygnus in beginner copy without a public source.

## Roy page development

Guiding principle: Roy is the human center, not another decorative archetype.

Opening draft:

Roy English is the man behind Jagwar Twin: singer, songwriter, mirror-holder, connection-maker, and the human center that every other figure in this world keeps circling. He is also, and this part matters, a real person, with a birthday, a body, a history, and an ever-growing number of tattoos.

Core thesis:

Everything Roy has built — the albums, the circus, the shadow in the face paint, the mirrors, the whole glittering apparatus — is an elaborate machine for returning a feeling to a seven-year-old boy who got buried for carrying it.

Relational map:

Every other character here is really a position relative to Roy. Sir Lucius is the protector-mind that broke off from him. Mary is the voice of blessing reaching back toward him. The Ballerina Boy is the buried child he is reaching for. The Jaguar is the lens he sees through.

Concrete anti-abstraction line:

Pull Roy out of the middle and the whole cast collapses into abstractions. Lucius becomes a costume. Mary becomes a symbol. Brandon becomes a wound with no body attached. The Jaguar becomes a logo. Roy is the one with a body, a birthday, a voice, and a bruise.

### The Ballerina Boy

The earliest injury the work keeps circling happened at seven, before later roles had names. A boy did ballet at a Christian school, the only one in his class. Adults read it through the wrong lens. Girls around him said he was not a real boy. He prayed to be made good and buried the part that came alive on stage.

He came home crying. He never danced again.

I never danced again / I killed that ballerina boy / I buried him inside

Roy is not simply that child. He is the later self who turns around and goes back for him.

In the released song, the father says: there’s nothing wrong with you, my son.

The wound says hide. The blessing says nothing is wrong with you.

A good boy does not make a good man.

The adult has figured out that the goodness he was sold was a cage. The recovery is Roy going back to un-bury the boy the good boy replaced.

### Mind as beautiful danger

### Obsidian Publish image/caption float

Obsidian Publish sanitizes user wrappers such as div, span, paragraph, figure, and figcaption. It preserves images but may strip the user’s wrapper. Inspection found Publish-generated wrappers:

- image wrapper: .el-img
- immediately following caption wrapper: .el-p

Use a unique alt marker on the exact target image, then target only that image wrapper and adjacent caption in publish.css. Target marker:

<img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/lucius_left.png" alt="hom-lucius-float-card-main">

The rule should set the matching .el-img and immediately following .el-p to float right, width 250px, with responsive mobile reset. The important point: this affects only the exact image with that unique alt marker and only the following caption block.

## Image generation: Lucius, Roy, Mary trio

The session attempted a triangular illustration of Lucius, Roy, and Mary/Sophia.

Desired composition:

- Mary between Lucius and Roy, slightly elevated or standing behind them.
- Bright backlight behind Mary, like sun through a window. Backlight/lens flare/halo hides her face; her face/head is not itself a glowing source.
- Lucius left, Roy right.
- Desired style: close to supplied reference images, less generic cinematic gothic art.

The model repeatedly drifted into photoreal/gothic fantasy or feminized/sexy versions of Lucius. Recommended workflow:

1. Generate Lucius alone from his reference photos.
2. Generate Roy alone with exact tattoo-layout reference.
3. Generate Mary alone with the backlight correctly behind her.
4. Composite or do a final trio generation from successful references.

### Roy tattoo rules

The user supplied many tattoo references and set hard rules:

Mary’s light must come from behind her, like sun through a window. It should cause backlight, lens flare, and overexposure that hides her face. Her face/head should not emit light.
