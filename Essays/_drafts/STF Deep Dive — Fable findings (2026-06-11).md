## F1 — THE WATER-LINE: Eye Alaska → Subject to Flooding → "I am the shore" — a sixteen-year arc in three self-namings **[G anchors · W arc — the headline]**

Brandon's **own 2010 gloss** of his band's name ([[Sources/Transcripts/Eye Alaska Interview - 2010]]):

> *"'Eye Alaska' … means to look to … **that which the sea breaks against.** And it's basically just … people who other people follow are usually the **people who have gone through things to make them wiser.**"*

(The real Aleut etymology — *aláxsxaq*, "the object against which the sea breaks" — matches his gloss. The transcript's own JT-relevance note extracts only the Jay-Z/platform point; **the shore-meaning has never been read against the catalogue.**)

Now lay the three self-namings in a row:

| Year | Self-naming | The water-relation |
|---|---|---|
| **2008–10** | **Eye Alaska** — *"look toward that which the sea breaks against"* | **Looking toward the shore** — aspiration; the wise are defined as the sea-broken |
| **2018** | ***Subject to Flooding*** | **Being the flooded** — the sea overwhelms the self; the album lives underwater |
| **2026** | *"**I am the shore.** I am not the wave… I'm the one who stays"* (X, 04-10; [[Brain/Characters/Roy]]) | **Being the shore** — the self has become the thing the sea breaks against |

**The reading `[W]`:** the artist's relationship to water is the longest continuous thread in his self-conception — *look toward the shore → be flooded → become the shore.* The 2026 aphorism is not a new image; it is the **2010 band-name's meaning, finally claimed in the first person.** And his 2010 gloss already defined the shore as *the wise* — "people who have gone through things" — so *"I am the shore"* (2026) is also a claim to have become **the Wise One**, which is the Circle-myth's name for the one who *remembered* (`[~]` on that last step — registers held apart per figure-distinctness).

**Bonus inside the same name `[W]`:** *"**Eye** = I / to look"* — the **eye/I pun is in his very first band name**, age ~20. The catalogue's entire seeing-axis (El Roi "the God who *sees* me," the Eye of Horus, the Jaguar "who *looks* into souls," the Watchers) is anticipated by a name that fuses *I* and *eye* and points both at *that which the sea breaks against*. The first artifact of his career already contains the two deepest systems: **the eye and the water.**

## F6 — D-R-E-A-M / C.R.E.A.M.: the spelled hook **[~ — the big leap, offered as a leap]**

*Dream / Dream*'s hook is the album's only **spelled** word: *"D-R-E-A-M / Dream, you gotta dream."* The most famous spelled five-letter hook in American music is Wu-Tang Clan's **C.R.E.A.M.** — *"Cash Rules Everything Around Me"* — and **DREAM differs from CREAM by one letter.** Roy is on record as a hip-hop-formed listener (*"the Kinks, and the Stones, to **Biggie**"* — Atwood `[G]`; Jay-Z as a named formative influence — Eye Alaska 2010 `[G]`), and a serial interpolator (Mercer on this very track; Rockwell; Bricusse/Newley).

**The reading `[~]` — if the echo is real:** the substitution *is* the thesis. Wu-Tang diagnosed **cash** as the thing that rules everything; the debut's track 2 swaps in **the dream** — D for C, *Dream Rules Everything Around Me* — the American Dream as the ruling force. And the later catalogue runs the substitution **backwards**: Lucius's bargains re-collapse the dream into cash (*"you want a mansion… advances… fans"* — the dream priced out in EP2). The one-letter swap would be the whole Roy/Lucius economy in an acronym.

**Upgraded 2026-06-12 (Jen supplied the full C.R.E.A.M. text — two corroborations added):**
1. **The dream/cash opposition is internal to C.R.E.A.M. itself** — *"But it was just a **dream** for the teen, who was a fiend"* / *"A man with a **dream** with plans to make **C.R.E.A.M** — which failed; I went to jail at the age of 15."* Wu-Tang's verses already stage *dream crushed into cash* — so a DREAM-for-CREAM swap wouldn't pun on their acronym, it would **answer their argument** (reopen the case they closed). Strengthens the reading; tier unchanged.
2. **Verse + hook structure rhyme:** both verses run youth-crime-survival (*"small crimes but we did our time / kids in our prime"* ↔ *"went to jail at the age of 15 / life as a shorty shouldn't be so rough"*), and both hooks share the **SPELL → imperative** form (*"C.R.E.A.M., get the money"* ↔ *"D-R-E-A-M, you gotta dream"*) — same slot, same function.
Extension noted: a **three-currency series** in the same grammatical slot — C.R.E.A.M. (*cash* rules) → [[Sources/Lyrics/Pay Attention (feat. little luna)|Pay Attention]] (*attention* rules: "everybody's gotta **pay** me") → Dream/Dream (*dream* rules). `[~]`.
**Counter-evidence that keeps it `[~]`:** the credits are Mercer + Vaughan only, and Roy's documented practice is to **credit interpolations** — so no-Wu-credit means allusion-tier at best (Beck-"Loser"-class, not Mercer-class); spelled hooks are common in pop (R-E-S-P-E-C-T, D-I-S-C-O); youth-crime verses endemic to the genre. Convergence of weak signals — exactly what `[~]` is for.

**Discipline:** this is the pass's most speculative item, and it stays `[~]` pending (a) an ear-check of the hook's cadence against C.R.E.A.M., (b) any Roy receipt. The *spelling* device itself is independently notable `[W-lite]`: spelling is how children learn words (the nursery-substrate at track 2 of the debut), and on the letter-mysticism axis (`[~]`, [[The Angels and the Angles]]) a word built letter-by-letter is the *Sefer Yetzirah* gesture in pop form — *"Dream, dream, **out loud**"* = the word spoken into being.
