## Context you need
"Eye Alaska" was Roy's pre-Jagwar-Twin band (c. 2007–2010), under his birth name. *Subject to Flooding* is the 2018 debut. The 2026 lines below are public X (Twitter) posts from the Lucius era. (Full background: the Primer.)

## 1. The three self-namings [G anchors]
- **2008–10 — "Eye Alaska."** Roy's **own 2010 gloss** of the band name (Eye Alaska interview): *"'Eye Alaska' … means to look to … **that which the sea breaks against.** And it's basically just … people who other people follow are usually the **people who have gone through things to make them wiser**."* (The real Aleut etymology — *aláxsxaq*, "the object against which the sea breaks" — matches his gloss.)
- **2018 — "*Subject to Flooding*."** The debut album title. Roy's stated meaning: *"we should all let go of ego… we are all Subject to Flooding"* — the self overwhelmed; vulnerability as universal condition.
- **2026 — "I am the shore."** Public X post (2026-04-10): *"I am the shore. I am not the wave… I'm the one who stays."*

## 2. The arc [W]
Laid in a row, the water-relation inverts and resolves:

| Year | Self-naming | Relation to the water |
|---|---|---|
| 2010 | Eye Alaska — *"look toward that which the sea breaks against"* | **Looking toward** the shore — aspiration; the wise are defined as the sea-broken |
| 2018 | *Subject to Flooding* | **Being the flooded** — the sea overwhelms the self; the album lives underwater |
| 2026 | *"I am the shore… I'm the one who stays"* | **Being the shore** — the self has become the thing the sea breaks against |

So: *look toward the shore → be flooded → become the shore.* The 2026 aphorism reads as the **2010 band-name's meaning finally claimed in the first person** — sixteen years to become the thing the young band only pointed at.

**The further turn [~].** Roy's 2010 gloss already defined the shore as *the wise* — "people who have gone through things to make them wiser." So *"I am the shore"* (2026) is also a quiet claim to have **become wise through what he went through** — which rhymes with the later catalogue's "Wise One" figure (the one who *remembered*). Held at **[~]**, registers kept apart: this is a resonance between Roy's self-description and a mythic figure in his own work, NOT an identity claim.

## 3. The bonus inside the first name [W]
**"Eye" = I / to look.** The **eye/I pun is in his very first band name**, at roughly age 20. The later catalogue's entire **seeing-axis** — "El Roi" (the God who *sees* me) on Sir Lucius's neck; the Eye of Horus; the jaguar "who *looks* into souls"; the 2024 single "The Watchers" — is anticipated by a name that fuses *I* and *eye* and aims it at *that which the sea breaks against*. The first artifact of the career already contains the catalogue's **two deepest systems at once: the eye and the water.**
