## Governing protocol

### Purpose

The dossiers propose readings and findings about Jagwar Twin’s 2018 debut album, *Subject to Flooding*, and in some cases longer threads running from that debut into the later catalogue. ### Retrospective-coherence guard

2018 Roy does not use the later Sir Lucius, Circus, Algorithm, El Roi, or mirror-metaphysics vocabulary. A 2018-to-2026 line may support continuity of preoccupations, but not intentional encoding of later mythology unless contemporaneous public evidence exists.

Useful formulation:

Required vetting outputs:
