# Dossier 02 — The Flood's Double Meaning

**What to vet.** That the album's central element — the **flood** — is **double-valenced**, and that **Roy's own statements supply both poles**: flooding as *drowning* (the threat) and flooding as *the fall of the walls of perceived separation* (the cure). The album's title would then **contain its own remedy.** Judge whether the double-reading is in the material or imposed.

---

## Context you need
The title *Subject to Flooding* is the album's organizing image. Roy's stated meaning (Primer) is "let go of ego… we are all subject to flooding," and retrospectively the flood = polarization/separation. The song "Loser" (track 3) contains the Jericho line. (Full background: the Primer.)

## 1. The Jericho key [W — four Roy-voice anchors]
The "Loser" reading already on file treats *"Jericho fell like a wave"* as *sound destabilizes systems*. What's undrawn is the **lock between that lyric and Roy's own era-statements**:
- *"Jericho fell **like a wave**"* — the wall-collapse rendered AS water ("Loser," lyric [G]).
- *"That **breaking down of walls** really lead to the creative output"* — Roy on the album's own making (Artist Waves 2018 [G]).
- *"There is only **perceived separation**"* — the walls aren't real (Prelude Press 2019 [G]).
- *"…we should all **let go of ego**… After all, we are all **Subject to Flooding**"* — the title's stated meaning (launch press [G]).

**The synthesis [W].** Read one way, flooding = drowning (the threat — "Precious Time," "Shine"'s sea). Read the other way, **flooding = the fall of the walls of perceived separation** — Jericho's wave. So *"we are all subject to flooding"* means both *we can all drown* AND *the ego-walls between us can all come down* — **vulnerability and communion as the same exposure.** The album's title contains its cure: what the flood destroys is the **separation** the album diagnoses as the disease.

**The proof-text mechanism.** In the Bible, Jericho's wall falls **to collective sound** (Joshua 6 — trumpets + the people's shout). That is, eight years early, the mechanism the later catalogue calls the **Circle** — *"the crowd sings it back."* The wall comes down to a shared voice.

## 2. The 2026 answer [W]
The thread is alive at the Lucius era's height. Public X post (2026-04-13): *"The waters are rising but so am I"* — which restates the 2018 closer "Shine"'s chorus verb (*"when I **rise** to a brand new morning"*). With Dossier 01's water-line, this completes the figure: the 2026 Roy answers the flood the way **"Shine"** did (rise, don't outrun) — not the way "Hell Of A Night" did (party through it). The rising-self meets the rising-water.

## 3. The Sirens on the drowning album [~ — small and sharp]
"Hell Of A Night Pt. 2" (track 11): *"**Sirens call out making their sound** / Hallelujah, hallelujah."* The reading on file is *police sirens reframed as praise.* Add a second register: on an album **about drowning**, "sirens… making their **sound**" are also the Greek **Sirens** — the singers whose music lures sailors into the water. The line treats them as *vocalists* ("call out," "their sound," answered by a sung *hallelujah*). The drowning-singers belong on the drowning album, and they prefigure the catalogue's later **fatal-attention music** (the flame the moth mistakes for a call; the algorithmic hook). **[~] only** — the pun rides the surface police-siren reading; do not promote.

## 4. The repost signal [G-repost, low weight]
On premiere-takeover day (2026-03-23), Roy **reposted** a fan post — *"Jericho, when the walls fell?"* — so the wall-fall image is still in his curation at the Lucius era's height. Caveat: it's a fan's post that Roy amplified, not his own composition; weight it as a *curation* signal, not authored text.
