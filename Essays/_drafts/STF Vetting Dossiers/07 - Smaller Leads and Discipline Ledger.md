## Smaller leads (one paragraph each)

- **The jaguar told in both directions [G ×2 · W].** One 2018 interview has the jaguar look *into others, then back into itself*; a 2019 interview has it look *first into itself, then into others.* Not an inconsistency — the mirror-loop has **no fixed starting point** (the reciprocity *is* the doctrine). Worth one line on the Jaguar page so a future reader doesn't flag it as a contradiction.

- **The ledge triad [~].** Three relationships to the same edge: *"I might **jump** 'cause I feel untouchable"* (Good Day — the manic jump) · the age-35 despairing edge (Lucius Lullaby, 2026) · *"I followed anyway… **stepped off the ledge**"* (the SOL poem — the faithful step). Inflation, collapse, surrender. The first is already noted on the Good Day essay; the *triad* is the add.

- **The roof/ceiling series [~].** *"hands up and wave 'em **to the ceiling**"* (Hell Of A Night) · *"ain't got no **ceiling** up on the roof"* (Superhuman — limitlessness) · *"**tear the roof off** of my house"* (Bounce, 2026 — self-exposure). The party-gesture aimed at the ceiling becomes the ceiling's *removal* — exposure escalating across eras.

- **"Fake it like your passport" (Hell Pt. 2) [~].** Forged identity-papers inside the *No Fake Friends*–adjacent album — the persona as counterfeit documents, one track after the fake-detector song ("N.F.F."). The fake/real axis runs tracks 8→11 as a unit: *detect fakes → become one to cross the border.*

- **"Ain't gonna be no face in the crowd" (Superhuman) [~] + the face census [W-lite].** Surface reading: anti-anonymity (everyone seen). The idiom is also the title of the 1957 Kazan film *A Face in the Crowd* — the canonical entertainer-corrupted-by-media-fame story (Lonesome Rhodes as a proto-Lucius); resonance only. But the **face-language census** it joins is real: *disguise on my face* (Long Time Coming) → *for the faceless* (Long Time Coming) → *face in the crowd* (Superhuman) → *Happy Face* (2020) → face-paint (2023→). **The album conducts a face-inventory before the mask era begins.** *(Curio at [~]: the Vaughan banter sampled on the album and the Kazan film are both 1957.)*

- **The faceless inversion has a direction [W-lite].** 2018 *dedicates the song TO the faceless* (they receive — "Long Time Coming"); 2025–26 the faceless one *delivers the blessing* (she gives — the Ballerina Boy outro, Weirdness, #1 Champion). The dedication comes back as benediction — the crowd Roy blessed returns as the voice that blesses him. The registers must stay apart; the **direction-swap** is the observation.

- **Ann Morrison Park (N.F.F.) [?].** A real park — in **Boise, Idaho**, not LA. A concrete biographical anchor nobody has chased: why Boise? (Touring memory? A person's hometown? A childhood chapter?) Pure lead, no claim.

- **Two items already carried in other dossiers** (noted here so they aren't double-counted): *"The waters are rising but so am I"* (2026) restating "Shine"'s *rise* → see Dossier 02; the **"Ryan" in the Arrowhead voice memo** [?] → see Dossier 05 (pinning which Ryan dates the collaborator continuity).
