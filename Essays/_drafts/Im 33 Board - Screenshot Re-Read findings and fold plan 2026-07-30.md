### F2 · Misidentified artwork: not Blake `[G]` correction

Transcript §16 line 242, gallery Group 10, and `Board - The Field` all name **Blake's *Jacob's Ladder***. Verified at 3× zoom (July `7.11.23`, `7.11.47`): it is **not** Blake.

It is **The Ladder of Divine Ascent**, the 12th-century Sinai icon after **John Climacus**: gold ground, Greek inscriptions, a diagonal wooden ladder, monks in dark habits climbing, **black winged demons hooking them off mid-climb** (one falling head-first), a choir of angels upper left, Christ receiving the topmost monk upper right. Blake's *Jacob's Ladder* is a serene blue-and-gold spiral stair with no demons and no assailants.

**Why this matters beyond tidiness.**
1. **Opposite semantics.** Blake = untroubled ascent. Climacus = ascent **under attack**, figures pulled off at every rung, only some arriving. That is a far better fit for *"Transformation is directional, not guaranteed"*, the FAILURE MODES table, and the Lucius protective-overreach reading.
2. **It has propagated to 6 files, two of them public-vault staging.** `Jacob and Esau.md` and `Jacob and Esau — The Deep End.md` build a **Bethel / Genesis 28** argument partly on "Blake's *Jacob's Ladder* is pinned on the board." Climacus is a **monastic** ascent icon, not Bethel. The Genesis-28 anchor weakens and must be re-hedged before that page ships.
3. **Damage is bounded.** [[Framework Registry]] already files it class **(b) — "present only by analyst identification"**, i.e. the vault knows Roy never named it. So this is a wrong analyst ID, not a wrong Roy claim.
