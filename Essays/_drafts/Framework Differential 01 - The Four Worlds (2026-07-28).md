## The verdict, up front

**`grounded-label / unpopulated-schema`.** Roy demonstrably **holds** the Four Worlds. He names it three times, once in his own voice about the mark at the center of his own body. He never once **operates** it: he never names the four, never orders them, never assigns anything to them, and never uses any of the tradition's standard keys for them. So the honest disposition is **the doctrine added to the existing portable-doctrine reference, and a short fenced anchor-section on the Tree page** recording that Roy names it and never operates it (§7, revised). It is **not** a structural axis, **not** a reading key for songs, and **not** something to map member-for-member onto our own fourfolds (§6, corrected).

That verdict is more useful than either answer Jen might have feared: it is neither "nothing here" nor "a hidden master key." It is *a real item in his kit that he never picked up*, which is itself a finding about how he works.

## 6. Collision hazards — corrected 2026-07-28 after Jen's challenge

> ⚠ **This section originally over-stated the hazard, and Jen caught it.** Her objection: *"are the 4 worlds not generally mapped to the keilim in Jewish mysticism?"* She is right, and the correction sharpens the guard rather than removing it. The superseded framing said, in effect, "do not connect the worlds to the vessels." That was wrong. The corrected framing is below.

### 6a. In the tradition, the worlds and the vessels are ONE doctrine, not two

This must be said plainly because the earlier draft implied otherwise. In Lurianic Kabbalah the Four Worlds and the *kelim* are not separate systems that someone later mapped onto each other. They are the same system described from two angles:

- **Each of the four worlds is itself a complete Tree of ten sefirot**, that is, a full set of vessels. Worlds are not *adjacent* to vessels; worlds are *made of* vessels.
- **The shevirah is what produces the lower worlds.** The shards of the broken vessels fall and become the substance of the worlds beneath Atzilut; the *kelipot* descend to the lowest. The breaking is the origin of the ladder.

So there is no bridge to invent here. The tradition supplies it. **Connecting the vault's vessel-doctrine to the four-worlds material is not a manufactured correspondence, and the guard must not forbid it.**

### 6b. But there are TWO different "worlds" taxonomies, and conflating them is the classic error

This distinction is the one the vault actually needs, and it is currently missing from [[Sources/Reference/Shevirat HaKelim (Lurianic doctrine reference)]]:

| | The **process worlds** | The **ABiYA ladder** |
|---|---|---|
| Members | Olam HaNekudim (Points) · Olam HaTohu (Chaos) · Olam HaTikkun (Rectification) | Atzilut · Beriah · Yetzirah · Assiah |
| What they are | **stages of the cosmic drama**, conditions | **standing ontological strata**, a ladder |
| Count | three | four |
| In the vault | ✅ **present** in the doctrine reference | ❌ **absent entirely** |

They are related, the ABiYA worlds emerge from the Tohu-to-Tikkun movement, but they are **not the same list**, and the vault's reference file has the first and not the second. **That, precisely, is the gap.** Not "the Four Worlds are missing from a vault that knows nothing about worlds," but "the vault holds the process-worlds and lacks the ladder."

### 6c. The real hazard, stated correctly

The hazard is **not** worlds-to-vessels. It is aligning ABiYA with the vault's **own two constructed fourfolds**:

1. **The four fates of the vessel** — hold, open, shatter, hatch ([[Brain/Concepts/The House & the Vessel — Container Imagery (hold, open, shatter, hatch)]]).
2. **The four-container sub-typology** — prison, initiatory, vessel, husk ([[Keilim - The Breaking of the Vessels]]).

Two reasons that specific alignment would be a category error, not a finding:

**(i) They are different KINDS of category.** The four fates are *events*, what happens to a container (a verb list). The four worlds are *levels*, what plane a container exists on (a noun list). A vessel at any of the four worlds can hold, open, shatter, or hatch. These are **orthogonal axes**, so a 1:1 alignment would not be a correspondence at all, it would be a coordinate confusion that happens to look elegant because both lists have four members.

**(ii) The vault's own two fourfolds do not even map onto each other.** Test it:

| Four fates | Nearest container-type |
|---|---|
| HOLD | **two** matches, prison *and* husk |
| OPEN | kli / vessel |
| SHATTER | **no match**, shattering is an event, not a container type |
| HATCH | initiatory |

Four fates resolve to three typology slots, with one fate covering two types and one orphaned. **If our two fourfolds cannot align with each other, aligning either with a third fourfold from outside is pattern-matching, not structure.** The four-ness in both is an artifact of how each list was assembled: the container typology reached four by finding a gap in a three-item list (the Sir Rabbit poem supplying "a missing fourth sub-type"), and the four fates are a constructed synthesis. Neither predicted four; both arrived at it.

**Corrected guard to carry onto any node:** *The Four Worlds may be related to the vessel doctrine, because the tradition itself relates them. What may NOT be done is aligning ABiYA member-for-member with the four fates or the four-container typology. Those are our categories, they are a different kind of category (events and types, not strata), and they do not align with each other.*

### 6d. A live question this raises about the vault's own architecture

Worth Jen's attention independently of the Four Worlds: **the vault carries two overlapping fourfold container-taxonomies that do not align.** They are two carvings of one concern (what a container does with what is inside it), made at different times for different purposes. The Keilim page treats House & Vessel as "the broader architectural imagery audit" and itself as the Lurianic application, so they are already framed as related-but-distinct. That may be correct. But the non-alignment above is a small architectural smell worth a deliberate look: are these one concept that should be reconciled, or two that should be more clearly separated? Flagged, not answered.

### 6e. The hazard that stands unchanged

The **tetramorph mapping** (Roy/Lucius/Mary/Jaguar as four claimants) is **RULED-OUT** in the [[Claim Ledger]] and flagged there as "the line most likely to drift." A Four Worlds node is an obvious back door for it to walk back in through, since four worlds and four figures is exactly the shape of the retracted claim. Name it as ruled-out on the node itself.
