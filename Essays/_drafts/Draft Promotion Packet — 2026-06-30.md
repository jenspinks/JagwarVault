# Draft Promotion Packet — 2026-06-30

This packet reviews every draft item currently tracked in `_System/Drafts Fold-Status.md` and supplies either a promotion-ready public/canon version or a clear reason not to promote. It is intentionally conservative: public-facing promotion means the piece can stand without private material, unverified social allegations, overbroad source claims, or draft-only speculation.

## Promotion rules used

A draft can be promoted if it has a stable canon home, public-source anchors, clear claim tiers, and a counterargument/guardrail where the interpretation is ambitious. A draft should remain non-public if it is a context primer, a working adversarial packet, a private/source-sensitive mining note, or a bundle of open leads rather than a coherent essay.

When a draft has already been folded into canon, the promoted version is not a new essay. The correct promotion action is bookkeeping: stamp the draft `status: folded-to-canon`, add `folded: YYYY-MM-DD`, and ensure the canon page links back to the draft as provenance.
