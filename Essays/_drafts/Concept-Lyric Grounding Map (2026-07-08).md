# Concept ↔ Lyric Grounding Map (2026-07-08)

> **What this is.** A CONCEPT → SONG/LYRIC grounding index. Every concept page in the vault was tested against all **47 released-song lyrics**, then each proposed lyric anchor was **adversarially verified**: the verbatim line was re-checked against the source file, and the reading was stress-tested for apophenia (word-present / meaning-absent false friends, imported-framework overlays, extra-lyrical grounding smuggled in as lyric). The output per concept is a **net_grounding** verdict and a per-anchor tier. This is a read-only findings draft. Nothing is folded.

> **How to read the tiers.** `[G]` grounded (line + meaning both present) · `[W]` strong read (≥2 anchors, hedged) · `[~]` resonance (framework correspondence, attributed). A trailing **↓** means the tier was *downgraded* during verification from the originally proposed tier (the verified tier is the one shown). **Rejected** links are omitted from the index (surfaced only where a concept lost *all* of them, and in §Negative evidence). No tier was ever upgraded past what verification supports; a couple were corrected *up* only where a credited interpolation had been under-tiered (noted inline).
