### Canon thesis `[W]`
A surveillance-dream song: the self is replicated, consumed, judged, and watched by a fascinated crowd-machine, while it tries to work out whether it's awake, dreaming, or already a clone of itself. Where *City of Angels* externalizes the horror, "The Watchers" internalizes it; where *Lucius Lullaby* sedates, this is the paranoid alarm clock. Keeper: "The Watchers is the moment the Hall of Mirrors grows eyes."
