# The Overarching Picture: The Caption Corpus as Roy's Second Text

## Thesis

Read whole and in date order, the social-caption corpus (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, the war-lore posts) stops being marketing and becomes a second authored text running parallel to the catalogue [W]. It does four things at once: it tells one serialized myth in dated installments; it publishes a curated curriculum (scripture, Gurdjieff, Bailey, Fortune, Levi, Jung, Steiner, Hall, Watts, Tolkien, Rowling, Disney) whose sequence tracks the album campaigns; it encodes a small, stable doctrine in aphorism form; and it functions as paratext that teaches the audience, in real time, how to read the albums the theatrical layer is busy dramatizing. Roy names the practice himself in a 2024-09-27 reply: "lore shadowing" [G caption; the method-reading held W]. The unifying doctrine underneath everything is the serve-hinge: the mind as servant of the heart, division as both wound and initiation, the exit inward rather than outward. Every layer states it in its own register.
