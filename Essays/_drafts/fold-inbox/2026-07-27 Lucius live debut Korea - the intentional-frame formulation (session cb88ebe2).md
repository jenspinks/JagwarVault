## 1. The formulation (the thing Jen asked to keep)

Seeded as Claude's synthesis in-session; **adopted and extended by Jen 2026-07-27, so held at `[P]`**:

> **"The frame was intentional, and what filled it was real, and this work's entire method is building frames where the real thing has to happen."**

Jen's extension, verbatim:

> **"The project functioning to create frames of spectacle that draw the eye to witness real magic and transformation."**

The claim, unpacked: intentionality in this work operates at the level of **conditions, not outcomes**. Roy designs the frame (costume, setlist placement, staging geometry, camera position) and then channels rather than acts inside it (grounded: *"i realized i cant act i just have to let him come through"*, [[Brain/Characters/Sir Lucius]] §Performance/embodiment). So "was it intentional or was it real" is a false alternative: the design guarantees that something real will occur and be witnessed; it does not script what the real thing is.

**The delta:** a project-scale method statement unifying song-scale Trojan Horse, staging-scale rigged-instrument, and performance-scale channeling into one sentence. No page currently states it.

## 3. The Korea evidence, graded (compressed from the session analysis)

Four separate intentionality objects, four different answers:
1. **Conditions of the melt** (winter eared hood + heavy wool + jacket + water-soluble paint, high-80s daylight): **intentional, near certain.** Wardrobe is always chosen; the outcome was physically inevitable; stage-durable paint exists. The rigged-instrument structure exactly.
2. **The melt as image** (dissolving during Happy Face, the mask-song, descent timed after the melt advanced): **strong read, intentional in design, environment-paced.** Third channel: @jagwartwin **reposted the melted-face fan footage within hours**, including worn-paint festival-grounds selfies. Retroactive canonization at minimum ("Put it in the lore cannon").
3. **Communion staging** (repeated mic-outs; the descent; a stabilized camera in the pit): **likely planned in structure.** Two-channel highlight: the mic held out on **Loser's final chorus**, the corpus's textbook I→we communion pivot (*"We're singing / Baby, I'm a loser"*, [[Communion vs Spectacle]] §sing-back device). The staging performs the lyric's own architecture.
4. **The emotional texture** (hand-clasp, foreheads, mutual desperation): **not scripted, and canon says it couldn't be** (channeled, not acted `[G]`). And "Roy breaking character" may be a category error: the **Pleading register** (childlike, dependent, real, "the layer the other two defend") plus "no audience → no Lucius" means a Lucius starving for embrace when handed ten thousand mirrors is the character with defenses down, not the character abandoned. `[W]`

## 4. The falsifier collision `[W]` (highest-value single finding)

[[Communion vs Spectacle]] (written 2026-06-30) names its own falsifier: *"a Lucius-voiced song genuinely inviting distinct-voiced return."* **Korea staged it live**: the Lucius-costumed performer repeatedly invited distinct-voiced return (mic-outs, shared mic, singing with individuals), musicality sacrificed for it. Two canon-compatible resolutions, matching Jen's two hypotheses:
- (a) **Deliberate doctrine evolution**: the album was "Lucius handed the microphone"; the live show is Lucius handing it back. The shadow performing communion = the integration arc staged.
- (b) **The voice-split held**: the costume said Lucius, the behavior was Roy-voiced by the concordance's own markers; the Roy voice took the body mid-set.
Also note the direction delta from the documented live grammar: Live Underground was assessed "genuine-but-misdirected communion" (aimed at the brand); Korea's was aimed at **persons** (eyes, hands, foreheads), i.e. toward the witnessing pole of [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]. If sustained across shows, the arc is moving.
