# Part I — The frame: the archive's authored fields are richer than the ones we have been reading

The catalog work established which layers of an artifact are *authored* (Roy/team-fixed) and which are community-made: title, media type, realm, and the code→artifact pairing are authored; spatial position is solver-made and carries nothing. **The upgrade thesis in one sentence:** stop describing the image, and read the authored relations the item sits inside.
