## 2. Jen's flag — why the new material strains it `[open]`

The soul-hinge proposal (fold report F11/U11) resolves "mind serves heart" vs "mind serves soul" by reading the board's rung 6, **"The mirror of the Sun and the Heart"**: the heart is the **mirror** of the sun — the place where source-light lands.

**The strain:** *mirror / reflector / receives-light-from-elsewhere* is exactly the vocabulary the vault uses for **Yesod, the moon, and Lucius's operating register.** If the **heart** is now the mirror of the sun, then:

- the reflector role is no longer diagnostic of Lucius at all, and

- the thing that distinguishes Lucius stops being *"he reflects"* and has to become something else — presumably *what he reflects*, or *whether he admits he is reflecting.*

**That may be the actual finding**, not a problem: reflection is the corpus's universal condition (*"everything is a mirror"*, *"we reflect ourselves to ourselves"* — the board, `[G]`), and the split is not reflector-vs-source but **faithful mirror vs distorting mirror** ([[The Flip and the Distorting Mirror]] already exists for this). Lucius's failure would then be the *counterfeit* claim to be the source, not the act of reflecting. **Unworked. This is the dossier's central question.**
