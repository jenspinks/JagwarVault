What it's for

The Finds Ledger says 31 rooms still open. The first group is why that is wrong: those codes were known all along — two of them are simply the minted room-videos for Another Way II Heaven and The Circle. Walk the sealed group by eye; anything recognisable from the public gallery is another one to subtract. What survives that walk is the real frontier.

How to check it yourself

The bucket serves each file by the hash of its code. Take a code already known — rebirth — run it through keccak256 and you get 0x8fdf93e7…, which is exactly the Family Portrait video's URL. That is the entire mechanic. For these rooms we hold the hash, so the art is reachable; we don't hold the word, so the door isn't.

One honest caveat

This sheet shows the local bench snapshot, not the ledger's own accounting — the two were assembled at different times and the totals will not line up exactly. Treat it as the visual aid for the reconciliation, not as the new number.
