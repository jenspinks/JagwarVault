## Grounded

Debut studio album. Produced with a "we, not me" collaborative ethos (Roy's public framing). Roy's stated theme: individualism, authenticity, removing masks. Breakthrough single: "Loser" (premiered Zane Lowe / Beats 1, 17.4M Spotify streams). Sports sync "Long Time Coming" and "Shine" (standalone single from this era) were commercially successful. The album was released via Atlantic Records.
