# So You Found Jagwar Twin

🎪 Welcome to the Circus! 🎪
You probably arrived through a song, and maybe you are feeling a bit confused.  

Maybe it was [[Happy Face]], the hit that smiles at you while it ticks like a timebomb. Maybe it was [[Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa)|Bad Feeling]], the one that sounds like a party until the floor drops out. Maybe it was [[Welcome to the Circus]], because apparently the algorithm has a sense of humor and a red coat.

Good! However you got here, that song was a door. The slightly alarming news is that the door opens both ways.
<img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/lucius_left.png" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 16px; margin-bottom: 8px;">

Here is the whole thing, up front, before the tent swallows you:

<strong>Jagwar Twin is a mirror disguised as a pop project: a modern myth about going down into the dark, finding the self you buried there, teaching the mind to serve the heart, and discovering that the way back runs on love, not conquest.</strong>

🎠 That is the part to hold onto. Everything else is furniture. Beautiful furniture, haunted furniture, possibly cursed furniture, but still sofas all the way down.

The rest of this page just walks that one sentence, clause by clause.

## A mirror disguised as a pop project

Jagwar Twin is the project of Roy English: singer, songwriter, mirror holder, connection-maker, occasional ringmaster of meaningful nonsense.<img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/roy_port.jpeg" style="float: left; width: 120px; margin-right: 16px; margin-bottom: 8px;"> On the surface, these are catchy pop songs with big hooks, creepy smiles, circus lights, and swirling melodies. The hooks are the candy coating, and Roy has as much as said so: the whole thing is a Trojan horse, steak to hide the meds, a fairy tale to hide the philosophy.

Hiding what? A mirror. Roy gives us his mission again and again: to be the clearest mirror he can be, to remind people who they truly are. So this is not finally a story about a man with excellent cheekbones and a complicated relationship with apples. It is pointed at you. Stay long enough and the songs start asking their questions in your direction: which of your voices are true, which masks helped you survive, and whether you still know the way home.

## Going down into the dark

Start with the name on the door, because it gives the game away. Roy has been telling the story for years, never quite the same way twice: the jaguar is the creature that can look into its own dark places and into the souls of other people, and each kind of seeing teaches the other. The jaguar does not avoid the dark. It walks in on purpose, learns the terrain, and comes back out pulling others into the light with it. And the Twin? Roy names it flat out: the dark and the light. The name was the spoiler all along.

Roy likes to hand people a line he credits to Jung: the taller the building, the greater the shadow. The more light you are trying to hold, the deeper the dark it casts. He has been down there himself and says so plainly: if I can do it, anybody can do it. Into the dark, back out, others in tow.

## Two poles, one person

There is Roy, the heart of the project. And there is Sir Lucius, <img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/lucius_apple.png" style="float: left; width: 120px; margin-right: 16px; margin-bottom: 8px;">the sharp voice in the white face paint, the green apple, the cigarette, the grin that knows exactly where to poke the wound.

In the story, they are not two parallel characters; they are two poles of one person. The same tensions run under the whole catalogue, heart and mind, the real face and the performed one, and the later chapters give them names, costumes, and a stage. Once they have the stage, much of the later story is the two of them arguing, bargaining, masking, performing, and trying to break the loop.

<img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/shadowboy.png" style="float: left; width: 65px; margin-right: 12px; margin-bottom: 0px;">Lucius is not the bad half. That would be too easy, and honestly would not make for a compelling story arc. He seems to begin as protection: the part of the mind that stood up when something hurt too much. He is brilliant, fast, theatrical, cruel when he thinks cruelty will keep the vulnerable, soft, buried bits safe, and very good at making the cage sound sensible. He tells the truth, which is where the danger lies. The truth without heart can still become a weapon, and the trouble starts when the guard takes the throne and calls the prison home. So: trust him. Well, do not trust him either. He is messing with you, but some of it is for your own good.

Once you hear that, the songs start changing shape.
 
 <img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/ball1.png" style="float: left; width: 20px; margin-right: 12px; margin-bottom: 5px;">[[Happy Face]] stops being just a smile and becomes an order: put the mask on, and if you are sad, do not let it show. 
 
 <img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/ball1.png" style="float: left; width: 20px; margin-right: 12px; margin-bottom: 5px;">[[Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa)|Bad Feeling]] stops being just a swaggering chant and starts sounding like the hair on your arms standing on end before the mind can explain why.
 
 <img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/ball1.png" style="float: left; width: 20px; margin-right: 12px; margin-bottom: 5px;"> [[Welcome to the Circus]] stops being *only* a banger and becomes the ringmaster's sales pitch: step right up, take your seat, scream and shout, consume the spectacle and in turn be consumed by it.

## Teaching the mind to serve the heart

The songs never ask Roy to kill the shadow or amputate the mind. Nobody wins that fight, and winning it was never the point. What they keep pointing at instead is an order: the mind coming back into the service of the heart. Jung, whom Roy quotes by name, would call that integration: the shadow not defeated but taken back in. An embrace, not a funeral. Roy gives us the mantra straight, in the LOOP era: my mind must serve my heart. The heart in this story is not sentimental mush, either. It has a blade. It has to cut through the mask without becoming another mask, and learn to see itself.

## The way back runs on love

The force that can turn the loop into a circle has an embarrassingly simple name: love. Yes. That one. Not the greeting-card kind: the kind that stays open after injury, meets another person as a mirror rather than an audience, and lets connection change you without erasing you. The carnival runs on your attention. The way home runs on love.

## The old languages it speaks

The songs speak several old languages at once, fluently, and mostly without footnotes. In Jung's terms, Lucius reads as a shadow, and the buried child as the self the shadow was built to protect. In the language of [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life|Kabbalah]], the catalogue keeps climbing a Tree, and Roy has sketched that climb himself on his own board. The [[Hermeticism - As Above So Below|Hermetic]] motto is printed right on his title cards: as above, so below, the small story and the big one rhyming all the way up. The [[Gnosticism - The World as Trap|Gnostics]] would recognize the trap at a glance, a false show mistaken for the world. But listen closely: where those old maps say escape, these songs keep answering return, repair, come home. And under all of it runs the oldest current on the page, the jaguar myth, the creature that enters the dark on purpose.

None of this is homework. Roy seems to hold every framework the same way this guide does: loosely, as tools rather than gospel. You can enjoy every song without knowing any of it. But if you like trapdoors, every one of these has a room under it.

## The rest of the cast, briefly

And then there is Mary, the third voice. She walks in already named, in [[No. 1 Champion (la Conquistadora)|No. 1 Champion]]: Mary, Mary, so contrary. Mary, Mary, such a revolutionary. Keep it simple for now: this guide reads Mary as the presence that calls the hidden self back into the light, the one line Lucius cannot metabolize: stop pretending, baby, you were born to shine. ✨ Whether every blessing-voice in the catalogue is hers is an open question. This guide leaves the wings partly dark on purpose.

Much of the drama plays out inside what we can call [[Concepts/The Circus]], one very loud room in the myth-world, not the whole of it. The attention machine. The show that never closes. The place where they love it when you lose control. It looks like the music industry, Hollywood, your feed, and suspiciously like the inside of a human head at 2:17 a.m.

## The story so far

Three albums, three movements.

🌊  [[Subject to Flooding]] is the flood and the wound. 

🎰 [[33]] is waking up inside the machine.  

🎪 [[Lucius Lullaby (album)|Lucius Lullaby]] is the strange turn where the shadow/protector gets the microphone, his name on the marquee, and enough rope to decorate the tent.

And one song keeps returning: [[The Circle]]. Roy calls it the favorite song that has ever come through him, and he has said, on the record, that he thinks The Circle is and will be Jagwar Twin's biggest song. Not the biggest so far. The biggest, someday, whether or not he is around to see it. He really believes that. He even gives us the growth law: the circle will grow from the inside of the inside. No, you are not supposed to understand that yet. That is what the rest of the guide is for.

You do not need a decoder ring or a tarot deck to navigate this, but if you have them available, backup never hurts. Here is a simple map to get you started. One caution before you memorize it: these are recurring emblems in a living story, not a substitution cipher, and a few of them trade hands.

Lucius holds 🍏 the green apple, 🐦‍⬛ the crow, 🚬 the cigarette, 🗡 the dagger, and 🧠 mind divorced from heart (distortion).
 


 
 


Roy has 🩷 the heart, ⚔  the sword, 🕊️ the dove (but not some simple dove of peace, he's called 🦢 Cygnus and he holds to that name's tradition), and 🎶 the song that he arrived with...the one that keeps trying to get free.   

Mary carries 🩸🧬 the wound and 🌹the blessing: the part that fell, and the part that still remembers how to rise.

The 🎪 Circus holds the spectacle.

One more thing, before you choose. If you are one of the weirdos out in the shadows waiting for someone to say it is okay: it is okay. [[Weirdness]] sings it with a full choir behind it: your weirdness is the sunshine. Please do not fade into the crowd. Imagine the dull beige world that would be left behind. We cannot lose any of you. 🖤

Now pick a door.
