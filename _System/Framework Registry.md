# Framework Registry — the lens register

**Why this exists.** The vault has excellent discipline for *claims* ([[Claim Ledger]]) and for *provenance* ([[Brain/Concepts/Inherited Substrate]]). It has had no discipline for **lenses**. A framework that fit was adopted, and the search stopped there. Jen named the failure on 2026-07-28: *"many times we found a concept that did fit and applied it and stopped looking… I want to make sure we have the BEST fit, and the other (larger/recognized) fits are documented."*

The pieces of the fix already existed and fired case-by-case, after the fact: the [[Brain/Concepts/Inherited Substrate|tier ladder]], the stream-discipline guard, the no-master-key rule, [[feedback_two_channel_rule|the two-channel rule]], and the Counterarguments sections. What was missing is a **differential gate with institutional memory**: a place where a lens cannot be adopted until its rivals have been run on the same anchors, and where that comparison is *remembered* so the next session does not redo it or quietly forget it.

**Relationship to the other trackers.** [[Claim Ledger]] = contested claims (struck / ruled-out / private-only / load-bearing). **This file = the lenses those claims are read through.** [[Brain/Concepts/Inherited Substrate]] supplies the tier law every row obeys. [[Theme Tracker]] supplies the analytical vocabulary. When this registry and a source page disagree, **trust the page** and update the row, same convention as the Ledger.

## The gate — a framework is not adopted until all five are done

**1. Name the discriminating markers first, before searching.** Not the framework's mood, its **fingerprints**: the names, numbers, images, and structural moves its neighbours do *not* share. A framework whose markers are all shared with three rivals cannot be *found* in a corpus at all, only projected onto it. Writing the marker list down before the sweep is what stops the search from becoming a search for confirmation.

**2. Sweep the pre-interpretive stratum only.** `Sources/**`, `Brain/Media/`, `Brain/Roy Voice/`. **Never** `Essays/`, `Brain/Concepts/`, `Brain/Characters/`, `Brain/Songs/`, `_System/`. This is the anti-contamination clause and it is the load-bearing step: **a framework must never earn points from our own prior framework pages**, or the vault begins confirming itself and the [[CLAUDE|named risk]] arrives by the front door.

**3. Weight by evidence-kind, not count.** Per [[feedback_evidence_weighting]]: recurrence is the cheapest evidence; contrast, transformation, and uncopyable anchors are the dear ones. Ten ambient hits lose to one uncopyable one. A quote Roy reposted is cheap. A gloss he gave for a permanent mark on his own body is dear.

**4. Run the competitors on the same anchors, and include Roy's own incumbents.** The serious rivals are usually **not other traditions**. They are Roy's own other schemas, which is why §The syllabus below enumerates every level-schema he has actually drawn. A framework wins only if it explains at least one anchor its runner-up cannot.

**5. Record verdict + falsifier + re-audit trigger.** What would overturn this, and what new evidence would reopen it. A row without a falsifier is an opinion, not a finding.

**And the standing two-channel gate:** per [[feedback_two_channel_rule]], a fit folds only on **two independent channels**, and a grounded framework is never itself evidence for a specific gesture. One written phrase is one channel. Check that the two channels are genuinely independent, not the same note copied twice.

### ⚠ Step 6, added 2026-07-28 — before calling a correspondence "manufactured," check whether the tradition already makes it

Learned the hard way on Row 1. The first draft of the Four Worlds dossier warned against connecting the four worlds to the vault's vessel material, on the reasoning that joining two fourfolds would fabricate a correspondence. **Jen challenged it: *"are the 4 worlds not generally mapped to the keilim in Jewish mysticism?"* She was right.** In Lurianic Kabbalah each world *is* a full set of vessels, and the shevirah's shards *constitute* the lower worlds. The link is internal to the doctrine; there was nothing to fabricate.

**The gate had an asymmetry.** Steps 1 to 5 are all built to catch **over**-claiming, which is the vault's historical failure mode. They contain no brake on **under**-claiming, and this is the second time that asymmetry has bitten: [[Brain/Concepts/Inherited Substrate]]'s own Counterarguments section already flags it (*"the method is asymmetric… there is no symmetric brake on treating genuine internal architecture as mere inheritance"*), and the Walter Russell false-negative was the first instance. An over-cautious guard is not automatically the safe choice; it can suppress a real structure just as a loose one can invent a fake one.

**So, before writing any "do not connect these" guard, ask two questions:**
1. **Does the tradition itself already relate these two things?** If yes, the guard is wrong as written. Say what the tradition says, then guard the *specific* move that would still be illegitimate.
2. **Are the two lists the same KIND of category?** Row 1's real hazard turned out to be a category error, not a fabrication: the four fates are *events* (what happens to a container), the four worlds are *strata* (what plane it exists on). Orthogonal axes that both happen to have four members. **Matching counts is the weakest possible reason to align two lists**, and a shared count across different category-kinds is a warning sign, not evidence.

A useful diagnostic fell out of it: **if two of our own lists share a count but cannot be mapped onto each other, the count is an artifact of how each was assembled, not a structure.** The four fates resolve to three of the four container-types, with one fate covering two types and one orphaned. Both lists *reached* four; neither *predicted* it.

**Corollary, added the same day: a counter-read is a claim, and must be retrieved-against like any other.** Row 1 floated "Christ-identification vs Christ-orientation" as a live contest and kept the losing side on the page as loyal opposition. The corpus had already settled it (*"Jesus is my North Star"* `[G]`; the 2023 post relocating **John 14:6** to *"within us"*), and the dossier had even written that counter-read's own falsifier without going to look for it. **Manufacturing balance is not rigor.** An objection generated from general skepticism rather than from the corpus makes *settled* material look unsettled, which corrodes confidence in what is genuinely grounded. Sweep for the objection before staging the contest; if the corpus refutes it, say so and move on.

## Verdict vocabulary

Built for a syncretist. Roy demonstrably stacks traditions **on purpose**, so "which single tradition is this?" is sometimes the wrong question, and the vocabulary has to be able to say so.

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **best-available-frame** | Wins the differential; explains anchors the rivals cannot. |
| **deliberate-confluence** | Two or more traditions are present *because Roy put them together*. The stacking is the finding; picking one would falsify it. |
| **compatible-but-undiscriminated** | Fits, but so do its rivals on the same anchors. Present side by side (the [[Keilim - The Breaking of the Vessels]] three-streams pattern). **Never break the tie on elegance.** |
| **grounded-label / unpopulated-schema** | Roy names it but never operates it. Record the naming; build nothing on it. |
| **watchlist** | No Roy-side anchor yet. Logged so it is not rediscovered from scratch every six months. |
| **ruled-out** | Tested against primary sources and failed. Must be **primary-source-grade**, per the Walter Russell false-negative caution: a weak search is not a ruling. |

## Maintenance

- **One row per lens.** A new framework gets a row *before* it gets a page, not after.
- **Re-run a row** when a new Roy artifact surfaces (an unread HoM leaf, new ink, a new board), or when its falsifier is touched.
- **Per-file markers remain authoritative.** When this registry and a source page disagree, trust the page and update the row.
- **Row order of operations:** Row 1 = B1 Four Worlds (done). **Row 2 = A1 Alchemy**, on priority: it is the only lens embedded in the frontmatter schema itself, and it has never been tested.
