# Cross-Vault Fold Status — PUBLIC → SOURCE

**Why this exists.** On **2026-06-30** the public Field Guide (`Vaults/The Jagwar Files`) was scaffolded by migrating out of this vault. Since then the interpretive centre of gravity moved into the Field Guide (reader pages get written, and new readings are born in the act of writing them), but this source master — the analytical authority — never received most of it. Nothing tracked that: [[Drafts Fold-Status]] and [[log]] stop at the vault boundary, and the Field Guide's `_internal/provenance.tsv` maps only SOURCE → PUBLIC *origin*, never PUBLIC → SOURCE *fold state*. So public-born readings were structurally invisible here. This ledger is the fix.

**Convention (same as [[Drafts Fold-Status]]).** When a row is folded, set the target page's provenance backlink to the PUBLIC home, stamp the row `✅ folded (date)`, and where a whole PUBLIC page is now fully represented here, note it. A row is *not* done until the reading is in the structured layer (`Brain/`) or its essay, not just acknowledged.

**Seed.** Rows below are the 27 verified divergences from the cross-vault audit of **2026-07-11** (8-finder + adversarial-verify sweep; 56 raw → 32 unique → 27 confirmed; the 5 already-in-source rejections and several finder confabulations were dropped in synthesis). Direction is PUBLIC → SOURCE unless marked ⏪ reverse. All rows start `⬜ pending`.

> ⚠ **Tier discipline holds across the boundary.** Fold at the tier the reading earns here, never inflate a Field-Guide reader-simplification into a grounded source claim. Two rows carry hard caveats (Qoph/Moon, the board-coordinate reverse-flag) — read them before folding.
