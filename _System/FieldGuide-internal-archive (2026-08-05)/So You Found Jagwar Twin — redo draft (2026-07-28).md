# So You Found Jagwar Twin

🎪 Welcome to the Circus! 🎪
<img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/jagwar-twin.jpg" style="float: center; width: 300px; margin-left: 16px; margin-bottom: 8px;">

You probably arrived through a song. Maybe it was [[Happy Face]], the radio curse that smiles while it bleeds in the backseat of a taxi cab. Maybe it was [[Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa)|Bad Feeling]], the one that sounds like a party until the floor drops out. Maybe it was [[Welcome to the Circus]], because apparently the algorithm has a sense of humor and a red coat.



However you got here, that song was a door.

Jagwar Twin is the project of Roy English: singer, songwriter, 
mirror holder, occasional ringmaster of meaningful nonsense. On the surface, catchy pop songs with big hooks, creepy smiles, and circus lights. Underneath, the songs tell one long story, and the story runs on one engine.

## The engine

<img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/roy_port.jpeg" style="float: left; width: 120px; margin-right: 16px; margin-bottom: 8px;">There is Roy, the heart of the project. And there is Sir Lucius, the sharp voice in the white face paint, the green apple, the grin that knows exactly where to poke the wound. They are not two parallel characters; they are two sides of one person. Most of the catalogue is the two of them arguing, bargaining, masking, performing, and trying to break the loop.

And the fight is not about destroying one side. We are given the resolution from the start during the release of [[LOOP]]: *"My mind must serve my heart."* Not silence the mind. Not slay the shadow. Put it back in service. Tame the beast. 

That is the part to hold onto. Everything else is furniture. Beautiful furniture, haunted furniture, furniture that drew your eye...but if your mind follows your heart everything else will work out, eventually. 
## The toolkit

The furniture does arrange itself, though (haunted, remember). A handful of ideas keep coming back, album after album, and once you can spot them you can start reading this catalogue on your own. Here they are, each with the best place to catch it in the act.

**The Twin**: most everything here comes in paired opposites. Roy has said it plainly since 2019: *"The twin is just the nature of everything we've been talking about. The dark and the light."* Light and dark, heart and mind, the singer and his shadow: nearly every pair in the story descends from this first split. It is on the front door, in the project's own name. Deeper: [[Duality]].

**Heart and Mind**: the split, personified. Roy carries the heart; Lucius is what a mind sounds like when it loses its cricket <img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/Jiminy_Cricket.webp" style="float: left; width: 50px; margin-right: 16px; margin-bottom: 8px;"> (see Pinocchio). The best seat in the house is [[The Interview]], seven short theatrical episodes where the two of them sit down face to face. Deeper: [[Heart and Mind]].

**The Mask**: put the smile on, hide the hurt. [[Happy Face]] reads as the order itself: if you are sad, do not let it show. Watch how often a face in this story is painted, performed, or frozen. Deeper: [[The Mask]].

**The Circus**: the attention machine, the show that never closes. It looks like the music industry. It looks like a stage. It also looks suspiciously like your feed. [[Welcome to the Circus]] is the ringmaster's sales pitch: step right up, scream and shout, consume the spectacle and be consumed by it in turn. Deeper: [[Concepts/The Circus|The Circus]] and [[The Algorithm]].

**The Loop and the Circle**: the center of gravity for the whole catalogue. A loop is repetition that changes nothing: the same orbit, no arrival. A circle has a center, and a way home. [[The Circle]] sings the founding myth, where *"a wise one found a small cave where the Jagwars used to sing, and the wise one remembered she once held the string,"* and its refrain gives the direction of travel: *"the inside of the inside must be where you go."* Three years later, [[LOOP]] names the trap. Deeper: [[The Loop and the Circle]].

**The Mirror**: when *33* came out, Roy wrote that he prayed the album would serve *"as a mirror and a reminder of who you are."* He describes the jaguar itself as the creature who looks into the souls of others and then back into itself: *"humans are mirrors for each other."* The songs want to reflect you, not just entertain you. They want to show you to yourself. Deeper: [[The Mirror]] and [[Source vs Reflection]].

**Inversion**: the dark trick of the whole story: keep the shape, reverse the direction. Roy wears one example on his skin, a tattoo reading *"Fear functions like prayer in reverse."* The Circus reads as the Circle inverted: same ring, opposite spin. A lullaby, sung by the wrong voice, starts to sound like sedation. Deeper: [[Inversion]] and [[The Circle (Lucius Version)]].

**Waking up, and remembering**: another tattoo says *WAKE UP*. <img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/mufasa-remember.gif" style="float: left; width: 250px; margin-right: 16px; margin-bottom: 1px;">[[The Watchers]] gives the same instruction out loud, and [[It's Your Time]] makes it kind: *"you forget where you come from… find your way back."* In this story nobody becomes someone new. They remember who they are. Deeper: [[Roy's Tattoos]].

**Seen vs. watched**: on Roy's throat sits *El Roi*, a name of God from Genesis meaning "the God who sees." The songs keep asking whether being watched is the same thing as being seen, and [[The Watchers]] answers with a borrowed, paranoid hook: *"I always feel like somebody's watching me."* An audience watches. A witness sees. Deeper: [[El Roi]] and [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]].

## The cast, and what they carry

<img src="https://plumcreekranch.com/jagwar/lucius_apple.png" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 16px; margin-bottom: 8px;">

[[Roy]]: the heart. His side of the map is warmer but not weaker. He carries 🩷 the heart, ⚔ the sword, 🕊️ the dove (no simple peace-dove; he is called 🦢 Cygnus and holds to that name's tradition), and 🎶 the song that keeps trying to get free.

[[Sir Lucius]]: the mind divorced from the heart. Not simply a villain; that would be too easy. He seems to begin as protection, the part that stood up when something hurt too much: brilliant, fast, theatrical, cruel when he thinks cruelty will keep the soft, buried bits safe. He tells the truth, which is where the danger lies. Truth without heart can still become a weapon. He holds 🍏 the green apple, 🐦‍⬛ the crow, 🚬 the cigarette, and 🗡 the dagger.

[[Mary]]: the third voice, the presence that calls the hidden self back into the light. She is the exiled source. She carries 🩸 the wound and 🌹 the blessing, and her message is the one Lucius cannot metabolize: you were born to shine. ✨

And underneath all three, a buried child. [[Ballerina Boy]] is the song that stands closest to the wound everything else was built to protect.

## The older maps

Roy does his homework in public. He has taught Alice Bailey's *Labours of Hercules* on a live stream, posted Gurdjieff on waking up, and opened the [[Down to You]] video with a title card reading AS ABOVE SO BELOW. There is even a spreadsheet: [[The I'm 33 Board]], a board of his own making where a surprising amount of this cosmology sits in plain text. You do not need any of it to love the songs. All of it is waiting downstairs in [[The Deep End]].
