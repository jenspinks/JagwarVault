## §2 Received text (verbatim)

## Overall judgment

The site does not need less depth. It needs a much smaller visible vocabulary and a more deliberate teaching order.

The earlier simplification plan was conceptually right: "a story with doors," with the cathedral preserved behind it. The sidebar work partly accomplished that, but the homepage and hubs gradually became catalogs again. A newcomer is still shown the architecture of the vault before they understand the story the architecture is describing.

The right model is:

1. Understand one human story.
2. Learn three or four organizing ideas.
3. Choose a path based on what interests you.
4. Encounter the full archive only after choosing depth.

The surface should feel like a small, lucid site built over an enormous one.

## What the audit found

The public vault contains 215 Markdown pages and approximately 616,000 words. The median page is 2,481 words; 188 pages exceed 1,000 words and 93 exceed 3,000. That size is not inherently a problem because most of it can remain below the waterline. The problem is that the entrances frequently reveal the size before they establish a reason to care.

The deployed homepage spends its first screen on the theatrical hero and disclaimer. The actual "Start here" choice begins below the fold. It then offers seven section choices, followed by sources and site methodology, before the visitor has learned the central relationship. You can see that structure in [The Jagwar Twin Field Guide](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/Start%20Here/The%20Jagwar%20Twin%20Field%20Guide.md:8).

The linked newcomer page promises "the whole story in five minutes, no homework," but it is 1,056 words and requires approximately 25 paragraphs and more than three desktop screens before reaching "Pick a door." Before the choices appear, it introduces Roy, Lucius, Mary, the Circus, three albums, multiple songs, two character symbol kits, the Algorithm, masks, the heart/mind structure, Cygnus, and several interpretive claims. The actual navigation begins at [So You Found Jagwar Twin](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/Start%20Here/So%20You%20Found%20Jagwar%20Twin.md:71).

The Concepts hub is the clearest example of the remaining problem. It correctly says "Start with these five," but then puts nearly the entire conceptual ontology beneath those five on the same page: Pattern, Jaguar, Machine, Inversion, Audience, Algorithm, Consumption, Aquarium, multiple mirrors, Protector, Upload, Labyrinth, Hall of Mirrors, sacred traditions, mythological correspondences, and scholarly tools. At 1,732 words and roughly 56 links, it is still an encyclopedia presented as an introduction. See [Concepts](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/Concepts/Concepts.md:12).

This is especially instructive because the existing [Site Simplification Master Plan](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/_internal/Site%20Simplification%20Master%20Plan.md:29) already specified the correct architecture: story first, doors second, stacks third. The sidebar CSS confirms that much of the shelving work was implemented in [publish.css](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/publish.css:698). The next pass should therefore concentrate on sequencing and consolidation, not another large-scale navigation redesign.

## The proposed surface model

A newcomer should be able to leave the first five minutes holding four statements:

1. **One singer, two voices.** Roy and Sir Lucius enact two parts of one divided self.
2. **The mind was built to protect the heart.** Lucius is not merely a villain; the protector becomes dangerous when it takes the throne.
3. **The Circus turns protection into a system.** Performance, attention, masks, metrics, and repetition can keep the self moving without bringing it home.
4. **The songs search for a return.** The Circle, the heart, true witnessing, Mary's blessing, and the remembered song all point toward restored relationship rather than destruction of one side.

That is enough structure to make the songs and characters legible. Everything else can unfold from those statements.

I would not teach "Heart and Mind," "Duality," "Loop and Circle," "Circus," and "Source vs Reflection" as five separate concepts during the first encounter. Heart and Mind and Duality are two descriptions of the same initial condition. The Loop, Circus, Algorithm, Aquarium, and Labyrinth are related models of entrapment. Source vs Reflection, the Mirror, witnessing, and the Digital Double belong to a shared relational question.

The surface should therefore use four plain-language chapters or questions:

| Surface question | What it quietly consolidates |
|---|---|
| **Who is speaking?** | Roy, Lucius, Mary, the divided self |
| **What went wrong?** | Heart and Mind, Duality, Protector |
| **What keeps the split running?** | Circus, Loop, Machine, Algorithm, Audience |
| **What might bring it home?** | Circle, Source and Reflection, witnessing, remembering, song |

These are teaching containers, not destructive merges. The existing pages and URLs remain available underneath them.

## Recommended front door

The homepage should contain the explanation currently deferred to the next page. It should not require an initial "Enter the Guide" click followed by another introduction.

A possible first-screen structure is:

> **Jagwar Twin is a music project about one person arguing with the part of himself that learned to survive.**
>
> Roy carries the heart. Sir Lucius is the sharp protective mind cut off from it. Mary is the voice that calls the buried self back.
>
> **Hear three songs · Meet the voices · Follow the story**

A smaller fourth link can say **Open the full Field Guide**. The unofficial-guide disclaimer should remain, but as two quiet sentences below the first choices or in the footer. Sources, methodology, confidence terminology, and the affectionate-nonsense joke should not occupy beginner attention.

The hero can remain visually distinctive, but it should be shorter. At present it creates atmosphere without conveying the central premise. The visitor should understand what Jagwar Twin is before being asked to "enter."

## What to do with the current hubs

The existing short [Reading Paths](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/Reading%20Paths/Reading%20Paths.md:8) system is one of the site's best-designed surfaces. Its pages are only around 330–413 words and already give a reason for each step. It should be promoted as **Ways In** or **Trails**, while the filename and URLs remain unchanged.

The first paths should be based on why someone arrived, not on the site's taxonomy:

- **I came from "Happy Face."**
- **I want to understand Roy and Lucius.**
- **I noticed the symbols.**
- **I found the Hall of Mirrors.**
- **Take me to the Deep End.**

The Concepts hub should show only the four surface questions. Under each card, offer one short answer, one representative song, and one "go one layer deeper" link. The complete concept catalog should live in The Map or a "Browse every concept" utility at the bottom.

The Characters hub should lead with Roy, Lucius, and Mary only. Brandon, Sir Rabbit, the Wise One, the Crowd, the System, BMRC, SARAVI, and the Jaguar remain available under "More figures and forces." The current hub is reasonably organized, but its first paragraph attempts to explain all ontological categories before presenting the cast. See [Characters](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/Characters/Characters.md:9).

The Symbols hub has a strong basic map, but it immediately expands into the rest of the shelf. Keep the Roy/Lucius/Mary pocket map visible and put everything else behind "More recurring signs." The existing table in [Symbols](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/Symbols/Symbols.md:12) is useful; the newcomer does not need the banana, Beth, the Fool, the Wheel, the Thread, the full tattoo system, and the color ladder during the same visit.

Albums & Eras is already the cleanest major hub. Its three-act structure is memorable and should become a primary navigation route. The music should be treated as evidence and experience, not merely another shelf beside Concepts and Symbols.

Hall of Mirrors should remain a distinct attraction. It is not part of the minimum conceptual curriculum. A visitor who arrives through it should get a dedicated trail, while everyone else encounters it after understanding the central Roy/Lucius structure.

## Page rules for the iceberg

Every surface page should follow a firm attention budget:

- No more than approximately 400–600 visible words before the visitor makes another choice.
- No more than three primary destinations at the end.
- No more than five inline links before the "go deeper" boundary.
- One plain-language gist.
- One song, scene, or artifact that lets the visitor verify the idea.
- One person or adjacent concept.
- One explicitly marked Deep End link.

The deeper material can remain on the same page in collapsed drawers. The current Heart and Mind page demonstrates the right underlying technique: a concise gist and "Where it enters," followed by optional maps, readings, questions, receipts, and Deep End material. See [Heart and Mind](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/Concepts/Heart%20and%20Mind.md:14). The problem is inconsistency and link proliferation; even that page ends with six "Read next" links rather than three.

Depth should always be consensual. Labels such as **Short version**, **One layer deeper**, **Below the waterline**, and **Deep End** tell readers what they are choosing. Dense pages should not apologize for being dense, but they should never masquerade as beginner pages.

## Priority implementation order

I would execute this as a focused second simplification pass:

1. **Rewrite the homepage.** Shrink the hero, state the human premise, show three doors, and move sources and disclaimers down.
2. **Replace or radically compress "So You Found Jagwar Twin."** Move the choices to the top, reduce the body to the four statements, and eliminate most inline exits. The existing long version can become "The Story So Far" one layer down.
3. **Convert Concepts into four umbrella cards.** Move the exhaustive list to The Map or a full catalog subsection.
4. **Promote Reading Paths as Ways In.** Add arrival-based routes and link them directly from the homepage.
5. **Tier Characters and Symbols.** Roy/Lucius/Mary first; core symbol pocket map first; everything else explicitly secondary.
6. **Standardize the surface-page budget.** Apply the same gist, evidence, three-door, and Deep End structure to the first-hour pages.
7. **Clean the utility layer.** Keep The Map, sources, methodology, and archive indexes searchable but outside the newcomer sequence.

There is also a publishing-governance issue worth separating from the design work. Ninety-seven files in the public vault carry `status: draft`, and live pages demonstrate that this metadata does not itself prevent publication. The homepage even routes directly into a draft-status introduction. A later quality pass should create a real publication manifest or validator so "draft" means something operationally. The malformed source link at [homepage line 37](/Users/jenniferspinks/Vaults/The%20Jagwar%20Files/Start%20Here/The%20Jagwar%20Twin%20Field%20Guide.md:37) is a small example of why that gate would help.

## Final recommendation

Keep the cathedral. Remove the requirement that visitors understand its floor plan before entering.

The site's simplest possible spine is not a list of concepts. It is a story: one self splits, the protector takes power, the Circus turns that split into a system, and the songs search for a relationship that can bring the parts home. Once that is understood, Duality, Kabbalah, the Algorithm, the Moon, the artifacts, the boards, and the Hall of Mirrors become discoveries instead of prerequisites.
