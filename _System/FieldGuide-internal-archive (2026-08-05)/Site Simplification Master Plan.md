# Site Simplification Master Plan (v2)
*"JT for kindergarten" up front, the cathedral still standing behind it.*

**Status:** Jen approved the direction 2026-07-06 and revised the plan before execution. This v2 folds in that review. The one question the whole project is judged by: **can someone who only knows "Happy Face" understand the site in five minutes and want to click one more thing?**

---

## 1. North star

**Roy's brief (private relay, INTERNAL ONLY):** organize for new people; basics feel buried; "if you're new, start here"; outline the basics, "JT for a kindergarten"; uniform off-white nav titles; simplify the color palette.

**The character keys (internal framing):** Lucius = green apple, crow (Corvus), cigarettes, "truth and light divorced from heart," the intellect, El Roi on the neck, could link Hod/Mercury. Roy = dove (named Cygnus), El Roy, sword, heart, could link Netzach/Venus.

**⚠ PROVENANCE GUARD (hard rule):** these keys GOVERN the site's structure, but public pages never call them "Roy's keys" or imply private access. Public framing: **"the basic symbol map"** or "core character symbols," in the site's normal hedged voice. Internally: Roy called it out, center it. The keys' content is independently defensible from public canon, so the map needs neutral titling, not heavy hedging.

**Jen's constraints:** core canon forward; centrality over epistemic groundedness up front, strict provenance discipline under the hood; hedge inline ("this likely points to…"); bury weak material deep, never delete; tempting teasers; warm and simple in public, rigorous in private.

**The measured problem:** 168 pages, 608,000 words, median Concept page 4,205 words. The current "New?" block links to the two heaviest pages on the site. Roy's recommended listening order (The Circle → Happy Face) is buried mid-page in Happy Face. Reading Paths exist and nothing routes into them.

## 2. The architecture

The site becomes **a story with doors**: story first, doors second, stacks third.

- **One new entrance:** `Start Here/The Story So Far.md`, the five-minute telling. The single dominant "new here?" destination.
- **A stupidly simple sidebar:** 7 sections, story-ordered: **Start Here · Albums & Eras · Characters · Symbols · Concepts · Hall of Mirrors · The Deep End**. Folders sit collapsed by default, so the at-rest sidebar is 7 rows.
- **Expansion caps (the real overwhelm metric):** a folder click should never unroll a wall. Targets: Concepts ≤ 9 visible files, Symbols ≤ 9, Characters as-is (hub already curates), Albums & Eras shows the three acts + Singles with songs nested beneath. Everything past the cap is reached through hubs and inline doors, not the sidebar.
- **Nothing moves:** the folder tree is frozen. All restructuring = new pages, rewritten hubs, and CSS (hide + reorder). Zero URL breakage.
- **The Deep End:** the deep pages stay live and searchable, hidden from the sidebar, entered through a marked door. Tagline (Jen's): *"Older maps, stranger symbols, longer essays. Not required. Very much available."*

## 3. The funnel

**Homepage: one card, one line, three doors. Nothing else above the fold.**
> **New here? Start with The Story So Far.** Five minutes. No homework.
> Rather listen first? Start with "The Circle," then "Happy Face."
> Doors: **Meet the Characters · Decode the Symbols · Go Deeper**

Disclaimer becomes two straight sentences; the "even the real parts are not real" joke moves to a footer where insiders will still find it.

**The Story So Far → Trails → hubs → core pages → The Deep End.** Reading Paths are re-presented as **Trails** (display retitle only: H1s and door-copy change, filenames and URLs do not). The Story page's "go deeper" door offers trails by name: *The Beginner Trail · The Lucius Trail · The Symbols Trail · The Deep End Trail.* A new reader who wants guidance is handed a trail, never a catalog.

## 4. The Story So Far (drafting spec)

**550–650 words, 7 beats, children's-book cadence, zero epistemics vocabulary, hedges inline.** The reader should walk away holding exactly this (Jen's north-star paragraph): *Roy and Lucius are two voices in one story. Lucius is the sharp mind, the protector: the green apple, the crow, the smoke, truth without heart. Roy is the heart: the sword, the dove named Cygnus, the one trying to choose. Mary is the third voice that calls the buried self back into light. The Circus is the system they're trapped in. The songs are the trail.*

The 7 beats:
1. **One singer, two voices** + the engine line: "Roy and Lucius are, in the story, the same person. Most of the songs are the two of them talking. That's the whole engine; everything else here is detail."
2. **Hear it first:** The Circle → Happy Face, then Welcome to the Circus, Weirdness.
3. **The protector.** "He saved Roy first. Then he took the stage." Apple, crow, cigarettes. → door: Sir Lucius. (Sentences borrowed from the pilot page's balanced opening; all three registers held.)
4. **The heart, and the third voice.** Roy: dove/Cygnus, sword, El Roy. Mary: "you were born to shine." → doors: Roy, Mary.
5. **The ring they fight in.** → door: The Circus.
6. **Three acts:** Subject to Flooding (the flood and the wound) · 33 (waking up inside the Circus) · Lucius Lullaby (the protector gets the microphone). → doors: album pages.
7. **The basic symbol map** (two-column pocket table, EXACTLY the keys, neutral title) + four doors: Meet the cast / Walk a trail / Decode the symbols / The Deep End.

## 5. Page templates

**Core pages = the Collapsible-Core template** (already piloted on Sir Lucius; that page and its session are untouched by this workstream). Adapted per type. Additions: a one-line story ribbon on core and song pages; ≤120 words above the first fold; Read next = exactly 3 links (one sideways, one deeper, one song).

**Depth-section renames, APPROVED, sequenced after the Lucius pilot is signed off:** The grounded core → **On the record** · Strong readings → **Between the lines** · The esoteric framework → **The older maps** · Counter-readings → **Other ways to read it**.

**Deep pages:** two-line header ("You're in the deep end. The short version lives at [[X]]."), a ~100-word short version, then the essay uncut. Dense is the point down here. Word-count badges ("6,300 words, bring water") live ONLY on the Deep End index, used sparingly; the joke helps readers consent to depth, it should not become a dashboard.

## 6. The teaser system

- **Locked-Door pattern** for inline doors: one concrete image + one open question + one link, placed where the reader would ask. Never "see also."
- **The Deep End index:** placard line per page (teaser hooks for all 81 pages already drafted; see Appendix A column 3).
- **The two-door rule (hard gate):** no page is hidden from the sidebar until it has ≥2 inbound doors from visible pages. Audited by script before each publish.
- **Open Questions** keeps its register and gets routed from the Story page's trails door.

## 7. Voice, tone, and safety

- Public voice: warm, simple, hedged inline. No confidence-tier vocabulary above the fold. Rigor serves the connection (the Joni lesson); nothing on a core page should sting if Roy reads it.
- Provenance discipline stays strict under the hood (public / private / strong read / deep speculation, never mixed), it just stops being the front door.
- Standing rules: no em-dash where a comma works; SARAVI and The Wise One remain fully separate; no Hall of Mirrors solutions or private codes; private relays direct, they are never cited.

## 8. Color (finish Roy's ask)

Approved: palette = charcoal + cream + gold + one carmine accent. Nav labels nudged toward true off-white; the Roman-numeral chips go all-gold; active-page glow all-carmine. (Uniform bolder labels already shipped.)

## 9b. Model and effort schedule (built-in switch points)

It is wasted on mechanics (CSS, code reassembly, plumbing, publishing), where Opus or Sonnet give identical results. Switch down the moment a task becomes assembly. You can either flip the main model at the 🔀 marks, or stay on Opus and let me route the Fable steps to subagents.

## 12. Risks

1. **Story-first flattens Lucius.** The Story page states causality ("he saved Roy first"); every character line checked against all three registers (manipulation, shadow, protective love).
2. **A new era breaks the three-act frame.** The frame lives only on two short pages (Story So Far, Albums hub); re-cutting later is a 30-minute edit.
3. **Depth goes dark instead of tempting.** The two-door rule gates Phase 4; the Deep End is dressed as an attraction; CSS-hiding degrades safely (if Publish ever breaks it, the site reverts to today's nav and nothing sensitive leaks, because nothing sensitive rides on hiding).
4. **Session collision.** Sir Lucius pages untouched; renames wait for pilot sign-off.
