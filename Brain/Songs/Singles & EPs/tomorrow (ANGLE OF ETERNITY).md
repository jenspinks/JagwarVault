# tomorrow [ANGLE OF ETERNITY]

Type: Song · standalone single (2024), Jagwar Twin × lovelytheband collab `[G]`; writers unconfirmed `[?]`. Lyric: AZLyrics single-source, streaming-unverified `[?]` (full text filed).

**Core:** a threshold-anthem of remembrance. Standing at the doorway of mortality, the song dissolves certainty rather than resolving it through doctrine, until what remains isn't *"I know"* but *"I AM."* Death becomes a doorway, life becomes remembrance, reality becomes holographic, and presence is the only thing left standing. `[W]`

## Grounded
*(All lyric bullets inherit the AZLyrics `[?]` above.)*
- **Title stylization:** lowercase *"tomorrow,"* uppercase *"(ANGLE OF ETERNITY)."*
- **"I AM" climax:** the outro is a massive repeated chant of *"I AM."*
- **Doorway bridge:** *"if death is just a doorway… where you goin'?"*; *"do you remember?"*
- **Hologram line:** *"We're all holograms / but even if that's all we are…"*
- **Surrender hook:** *"If we live like we ain't gonna die."*
- **Held uncertainty:** *"Maybe I don't know, I don't know,"* recurring.
- *"In a war that I lost and won."*

## Strong Read
- **[W] Remembrance, not attainment (the lead)** — *"do you remember?"* / *"take my hand, I'll help you remember"*: salvation recovered, not acquired (anamnesis). Grounded cross-song pattern: [[The Circle]] (*"the wise one remembered she once held the string"*) → [[Soul Is A Star]] (*"do you know who you are?"*) → *tomorrow*. Speaker = psychopomp/guide through the death-doorway.
- **[W] The [[Playing to the Gods]] inversion** — both hinge on *"if [conditional] → deathlessness"*: Lucius clutches (*"if I make it out alive, then I'm never gonna die"* `[G]`), *tomorrow* releases (*"if we live like we ain't gonna die"*). Survival-clutch vs surrender. *(Both lyrics `[?]`; the parallel hook is the reliable part.)*
- **[W] "I AM" = the divine name, hedged for the collab** — Exodus 3:14; contrasts Lucius's externalized boast (*"I'm the one they're talking 'bout,"* Welcome to the Circus): identity in source vs in audience. Writers `[?]` gate the pure-Roy attribution.
- **[W] Holograms = information projected into form** — less nihilistic than it sounds: the visible form expresses deeper information; presence (*"I AM"*) is secure, so no panic, unlike [[LOOP]]/[[Bounce]] simulation-dread. (Public holographic-principle grounding.)
- **[W] Title's "angle" = perspective** — orientation toward reality: fear/mortality/separation vs eternity/remembrance/presence. The doorway is fixed; your angle isn't. The angle→angel pun stays `[~]`.
- **[W] Uncertainty integrated, not resolved** — *"maybe I don't know, I don't know / and that's fine with me"*: holds both poles ([[Duality]] polarity), mature, not merely inspirational.

> Tier note: the angle/angel framework is **private-only** ([[The Angels and the Angles]]), not cited as grounded; the structure/breath idea is re-grounded on the public logos tradition `[~]`.
