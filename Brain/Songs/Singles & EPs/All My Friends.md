# All My Friends

Standalone single, **2023-05-26**, Big Loud. Writers **Roy English, Colin Brittain**; producer **Colin Brittain** (Spotify-verified, [[Sources/Lyrics/All My Friends]]). Tour video = white-on-black Earth + figures + flying saucers ([[Master Source List]] §3). Lyric Spotify-stable.

## Voicing
[[Roy]], first-person → communal — pre-persona-arc (2023; pre-Lucius billing). Roy's release-week X post grounds the communal frame: *"I released this song today and played it in LA for the first time with all my friends"* (05-27, [[Sources/Quotes/Roy X Posts - 2023 (May-Dec) DRAFT]]).

## Moves
- [W] **Communal presence, not nihilism** (supersedes "communal nihilism"): the end isn't fixed or denied — the answer is *go out together, not alone* (solidarity, not doctrine). Roy-backed (played it *with friends*). Held tension: beautiful (chosen love) **and** unsettling (accepting the going-down) — the [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Life Is Good|Life Is Good]] double-edge; both 2023 songs meet world's-end with chosen togetherness.
- [W] **Clone vs. friends:** loneliness *inside overpopulation* (replicated, not connected); the crowd flattens to a clone, friends restore particularity → [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]] (city sees a copy; friends see you). **Retrospective seed** of [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/City of Angels|City of Angels]]' *"everyone looks just like you"* (2026) — architecture, NOT 2023 intent.
- [W] **Jenny — dance traded for the answer:** embodiment → certainty (preaching); *"big red flag"* double-coded (banner + warning sign). The song trusts being-together over her answer. `[~]` dancer-who-"used-to-be" ↔ [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy|Ballerina Boy]]. `[?]` **"Jenny / = / 408"** — public release-day post (05-26); meaning open (number-song trope / area code / timestamp — none asserted).
- [W] **Spin ≠ win:** *"can't stop spinning / thought I was winning"* = the [[Brain/Concepts/The Circle|Loop]] (rotation ≠ progress); *"no time left on the clock"* = game-clock — winning collapses when the clock ends, pivot to *be-with* (`[~]` clock ↔ [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Precious Time|Precious Time]]).
- [W] **"Flying on this rock" — universalized:** Earth = the vehicle; the species as a friend group on one ship; intimate chorus + planetary frame = one image at two scales (the tour-video Earth animation literalizes it).
- [G/W] **1981 drum = the suspended drop:** *"In the Air Tonight"* = prolonged tension before the fill; the societal "drop" felt approaching like that drum — and dread as **inherited** (a rhythm arriving through time). Allusion `[G]`, function `[W]`.
