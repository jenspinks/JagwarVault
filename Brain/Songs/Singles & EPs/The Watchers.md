# The Watchers

Type: Song · **standalone single / 3-song EP**, 2024-10-04, **Big Loud Records** (NOT on [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]]). Writers **Roy English, Ryan Daly, Ryan Spraker, Rockwell**; **credited interpolation: Rockwell, "Somebody's Watching Me" (1984)** (Apple credits `[G]`; lyric still `draft`).

**Core:** [W] **Surveillance-dream song**: the self replicated, consumed, judged, watched by a fascinated crowd-machine, unsure if it's awake, dreaming, or **already its own clone**. [[City of Angels]] externalizes replication horror, this internalizes it (*maybe I'm the copy*); [[Lucius Lullaby]] sedates, this is the **alarm clock**. Keeper: **the Hall of Mirrors grows eyes**, reflection escalating into surveillance.

## Grounded
- Hook: *"(I always feel like somebody's watching me)"* + *"And I have no privacy"* (Rockwell). Lineage `[G]` with Bricusse/Newley, Joni, Mercer ([[Dream - Dream|Dream]]): **participation in an older song** ([[Inherited Substrate]]).
- Clone-fear: *"I'm afraid I'm a clone / I think I might be… on my way to my own demise."*
- Dream + awakening: *"Tell me, is it just a dream?"* → repeated *"Wake up."*
- Crowd + consumption: *"More fascination / intoxication / I'm making bacon… now they want candy / but I'm not Mandy Moore"*; *"the whole place is dancing like Michael Jackson."*
- Scapegoat: *"You betrayed me / tried to frame me… tried to make me out to be the bad guy"*; *"runaway train 'bout to crash."*
- Credited instruments: **theremin** (Ryan Daly), mellotron, accordion.
- Paratext: Roy's IG Story, Jung CW 18 "The Significance of Dreams" (signs vs symbols), cropped past legibility, centered on Horus / the cross, scored to this song. Roy-posted Gurdjieff: *"man is asleep… he must wake up."*
- Rockwell facts `[G]` (Rolling Stone dossier, §Sources): Rockwell = **Kennedy William Gordy, Berry Gordy's son**; stage name + fake accent to shed nepo-copy status; wrote it from a floor-prayer + testimony vow; "King of Paranoia Rock"; domestic watcher; **MJ sang the hook uncredited**; Gordy dismissed the demo then claimed it.
