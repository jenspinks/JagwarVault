# Good Time (1:08)

Type: Song · Standalone single, released **2023-09-29** (MV 2023-10-11), Big Loud, 2:36. Writers Roy English / Lucky West / Elijah Noll / Josh Dun; producers Roy English / Lucky West / Elijah Noll. Lyric + credits **Spotify-verified** (2026-06-10).

**Core:** the song where **Sir Lucius is born.** Roy's public origin: a dream, he a rabbit on a highway, a clown-makeup rabbit in the passenger seat, *"here to help"* yet *"manipulating me in some way,"* ended by a lightning-bolt semi-truck at 1:08 (the title). Bright party-pop rides directly over that. `[W]`

## Grounded
- **Dream-origin, Roy-voice** (dream-story TikTok, §Sources): party → Drake's white minivan (*"it's your turn to drive"*) → Roy alone **as a rabbit**, *"looking for something, but I didn't know what I was looking for"* → *"I'm Sir Lucius… I'm here to help you… He had all the answers… I had a weird feeling in my heart… telling the truth, but he was manipulating me in some way."* `[G]` that Roy tells it this way.
- **The ending:** *"white semi truck with a lightning bolt comes, boom"*; Roy wakes at **1:08**; the crash then manifests in waking traffic that day. `[G]` as told.
- **Dream-callback lyric, Spotify-verified:** *"Just a rabbit in a road rage"*; *"I don't know what I'm looking for"* (the dream's exact phrase); party frame *"step right up, get on the floor."*
- Verse 2: *"You're a star, you know you are / Stick with me, I'll take you far"*; pre-chorus: *"I'm a mastermind / I don't need a witness."*
- *"Truth is on the mountaintop where man can pray to lightning"*; *"Youth is not a fountain, it's a hand grenade in hiding."*
- **2023 face-paint video** (full makeup + knight armor + *"I'm a mastermind"*): earliest assembled-Lucius iconography, three years before the 2026 co-credit. → [[Sir Lucius]].
- Credits corroborate the TikTok session detail (Lucky West + Elijah Noll; Josh Dun = Twenty One Pilots).
- **2014 self-portrait** (`DON'T BE A BLACK HOLE`) already wears this song's forehead-cross face-paint. → [[Artifacts - Drawings and Sketches]].

## Strong Read
- [W] **The song IS the dream.** Near-verbatim callbacks (rabbit/road-rage, "looking for," the party frame); sung from inside it, not about it.
- [W] **Lucius's voice in the verses.** V2 = the passenger-seat pitch (flattery + the offer to lead); pre-chorus = his self-designation; the face-paint figure sings his own arrival.
- `[G]` words / [W] mapping: **truth-that-deceives + heart-over-mind, dated 2023.** The heart registered what the mind couldn't argue against ([[Duality]]); grounds three-register Lucius ("here to help" AND "manipulating" in one figure).
- [W] **"Step right up" = proto-Circus barker.** The bright version of [[Welcome to the Circus]]'s call; the party that is already the trap.
- [W]/[~] **"I don't need a witness" = anti-witnessing** (vs [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]); pointed given the El Roi throat axis.
- [W]/[~] **Lightning as truth/revelation.** The mountaintop lyric welds the dream's lightning-truck to truth-at-the-summit (Mountain motif); the crash as revelatory full-stop.
- [W] **Rabbit vs rabbit = self vs self.** Lucius is an adaptation of Roy's own rabbit-nature, not an invader; Dweller logic ([[The Dweller on the Threshold]]).
- [W] **Lucius arrives only after Roy takes the wheel.** An answer to not-knowing, not a teacher; the figure with all the answers fills the vacuum of direction.
- [W] **"I don't know what I'm looking for" = the anti-Lucius confession.** Roy keeps moving in honest uncertainty; closer to [[tomorrow (ANGLE OF ETERNITY)|tomorrow]] than to a party anthem.
