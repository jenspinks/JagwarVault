# BALLERINA BOY

Type: Song · standalone single, **June 13, 2025** (Apple Music); ℗ 2025 **Big Loud Rock / Mercury (UMG)**; 3-track (~9 min; other 2 unpinned). **Not on [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]]** despite the pre-album window ([W] the *why* = interpretive; no public statement found). Voice: [[Roy]], first-person autobiography (lyric explicit); direct, no Lucius mask.

**Core:** The wound-song / origin chamber of the arc: the buried child saying *come back for me* ([[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Not Your Homie]] inverted). Age-7 shame buries the true embodied self; the adult fork chooses inward rescue: blessing over armor, integration over achievement.

## Grounded
- **The wound (lyric):** age 7, Christian school, ballet, *"I came alive when I was on that stage"*; teacher to dad *"we think your son is gay"*; child: *"gay must mean bad… I prayed to God to make me good"*; girls: **"you're not a real boy" (×4)**; dad: *"there's nothing wrong with you, my son."*
- **The burial:** *"I never danced again / I killed that ballerina boy / I buried him inside / It's like I didn't have a choice."*
- **The loop forward (V2):** at 17 a band, *"music just wasn't landing"*, can't afford an egg McMuffin; the chant returns: *"you're not a real boy / what makes a real boy?"*
- **The fork:** *"Do I kill myself or come back for that kid I let go / This song's for Ballerina Boy / This song's a prayer for hope."*
- **Spoken outro, older motherly female voice** (Jen, audio): *"…something clicks inside / and says 'this is me' / you are one of a kind, God's design / so my son, let your light shine / … the moon reminds us that even in the darkness there is light / there's no one else like you / out there."* Two blessings braided: the father's = the narrated in-verse line; the outro *"my son"* = the feminine voice, not paternal.
- [G] **Roy tells the wound outside the lyric** (MB conversation, ~2021–22): mom enrolled him in dance (only boy), the girls' chant, *"I'm never gonna dance again"*, shame *"pushed so deep into the subconscious"*; adult **dance videos = shadow-work** ([[Sources/Transcripts/MB-Conversation-Jagwar-Twin (Mental Health, Cancel Culture)]]). Literal autobiography, not only a character's lyric.
- [G] **Lucius mocks this song by name** (The Interview EP3, verbatim): *"your lowest performing song… Ballerina Boy. When you're vulnerable you LOSE!"*
- [G] **Pinocchio sits on Roy's own 22-film board** (*"Pattern Represented in Movies"*): Roy-curated, not a critic's import.
