# BALLERINA BOY

Standalone single, **released June 13, 2025** (none — Apple Music); ℗ 2025 **Big Loud Rock, under exclusive license to Mercury Records (UMG)** (none). 3-track release (~9 min; other 2 tracks not yet pinned). **Not on [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]]** despite the pre-album window — absence reads as significant ([W] *why* off-album = interpretive; no public statement found). The wound-song / origin chamber of the whole arc.

## Voicing
[[Roy]], first-person autobiographical (none — the lyric is explicit). Direct, undefended — no Lucius mask. **Spoken outro = an older *motherly female* voice** (none — Jen, audio) delivering the WHOLE blessing: *"We don't really know who we are until we try things / and something clicks inside / and says 'this is me' / you are one of a kind, God's design / so my son, let your light shine / … look up to the sky / the moon reminds us that even in the darkness there is light / there's no one else like you / out there."* → divine-feminine / [[Mary]]-class (the clearest such appearance). **Correction:** the *"my son, let your light shine"* line is the **mother/divine-feminine** voice, NOT paternal; the father's blessing is the in-verse *"there's nothing wrong with you, my son."* So the song braids a father's narrated line and a Divine-Mother outro.

## The wound (none — all in lyric)
Age 7, Christian school, ballet, "came alive on that stage." Teacher → dad: "we think your son is gay." Child: "Gay must mean bad… prayed to God to make me good." Girls: **"you're not a real boy" (×4)**. Dad: "there's nothing wrong with you, my son." → **"I never danced again / I killed that ballerina boy / I buried him inside / It's like I didn't have a choice."**
