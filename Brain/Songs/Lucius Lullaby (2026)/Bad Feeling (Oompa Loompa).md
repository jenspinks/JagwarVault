# Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa)

Type: Song · [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]], **track 5**; acoustic-live = 15. **2023-12-15 single** first, *Wonka*-film-timed. Writers **Roy English, Ryan Daly, Sean Van Vleet + Bricusse & Newley**; **Josh Dun** (TØP) drums; Roy **whistle + melodica**.

**Core [G/W]:** **The candy-factory alarm.** Where [[Great Time to Be Human]] forces horror *through* a smile, this is the smile that can't suppress the dread underneath: the body's alarm (*"bad feeling"*) more honest than the official story (*"everything's perfect"*).

## Grounded
- **Roy's framing:** *"a chocolate covered reminder to trust your gut"* — candy over dread; intuition the reliable instrument (his **"Trust your guts" tattoo**).
- Paradise vs leak: *"(They say) everything's perfect here / the sun is shining / nobody's crying"* vs *"I got a bad feeling 'bout you."* Outro: *"It's true."*
- **Oompa-Loompa interpolation [G]:** Bricusse + Newley credited (1971 *Willy Wonka*); cautionary-chorus function kept, *"a perfect puzzle for you"* → *"a bad feeling 'bout you."*
- **Lucius claims it (EP1):** *"happy face, bad feeling… those are me. You need me."*
- Texture: distorted lullaby, haunting whistles (toy instruments gone eerie).
- **"Everything's Perfect Here" = a Roy artwork [G]:** titled Roy's first gallery show + pop-up (Lauren Powell Projects, LA, **2024-01-20**, glowing orange sculpture; [[Sources/Quotes/Roy X Posts - June 2026 (aphorisms)]] §2024): a staged **installation** two years pre-album.
- **Cross-album twin [G cross-ref]:** [[I Like to Party]] (33): *"We don't want 'em awake / bake a truth with a lie / then we sell 'em the cake"* — same sell-a-sweet-lie-to-a-sleeping-crowd.

## Strong Read
- **[W] Cautionary chorus → intuition alarm:** Wonka's chorus sings *after* each child's downfall; here, *before* collapse: **"why does this perfect place feel wrong?"** The puzzle→bad-feeling swap performs the shift.
- **[W] Wonka factory = [[The Circus]], nursery version:** candy paradise hiding surveillance, punishment, sorting, a controlled worker-chorus. **[~]** The Oompa-Loompas = **consumed labor** (imported, paid in cacao, test-subjects): the warning chorus is the factory's product/victim.
- **[W] Toxic positivity = system speech:** *"(They say)"* marks perfection as **the system's claim**; *"nobody's crying"* may mean crying hidden/forbidden; *"It's true"* points at the dread, not the paradise. Audio of the [[Happy Face]] mask.
- **[W] Intuition as counter-epistemology:** *"The truth is a lie that nobody can tell"* (*tell* = speak AND distinguish; cf. [[Lucius Lullaby]] "nothing's what it seems"; [[Inversion]]); the gut = the last honest instrument.
- **[W] Consumption / on-demand cannibalism:** *"carnivores and cannibals, I want it on demand-able / mandible, jaw split… gossip"*: attention economy as **mouth** = [[Consumption]] (cf. [[Welcome to the Circus]] "chew you up, spit you out"); manic rhyme = clever-nonsense register (cf. [[Not Your Homie]]).
- **[W] Lucius = alarm AND contamination:** claiming doesn't falsify; the *Wonka*-timed single proves the paradox: a genuine alarm that was itself a calculated content-play. **Threat-detector and factory foreman in one.**
- **[W] Smaller:** *"dance 'til we drop"* = danse macabre (cf. NYH "ashes we all fall down," GT2BH "danced to destroy") · gaze turns outward: *"bad feeling 'bout **you**"* · *"Are you fucking serious?"* = rupture-point, the only un-coated line · interpolation = album method (Wonka credited [G]; nursery rhymes GT2BH/NYH/#1 Champion; Dylan "serve somebody" in [[Lucius Lullaby]] = artist-attributed [G], not a credit).
