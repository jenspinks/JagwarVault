# Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa)

Type: Song — **Track 5**, [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]]. **Originally a 2023-12-15 single** (timed to the 2023 *Wonka* film) → folded into the album; acoustic-live = track 15. Writers: Roy English, Ryan Daly, Sean Van Vleet, + **Leslie Bricusse & Anthony Newley** (the *Willy Wonka* songbook); **Josh Dun** (TØP) drums; Roy on **whistle + melodica**. **The candy-factory alarm.** *(Reviewer round folded 2026-06-07.)*

## What it is (grounded)
- **Roy's own framing:** *"a chocolate covered reminder to trust your gut"* — candy coating over dread; intuition as the reliable instrument (links his **"Trust your guts" tattoo**).
- A manufactured paradise — *"(They say) everything's perfect here / the sun is shining / nobody's crying"* — under which the dread leaks: *"I got a bad feeling 'bout you."*
- **The Oompa-Loompa interpolation [G]:** Bricusse + Newley credited = the 1971 *Willy Wonka* songbook; *"Oompa Loompa, doompa-de-doo"* is theirs. Roy keeps the **cautionary-chorus function** but swaps *"I've got a perfect puzzle for you"* → *"I got a bad feeling 'bout you."*
- **Lucius claims it (EP1):** *"happy face, bad feeling… those are me. You need me."* — one of the algorithm-friendly hits he takes credit for.
- Texture: a **distorted lullaby with haunting whistles** (whistle + melodica — toy/schoolroom instruments turned eerie).

## Thesis — the candy-factory alarm [G/W]
Where [[Great Time to Be Human]] forces horror *through* a smile, Bad Feeling is **the smile that can't suppress the dread underneath.** The body's alarm ("bad feeling") is treated as **more honest than the official story** ("everything's perfect"). Roy's gloss makes intuition the center, not paranoia.

## Cautionary chorus → intuition alarm [W]
In *Wonka*, the Oompa-Loompas sing **after** each greedy child's downfall (moral-lesson-after-processing). Bad Feeling moves the chorus **before** collapse: not *"what did this child do wrong?"* but **"why does this perfect place feel wrong?"** The *"perfect puzzle for you" → "bad feeling 'bout you"* swap performs exactly that shift.

## The Wonka factory = the Circus (nursery version) [W]
Wonka's candy paradise hides surveillance, punishment, moral sorting, and a **controlled worker-chorus** — a spectacle-machine that turns appetite, failure, and punishment into entertainment = [[The Circus]] in early-childhood form. **[~]** The Oompa-Loompas are themselves **consumed labor** (imported, paid in cacao, test-subjects — one turned to a blueberry): the chorus that sings the warning **is itself the factory's product/victim** — the crowd that sings is the crowd that's eaten.

## Toxic positivity = system speech, not reassurance [W]
*"Everything's perfect here / nobody's crying"* is **enforcement** — the audio of the Happy-Face mask. The *"(They say)"* tag marks it as **the system's claim, not the singer's**; *"nobody's crying"* may mean crying has been **hidden / edited / forbidden**, not that no one's hurt. The outro *"It's true"* points at the **dread**, not the paradise. Cross-album twin: [[I Like to Party]] (33) — *"We don't want 'em awake / bake a truth with a lie / then we sell 'em the cake"* (Devil's-Food cake) — the **same sell-a-sweet-lie-to-a-sleeping-crowd** [G cross-ref].

## Intuition as counter-epistemology [W]
*"The truth is a lie that nobody can tell"* — ordinary truth-testing has failed (*"tell"* both ways: can't *speak* it, can't *distinguish* it; cf. [[Lucius Lullaby]]'s "nothing's what it seems"). The body's alarm becomes the last honest instrument — grounded by Roy's *"trust your gut"* + the tattoo.

## Consumption / on-demand cannibalism [W]
V2 turns the crowd into appetite — *"carnivores and cannibals, I want it on demand-able / mandible, jaw split… gossip."* The attention economy as a **mouth**; people consumed as content / scandal / outrage = [[Consumption]] (cf. [[Welcome to the Circus]] "I'll chew you up, spit you out"). The manic internal rhyme (misunderstandable / demand-able / mandible) = the catalogue's **clever-nonsense** register (cf. [[Not Your Homie]]'s "strategic nonsense").

## Lucius — alarm AND contamination [W]
Lucius claims the track (EP1), so the speaker is Lucius-mode — but that **doesn't falsify the warning.** The release proves the paradox: a genuine *"trust your gut"* alarm that was **itself a calculated content-play timed to the 2023 *Wonka* film** — the song exposes the candy factory while *being* a strategically-timed candy-factory product. **Threat-detector and factory foreman in one.**

## Smaller [W]
- **"Dance 'til we drop" = danse macabre** — the crowd dancing itself to collapse while the system insists all is perfect (cf. [[Not Your Homie]] "ashes we all fall down"; GT2BH "danced to destroy"). The *"drop-drop, drop"* is the fall.
- **The gaze turns on the listener:** the one honest line aims outward — *"bad feeling 'bout **you**"* — at the audience dancing along and consuming on demand.
- **"Are you fucking serious?" = the rupture-point** — the lone un-jingled, profane aside punctures the candy surface; the only un-coated line.
- **Interpolation as method** — recoding a known cultural song-cell is the album's signature: the **credited** Wonka interpolation here (Bricusse/Newley `[G]`); public-domain nursery rhymes in GT2BH / NYH / #1 Champion; and — an **artist-attributed allusion** (`[G]`, via Roy's YouTube-Short caption) *not* a credited interpolation — Dylan's *"serve somebody"* in [[Lucius Lullaby]].

## "Everything's Perfect Here" = a Roy artwork, not just a lyric [G]
The chorus phrase became the title of Roy's **first gallery show + pop-up** — *"Everything's Perfect Here"* (Lauren Powell Projects, LA, opened **2024-01-20**), centered on a **glowing orange sculpture** ([[Sources/Quotes/Roy X Posts - June 2026 (aphorisms)]] §2024). So the *"they say everything's perfect here"* toxic-positivity line is **load-bearing across media** — Roy built a physical art-environment around it, two years before the album track. The gallery framing (a *staged* perfect space you walk into) reinforces the §toxic-positivity reading: the "perfect here" is an **installation**, a manufactured environment, not a description.

## Resonance [~]
- **Corrupted "That's Amore" moon + luna/lunacy:** *"when the moon hits the sky, I'm a little unwell"* inverts Dean Martin's moon-romance into sickness — **"unwell under the moon" = lunacy** (luna → moon-madness). In the vault light-model, reflected/indirect light is **Yesod** — the image-realm **Lucius works *through*** (the morning-star's glamour, *not* the moon itself; the Moon is Roy's — re-mapped 2026-06-14, [[Brain/Concepts/Source vs Reflection]]): the dread arrives under *his* borrowed light. (Matchbox Twenty "Unwell" = thematic only, not a claimed allusion.)
- **Candy paradise = false Eden / Maya:** a closed system of tests and punishments; resonates with Roy's posted epistemics (Kierkegaard *"the crowd is the untruth"*; Bailey *"illusion is the first product of the mind divorced from the soul"*; Blavatsky/Steiner on Maya). *"They say everything's perfect here"* = Maya / the crowd's untruth.
- **Sugar as anesthetic / decay:** sweetness now, decay later; the tooth-decay nightmare sits in the **adjacent** track [[Welcome to the Circus]] (4), not here.
- **Jungian shadow alarm / Gnostic archonic appetite** — keep as resonance; the grounded "spectacle as a devouring system" is strong enough alone.

## Boundary (do not re-contaminate)
*"teeth falling out," "no exit sign in my maze," "spinning you 'round," "chew you up, spit you out"* belong to **[[Welcome to the Circus]]** (track 4) — **NOT** this song. (Adjacent track; treat as connection only.)

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
<!-- anti-totalization defense (CLAUDE.md ANTI-PATTERNS #1/#2) -->
- **Roy's own gloss is modest; the vault's read is grand.** He frames it small `[G]` — *"a chocolate covered reminder to trust your gut."* That's intuition, personal. The vault reads it large: Wonka-factory = the Circus = a spectacle-machine = Consumption + Algorithm devouring the crowd. The gap between **stated scope (a gut-feeling)** and **imputed scope (a systems critique)** is itself the counterargument — honor the author's smaller frame as the floor, the systems-read as `[W]` on top. `[P]`
- **The grotesque imagery may be playful horror, not a structural system-claim.** *"Carnivores and cannibals… on demand-able… mandible, jaw split"* is vivid candy-horror wordplay (the page even names it "clever-nonsense"); reading it as "the attention economy as a mouth" `[W]` is one option, but visceral imagery for its own eerie effect is another. Not every devouring image encodes [[Consumption]]. `[W]`
- **"Dread under perfection" may be plain anxiety, not Maya.** *"When the moon hits the sky, I'm a little unwell"* reads as ordinary unease/depression at least as well as luna→lunacy + Kierkegaard's "crowd is untruth" + Bailey's illusion-doctrine stacked on top. Those are `[~]` framework-imports onto a mood piece — keep them resonance, and don't let four traditions co-sign one line. `[~]`
- **Falsifier:** the candy/Wonka aesthetic being primarily a *2023 film tie-in marketing hook* (the single was timed to the *Wonka* release) would reframe the "spectacle-machine" depth as commercially-occasioned dressing — a possibility the page itself half-concedes ("threat-detector and factory foreman in one").
