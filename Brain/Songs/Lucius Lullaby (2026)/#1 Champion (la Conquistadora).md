# #1 Champion (la Conquistadora)

Type: Song — Track 8, [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]]. Writers: **Cici Ward, Roy English, Tony Esterly** ([[Sources/Credits/Lucius Lullaby]]); string + choir arrangement. The ego's boast-anthem **hijacked and unseated by Mary.**

## What it is (grounded)
- A braggart's chorus, repeated throughout: *"I'm the number one champion in this town / A-B-C, count 'em… you got a little bit of money but it don't come cheap / if you don't know 'bout me, you should know by now."*
- Per **EP7**, **Lucius plays it as "another song about ME"** — claiming the championship anthem as his. The irony: it **unseats** him.
- **Mary is named and rendered faceless:** *"Mary, Mary, so contrary… Saw a halo but I didn't see a head… La Conquistadora with a gun in her hand… fawning over no man… such a revolutionary."*

## The two voices [W]
- **The champion = ego / Lucius-mode braggadocio.** Status, money, fame, *"know 'bout me"* — a spectacle-self announcing its rank.
- **Mary = the sacred-feminine who dethrones it.** The champion desires/objectifies her (*"never noticed what was underneath that dress"*) but **cannot see her** (*"saw a halo but I didn't see a head"* — only the holy sign, or the body, never the face/person); he *"made a move like a fool, dropped me dead when she said…"* **Mary drops the #1 champion.** So Lucius's "song about ME" is the song where the ego is unseated — the album's anti-ego corrective.

## Mary, rendered
- **Faceless [G].** *"Halo but I didn't see a head"* = the grounded **faceless divine-feminine** — the same marker as [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Weirdness|Weirdness]] (*"face was out of sight"*) and [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy|Ballerina Boy]]'s motherly outro. **This is the real faceless-Mary anchor the [[Mary]] tracker turns on** (vs the corrected Lucius Lullaby false-positive).
- **Armed, autonomous, revolutionary [G].** *"La Conquistadora with a gun in her hand… fawning over no man… such a revolutionary"* — not the meek Mary; a militant, self-possessed conqueror.
- **"Mary, Mary, so contrary" [G]** — the **Mother Goose** nursery rhyme ("…quite contrary"); the album's "twisted adolescent themes" substrate again.
- **Marian titles, repurposed [W/~].** *"La Conquistadora"* is a **real Marian title** (*Our Lady of the Conquest* — the oldest Marian statue in the U.S., Santa Fe), here **militarized** ("with a gun"). *"Queen of California"* repurposes the Marian *"Queen of Heaven"* into the City-of-Angels / Hollywood register (cf. [[City of Angels]]).
- **"Queen of California on the corner doing her time" [W].** The sacred-feminine brought low — street-corner, serving a sentence — yet still "Queen." A **Magdalene** register (fallen-and-holy) `[~]`.
- **"Stumbled into the church… on her knees… quite a scene, making everyone stare" [G].** Disruptive devotion — sacred but scandalous, not decorous.
