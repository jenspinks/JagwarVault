# Great Time to Be Human

Type: Song · [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]], **track 3**. Writers (5): **Roy English, Ryan Spraker, Tom Peyton, Sean Van Vleet, Josh Dun(n)** (most-collaborative LL track); **Josh Dun** drums; **little luna** BG; vocals **"ROY" + "sir lucius"** ([[Sources/Credits/Lucius Lullaby]]).

**Core:** the album's **toxic-positivity anthem**: a children's sing-along staging human history as cheerful spectacle. [W] The cruelty is not the verses' horror but the **smile forced over it**, ideology papering over what they showed: [[Lucius Lullaby]] confesses the lie, [[Happy Face]] instructs the mask, GT2BH shows the mask become a civilization.

## Grounded
- Two fable-verses (*"There once was a boy / girl…"*) of grotesque events (smothering love, fratricide, a woman committed & shaved, predators, a botched god), framed by the refrain *"What a great time to be human! / rainbows, butterflies / follow your bliss / everything keeps blooming."*
- Hinge: *"The crooks and believers all sing the same song."*
- **Capitalized archetypal stations** (verified, Jen 2026-06-07): *Mother, Man, Lover, The Fountain, The Mountain, Someone, They, The Chemist* — stations, not transcription noise [G cap / W reading].
- Dog/god in the text: *"She went to The Chemist to make her a dog / but he was dyslexic, so he made a god / God, god, god (Woof!)."* **Lucius yells it in EP7** at the bound Roy (not synced to the track); his next *"another song about ME"* points to [[No. 1 Champion (la Conquistadora)]], not this song.
- Ad-libs: *"oops! / yay! / boo hoo / Woof!"*; lone *"Goodbye"* after "Shanghai Summertime."
- Album billing: *"twisted takes on adolescent musical themes for hands that rock the cradle."*
- **Thought Forms** artifact (*"Man anthropomorphises his gods"*) literalized by dog→god ([[Artifacts - Drawings and Sketches]]).

## Strong Read
- **[W] Crooks/believers → [[Communion vs Spectacle]]** (seeded here; key): moral opposites **function identically** under the spectacle; [[City of Angels]]'s *"everyone looks just like you"*, moral register. Communion = distinct voices singing truth ([[Brain/Concepts/The Circle|Circle]]); spectacle = voices forced into one song ([[The Circus|Circus]]); crowd synchronized, not awakened.
- **[W] Fable diptych = failed-systems anthropology**, asymmetric: the boy (kinship + religion) matures *into repetition*: the Lover's spell to bring back his Mother = **false-feminine substitution** (re-animates the wound; [[Mary]] restores); the girl (youth + medicine + creation) is *dispossessed* (punished, institutionalized). Preacher + gong = ceremony without transformation; *"bloodsuckers with crocodile shoes"* = predator + consumer, wound dressed as party.
- **[G text / W] Dog/god = counterfeit creation / failed alchemy:** Reversal literalized ([[Inversion]]): dog asked for (creaturely love), god made **by error** (abstraction, worship), divinity without integration; *"(Woof!)"* refuses full deification (the dog trapped inside the god). Kin: Lucius (false light), [[City of Angels]] (false immortality), Lucius counterfeiting [[Mary]].
- **[W] Empty Mirror:** Roy's tattoo ("looking at yourself and seeing nothing"): the cheer reflects the desire, never the reality (`[[The Empty Mirror]]`).
- **[W] Ad-libs = spectacle-buttons:** children's-show cues at the wrong moments; algorithmic flattening, horror as content.
- **[W]** *"everything keeps blooming"* = growth ≠ healing; *"Goodbye"* reabsorbed into the chorus = even leaving consumed.
- **[W] Voice:** Lucius staging Roy's lament as children's spectacle (both credited); too grief-conscious for pure Lucius; don't flatten.
