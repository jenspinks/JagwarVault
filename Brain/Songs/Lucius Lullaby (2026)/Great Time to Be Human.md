# Great Time to Be Human

Type: Song — **Track 3**, [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]]. Writers (5): **Roy English, Ryan Spraker, Tom Peyton, Sean Van Vleet, Josh Dun(n)** (the most-collaborative LL track); **Josh Dun** (Twenty One Pilots) on drums; **little luna** BG; vocals **"ROY" + "sir lucius"** ([[Sources/Credits/Lucius Lullaby]]). **The album's toxic-positivity anthem** — a children's sing-along staging human history as cheerful spectacle. *(Reviewer round folded 2026-06-07.)*

## What it is (grounded)
- Two fable-verses (*"There once was a boy / girl…"*) of grotesque events — smothering love, fratricide, a woman committed & shaved, predators, a botched god — framed by the refrain *"What a great time to be human! / rainbows, butterflies / follow your bliss / everything keeps blooming."*
- **Capitalized archetypal stations** (verified, Jen 2026-06-07): *Mother, Man, Lover, The Fountain, The Mountain, Someone, They, The Chemist* — the nouns are **stations, not transcription noise** [G cap / W reading].
- The **dog/god line** is in the text. **Lucius yells it in EP7** at the bound Roy (*"she went to a chemist to make her a dog, he was dyslexic and made a god"*) — his yells aren't synced to the playing track, and the *"another song about ME"* he says next points to [[#1 Champion (la Conquistadora)]], **not** this song.

## Thesis — the refrain is the violence [W]
The cruelty isn't in the verses' horror — it's in **forcing that horror through a smile.** *"Rainbows, butterflies / follow your bliss / everything keeps blooming"* is the ideology papering over what the verses just showed. Companion to [[Lucius Lullaby]] (*"it's all just a lie"*) and [[Happy Face]] (the mask): Lucius Lullaby says the beautiful life is a lie; Happy Face says put on the mask; **GT2BH shows the mask become a group activity — a civilization.** The horror is obvious; the cruelty is in the **cheer**.

## "Crooks and believers all sing the same song" → Communion vs Spectacle [W] (key)
Crooks and believers should be opposites; the spectacle absorbs the moral categories and makes them **function identically** — [[City of Angels]]'s *"everyone looks just like you"* moved into the **moral** register. Forces the distinction now seeded as [[Communion vs Spectacle]]: **communion = many distinct voices singing truth together (the [[Brain/Concepts/The Circle|Circle]]'s chorus); spectacle = many voices forced into the same song (the [[The Circus|Circus]]'s crowd).** The crowd here is not awakened — it's **synchronized.**

## The fable diptych [W]
A distorted Adam/Eve / **failed-systems anthropology**: the **boy** (mother / brother / lover / preacher = kinship + religion), the **girl** (fountain / youth / mountain / institution / chemist / god = youth + medicine + creation). Asymmetric: **the boy matures *into repetition*** (becomes Man, repeats the mother-wound through the Lover); **the girl is *dispossessed*** (loses youth, punished, institutionalized).
- **"Lover casts a spell to bring back his Mother" = false-feminine substitution:** the erotic partner conscripted as a magical surrogate for the lost mother — grief routed into **repetition, not integration.** The counterfeit feminine **re-animates the wound**; the true feminine ([[Mary]]) restores to wholeness.
- **Preacher + gong = ceremony without transformation** — ritual noise performing meaning without producing healing.
- **"Bloodsuckers with crocodile shoes" = predator + consumer, death worn as status** — the industry / "new kings," but **broader**: any institution that profits from the wounded body then dresses the wound as a party ("diamonds and spoons").

## Dog/god = counterfeit creation / failed alchemy [G text / W]
*"She went to The Chemist to make her a dog / but he was dyslexic, so he made a god / God, god, god (Woof!)."* The vault's **Reversal** operation literalized (dog↔god; cf. *Rorrim Ehtni* / the Empty Mirror — [[Inversion]]). She asks for a **dog** (companion, creaturely love, humble attachment); the broken maker produces a **god** (abstraction, authority, worship) **by error** — divinity manufactured **without integration** (failed alchemy: elevation without understanding). The **"(Woof!)" refuses full deification** — the made-god still **barks**: the dog is *trapped inside* the god; reversal both ways at once. Core tag: **counterfeit divinity / maker-without-wisdom** — kin to Lucius (false light), [[City of Angels]] (false immortality), Lucius counterfeiting [[Mary]].
- [~] **Demiurgic typo:** god-by-clerical-error is a Gnostic subversion of the Creator (the Demiurge = ignorant/botched lower maker); framing god-making as *dyslexia* mocks divine intentionality. Framework correspondence; the dog/god *text* is [G].

## The Empty Mirror operation [W]
*"The Empty Mirror"* is one of Roy's tattoos ("looking at yourself and seeing nothing"). The cheer **reflects back the desire** (rainbows, butterflies, bliss) while the **reality** (the fables) stays un-reflected — you look into the song's smile and see nothing of what just happened. [[Inversion]] / `[[The Empty Mirror]]`.

## The ad-libs = spectacle-buttons [W]
*"oops! / yay! / boo hoo / Woof!"* — children's-show emotional cues fired at the **wrong moments** (a brother smothered → *oops*; kids dance to destroy → *yay*). **Algorithmic emotional flattening:** horror becomes content, and content needs reaction sounds — the algorithm laughing at the boy who lost his mother.

## Smaller [W]
- **"Everything keeps blooming"** — growth ≠ healing; life continues *regardless* of suffering (beautiful and brutally indifferent).
- **"Goodbye" swallowed by the refrain** — the lone exit-signal (after "Shanghai Summertime") is reabsorbed into the chorus; **even leaving is consumed by the cheer-machine.**

## Voice [W]
Roy's lament **inside** Lucius's grin — too observant/grief-conscious to be *pure* Lucius; best read as **Lucius staging Roy's lament as a children's spectacle.** Both credited (ROY + sir lucius). Do not flatten to pure Lucius.

## Resonance [~]
- **"Follow your bliss" = Joseph Campbell**, soured into toxic-positivity anesthesia (inherited-substrate; the album borrows and sours familiar phrases — cf. "put on a happy face," "all your dreams are coming true"). The target is bliss-as-bumper-sticker, not Campbell.
- **"Shanghai Summertime" = *shanghaied*** (coerced/kidnapped aboard a ship): a sunny image hiding coercion — rhymes with the album's captivity/servitude logic (*"you gotta serve somebody"*; the EP6 kidnapping). `[?]` could be only exotic imagery — same operation either way.
- **Nursery-rhyme substrate:** *"There once was a boy/girl…"* joins *"Mary, Mary, so contrary"* (#1 Champion), *"ring around the rosies"* (Not Your Homie), *"Oompa Loompa"* (Bad Feeling), *"Row Row Row Your Boat / life is but a dream"* (Lucius Lullaby) — the album's stated method, instanced.
- **Josh Dun's drums → parade/march/circus percussion** turning nursery material into a grotesque procession `[~/?]` — can't confirm by ear.

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
<!-- anti-totalization defense (CLAUDE.md ANTI-PATTERNS #1/#2) -->
- **Flatter read: dark-comedy irony, not a civilizational critique.** "Cheerful tune, horrific lyrics" is a recognizable songwriting device (Lehrer, Newman, "Pumped Up Kicks") — the irony *is* the joke. The vault reads it as Communion-vs-Spectacle and a synchronized-crowd anthropology; a plainer read is a blackly-funny exercise about human awfulness. Hold the ideology-critique `[W]`, the gag-reading as live. `[P]`
- **Five co-writers strain single-author theological encoding.** This is the **most collaborative LL track (5 writers)** — the least likely place for deliberate single-author cosmology. Per [[Inherited Substrate]], the gendered fable-anthropology and dog/god alchemy are more plausibly **emergent from a writers' room** than encoded; tier the deep readings `[W]` and the intent `[?]`. `[W]`
- **"Dog/god" is a pun before it is the Demiurge.** dog↔god is among the oldest reversals; *"dyslexic, so he made a god"* is a wordplay gag. Reading it as the [[Inversion]] operation + Gnostic botched-Creator builds cosmology on a joke — the page is honest to mark the Demiurge `[~]` and the text `[G]`, but the *weight* the reading carries still outruns the line. `[~]`
- **Falsifier:** Roy framing the song as gallows-humor (sweet melody / awful content, for the effect) would demote the Communion-vs-Spectacle thesis to overlay; the fable verses being absurdist vignettes rather than a failed-systems anthropology is already an open question below.
