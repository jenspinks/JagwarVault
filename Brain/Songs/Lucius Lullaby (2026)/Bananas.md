# Bananas

Type: Song · **Track 7**, [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]] (Moths to the Flame 6 → #1 Champion 8) · Writers **Roy English + Ryan Spraker** (full personnel pending) · Lyrics Apple-verified.

**Core:** the album's **id-permission chant**: Lucius weaponizing childish permission (*"hey kids… well, alright"*) into animal regression, destructive appetite, dissociated shadow-action. The cradle-rocker (album billed for *"hands that rock the cradle"*) teaching the nursery to go bananas: *a banana peel under the conscience, funny until you realize the fall is the point.*

## Grounded
- Permission address: *"Hey, kids / do you ever wanna break shit? / do you wanna get wasted?"* → *"Well, alright."*
- Moral split in child-language: *"I wanna be Good, but it's fun to be Bad / I wanna be nice, but I always get mad"* (Good/Bad capitalized, archetypal → [[Great Time to Be Human|GT2BH]]).
- Dissociation: *"I hear voices in my mind I don't remember / [Did I do that?]"* · regression: *"Just the monkeys feeling funky"* · blocked: *"ready to blow," "nowhere to go"* · arson: *"I wanna light up the stove / and burn it down."*
- **Banana = recurring Lucius trigger-prop** off the fruit-bowl altar (candles, Egyptian statue): EP2 browning banana → [[Not Your Homie]], EP3 → [[Welcome to the Circus]]; staging [G] → [[The Banana]].
- **Roy's PUBLIC #bananas caption:** *"Nowhere is not a destination but you can arrive in an instant."*
- **[G] Roy's public sterile gloss** (@jagwartwin IG comment, instagram.com/reels/DWo9DYzAUZ8/, [[Sources/Quotes/Roy IG Reel - Bananas (sterile gloss)]]): *"most bananas are sterile and cannot reproduce by seed ;)"*.
