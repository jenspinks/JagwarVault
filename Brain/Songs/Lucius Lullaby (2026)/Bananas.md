# Bananas

Type: Song — **Track 7**, [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]] (neighbors: Moths to the Flame 6 → Bananas 7 → #1 Champion 8). Writers: **Roy English, Ryan Spraker** (full personnel pending). The album's **id-permission chant** — Lucius weaponizing childish permission into animal regression, destructive appetite, and dissociated shadow-action. *(Reviewer round folded 2026-06-07; private-call material quarantined — see Boundary.)*

## What it is (grounded)
- Direct address + permission: *"Hey, kids / do you ever wanna break shit? / do you wanna get wasted?"* → *"Well, alright."*
- The moral split in child-language: *"I wanna be Good, but it's fun to be Bad / I wanna be nice, but I always get mad"* (Good/Bad capitalized — archetypal, like GT2BH).
- Dissociation: *"I hear voices in my mind I don't remember / [Did I do that?]"*; animal regression: *"Just the monkeys feeling funky"*; arson: *"burn it down."*
- **The banana = a recurring Lucius trigger-prop** (EP2 browning banana → [[Not Your Homie]]; EP3 → [[Welcome to the Circus]]), lifted from a fruit-bowl altar. See [[The Banana]].
- **Roy's PUBLIC #bananas gloss** (posted): *"Nowhere is not a destination but you can arrive in an instant."*

## Thesis — the id-permission chant [W]
Lucius weaponizing **childish permission**: *"hey kids… well, alright"* → a chant for animal regression, destructive appetite, and dissociated shadow-action (invitation → animalization → moral split → dissociation). **The cradle-rocker teaching the crowd to go bananas.** The strongest compact line: *Bananas is a banana peel under the conscience — funny until you realize the fall is the point.*

## The hijacked offering [W]
The fruit bowl sits on an **altar** (candles, Egyptian statue = the concealed Mother/Shekinah → [[Mary]]). Lucius lifts the banana — an **offering** — and uses it as a **PA to broadcast his own songs** (EP2/EP3), then sets it back: the Mother's offering rerouted into a delivery system for Lucius's voice. See [[The Banana]] / [[Sir Lucius]].

## The sterile loop [W]
The Cavendish banana is a **sterile clone** (public botanical fact) — identical, propagated only by cutting, all wiped out together by one fungus *because* identical. Maps onto Lucius's **Loop** ([[Brain/Concepts/The Circle|Circle↔Loop]]) + [[City of Angels]]'s *"everyone looks just like you"*: **real light in a sterile vessel — you can hear the song, you cannot inherit it.** **[G] Roy stated it publicly** — @jagwartwin IG comment on the *Bananas* reel: *"most bananas are sterile and cannot reproduce by seed ;)"* ([[Sources/Quotes/Roy IG Reel - Bananas (sterile gloss)]], instagram.com/reels/DWo9DYzAUZ8/); the fuller *flawed-genes / inherited-trauma / looks-healthy* gloss is from Jen's firsthand in-person witness. So the reading is **double-anchored** — Roy's own public gloss **and** public botany.

## "Nowhere to go" — the wound under the chaos [W]
The id erupts because the energy is **blocked** (*"ready to blow," "nowhere to go"*). Roy's PUBLIC #bananas caption reframes it: *"Nowhere is not a destination but you can arrive in an instant"* — so the song stages Lucius's **trapped** nowhere against Roy's **transcendent** nowhere (same word, two doctrines). Not cartoon nihilism — a sealed system's symptom.

## The monkey = regression [W]
*"Just the monkeys feeling funky"* collapses human → animal — the id as a monkey (appetite running the body while consciousness lags). `[~]` Roy posts **Gurdjieff** on exactly this (*"man is no longer a wild animal, but he has not yet become a human being"*; *"most people are ruled by moods, impulses… they mistake for themselves"*) — Roy-endorsed, not imposed. Same regression register as the music-box ([[The Circle (Lucius Version)]]) and [[I Like to Party]].

## Dissociation after shadow-action [W]
*"I hear voices in my mind I don't remember / Did I do that?"* — the self divided from the part that acts (Jungian shadow-possession). Integration would say *"yes, that was me — what is it trying to say?"*; Lucius's version stops at *"Did I do that?"* and keeps the party going. `[~]` *"Did I do that?"* echoes Steve Urkel's catchphrase — childhood-TV recoded (the album's method); intent depends on inflection — keep `[~]`.

## Duality in kindergarten language [W]
*"Good/Bad, nice/mad"* = the Roy/Lucius split stripped to its crudest fracture — the shadow **before it becomes strategy.** Capitalized Good/Bad = the album's archetype-capitalization habit. → [[Duality]]. **Shadow vitality without integration:** *"fun to be bad"* isn't false (repression makes forbidden energy feel alive) — but it's released here **without heart, memory, or responsibility.** (Roy's wound under Lucius's hosting — don't flatten to pure cartoon.)

## Domestic combustion — the recurring stove [W]
*"I wanna light up the stove / and burn it down"* — hearth/home/mother-space turned ignition. Cross-track stove: [[Happy Face]]'s *"flip the switch, flip the stove / world gone mad, let's start the show."* What should nourish becomes the fire / the switch for the show.

## Arc function [W]
A **logical** arc (NOT the tracklist): Happy Face (pretend you're fine) → Bad Feeling (the body knows) → **Bananas (the repressed self blows through the mask)** → Welcome to the Circus (the machine turns the blowup into spectacle). On the album, WTTC is track 4, Bananas 7. Bananas = **the tantrum a system produces by forbidding honest feeling and then monetizing the explosion.**

## Resonance [~]
- Banana = comedy/slapstick + monkey-cue + cheap-commodity sweetness; the peel you slip on.
- Pied Piper / corrupted children's entertainer; **failed catharsis** — real cathartic energy that doesn't heal because nothing is remembered, owned, or integrated.
- The browning/decay layer: overripe = devotion left too long, offerings going unredeemed.
