# #1 Champion (la Conquistadora)

Type: Song · [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]], **track 8**. Writers **Cici Ward, Roy English, Tony Esterly** ([[Sources/Credits/Lucius Lullaby]]); string + choir. Lyrics Apple-verified. *(Reviewer fold 2026-06-08: ego-death → diagnosis.)*

**Core:** the ego's boast-anthem that **diagnoses itself by how it looks at Mary**, and is unseated: the chorus is hers `[G]`; Lucius takes the title by drowning her out, ending alone.

## Grounded
- **Chorus = Mary's voice `[G]`** (Jen audio/visual 2026-06-15): the count, the verdict (*"You got a little bit of money, but it don't come cheap"*), *"know 'bout me,"* and the title *"I'm the number one champion in this town."* **No Lucius line of his own**: he joins her on the title, then drowns her out and finishes alone, last repeat **a cappella after the music ends**. *(Supersedes "champion's chorus"/"title-line shared.")*
- **Hinge `[G]`:** *"…dropped me dead when she said"* cuts into *"A-B-C, count 'em, one, two, three"*: what she "said" = the count.
- **EP7 `[G]`:** Lucius plays it as *"another song about ME."*
- **Mary named, faceless `[G]`:** *"Mary, Mary, so contrary… Saw a halo but I didn't see a head… La Conquistadora with a gun in her hand… fawning over no man… such a revolutionary."* "So contrary" = Mother Goose. [[Mary]] tracker's faceless anchor (vs the Lucius Lullaby false-positive).
- **Disruptive devotion `[G]`:** *"Stumbled into the church… on her knees… quite a scene, making everyone stare."*
- **Marian titles real:** *La Conquistadora* = Our Lady of the Conquest (oldest US Marian statue); *"Queen of California"* sits on *"Queen of Heaven."*
- **"— El Roy" caption `[G]`** (/reel/DXPpfvbAcHh/, [[Sources/Quotes/Roy IG Reel - 1 Champion Visualizer (El Roy caption)]]): *"I saw her divinity, but not the one who held it, or where it led. / I followed anyway, white rose in my left hand, and stepped off the ledge."* Jester-ruff `[G]`: [[Sources/Quotes/Roy IG Post - Half the Man (Father)]]. *(De-attributed from SOL 2026-06-27; home = here.)*
