# Not Your Homie

Track 11, *Lucius Lullaby*. Pre-album single, **released Oct 31, 2025** (none — streaming). Credited: Jagwar Twin, **MERCY**, Sir Lucius (none). Middle of the public chain **LOOP → Not Your Homie → Welcome to the Circus** (antiMusic press, none). Credits (none — Qobuz/Apple, verified 2026-05-26): writers **Roy English, Mercedes Colletta Britton, Dave Burris**; producers Jagwar Twin + Dave Burris; mix/vocal-prod Roy English; master Becker + McCorkle. → [[Master Source List]].

**MERCY / chorus origin [G]:** Roy (X, 2025-10-31, public): *"She literally sent me a joke voicenote singing this chorus after getting back from a party and I knew we had to make this a sir lucius song."* → MERCY **originated the chorus hook** — the title/hook of the song came from her, not Roy. Roy also characterizes it publicly as *"the ultimate introverted extrovert anthem"* — his own framing: Lucius = the socially functional performance over the introverted Roy underneath. ([[Sources/Quotes/Roy X Posts - June 2026 (aphorisms)]] 2025-10-31.)

**Frame:** *strategic* nonsense — Lucius demonstrating the algorithm rewards caricature over authenticity, and being right. Shallowness = thesis, not failure. **The bargain (The Interview EP2, "The Rise of sir lucius," Oct 1 2025, public — none; verbatim per transcript of the episode → [[Master Source List]] §2):** Roy brings *"my favorite song I've ever written… **The Song of Love**"* (= [[SOL]]) and sings it; Lucius **"STOP!!! No one wants this love stuff. Your label doesn't want to put this out"** — invokes producer **Joey Moi** (real Big Loud hitmaker), money, the people Roy employs, the "37 / parents' house" jab; plays NYH off a pressed banana. Lucius **"this is what the people want"** / Roy **"it doesn't mean anything"** / Lucius **"exactly. It's nonsense. It's all nonsense."** Roy concedes **"Fine, I'll give you this one."** So the NYH bargain explicitly **refuses SOL and substitutes NYH** (corrects an earlier hedge). [Note: SOL is overridden *again* in EP3, this time for [[Brain/Songs/Welcome to the Circus]] — two override beats, not one.]

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
<!-- anti-totalization defense (CLAUDE.md ANTI-PATTERNS #1/#2) -->
- **Roy frames it light; the vault reads it heavy.** His public framing `[G]` is *"the ultimate introverted extrovert anthem"* and *"strategic nonsense"* — self-aware, almost comic. The Moves section stacks El Roi/Da'at, Yesod/moon-becoming-sun, the Beast/Hercules, the Protector, and the Thread onto it. That gap — **stated frame (a caricature-anthem) vs imputed frame (an esoteric psychomachia)** — is the counterargument; the dense readings are `[P]`/`[W]`, not what the song says of itself. `[P]`
- **The hook was a collaborator's party-joke.** `[G]` MERCY *"sent me a joke voicenote singing this chorus after getting back from a party."* The song's central "you're not my homie" gesture **originated as someone else's joke**, not Roy's encoded statement — a strong [[Inherited Substrate]] caution against reading deep single-author intent into the hook. Real, generative collaboration ≠ authored cipher. `[W]`
- **Some phrase→concept maps are reaches.** *"John Wick with my pencil / stick it in your eye"* is tough-guy bravado; "eye = perception, Lucius manages what you can see" finds the eye-motif by looking for it (the page fairly tags it "resonance"). When nearly every line resolves to a CONCEPT-entity, the resolution stops being evidence. `[~]`
- **Falsifier:** Roy's own "strategic nonsense" framing *is* the song explaining itself as deliberately shallow viral-bait — if that self-aware-shallow joke is the whole point, the Da'at/Yesod/Beast/Protector apparatus over-reads it.
