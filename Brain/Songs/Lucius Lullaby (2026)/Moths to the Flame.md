# Moths to the Flame

Type: Song · **Track 6**, [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]] (Bad Feeling 5 → **Moths 6 → Bananas 7**). Writer: **Roy English** (solo). Vocal: **sir lucius** (spoken-word); BG: ROY, MERCY ([[Sources/Credits/Lucius Lullaby]]). Lyric Apple-verified → stable; reviewer round folded 2026-06-08.

**Core:** **The Counterfeit Flame**: a spoken-word anti-gospel in Lucius's cold Power mode ([[Sir Lucius]] §Three Modes) that debases the mystic's flight-into-the-flame into a population cull and detonates seamlessly into [[Bananas]]. **Moths = the syllabus; Bananas = the enactment** (third seam: 1→2, 9→10, **6→7**). `[W]`

## Grounded
- **Spoken-word**, not sung; sir lucius lead. Full text: [[Sources/Lyrics/Moths to the Flame]] (Apple-verified).
- The doctrine: *"Civilization has been a mistake… leave everything alone. The wild animals are wiser than we are. They just follow their instincts. And if a moth mistakes a flame for a mating call and flies into the flame, so what? It just keeps the moth population down."*
- **The seam (Jen, firsthand):** background music becomes *Bananas*; the video pulls back to Lucius atop a ~10-ft banana pile. Candle out → descending vocalization → id-eruption.
- **The visual:** candelabra, **three slots but one tall red candle** (center); **lit from his cigarette**; **blown out by him** at the end.
- Closing **descending female vocalization** (BG: MERCY = Mercedes Colletta Britton, real session collaborator).

## Strong Read
- **[W] Doctrine ↔ practice:** Moths grants the permission for regression (*"the wild animals are wiser… follow their instincts"*), [[Bananas]] enacts it (the sung id-eruption); read 6→7 as one unit.
- **[W] The profane altar:** three slots reduced to one = a triad collapsed to the isolated ego (lampstand / the Tree's three pillars; menorah inverted); lit from a cigarette (fire as addiction/consumption, the [[Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa)]]/stove system, [[Fire]]); blown out by the speaker himself before *"go bananas."* The demiurge move in one gesture.
- **[W] The casual cull = harm-as-product:** yearning for connection reframed as a biological flaw to exploit (*"so what?"*); the machine harvests the crowd it pretends to entertain; the [[Great Time to Be Human]] cheerful-horror engine. → [[The Audience]] (moths) / [[The Circus]] (flame/trap).
- **[W] Gurdjieff inversion:** Gurdjieff (already tied to [[Bananas]]): man is no longer a wild animal but *not yet a human being*; wake up, labor. Lucius preaches the 180°: regress to the animal, abandon the labor. Doctrinal inversion of a source the vault uses.
- **[W] Counterfeit phoenix:** the phoenix burns to be reborn *through* death ([[Brain/Concepts/The Circle|Circle]]); [[City of Angels]]'s *"never gonna die"*/upload skips the burning (taxidermy); the moth burns toward a **false light**, mistaking incineration for connection: death **without rebirth**. Two ways to miss the phoenix: refuse the burning, or burn for nothing. → [[Inversion]].
- **[W] Flame = attention:** the candle draws the eye; flying in = entering the feed to be seen ([[The Upload]]). *"Keeps the moth population down"* = the attention economy as a cull. Moths / [[The Watchers]] / [[The Upload]] = one machine read three ways (Eye, net, flame). → [[33 (album)]].
