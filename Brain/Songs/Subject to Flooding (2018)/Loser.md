# Loser

Track 3, *Subject to Flooding* (2018, Atlantic). Debut Jagwar Twin single (premiered Zane Lowe / Beats 1; 17.4M+ Spotify streams). **Composer (Apple Music): Matthew Pauling & Roy English.** Lyrics **verified-stable** (Apple Music).

## Voicing
[[Roy]], first-person, defiant-collective (*"just like you"*). Pre-Lucius.

## Roy's framing (grounded — artist commentary)
*"We're all on this beautiful planet. We're made of the same stardust. We're all losers, and we're also all brilliant, beautiful souls."* And: *"we all walk around with masks on."* So the song's central move is **solidarity-in-imperfection** — explicitly anti-mask and anti-hierarchy.

## Moves
- **Reclamation, not defeat** (grounded via Roy's framing). *"I'm a loser, baby, just like you"* turns the rejected label into solidarity — the despised self embraced (nigredo-as-acceptance), re-soled with solar defiance: *"I got sunshine in my mind / Wake up and I'm feeling fine."* The "loser" is reclaimed exactly the way [[The Mask]] is *removed* — by refusing the optimization pressure that makes "winner" the only acceptable face.
- [W] **Beck echo (inherited substrate — allusion, not licensed interpolation).** *"I'm a loser, baby"* unmistakably echoes Beck's "Loser" (1993, *"I'm a loser baby"*). No Beck composer credit → cultural allusion, not sample/interpolation. Same album as [[Dream - Dream]] (Mercer interpolation) and [[Superhuman (Interlude)]] (Chaplin sample) — *Subject to Flooding* practices relay/reference at multiple register-levels. `CONCEPT-InheritedSubstrate`.
- [W] **Media flattening, inverted.** *"Television on your frame / Everybody's all the same"* — the screen flattening identity into commodity-sameness (proto-[[Flattened Identity]] / proto-machine, [[Machine vs Human]]). The chorus INVERTS the flattening: where the screen says *"everybody's the same"* (homogenized for the market), the chorus says *"we're all losers, just like you"* (shared dignity beneath the pressure). Same flattening; opposite valence.
- grounded **Jericho.** *"Jericho fell like a wave"* — biblical (Joshua 6, the walls of Jericho fell). Fits the album's biblical-collapse frame ([[Subject to Flooding (album)]] = the Flood). Walls/boundaries collapsing — including the social walls that manufacture "winners" and "losers."
- [W] **"Wake up."** *"Wake up and I'm feeling fine"* — sincere awakening imperative; kin to the Chaplin Interlude's call. Pre-cynical collective awakening ([[Machine vs Human]]: the last pre-cynical collective vision pole).
- [W] **Time pressure / attention economy seed.** *"Click on your clock radio / Ain't got much time till I blow."* Media-saturated urgency — a small early note that later opens out into [[Online]]'s networked-time hook *"they know my every step."*
