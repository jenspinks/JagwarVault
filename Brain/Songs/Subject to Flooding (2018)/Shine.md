# Shine

Track 13 (**album closer**), *Subject to Flooding* (2018; streaming 2019); also a 2020-01-17 "Live In Studio" single. Writers: Matthew Pauling, Roy English (`[?]` not re-screenshotted). Lyric Spotify-stable ([[Sources/Lyrics/Shine]]). The album's **reflection → radiance** turn: the self that opened in the mirror ([[Brain/Songs/Subject to Flooding (2018)/Long Time Coming|Long Time Coming]]) closes trying to **become** the light ([[Brain/Concepts/Source vs Reflection]]).

## Voicing
[[Roy]], first-person/communal — pre-persona (no Lucius in 2018). The dawn after the album's flood-night.

## Grounded
- [G] **Roy's stated intent (American Songwriter 2020):** wrote it watching the war-film *Troy* — *"a chorus that felt happy, like the sun rising"*; *"even in dark times, there is always light if you want to find it"* (the **chosen-light** thesis); *"a song is a mirror" / "I just want to be a mirror"* (mirror-doctrine). ([[Sources/Transcripts/American-Songwriter-2020-Shine]].)
- [G] **The flood named + survived:** *"earthquake under your feet / but you're in the sea so you're gonna drown"*; *"everyone's against you, from your family to your country"*; *"watching homeruns while you bunting"* → the dawn: *"I was in the clouds, now the sun is coming out singing."*
- [G] **The rigged system:** *"they run the house and rig the game to get the upper hand."*
- [G] **Inclusivity:** *"we're all the same in every nation / Gender of my generation."* ⚠ **"Gender"** (Spotify + ear); Apple/AZLyrics mis-display the word as a surname — **flag the wrong word only; never quote the misread as a line** (prior external "buried pun" hallucination).

## Moves
- [W] **Dawn after the flood (closer function):** names the deluge but **stands its ground** rather than runs; the album's defensive momentum resolves into steady solar dawn.
- [G/W] **Chosen light, not blind optimism:** the violence/rigged-game named, the light chosen anyway — Roy-stated; same decision-not-denial move as [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Life Is Good|Life Is Good]] (its 2018 antecedent).
- [W] **Agency over circumstance:** *"when life was not a celebration, I had dreams and then I made 'em… no teacher teaching could ever teach"* — internal locus, refuses victimhood.
- [W] **The "shine" lineage — vocabulary seed, redirected later.** *Shine*'s verb is **first-person/communal** (self *claims* the light, *"sing it if you know you're gonna shine"*); the later [[Mary]]-class voice turns the same vocabulary into a **second-person blessing** — *"you were born to shine"* ([[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Weirdness|Weirdness]]), *"let your light shine"* ([[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy|Ballerina Boy]]), [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/MAGIK|MAGIK]]. **Guard:** Shine is the seed, **NOT** a Mary appearance — do not add to the Mary tracker.

## Resonance
- [~] **Solar closer / [[SOL]] bookend:** STF ends on the **rising sun** (Shine, 2018); *Lucius Lullaby* ends on **SOL** (*Sol* = sun, 2026). Declared radiance (sung outward) → withheld radiance (wordless breath). Architectural.
- [~] **Cinema escapism:** *"I wish that life was like a movie"* — light brush with the later spectacle/script thread (and the song was sparked watching a film).

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
<!-- anti-totalization defense (CLAUDE.md ANTI-PATTERNS #1/#2) -->
- **The author's frame is a sunrise optimism anthem.** Roy states it plainly `[G]`: a happy chorus "like the sun rising," *"even in dark times there is light if you want to find it,"* sparked by watching *Troy*. The vault's reading — albedo / reflection→radiance / "the self that opened in the mirror tries to *become* the light" / Source-vs-Reflection — is alchemy and mirror-cosmology layered on a chosen-light closer. Honor the stated frame as the floor; tier the cosmology `[W]`. `[P]`
- **"Shine" is among pop's most ubiquitous inspirational verbs.** Tracking a "shine lineage" from this 2018 song into the 2026 Mary-class blessings risks treating *"let your light shine" / "born to shine"* — gospel/inspirational boilerplate — as a deliberate motif-thread. The page's own guard ("Shine is the seed, NOT a Mary appearance") is the right brake; keep the lineage `[W]`. `[~]`
- **"Architectural" overstates the SOL bookend.** STF ending on a rising sun and *Lucius Lullaby* ending on SOL (8 years apart) is an elegant `[~]` rhyme, but lots of albums close bright; "architectural" implies design where coincidence or shared aesthetic is at least as likely. `[~]`
- **Falsifier:** if "Shine" is exactly the chosen-light sunrise anthem Roy describes, the albedo / reflection-becomes-radiance apparatus is overlay on a sincere uplift closer.
