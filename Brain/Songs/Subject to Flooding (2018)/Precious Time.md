# Precious Time

Writers **Roy English, Matt Pauling, Sarah Vaughan**; **Adam Hawkins mixes** (album exception — only tracks 1 & 12; Jeff Ellis mixes the rest); Gehringer master, S1 exec ([[Sources/Credits/Subject to Flooding]], Spotify-verified).
