# Superhuman

Type: Song · *[[Subject to Flooding (album)|Subject to Flooding]]* (2018, Atlantic), **track 6 of 13**. Writers Jeff Bhasker, Matt Pauling & Roy English (Apple). - [W] **Marching Protector:** solo trauma goes collective; "Left, right, left" locks the tender self in a moving tank. `CONCEPT-TheProtector`, armoring mode.

- [W] **Perfectionism performed:** the perfect-child persona gone collective; wound → persona ([[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy|Ballerina Boy]]) → people-pleasing → Protector/Lucius lineage.
