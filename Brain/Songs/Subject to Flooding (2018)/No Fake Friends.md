# N.F.F. (No Fake Friends)

Track 8, *Subject to Flooding* (2018, Atlantic). Official title styling: **"N.F.F. (No Fake Friends)"**. **Composition & Lyrics (Spotify Credits, verified 2026-05-28): Roy English, Matt Pauling, S1.** (S1 also Executive Producer; Pauling also Producer. Chris Gehringer Masterer, Jeff Ellis Mixer.) Lyrics **verified-stable** (Spotify). Full essay: [[Brain/Songs/Subject to Flooding (2018)/No Fake Friends|Essays/Songs/No Fake Friends]].

## Voicing
[[Roy]], first-person, autobiographical. Pre-Lucius. Direct address to absent "fake friends."

## Moves
- [W] **Social-perimeter Protector — the third face.** Where [[Brain/Songs/Subject to Flooding (2018)/Loser]] preemptively claims the low-status label (shame-preemption) and [[Brain/Songs/Subject to Flooding (2018)/Good Day]] armors the high-mood state (vibe-defense), **N.F.F. builds the social filtration layer**: explicitly **list-keeping**, exclusion as policy. *"I made a list and I kept all the names to remember that / I don't want no fake friends around me."* Same `CONCEPT-TheProtector` architecture; this is its **interpersonal interface** — the Protector decides who gets through.
- grounded **Post-scarcity origin story.** *"I was sleeping on the sidewalk / No money, no car / Everything I owned in a backpack"* + *"Hollywood I had the night watch / Looked down at my stars"* — explicit autobiography: literal poverty. Pair with [[Brain/Songs/Subject to Flooding (2018)/Good Day]] (*"'Cause I had nothing almost three years"*); same biographical wound, different adaptation. Where Good Day rebounds into hedonic compensation, N.F.F. rebounds into **trust-failure as policy**.
- [W] **Stars flattened into sidewalks.** *"Hollywood I had the night watch / Looked down at my stars"* — the **Hollywood Walk of Fame** stars under the speaker's body while he sleeps on concrete. Stars traditionally orient *upward* (transcendence, navigation, soul-direction); here the speaker **looks *down* at them.** The heavens have collapsed into [[Malkhut|Malkuth]]: transcendence flattened into pavement; destiny converted into infrastructure. And the speaker is not gazing upward in wonder — he is on **night watch**: **the receptive faculty of wonder converting into surveillance.** Stars → pavement; wonder → vigilance; openness → scanning. *One of the defining gestures of Subject to Flooding as a whole: the gradual militarization of receptive human faculties under conditions of prolonged instability.*
- [W] **The tragedy: he recovers altitude, not trust.** The song ends in success-imagery (Bali, *"seeing so clear"*, the list working, the perimeter holding). But the relational wound that opened the song — *"Where did my friends go when i had nothing?"* — is never repaired. It is **outclimbed**. **Altitude is not the same thing as home.** Same orbit-without-return structure as [[Brain/Songs/Subject to Flooding (2018)/Good Day]]'s travel itinerary.
- [W] **The list — archival before tactical.** *"I made a list and I kept all the names to remember that"* — the list functions FIRST as an **anti-erasure mechanism** (memory will not be rewritten) before it becomes filtration logic. People emerging from social collapse often become archivists; preservation of perception precedes the defensive-architecture hardening. The list later evolves into radar / perimeter / hypervigilance, but its origin is grief, not weaponization.
- grounded **EP2 weaponization.** In *The Interview* EP 2, Lucius cites the housing/financial wound directly: *"Having a mansion, a house, even a studio apartment at this point would be a start…"* — N.F.F.'s opening sidewalk imagery returns *as Lucius's leverage*. The scarcity recorded here is what later gives Lucius's bargain its bite.
- [W] **"Where did my friends go when i had nothing?"** The wound: people disappeared during scarcity. The Protector's response is not vengeance but **a list and a closed door**. **"When i had nothing"** (lowercase *"i"* in Spotify's rendering) is structurally significant: the small *"i"* preserves the diminished pre-success self even now that the success has arrived — the wound is still narrating.
- [W] **"Bali with the fam for the New Year / I'm seeing so clear."** New-success lifestyle (mirrors [[Brain/Songs/Subject to Flooding (2018)/Good Day]]'s Aspen/Paris itinerary — **orbit as travel**), but the clarity is *filtered clarity*: vision sharpens *because* the perimeter has tightened. The Protector sells filtration as enlightenment.
- [W] **"They ain't even on my radar."** Excluded people aren't enemies — they are **erased from attention itself**. This is a deeper psychic move than rejection: not "you're out" but "you don't register." Proto-[[Flattened Identity]] in reverse — flattening *others* down to background noise.
- [W] **"I'm taking off now."** Movement as escape (orbit-mode); same vocabulary as Good Day's *"I'm on a wave"* / Loser's *"Wake up and I'm feeling fine"* — STF's repeated solution-by-momentum (motion as anesthetic).
- grounded **Ann Morrison Park** (Boise, Idaho) — biographical anchor (early backstory). *"Always hated school so I dropped out / I'm taking off now"* — the dropout / outsider origin that the Protector later defends against.
