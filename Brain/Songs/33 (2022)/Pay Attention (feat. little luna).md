# Pay Attention (feat. little luna)

Type: Song · *33* (2022, Big Loud Rock), **track 3 of 10**, 3:04. Writers **Roy English + Matt Pauling**; producers both ([[Sources/Credits/33]]). **little luna featured, not a credited writer.** Full reading: [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Pay Attention (feat. little luna)]].

**Core:** **Attention** personified as a speaking power (voiced by JT) that **offers desire and harvests awareness** — the chorus pun *"pay me / pay Attention"* makes the spent currency identical to the extracted resource.

## Grounded
- Attention speaks in first person; carnival-barker pitch (*"Step right up to my Door… what's in Store"*).
- Names + minimizes a price: *"a little price you pay"*; *"Just a small price and a **rendezvous**."*
- Desires enumerated (money / car / *"big BIG house"* / *"Everybody"*).
- V2 light/mind line: *"They say Stars are Born from Light / And you're so much more than you **Mind**."*
- Bridge scale-up: *"all your ears to the back of the room"* → *"eyes all around my head"* → *"see anything when you see my view"* → *"behind the **Empires** that rise / What they televise is just my **disguise**."*
- Closing naming ritual: *"What's my name? / Attention!"* (×3), then *"MaMa."*

## Strong Read
- **[W] Attention = the hidden god of the modern world** — tempts by offering, cost understated because the payment *is* awareness.
- **[W] Counterfeit-light** — *"Stars are Born from Light"* / *"more than you Mind"* = sacred/consciousness idiom in the tempter's mouth → [[Sir Lucius]]'s channel-mistaken-for-source error; light/*ohr* ↔ [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Soul Is A Star|Soul Is A Star]].
- **[W] Lucius *function*, not figure** — persuasion-not-force, leverage on existing desire, concealment behind visible structures (*"behind the Empires… televise is my disguise"*). Prototype of [[Sir Lucius]].
- **[W] Form collects the toll** — the *"What's my name? / Attention"* call-and-response makes answering = paying attention; recursive loop, rhymes with [[The Circle|Circle]]/[[Welcome to the Circus|Circus]] (awareness pulled *outward*).
- **[W] little luna = procurer + desire-namer** (intro introduces the mark; pre-chorus voices the wanting). **⚠ vocal attribution is by-ear** — lyric sheet assigns no vocals; confirm against the recording. *(Supersedes the old stub guess that luna = a [[Mary]]/witness function — her textual role here is Lucius-adjacent recruitment/desire, not witnessing.)*

## Resonance
Mammon/idolatry · the Demiurge (awareness directed outward) · Hermes/trickster · the Ringmaster (*"step right up"*) · marketplace-of-souls bargain (the *rendezvous*). [~] naming-as-power (El Roy/El Roi). All framework-attributed, not authorial intent.
