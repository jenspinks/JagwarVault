# It's Your Time

Track 2, *33* (2022). Writers: Roy English, Matt Pauling. A **remembrance song disguised as a success anthem** — the earnest twin of [[Online]]. Terse, structural; markers [G]/[W]/[P]/[~]. **Full prose:** [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/It's Your Time]].

## Voicing
[[Roy]], sincere / un-ironic — no Lucius, no mask. The *33* "we come from space" cosmology stated **straight**, where the adjacent [[Online]] (track 1) states it as satire. `[W]`

## Loaded Phrases
- *"once you get to Earth you forget where you come from / gotta find your Way back"* → descent → forgetting → **return**; the obstacle is amnesia, not failure → `[~]` [[Gnosticism - The World as Trap|anamnesis]]
- *"A man is like an in-between / Animating Heaven and Hell"* → the integration keystone — the living middle that **animates both poles**, not chooses one → [[Jacob & Esau]] / [[The Thread]] `[W]`
- *"in your heart there is a Well / you can follow where it leads"* → heart-as-navigator → CONCEPT-TheThread `[W]`
- *"I ain't nothing but a Fool"* → Tarot Key 0 (the unconditioned traveler) **and** *"I'm no-thing"* ego-emptiness, at once `[W]`
- *"If you don't know who you are to **be** / you still don't know you **fell**"* → (V2 mutates: *"to '**me**' / your **Self**"*) — the **Fall** + the constructed-"I" vs the capital-S Self `[W]`

## Moves
- [W] **Remembrance disguised as success.** The motivational surface (*"it's your time… learn to fly"*) sells *becoming*; the verses say *remembering* — the Eye, the Well, *"all the stars under your Belt,"* the path **already exist**; the only thing missing is memory. The disguise *is* the song.
- [W] **The earnest twin of [[Online]] (tracks 1→2).** Same "from above" cosmology, opposite register: Online = external capture (the system knows you, *"they got my face"*); It's Your Time = internal amnesia (you don't know you, *"you forget where you come from"*). The machine remembers everything about you; you remember nothing about you.
- [W] **"Animating Heaven and Hell" = the integration thesis as ontology.** Not slay the shadow — be the in-between that vivifies the *whole* polarity (the Tiferet middle pillar; one blessing / two halves, [[Jacob & Esau]]; *animating* = [[The Thread]] itself).
- [W] **kairos vs panic.** Against [[Precious Time]] (awakening *too late*; the drowning clock), this is the **seized** moment — *"all my life I've been waiting for this."* Same noun, opposite charge.

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
- **It may just be a motivational single conformed to the mythology.** Two co-writers (English + Pauling); the seamless Online→It's-Your-Time move "fits the arc almost too well." Equal evidence for deliberate design and for retrospective smoothing — the "deliberate pair" reading is `[W]`, and only the *adjacency* (tracks 1→2) is `[G]`. `[P]`
- **The esoteric scaffold is inherited, not Roy-stated.** Per [[Inherited Substrate]], descent-and-return is a perennial Neoplatonic/Gnostic shape; reading *"you fell"* as **the Fall**, the Fool as Key 0, the Well as Tiferet are `[~]` tradition-correspondence, not authorial encoding. Roy wrote a remembrance anthem; the cosmology is the reservoir it draws on. `[~]`
- **Falsifier:** Roy framing it as a straight motivational single (no descent/remembrance intent) collapses the anamnesis reading to our overlay.
