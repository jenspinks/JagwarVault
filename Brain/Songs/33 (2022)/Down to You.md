# Down to You

Type: Song · *33* (2022), **track 7 of 10**, 3:48. Writers **Roy English + Matt Pauling**; **Pauling sole producer**. Single pre-album. Full reading: [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Down to You]].

**Core:** the **inward correction** to the attention-cluster. *"It all comes down to you"* = responsibility **and** descent (light from heaven, the crown-flame, "up from below" = emanation, both ways). Names the album's *"fame / money"* false gods and **refuses** them with the wilderness reply: *"one cannot live on bread alone."* Hands authority to the heart over the head.

## Grounded
- Hook: *"It all comes down to you… Down, down, down,"* ad-lib *"(up from below)."*
- Embody not acquire: *"In your mind, you must be it"; "be the beat you're beating"; "nothing ever changes 'til it changes when you be it."*
- Crown/light: *"the flame at the top of your head"; "to your mind, a light will find its way from heaven."*
- **Heart/mind stated:** *"once you find it, can't unwind it with your head on / so listen to your heart."*
- Reversible refrain: *"Heaven in the sky to the Earth below"* / *"Heaven in your eye from the Earth below"* / *"anywhere that you go."*
- Worship/false-gods (V2): *"What's the God you believe in? / Is it fame? Is it— (money…)."*
- Scripture: *"One cannot live on bread alone… something inside you pulling you into the feeling."*
- Refrain: *"you are the one."*

## Strong Read
- **[W] Title = double movement** — responsibility ("comes down to you") + **descent/emanation** (light from heaven, crown-flame, heaven→earth, "up from below").
- **[W] "Be it" doctrine** — embodiment precedes change (cf. [[It's Your Time]]'s remember-don't-acquire).
- **[W] Heart/mind split, explicit** — *"can't unwind it with your head on / listen to your heart"* = the project's [[Duality|heart/mind]] theology in a *33* lyric; the [[Sir Lucius|Lucius]]-as-mind frame.
- **[W] Crown-flame = divine spark localized** (Keter/halo/crown chakra); kingdom-within (`CONCEPT-Malkhut`, Luke 17:21).
- **[W] Wilderness refusal of the album's bargain** — names *fame/money* (the [[Pay Attention (feat. little luna)|Attention]] offerings) and answers *"one cannot live on bread alone"* (Matt 4:4). **Bread/cake axis:** [[I Like to Party]] sells the *"cake"* (Devil's Food / "sell 'em the cake"); Down to You refuses with *"bread alone"* — and even bread isn't enough without the inner "feeling."
- **[W] "You are the one" = sovereignty, not ego** — away from external authorities toward what only the self can become.

## Resonances
As above/so below (Hermetic; reversible heaven↔earth — cf. [[The Pattern]]) · divine spark / kingdom within (Malkhut) · individuation (be-it) · [~] the One (Neoplatonic / chosen-figure). *"Heaven in your eye"* brushes El-Roi/Eye. Framework-attributed.

## Open Questions
- "You are the one": responsibility / divinity / identity weighting? (descent + crown tilt toward divinity-realized.)
- Descending light = revelation / inspiration / emanation? (bidirectionality → emanation.)
- Deliberateness of the fame/money callback to tracks 3–4 (intent unconfirmed; retrospective-coherence guard).

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
- Reads as a straight **empowerment/Top-40 anthem** ("it's up to you") with light-and-heart flavor; the emanation/heart-mind/wilderness-refusal architecture is `[W]`, layered on a verified-but-spare lyric. The scripture is grounded; the "answers the bargain" structure is the read.
