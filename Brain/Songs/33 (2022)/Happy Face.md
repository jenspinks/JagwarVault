## Notes
Highest-profile single in the catalogue to date. Roy's documented framing on the societal mask: "put on a happy face / then everything's OK" — the lyric performs the toxic positivity it critiques. Directly connects to [[The Mask]], [[The Spectacle]], and the "Happy Face :(a): LIVE" release suggests Roy is aware of the ironic/tragic register of the title. The :(a) notation recurs in Lucius Lullaby era material and may be analytically significant.
