# Happy Face

**Album:** [[33 (album)]] (2022, Big Loud Rock) — **track 6 of 10**, 2:49. Breakthrough single; catalogue's most-streamed (70M+). Writers: Roy English & Matt Pauling ([[Sources/Credits/33]]). **Full analysis:** [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Happy Face]].

## Grounded
- Hook = instruction to **perform, not feel**: *"put on a happy face / then everything's okay,"* *"if you're sad, don't let it show,"* *"say you're good, say you're fine."* (lyrics, Spotify-stable)
- Algorithmic pressure: *"Hollywood on your timeline / telling you what to wear / and what to like and how to be"*; *"ticking like a timebomb"*; *"Death of my generation."*
- [G] **Lucius claims it by name (theatrical):** EP1 *"happy face, bad feeling… Those are me. You need me"*; EP3 *"then you tap me in, I give you Happy Face"* → *"hundreds of millions of streams"*; *"the algorithm, baby."* ([[Sources/Transcripts/The Interview (Roy vs Sir Lucius, theatrical EP1-7)]]) *(a character's claim, not autobiographical.)*
- [G] Roy's happiness-wish: *"creating happiness is all I want to do"* ([[Sources/Transcripts/Knockturnal-2019-Studio-Sessions|Knockturnal 2019]]).
