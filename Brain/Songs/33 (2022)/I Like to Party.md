# I Like to Party

Type: Song · *33* (2022), **track 4 of 10**, 2:57. **Album's most-streamed** (~12.6M). Writers **Roy English + Matt Pauling**; **Pauling sole producer**. Full reading: [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/I Like to Party]].

**Core:** an upbeat party anthem about people who **never leave the house** — *"we stay in and pretend that we're in the club"* = simulated participation. The narrator herds a crowd (*"the people are mine"*), the spectacle is a manufactured product, and a kingdom-within counter-awakening surfaces in the bridge only to be **cut off by the broadcast**.

## Grounded
- Hook contradiction: party enthusiasm + *"we don't go outside / we stay in and pretend that we're in the club."*
- Command/ownership narrator: *"follow me… sing along… lock up your door… one, two, three, four"*; *"give me attention, attention / the people are mine"*; *"a new direction / affecting the state of your mind."*
- Manufacture/media: *"Television on the news," "Devil's Food is a cake," "we don't want 'em awake," "bake a truth with a lie / then we sell 'em the cake."*
- **"Tell-A-Vision" = official JT paratext `[G]`** (Jen, 2026-06-16) — the band's shop sells a **Tell-A-Vision Tee**: a vintage TV with a woman's face on the screen, reading *"TELL-A-VISION"* ([[JT Shop - Tell-A-Vision Tee]]). The lyric text is *"Television on the news,"* but the official merch confirms the **tell-a-vision pun** as intentional JT language — the device that *tells a vision* `[W]`. (Lifts the prior *"[~] do not assert."*)
- Crowd in circles: *"running around like chickens with no head / in circles, they go."*
- Bridge two-awakenings: *"Devil waking up to claim the kingdom / People waking up to claim what's in them"* → *"Heaven is within your heart / you're the kingdom"* → **interrupt cuts it mid-word** (*"you're the King—" / "we interrupt our program"*) → back to chorus.

## Strong Read
- **[W] Simulated participation** — connection felt without connecting; the clap-along form *is* the deception.
- **[W] Narrator = the *Pay Attention* attention-force, operating** — *"the people are mine"* echoes track 3's acquisitive "I"; adjacency + shared author + lexical echo. Strengthened by Pay Attention's grounded casting (**Roy = Attention**). Still [W].
- **[W] Pied Piper structure** — counting + call-and-response enacts the herding (*"follow me… to the beat of my drum"*).
- **[~+] Whose drum you move to = whose creature you are** (reframed — *not* a "drum census"). The Piper's *"to the beat of my drum… follow me"* here (2021) sits between **a true drum** — *"I can hear your heart, it beats"* (Move To You, 2018) — and **an inherited dread-drum** — *"coming like a drum from 1981"* (All My Friends, 2023). The point isn't that the word "drum" recurs (that's the cheap version); it's that **rhythm = allegiance**: the heart's beat, the Piper's beat, the dread-beat are three masters, and which one you move to is which one owns you. `[~+]` — a framework reading of an allegiance motif, not a claimed authorial through-line. *(33 Deep Dive D12 — vetted up from "census" only on this reframe.)*
- **[W] Manufactured distraction** — *"bake a truth with a lie… sell 'em the cake"*; targets the information **system**. (Cf. the board *Tracks* song *"Let Them Eat Cake."*)
- **[W] The interrupt overrides the inner awakening** — the kingdom-within line is literally cut by *"we interrupt our program"*; the distraction-system reasserts. Kingdom-within = `CONCEPT-Malkhut` / Luke 17:21.
- **[W] Mass culture as secular ritual**; **[~] "in circles" = the Circle inverted** (spectacle/dissipation vs return).
- **[W] The Chaplin relay, re-voiced — not sampled.** The bridge's kingdom-within (*"people waking up to claim what's in them / Heaven is within your heart / you're the kingdom"*) **borrows the words of Chaplin's *Great Dictator* speech** (*"the kingdom of God is within man"*) — the transmission sampled *verbatim* on [[Brain/Songs/Subject to Flooding (2018)/Superhuman (Interlude)|Superhuman (Interlude)]] (2018), here **re-voiced as Roy's own lyric, not a clip** (Jen, 2026-06-16). Inherited-substrate relay at the lyric level (`CONCEPT-InheritedSubstrate`), carrying the humanist "kingdom within" into the *33* media-machine — the [[Machine vs Human]] arc.
- **[W] The broadcast war.** Three signals fight for the listener: **Tell-A-Vision sedation** (the system — *"we don't want 'em awake," "bake a truth with a lie"*), the **kingdom-within awakening** (*"claim what's in them"*), and the **"we interrupt our program"** counter-broadcast carrying Chaplin's borrowed humanist words — but the interrupt **cuts the awakening mid-word** and returns to the chorus. A fight over *who authors reality*: the spectacle-machine, the awakened self, or the borrowed human voice.
- **[W] Lucius-coded, not Lucius-voiced.** ILTP dramatizes the mechanism Lucius later personifies — spectacle-as-containment, false sweetness, media seduction, awakening-language converted to content — **before** Lucius is named or credited here. Coded, not a `[G]` Lucius vocal. → [[Sir Lucius]] / [[The Algorithm]].
