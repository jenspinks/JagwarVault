# Imagine A World

Type: Song · *33* (2022), **track 9 of 10** (penultimate), 4:44. Writers **Roy English + Matt Pauling**; both produce. Title distinct from Lennon's "Imagine." Full reading: [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Imagine A World]].

**Core:** an **"imagine / create your world"** capstone — imagination framed as **authorship, not escape** (*"playing your note in a song / written by you for your life"*) — running through wonder, false/**designed** gods (money, **OZ**), media conditioning (*"Those Thoughts take ahold of you"*), chosen hardship, and landing on **Eden relocated inside** (*"look inside… believe it… Conceive it"*).

## Grounded
- World located internally: *"it's all inside of your mind / it's up to you to decide."*
- Authorship: *"you can't get it wrong / playing your note in a song / written by you for your life."*
- *"Is there a God you designed?"*; money as *"cause and reaction."*
- Passive vs heroic options (*"do nothing… yell… fight"* vs *"be a hero… went your own way"*).
- *"You pick your parents… reflections of who you are (It's not a phase mom!)."*
- Conditioning: *"people start to label you / cable news / Those Thoughts take ahold of you."*
- God-list w/ a fraud: *"Brahma, Muhammad, and OZ / Horus, the Buddha, and Christ."*
- Internal Eden: *"The Garden Of Eden (In-A-Gadda-Da-Vida) / look inside you'll see it / believe it / Conceive it."*

## Strong Read
- **[W] Imagination = authorship, not escape** (write your own song/life) — the cosmogony of [[Another Way II Heaven]] made **participatory** (you design your world).
- **[W] Worldview = chosen lens** (same world: wonder/tragedy/opportunity/prison).
- **[W] Labeling + media narrow the wonder** — *"Those Thoughts"* / cable news = the attention-economy critique (tracks 3–4) re-entering.
- **[W] OZ = the designed / fraud god** — the man behind the curtain inserted among real deities, next to *"a God you designed"* → some gods are fabrications; ↔ [[Pay Attention (feat. little luna)|Pay Attention]] *"televise is just my disguise."*
- **[W] Adversity as chosen curriculum + mirror** — parents as *"reflections of who you are"* folds the mirror motif into family.
- **[W] Eden relocated to consciousness** — *believe → Conceive* (imagination as creation/birth); kingdom-within garden form (`CONCEPT-Malkhut`). *"In-A-Gadda-Da-Vida"* = the garbled external Eden surviving as a pop echo; the real one is inside.

## Resonances
Imagination-as-creation (Romantic/Hermetic/New Thought) · child-wonder · Eden-within · perennialism-with-a-trickster (the OZ insertion) · [~] Iron Butterfly garbled-sacred. Framework-attributed.

## Open Questions
- OZ: whimsy / perennialist inclusivity / fraud-god wink? (album context → the wink.)
- "Choose your parents" — perspective exercise, or brushing reincarnation/soul-contract?
- "Conceive it" — literal imagination-as-creation vs motivation?
- Does Roy **invert** the utopian "Imagine" register or sustain it? (Reads as sincere-with-a-warning, not inverted.)

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
- A sincere, indie-rock **imagination/empowerment anthem**; the curtain-god / Eden-within / mirror architecture is `[W]` layered on a verified-but-accessible lyric. OZ may be pure play.
