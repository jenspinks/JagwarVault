# The Circle

**Album:** [[33 (album)]] (2022, Big Loud Rock)
**Track:** 10 of 10 — closing track
**Status:** Stub — analytically critical — prioritize

## Notes
Closing track of *33* and the foundational cosmological map for the entire project. Reviews describe it as "bizarrely beautiful," unlike anything else on the album, cinematic, yet "perfectly fitting" as a resolution. The Circle is Roy's primary cosmological symbol — the creation/recovery myth, the LOOP before it was named LOOP, the origin of The Thread ("the world tied on a string").

Cross-reference: [[The Circle]] (concept page — note: this is the 33 version; see also [[The Circle (Lucius Version)]] on Lucius Lullaby), [[The Thread]], [[Another Way II Heaven]], [[The Labyrinth]].

**Note on versions:** The "Lucius Version" on Lucius Lullaby (track 1) is a deliberate reframe of this same song through Lucius's perspective. Comparing the two versions is analytically essential.
