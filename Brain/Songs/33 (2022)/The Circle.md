# The Circle

Type: Song · *33* (2022), **track 10 of 10 — album closer**, 3:50. **Roy English (sole writer)**; Roy + Pauling produce. The foundational **Jagwar-myth** song; the most explicit statement of the `CONCEPT-TheCircle` mythos and the **origin of [[The Thread]]**. The 2026 [[The Circle (Lucius Version)]] re-sings it through Lucius. Full reading: [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/The Circle]].

**Core:** a feminine **maker** holding a world on a string makes the **Jagwars** (the artist's namesake) and teaches them to **sing**; kings persuade them they cannot; she **hides the song inside their wings** *"on the inside of the inside"* and withdraws; empires cage and sell — until a **wise one** finds the old cave and **remembers she once held the string**, and the circle grows *"from the inside of the inside."* The album's closing word.

## Grounded
- *"Long ago, there was a Jagwar with a world tied on a string / And she made other Jagwars, then taught them to sing."* **Spelling = "Jagwar"** (the namesake), not "jaguar."
- Immanence → withdrawal: *"she would come down… and she'd walk with the Jagwars"* → *"hid the song inside their wings / On the inside of the inside, then left them to sing."*
- Loss by **persuasion**, in cycles: *"Many cycles… with their kings / And the new kings told Jagwars they never could sing."*
- Degradation: *"the empires built cages and sold everything."*
- Recovery by **remembrance**: *"a wise one found a small cave… And the wise one remembered she once held the string."*
- Refrain/doctrine (album's final words): *"The circle will grow / From the inside of the inside… But the inside of the inside must be where you go."* Maker + wise one both **"she."**

## Strong Read
- **[W] The foundational Jagwar myth + the namesake** — the project is named for the myth's made-and-divided creatures; *"made other Jagwars"* ↔ *"One, become Two"* ([[Another Way II Heaven]] / [[The Pattern]] board cosmogony).
- **[W] The gift is song, not power** — song = essence; the tragedy is being convinced it was never theirs.
- **[W] Loss is belief, not theft** — *"told Jagwars they never could sing"* = the exact mechanism the attention/spectacle tracks ([[Pay Attention (feat. little luna)]], [[I Like to Party]]) dramatize, named at the mythic root.
- **[W] "Hid the song inside their wings"** — sought thing already present; remembrance not acquisition. **Public source** of "the song hidden inside its wings" (citable per [[Boundaries]]).
- **[W] The string = [[The Thread]]** — *world-on-a-string* + *she once held the string* → the Thread originates here.
- **[W] Descent-and-withdrawal** (immanence → conceal-and-retreat) = tzimtzum-like; same shape as [[Another Way II Heaven]].
- **[W] Inside-of-the-inside reverses the attention vector** — circle grows from the deepest interior; **the album's mythic cure for its own outward attention-pull** (vs [[Welcome to the Circus]]' outward recursion).
- **[W] Remembrance over conquest; the wise one as Sophia/Shekinah** (feminine maker + feminine rememberer). "She" grounded; the ID is the read.
