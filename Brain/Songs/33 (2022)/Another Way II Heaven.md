# Another Way II Heaven

Type: Song · *33* (2022), **track 8 of 10**, 4:25. **Roy English (sole writer)**; Roy + Pauling produce. The album's **solo-authored creation myth**. Full reading: [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Another Way II Heaven]].

**Core:** a first-person cosmogony — a lone *"I"* that can't self-know speaks itself into two (*"One, become Two"*), births a mirror (*"I" in reverse → "You"*), a Darkness/Light whose collision makes *"a Sun… inside,"* then multiplies 4→11, risks self-forgetting at 9, with reunion (*"The Great Design"*) promised and escape forbidden.

## Grounded
- **The lyric = Roy's board cosmogonic poem** ([[Sources/Artifacts/Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)]] / [[The Pattern]]) — the **same creation myth composed twice** (song + standalone board text). Grounds the cosmogony as Roy's own intentional framing.
- Solitude → sight: *"I was just me / But who was 'I' to Know? / I wanted to see myself."*
- Division speech-act: *"I spoke the words 'One, become Two' / Then 'I' was in reverse… 'I' became 'You'."*
- Dyad/union: *"A Darkness and a Light"* → *"When we crashed and came together / A Sun was born inside."*
- Count: 1 → 2 → (no 3) → *"Next there was 4 / Process and Time"* → *"Split into Five (5)… Nine… I had to remind myself / That 'I' was still 'I'."*
- Door at 4: *"I opened up a Door / And I just released myself / To cover the sky."*
- Reunion: *"One day we'll get back together / And see The Great Design."* Number-range: *"From One and through Eleven"* / *"From 1:00 and through 11:00."*
- Title shift *"to"* → *"II."* Captor ad-lib: *"(You'll never get away)" / "Don't you ever try to get away."*

## Strong Read
- **[W→grounded-as-Roy's-myth] Creation by need for self-sight** — epistemic, not material; world from self-reflection.
- **[W] II = two = the way** — *"to Heaven"* → *"II Heaven"*; multiplicity is the route.
- **[W] The mirror is in the cosmogony** — *"'I' was in reverse"* → "You"; the project's mirror motif originates here.
- **[W] Coniunctio** — Darkness + Light collide → the Sun/Self; the Roy/Lucius dyad placed at the origin; light *produced by* the meeting (cf. [[Soul Is A Star]] Sun/Son).
- **[W] Nine = Yesod** (self-forgetting / channel-mistaken-for-source — the Lucius error). Kabbalah is Roy's board substrate; the 9=Yesod *label* is the reader's frame.
- **[W] The count is a clock; missing 12 = the return** (12→1 loops to the One; deferred to "The Great Design"). 1–11 = Sefirot + Da'at.
- **[W] Heaven = perception, not destination** (*"I see it different every Time"*).
- **[W] Captor underside** — *"you'll never get away"* = closed system ([[Sir Lucius]] loop). **Song-only — absent from the board poem.**

## Resonances
Emanation / Lurianic procession-return (tikkun = "Great Design") · cosmic mirror · hero's cycle · [~] Pythagorean unfolding (skipped 3 = the Sun, the union's offspring). Framework-attributed; Kabbalah substrate grounded via the board.

## Open Questions
- Skipped **3** = the Sun, or elided?
- Clock 1:00–11:00 → missing **12** = return-to-One?
- The *"never get away"* voice — the dividing One or a later intruder? (Lucius reading; note it's song-only.)

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
- Reads as an abstract, pretty **numerology/creation conceit** — the specific Kabbalah label-mappings (Yesod=9, Sefirot+Da'at=11) are `[W]`/resonance, not stated. **But** the cosmogony-as-intentional is unusually well-grounded (the board poem), so this is *less* speculative than most per-song framework reads.
