# The Labors of Hercules

Esoteric trial-cycle framework. [W] One working lens in the catalogue, not the master key (see Counterarguments).

## Public anchors (none)
Greek myth (Apollodorus etc.); Bailey *The Labors of Hercules* (1974); Tarot Strength (Arc. VIII); Roy's 3 publicly curated Bailey quotes ("Illusion is the first product of the mind when it is divorced from the soul"; "the path of liberation leads through the midst of the world of illusion"; "victim of illusion, propaganda, mass suggestion"). [W] The curation = ground-floor evidence Bailey is *in* the project, not imported.

## Leo labor (central) [W]
Nemean Lion = coordinated dominant personality (successful ego turned tyrannical). Cave w/ two openings; block one (seal emotional escape), drop weapons, strangle bare-handed = integrate, don't kill; energy preserved, authority changes. → [[The Mask|Strength card]] = earned steady state. [W] Lucius = the un-tamed lion; [[Welcome to the Circus]] = lion ravaging before the cave.

## Strongest lyric anchor (none)
[[Not Your Homie]]: "if you choose to tame The Beast / you've got to prove yourself to be / a champion." [W] *tame* (not kill) + *The Beast* + *champion* + *choose to* = tight Leo cluster. [[#1 Champion (la Conquistadora)]] reinforces (track 8).

## Counterarguments [W]
"tame the beast" is universal; "champion" may = winner not initiate; Bailey is one syncretic layer not spine; album arc has ≥1 valid frame; retrospective-totalization risk — test every Strong Read: "would it hold without Bailey?"
