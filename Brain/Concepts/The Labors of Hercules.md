## Labour IV — Cancer (none — IG Live) [NEW node, GROUNDED — Roy tells it in full]
Roy explicitly: *"cancer specifically is the **fourth labor**"* (grounds the numbering — no longer inferred; Aries 1 → Cancer 4 → Libra 7, consistent w/ the captured Libra = VII page). He tells the whole labour and applies it to himself (none — [[Sources/Transcripts/Roy IG Live - Bailey 12 Labours of Hercules]]):

**The labour (Roy's telling):** Hercules (the initiate/soul) must capture the thing he loves most — *a doe/wild deer*. **Two goddesses** counsel him: one = **instinct** ("like an animal… lower"), the other = **intellect / the mind** ("higher"). Acting from instinct fails; acting from intellect fails. A **third voice** — *"the synthesis between the instinct and the intellect… the intuition… super consciousness, the god voice thing in here"* — succeeds: he captures the doe, holds it, loves it. **Cancer's gift:** loves/nurtures deeply, psychic (= intuition). **Cancer's shadow:** *"holds too tight and won't let go."* **The lesson:** God commands he **let the doe go and sacrifice it on the altar of Apollo, the sun god**; it is *"born again… over and over again forever."* → *"letting go and not attaching to the outcome. Knowing that everything that comes to me is God's."*

**Self-application:** via natal **Venus in Cancer (Rx, 10th house)** — *"how I love, how I create, nurture… but you also have to let things go… as it relates to record labels… career… love."* **Method made explicit:** natal chart → sign → Bailey labour → soul-lesson. (Correlation: he posted the **Libra** labour [Story] and is **Libra Rising**; he taught the **Cancer** labour and has **Venus in Cancer** — he works the labours of *his own placements*.)
