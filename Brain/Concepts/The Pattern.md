# The Pattern

Type: Concept / Framework (Roy-named)

Roy's own term for **the single law that repeats at every scale** — "as above, so below," the same formula from solar system to atom, encoded in myths, movies, music, biology, number. **Grounded as his framework**, not interpreter-projection: the term is his, written on his own [[Sources/Artifacts/Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)|"I'm 33 this year"]] board — *"The Pattern is present on all levels"* (Hermetic Correspondence cell) and a 22-title list *"The Pattern Represented in Movies."* This page is the **hub node** for that artifact. **Guard:** Roy *holding* a Pattern-framework is grounded; that any given song "encodes the Pattern" is the same over-fit risk flagged for [[The Labors of Hercules|the labours]] — license the lens, not a master key.

## What it is (board, grounded)
A unified esoteric-scientific system Roy assembles on the board from named sources — **Bailey + Walter Russell + the Kybalion (Hermeticism) + Kabbalah + Jung + Plato** — around one claim: reality is one fractal correspondence, and the self can be *read* (and possibly *uploaded*) through it. Board framing artifacts:
- **Tab title:** *"How to upload myself to the internet."* Board title: *"I'm 33 this year."*
- The **Kybalion's 7 Principles** transcribed in full (Mentalism → Correspondence → Vibration → Polarity → Rhythm → Cause-and-Effect → Gender) = the operating rules. *"The All is Mind… Mind woven into matter."*
- *"What is Form? The interlocking of free-moving Force into patterns which then operate as a unity."*

## The spine: one ascent, many encodings [W]
The board renders a single **10-stage ascent** (descent→return) in parallel alphabets — strong internal evidence it's *one* schema, not loose collage:
- tone (the "AI musical scale"), color ("Light/Color scale"), virtue/vice ("Positive/Negative traits — 10 Stages"), body ("Body Part Stages": feet→crown), Japanese stage-names (body → … → highest self), and a 0–10 emanation list ("Nothing IS… I AM… The World"). 
- Container-figures for the ascent: **Tree of Life / sephirot**, **Jacob's Ladder / hopscotch Earth→Heaven** (marked *NChSh* = Nachash), **Noah's Ark "3 stories: Heaven, Man, Earth = 32"**, the **Hero's Journey** wheel ("Ordinary World → Rabbit Hole → … → Master of Twin Worlds → New Cycle Begins").
- Mapped to "**Review cycles of human consciousness throughout history**" → the Pattern as historical-cyclical, not only personal.

## "One become Two" → the split [W]
The board's cosmogonic poem: *"I spoke the words 'One become Two' … A Darkness and a Light … when we crashed … A Sun was born inside … Split into 5,6,7,8,9 … 'I' was still I … see The Great Design."* [W] This is the **[[Sir Lucius|Roy/Lucius]]** split written as cosmology — the artist-bio *"there's two sides to every story"* given a creation myth; coheres with Kybalion **Polarity/Gender** and the **Left/Right-hemisphere** cell (Order/reason/masculine + vs Chaos/creativity/feminine −). See [[Duality]].

## The upload thesis (board, grounded)
The Pattern's practical application: if the self is information in the field, it can be externalized. Board lists: *"Uploading my consciousness to the Internet"* (Pros/Cons), *"How to live forever?"* ("create something that lives beyond your lifespan / put music on chain"), and **"Steps to enter the digital dimension"** (gather memories → personality mask → digitize → cloud → teach AI who I am → make a body for the AI). The **"Cybernetics"** cell states the premise: *"All the world is mind. Mind woven into matter."*
