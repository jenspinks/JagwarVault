# The Manifesto Cards

Type: Concept — the recurring **credit-card / title-card device** stamped on Jagwar Twin releases since ~2019: a fixed graphic that hides an esoteric **manifesto** in the credits, **filtering for the audience willing to decode** (concealment-as-invitation; the casual scroller sees a flyer, the attentive viewer finds a thesis). The project's connective tissue across eras. **Full analysis:** [[The Visual-Canon Throughline (Manifesto Cards, the Realized Circus, the Silent Lucius) 2019-2026]].

## The template `[G]`
A **blackletter title** · an era-tag (e.g. *"LIVE UNDERGROUND IN LOS ANGELES"*) · **"PRESENTED BY / BROUGHT TO YOU BY/BUY JAGWAR TWIN"** (blackletter) · a **seeker-question** · a **sigil-triad** · a **micro-type manifesto-paragraph** (deliberately tiny — resolves only when you stop and zoom in).

The device **clusters 2019–2021**, pauses ~5 years (the 2022–2025 narrative videos don't use it), and **resumes in 2026 for *Lucius Lullaby*** (the *Live Underground* cards). The LL cards may be a distinct sub-series — see §The cipher.

## "Are you content?" — the device's thesis in three words `[W]`
Happy Face's *"ARE YOU CONTENT?"* is a **triple pun**: **content** = happy/satisfied · **content** = media-to-be-consumed (*are you the content?* — pairs with *"BROUGHT TO YOU BUY"*) · **content** = complacent/contained (the opposite of the other cards' *"what is it that you seek?"*). The whole **consumerism-vs-seeker** critique in three words. → [[The Algorithm]], [[Consumption]], [[The Spectacle]].

## The doctrines it has carried the whole time `[W]`
- **you-are-it / kingdom-within** — *"you're the Kingdom, you're the King"* (ILTP 2021) = *"you are it"* / *"you are the maze"* = Chaplin's *"the kingdom of God is within man"* ([[Superhuman (Interlude)]] / Luke 17:21).
- **the Devil / Lucius** — *"Devil waking up to claim the kingdom"* (ILTP 2021), **before** the named 2023 dream-origin ([[Sir Lucius]]).
- **as above so below** (Hermetic) → [[The Pattern]], on a card in **2021** (the root of WTTC's 2026 Hermes).
- **change-the-concept-of-self** = the mirror doctrine ([[Source vs Reflection]]); near-verbatim **Neville Goddard** (named-influence lead — verify).
- **burn-it-down → rebirth / "death of my generation"** → [[Nigredo]] (Happy Face card; staged years later in Bad Feeling's fire-close).
- **"NONE"** = the **No-Thing** ([[The Empty Mirror]]) as the answer to *"what do you seek?"*

## The cipher — OPEN, re-scoped to the LL wave `[?]`
The early wave (2019–2021) used esoteric-*consumerist/Hermetic* manifestos with **no Hebrew letters.** The **LL wave** introduced one (Bounce → Sefer Yetzirah **Beth**) — a *new* move — so the LL cards (Bounce, WTTC) **could be a fresh coded series.** With only two so far (Beth; WTTC's Hermes-riddle), the **{Vav, Beth, Beth, Lamed} → בבל letter-cipher stays `[?]` OPEN**, the **Lucius Lullaby card** the live test (a Sefer Yetzirah **Lamed** = strong confirmation + the Lamed=Libra⇄Labour-VII cross-key). *(Corrected 2026-06-25 from an over-hasty "retired" call — see the throughline essay.)* Full pre-registration: [[Sources/Quotes/JT IG Reel - bounce (Live Underground) 2026-06-10]].

## Cautions
The *artifacts* (template, text, sigils, dates) are `[G]` — documented public videos. The "one pre-architected work" reading they support is `[W]` (guard against retrospective-totalization). Not every release carries a card; the cards' content *varies* (which is the argument against a rigid cipher).
