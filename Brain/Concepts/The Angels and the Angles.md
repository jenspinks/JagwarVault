# The Angels and the Angles

Type: Concept — the **letter-mysticism substrate** the catalogue draws on: **consonants = angles** (sounded structure) · **vowels = angels = breath/spirit, _Ruach_** · the singer as the instrument the spirit is sung through. Seeded 2026-06-07.

## The framework (documented Kabbalah) [G — tradition]
- **Letters are the building blocks of creation** — *Sefer Yetzirah*: the cosmos is "traced, cut, weighed and inverted" out of the **22 letters** (+ vowel-points). Letters are the *formative powers of existence*.
- **Vowels = spirit, consonants = body** — the cleanest match. 13th-c. kabbalist **Ezra ben Solomon**: *"the vowels function among the consonants like the spirit in the body."* The *niqqud* (vowel-points, unwritten in an unpointed text) are **"the soul of the letters."** Hebrew is an **abjad** — consonants written, **spirit (vowels) added after.** → *consonants = angles (structure); vowels = the breath/spirit filled in.*
- **Letters = angels** — native to Kabbalah: the **Shem HaMephorash** (Exodus 14:19–21 → 72 three-letter Names → 72 angels via *-el/-iah*). The letters **are** the angels. → *angles = angels.*
- **The method = Abulafia** — ecstatic/prophetic Kabbalah: **permuting the letters with added vowel-tones** to reach prophetic vision; "vowels and musical accents took on spiritual significance… the combination of sound and letter… a direct encounter with the Divine Word." The letters become **"a ladder for the mind to climb."** → the **singer chanting vocables = the Abulafian technique**, the mouth as the instrument the spirit plays.

- **Nonsense-syllable vocables = vowel-spirit over consonant-structure** — the *"la-la" / "na-na" / "ba-ba"* textures across the catalogue (e.g. [[City of Angels]], [[Not Your Homie]], [[Bananas]]' *"go bananananana"*); cf. the "nonsense is the only sense that makes sense" writing-method ([[The Mouth]]). Pure vowel sound = the *angels* sung through the *angles*.
- **"Angels" named, "angles" implied** — [[City of Angels]]; the single **tomorrow (ANGLE OF ETERNITY)** literally fuses the pun in a title.
- **Tree-of-Life iconography** — [[Another Way II Heaven]] (tree-of-life ascension); the theatrical set's **Tree-of-Life pillars** + Egyptian altar (see [[The Banana]]).
- **The El Roi Hebrew tattoo** — *אל ראי* on the throat (public video still; [[Sir Lucius]]) — Hebrew on the **utterance-gate**; the *r-'-y* root braids *seeing / mirror (re'i) / lion (ari)*.
- **Roy publicly assembles Kabbalah** — the public *"I'm 33"* board lists Kabbalah among his sources ([[The Pattern]]); and his **Hebrew-language interest is public** (2019, [[Sources/Quotes/Roy FB Post - Diary of a Loser (Hebrew, Everything is a Mirror) 2019]] — *"a documentary on the Hebrew language… Everything is a mirror"*).

## Why it matters [~]
It reframes the album's "childish nonsense" as **technique, not decoration**: the vocables are the *vowel-spirit* (the angels) carried on the *consonant-structure* (the angles) — the mouth as the instrument the spirit is sung through ([[The Mouth]]).
