# The Realm

Type: Concept — **the parallel world Roy builds to route around the industry gatekeepers.** Where the [[The Circus|Circus]] is the algorithmic-gatekeeping system (the thing that blocks him), the **Realm** is the medieval/fantasy counter-world — ren-faire headlining, bardcore, the Inner Circle, blockchain releases — the distribution-and-aesthetic world built *outside* the gatekeepers' reach. Seeded 2026-06-14 (folded from the gatekeeper-saga handoff). Facts `[G]`; the routes-around-the-gatekeepers reading is `[W]`.

## The reading — the Realm routes around the gatekeepers `[W]`
The Realm is the **parallel distribution world** built outside label control: ren-faire **headlining** (his own stage, no gatekeeper), **blockchain** releases (*"the label has no rights there"* — [[Sources/The Game/Gatekeeper Saga (dumbledore)]]), and the **Inner Circle** (direct-to-fan, owned audience). Where the [[The Circus|Circus]] is the system that blocks and games attention, the Realm is the **escape hatch** — the world Roy builds when the gatekeepers won't let him through.

Structural complement to the [[Sources/The Game/Gatekeeper Saga (dumbledore)|gatekeeper saga]]: **Circus = the blockade; Realm = the route around it.** It carries the same double-bind — the Realm still has to win attention (the Spotify/radio campaigns), so the escape is never fully clean.
