# Flattened Identity

Type: Concept / **Algorithm pathology** — what the Machine does to a living self.

> **Created 2026-06-29** (status: **draft** — the definition is solid; it needs a **line-level audit** before stable; see Open below). Promoted from the pageless registry. It answers: *what does the Machine do to a living self?*

## Definition `[W]`
**Flattened Identity** is the [[Brain/Concepts/The Algorithm|Algorithm]]'s reduction of a **multidimensional self into a usable surface** — brand, face, role, statistic, avatar, content-type, target demographic, spectacle, or function. The move, repeated across the catalogue's machine pole:

> **person → profile · voice → content · witness → audience · artist → product · soul → star · Roy → role · Lucius → brand-mask · self → utility.**

It is **not merely "having a persona."** That's normal. Flattening is the **pathological** version: the backstage disappears, the role becomes compulsory, and the person is valued only by the role's *usefulness to the system.*

> **The load-bearing line:** *Flattened Identity is not the mask. It is the system insisting the mask is all there is.*

## What it is NOT (the distinctions that keep it sharp)
- **vs [[Brain/Concepts/The Mask]]:** a mask can be *chosen* — protective, ritual, theatrical, transformative. Flattened Identity is **imposed or rewarded** by the system until the person **cannot easily remove it.**
- **vs [[Brain/Concepts/The Digital Double]]:** the Double is the *copied/mediated* self. Flattening is **what the system does to that copy** — reduces it to a profile, content-object, or predictive pattern.
- **vs [[Brain/Concepts/The Mirror]]:** the Mirror shows the self *as* image. Flattening is when the image is **all the system will recognize.**

## The Lucius anchor `[W]`
Lucius is the **glamour of flattening** — the persona that *wins* by becoming **legible to the spectacle.** He is **not simply fake; he is the self optimized for the machine.** (This is why the "is Lucius real or a bit?" question is the wrong one — flattening is precisely the state where that distinction stops mattering to the system.)
