# The Audience

Type: Concept / Symbol

---

## Description

The Audience is the consuming collective that the Circus requires and feeds on. In the Jagwar Twin project, the Audience occupies a paradoxical position: they are simultaneously the *co-creators* (Roy's word for his listeners, rejecting the term "fan") and the *marks* (the ones the Circus performs for and processes). The same people. The distinction is whether they come as subjects doing their own work, or as objects consuming someone else's spectacle.

The Circus collapses the co-creator into the audience-member. The instruction *"take your seat, scream and shout"* is the moment that collapse is performed on the listener in real time.

- **Elias Canetti, *Crowds and Power*** — the crowd as its own entity; the physics of collective emotion

## The Co-Creator vs. The Spectator

This is the central tension. Roy's stated position is that listeners are *co-creators of the world* — the quality of consciousness the listener brings into the work affects the shared reality. This is an esoteric principle (consciousness as generative, not merely receptive) and also a responsibility-transfer: *the world you're creating inside your own head is the world we all have to live in.*

The Circus collapses this into pure spectatorship: *take your seat, scream and shout.* The seated, screaming audience member is the co-creator who has been reduced to a consumer. The project's arc may be about recovering the co-creator position from the spectator position — not escaping the Circus, but refusing to be reduced to audience within it.
