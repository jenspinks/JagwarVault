# The Two Birds — Orbit and Return (crow & dove)

**Roy's own character-symbol split — and the one universal text that already holds both birds on exactly the vault's core axis.** In his 2026-07-01 fundamentals Roy gave [[Sir Lucius]] the **crow (Corvus)** and [[Roy]] the **dove (Cygnus)** ([[Sources/Quotes/Roy - Character fundamentals for the Field Guide (relayed) 2026-07-01|Roy's stated fundamentals, via Jen]]). The assignment is `[G via relay]`; everything below is the reading it licenses. **Frame (non-negotiable):** the birds are **inherited substrate** — Genesis, Greek catasterism, the universal dove-tradition — that Roy *participates in*, not a cipher he authored. Read as **mythic emergence, not intentional encoding** (CLAUDE.md "inherited substrate"; [[The Pattern]]'s anti-totalization guard).

## Genesis 8 — the two birds natively encode Orbit vs Return `[W]` (the spine)
There is one public, universal text that holds **both** birds, split on **exactly** the vault's core axis, inside the **flood-frame the debut album is named for.** After the ark grounds, Noah sends out two birds:
- **The raven** *"went forth to and fro until the waters were dried up"* — and **never returns.** The **crow / raven = the Orbit bird:** sustained motion, no homecoming. This is [[The Protector]] §Orbit vs Return in a single image — the velocity that outruns the wound and *forecloses landing.*
- **The dove** returns, returns again bearing the **olive leaf**, and on her third flight does **not** return **because the world is dry and she has arrived.** The **dove = the Return bird:** even her final non-return is *homecoming, not circling* — the exact inverse of the raven's endless not-landing.

So **Orbit vs Return is natively encoded in the Ark story**, and Roy assigned the Ark's two birds to his two characters along precisely that line — on *[[Subject to Flooding (album)|Subject to Flooding]]*'s own frame. The board makes the animal ingress materially project-native: its human-shaped **Noah's Ark** is labelled *"main door — where animals come in."* Tier: that inscription and the character assignment are grounded (`[G]` / `[G via relay]`); the **Genesis mapping** is `[W]` — now five convergent anchors (Roy's assignment · Genesis 8 · the album's flood-frame · the board's animal-admitting body-Ark · the vault's existing Ark/vessel work). The board does not say the crow and dove enter through that drawn door; the image strengthens the vessel-and-bestiary field without proving a designed equation.

## The crow's second myth — Corvus, intelligence that thirsts `[~]`
Roy named the *constellation* Corvus, which carries its own precise fable ([[Sir Lucius]] §The crow — Corvus): Apollo's crow, sent with a cup for water, **dawdles and lies**, and is fixed in the sky beside **Crater the cup** — forever within sight of the water, never able to drink. **Intelligence condemned to unquenchable thirst by its own deceptive framing of the truth** — *"the shell is always hungry"* rendered as a Greek constellation. Counter-image already in the vault: the HoM artifact **Grey in A#** is a **dove on a chalice** ([[Sources/Artifacts/HoM Artifacts - Grey in A# + Stardust (validator batch) 2026-06-26]]) — the dove *attains* the very cup the crow is denied.

## Apollo's two birds — one god, split light and dark `[~]`
The **swan and the crow are both Apollo's.** The crow was **white** until Apollo scorched it black for its report (truth told without love gets you burned; the [[The Light-Bearer|light-bearer]] blackened); the swan sings truest at its death → **swan-song → [[SOL]]**, the wordless closer. Two birds of one god, one light and one dark — a clean twin-emblem for the Roy/Lucius pair, folding back into [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]] §The lateral twins (the sun-bound Mercury/Venus pair).

## Cygnus — the dove that carries the swan's name `[~]`
Roy's own detail: the dove's name is **Cygnus**, the *swan* constellation. **Hold the tension, don't resolve it** (per the fundamentals note): a dove *named* Swan is identity-on-identity — ugly-duckling logic, the buried [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy|child]] who was the swan all along, the name as a promise not yet kept. Cygnus is the **Northern Cross** (love-sacrifice) and flies the Milky Way beside **Lyra the lyre** — the singer set down beside his instrument. And doves are **Venus's** birds, which grounds Roy's **sword** on the heart-pole (*Venus Victrix*, armed love; [[Roy]] §the dove is Venus's bird).
