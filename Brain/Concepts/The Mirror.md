# The Mirror

Type: Concept / **Device (parent mechanism)** — the structure by which **self becomes visible as other.**

> **Created 2026-06-29**, promoted from the pageless registry. The Mirror is the **device**; its ethics, failure-state, operation, and grammar each have their own page. This page is the **hub**, deliberately *thin* so it does not duplicate them.

## Definition `[W]`
The Mirror is the catalogue's central **self-relation device**: the self becomes visible only by appearing as an **image** — double, audience, mask, reflection, recording, screen, eye, or "you." The project is *literally* named a **Hall of Mirrors** `[G]`, and the mirror/eye/*I↔you* grammar saturates the work (Empty Mirror tattoo; *Rorrim Ehtni Lived* = "lived in the mirror" reversed; *"I just want to be a mirror"* — [[Roy on Mirrors & Collective Consciousness]]; the pronoun arithmetic, [[Brain/Concepts/Pronoun Audit - I-you machine (33 vs Lucius Lullaby)|I 492 / you 478]]).

A mirror can **awaken, distort, trap, or transmit.** It is **not automatically false** — it becomes false only when it **claims sourcehood.**

> **The load-bearing line:** *A mirror is faithful while it knows it is a mirror. It becomes [[Sir Lucius]] when it forgets it is not the source.*

## The Lucius anchor `[W]`
Lucius is **not merely a "bad reflection."** He is a reflection that has **gained autonomy**: the image-system becomes agentic and starts speaking as if it *were* the source. That is where the mirror stops reflecting and becomes a **parasite** — it no longer returns the viewer to anything; it captures the viewer inside the image. (The device's worst outcome; the redemption is re-subordination, not smashing the glass.)
