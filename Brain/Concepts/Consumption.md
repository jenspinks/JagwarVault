# Consumption

Type: Concept / Symbol

---

## Description

Consumption is the mechanism by which the Circus processes its material — performers, audiences, and meaning are taken in, metabolized for fuel, and expelled. In [[Brain/Songs/Welcome to the Circus]], Lucius names this explicitly: *"I'll chew you up, spit you out."* The Circus is not neutral architecture; it is a digestive system.

Consumption in this project runs in multiple directions: the industry consumes the artist, the Circus consumes the audience, the algorithm consumes attention, the mask consumes the face. The figure being consumed varies; the mechanism is consistent.

## Traditional / Biblical Resonance

- **Jonah and the whale (Jonah 1-2)** — consumed and returned, *changed*, after three days. The prototype of consumption-as-transformation. The Circus consumes and *spits out* — same verbs, opposite outcome. No three days of darkness. No transformation. Just disposal.
- **The Eucharist inverted** — *"This is my body, broken for you"* (Luke 22:19) — the Christ-consumption that is voluntary and salvific. *"I'll chew you up, spit you out"* — the anti-Eucharist, predatory consumption, ejection without nourishment.
- **Sheol** — the biblical underworld described as a mouth (*"Sheol enlarges its appetite and opens its mouth beyond measure"* — Isaiah 5:14); the underworld as the consuming void
- **1 Peter 5:8** — *"the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour"* — predatory consumption as the adversary's mode
- **Daniel 7 / Revelation 13** — the beasts that consume kingdoms; empire as consumption system
- **Moloch** — the god to whom children were sacrificed (2 Kings 23:10; Jeremiah 7:31); consumption as false-god worship. Allen Ginsberg's *Howl* revives this image for industrial capitalism.
- **Capital / commodity** — Marx's analysis of labor as consumed and spat out as surplus value; the factory worker chewed up and expelled with minimal return

## The Cake Before the Circus

The 2022 *I Like to Party* music video — Roy force-fed cake by a jeweled hand from off-screen, escalating to cake smashed across his face and torso — is the clearest pre-naming visual of the consumption mechanism. Four years before WTTC names *"I'll chew you up, spit you out,"* the visual catalogue shows Roy as the thing being eaten. The cake is the algorithm-friendly content. The jeweled hand is the industry. Roy is what gets stuffed in and taken out.

## The Jonah Distinction

The Jonah story is the key comparative:

| Jonah's consumption | WTTC consumption |
|---|---|
| The whale swallows him whole | *I'll chew you up* — active mastication |
| Three days in darkness | No pause; immediate ejection |
| Prayer in the belly | No prayer noted |
| Returned to the surface | Spit out |
| Returns transformed | Returns as waste product |
| Obeys his mission after | Discarded; show over |

**Jonah is consumed and transformed. The Circus consumes and discards.** This is the difference between the initiation-in-darkness (the descent-and-return structure) and the industrial digestion (the consumption-and-disposal structure). Both involve going in. Only one involves coming back changed.
