# Pronoun Audit — the I↔you machine across *33* and *Lucius Lullaby*

Tests the hypothesis that ***33* runs on "you" and *Lucius Lullaby* runs on "I."** Per Jen's instruction (2026-06-28): **test, don't assume** — and score *function*, not only frequency. This page holds the **grounded frequency floor `[G]`** + the **function-scoring layer `[W]` (the next pass)**.

## ★ The grounded count `[G]` — hypothesis CONFIRMED at the frequency level
Whole-word, case-insensitive cluster counts over the lyric bodies (frontmatter + `[section]` labels + parenthetical stage-directions stripped). **I-cluster** = i/i'm/i've/i'll/i'd/my/me/mine/myself · **you-cluster** = you/you're/you've/you'll/you'd/your/yours/yourself. Reproducible (`/tmp/pronoun.py`; re-derivable from [[Sources/Lyrics]]).

| Album | Words | I-cluster | you-cluster | **I : you** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **33 (2022)** | 4,538 | 166 (3.7%) | 287 (6.3%) | **0.58 — "you"-dominant** |
| **Lucius Lullaby (2026)** | 3,344 | 134 (4.0%) | 82 (2.5%) | **1.63 — "I"-dominant** |

**The shift is real and large:** the I:you ratio **~triples** (0.58 → 1.63); the **you-density halves** (6.3% → 2.5%) while I-density barely rises (3.7% → 4.0%). So the move from *33* to *Lucius Lullaby* is driven less by the "I" *hardening* than by **the "you" collapsing** — the second person (relation, address, the other) drains out of the later record. *(Excluding the wordless SOL barely moves it: LL = I:you 1.61. Robust.)*

### Per-song (the texture under the average)
- ***33*'s "you"-engine** (other-directed): **Down to You** (you 80, I 1 — almost pure address-to-the-other), **Imagine A World** (you 35, I 0 — collective "you"), **It's Your Time** (you 45 / I 34 — balanced exhortation), **Soul Is A Star** (you 39 / I 19), **Online** (you 22 / I 12). The album's gravity is the second person.
- ***33*'s I-spikes:** **Another Way II Heaven** (I:you 7.0) and **Happy Face** (2.33) — the exceptions that keep it from being purely relational.
- ***Lucius Lullaby*'s "I"-engine** (self-assertion/performance): **#1 Champion** (I:you 5.5), **Bananas** (4.5), **Playing to the Gods** (I 4 / you 0 — the survival-clutch *"if I make it out alive"*), **Bounce** (3.0).
- ***Lucius Lullaby*'s residual "you"** = audience/refusal, not the beloved: **Not Your Homie** (you 29 / I 25 — the *"you don't know me"* boundary), **City of Angels** (you 10 / I 9).
- **Pronoun-evacuated** (LL): **Moths to the Flame** (0 I / 0 you — pure image), **Great Time to Be Human** (I 0 / you 2 — collective/ironic), **The Circle (Lucius Version)** (1 / 1 — mantra).

## The function layer `[W]` — the next pass (Jen's rubric)
Frequency proves the *quantity* shift; the **finding** is the *function* shift, which the per-song spread already gestures at. Score each "I" and "you" by **grammatical/rhetorical role**, not just count:

| Album | What "I/you" mostly *does* |
|---|---|
| **33** | **you as mirror, relation, awakening, contagion, plea** — the second person is the *other* being called toward (Down to You, Imagine A World, It's Your Time). |
| **Lucius Lullaby** | **I/you as performance, possession, recruitment, refusal, crowd-control** — the first person asserts/brands (#1 Champion, Bananas), the residual "you" is *audience/victim* (Not Your Homie's refusal), not the beloved. |
| **SOL** | **collapse of pronoun into voice / breath / source** — the album closer is **wordless** ([[Brain/Songs/Lucius Lullaby (2026)/SOL|SOL]] §wordlessness `[G]`), so person dissolves entirely: no I, no you, only breath. The end-state of the machine is the *cancellation* of the pronoun axis. *(Note: the 222-word count for "SOL" above is the lyric-file's EP2/Move-To-You ancestry text, NOT the wordless album cut — exclude it from the album-track reading; the wordlessness IS the datum.)*

**The reading this licenses `[W]`:** the catalogue's [[Brain/Concepts/Source vs Reflection|I↔you machine]] doesn't just tilt toward "I" — it **loses its second person.** *33* faces an other (witness/relation); *Lucius Lullaby* turns the address into spectacle (the "you" becomes the crowd, then is refused), and the self inflates to fill the space the other vacated — the **reflection claiming sourcehood** at the level of grammar ([[Brain/Concepts/Source vs Reflection]] §the third step / usurpation). SOL then voids the whole axis into breath. **Don't overclaim "the I hardens"** — the data says the *you drains*; that's the sharper, truer form.

## Caveats
- The count is **mechanical**: it can't tell "you" = beloved vs crowd vs God, or "I" = confession vs brand. That disambiguation **is** the function pass — do it by reading, per song, scoring role.
- Album membership = the 10/12 studio tracks (Brain/Songs folders); features counted as part of the track. SOL handled specially (wordless).
- This is two albums; *Subject to Flooding* (2018) is the untested third point — adding it would show whether the second-person was always there to lose.
