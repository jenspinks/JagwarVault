## ⚠ Stream discipline (non-negotiable)
- **Hermetic ≠ Gnostic.** Hermeticism is **world-AFFIRMING correspondence** (the cosmos is a single coherent law, *legible* and *climbable*); [[Brain/Concepts/Gnosticism - The World as Trap|Gnosticism]] is **world-REJECTING escape** (the cosmos is a false copy to flee). Same imagery (descent, ascent, the spark) can carry opposite teloi — label which.
- **Hermetic ≠ Lurianic.** The Hermetic Qabalah (Golden-Dawn, tarot, Qliphoth-as-Tree-of-Death) is **NOT** the Jewish-Lurianic Kabbalah (shevirah/tikkun/kelipot) — the [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]] hub's core guard. The vault's tarot/morning-star/Qliphah scaffold is **largely Hermetic**; the shevirah doctrine is **Jewish**. Always name the stream.

## The Kybalion's 7 Principles (the operating rules, `[G]` board-transcribed)
*"The All is Mind… Mind woven into matter."* The seven, lightly mapped (mappings `[W]`):
1. **Mentalism** — *"The All is Mind"*; reality is mental → the upload/consciousness cosmology ([[Brain/Concepts/The Upload]]).
2. **Correspondence** — *"as above, so below"* → **[[Brain/Concepts/The Pattern]]** (its full worked home) + the mirror ([[Brain/Concepts/Source vs Reflection]] / [[Brain/Concepts/The Mirror]]).
3. **Vibration** — nothing rests; everything moves → frequency/sound ([[SOL]], the Bounce «Beth» note).
4. **Polarity** — *"everything is dual"* → [[Duality]]; the Roy/Lucius split; the *"One become Two"* cosmogony.
5. **Rhythm** — the pendulum swing → [[Brain/Concepts/The Circle|Circle vs Loop]] (recurrence).
6. **Cause & Effect** — every cause has its effect → karma/the seed-doctrine.
7. **Gender** — masculine/feminine in everything → the Left/Right-hemisphere board cell (Order/reason/masc + vs Chaos/creativity/fem −); the divine-feminine thread ([[Mary]]).

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
<!-- anti-totalization defense (CLAUDE.md ANTI-PATTERNS #1/#2) -->
- **Board-transcription proves *engagement*, not *architecture*.** That Roy wrote the 7 Principles onto his "I'm 33" board is `[G]` evidence he *read and collected* the Kybalion — a reading-list, not a proven compositional grammar for the songs. The leap from "he transcribed it" to "the catalogue operates on it" is `[W]` at most; a personal study-board is curation, and the strongest thing that lifts it toward *operative* is the separate fact that board "Tracks" **surface as HoM codes** — so keep board-Hermeticism `[G]` but song-level Hermetic structure `[W]`/`[~]`. `[W]`
- **Seven near-universal principles fit *any* catalogue.** Duality, rhythm, cause/effect, vibration, mentalism are so broad they appear in nearly every wisdom tradition; mapping songs onto them risks unfalsifiable pattern-matching — the very "master key" overfit the page warns against, turned on the hub itself. A reading is only worth its `[W]` if it would *fail* on some songs; note where the principles **don't** illuminate, not only where they do. `[~]`/`[?]`
- **Roy's own disclaimer cuts against systematizing.** *"I would map a crow onto Mercury but that's just personal. I don't recommend you do it"* `[G]` is the author explicitly disowning the correspondence-method as idiosyncratic — so building a Hermetic *frame* over the catalogue is, in part, doing the thing he disrecommends. Honor it as a hard ceiling on intent-claims, not just a footnote. `[P]`
- **Falsifier:** if the song-level mappings can't be told apart from chance (any 7 broad principles fit anything), the Hermetic frame is *decorative*, not operative — and only the board-Tracks-as-HoM-artifacts evidence rescues it.
