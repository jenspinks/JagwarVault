# The Dweller on the Threshold

> **Full reading:** [[Essays/Concepts/The Dweller on the Threshold]]

## Definition
[W] Guardian at the inner-world boundary, made of the practitioner's own unintegrated material. Self-generated, accumulated; grows with what is disowned. Cannot be killed (it is the self); only recognized, named, reintegrated.

## Lineage (none)
Bulwer-Lytton *Zanoni* (1842) → Blavatsky/Steiner (Lesser vs Greater Dweller) → Jung's Shadow (same function, no term).

## Application [W]
[[Sir Lucius]] = Dweller for [[Roy]]: self-generated ("I made you"); grows with disowned material; mirror-anchored (Rorrim Ehtni 2008); not defeatable by force (STOP SIR LUCIUS rebellion fails, Roy captured); threshold = [[Lucius Lullaby (album)]]. [P] Not "Lucius=villain" — the form Roy's unintegrated material took: genuinely his, genuinely accumulated, genuinely the obstacle, all at once.

## Distinctions [W]
Not Shadow-alone (Dweller = Shadow given face/name/voice). Not external demon (the demon-framing is itself the Dweller's defense). Not moral failure (adaptation to survive). Not necessarily destroyed — can become guide once recognized (Steiner).
