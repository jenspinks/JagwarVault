# Inherited Substrate

Type: Concept / **Methodological law** — the vault's provenance discipline.

> **Created 2026-06-29**, promoted from the pageless registry. This is **not a theme page** — it is the **rule that governs tiering across the whole vault.** It answers: *where did this material come from, and how strongly can we claim the connection?*

## The law
**Inherited Substrate** is material Roy / Jagwar Twin **receives, quotes, interpolates, transforms, or unconsciously shares** with prior traditions — credited interpolation, named influence, public-board source text, family transmission, cultural inheritance, and broad shared-symbol reservoirs. The catalogue is **built from** prior songs, nursery rhymes, biblical language, occult systems, family memory, older-artist lineages, and public-domain myth.

> **The discipline (the load-bearing line):** **Not all resemblance is reference. A claim rises only when the substrate is anchored.** **Inherited Substrate licenses comparison without laundering it into proof.** ([[CLAUDE]] guard: *"not every symbol is JT-authored… prefer participating in an older form over the artist deliberately encoded this, whenever a symbol predates the work."*)

So the core split: **inherited substrate** (received & reworked from the wider culture) vs **internal architecture** (built *inside* the JT mythos — Lucius, the loop/Circle, the Algorithm, mirror recursion). Naming which is which is what keeps every symbolic reading honest.

## The repeatable test
Before promoting any cross-reference, ask: **Did Roy *receive* this, *cite* this, *transform* this — or are we only recognizing a shared symbolic ancestor?** Credit/statement → `[G]`. Breadcrumbs + context → `[W]`. Same reservoir, no transmission → `[~]`. Pretty but unanchored → `[?]`. Failed the primary source → ✗ ruled out. **This is the page that stops tier-inflation.**

## The grounded floor — the relay/curation practice `[G]`
The *Subject to Flooding* credit pass established **composer/credit verification as authoritative** and revealed a deliberate **relay/curation practice**: the catalogue *builds by relaying credited cultural transmissions* ("the song handed down"). Multiple documented external credits on one album (Mercer, Vaughan, Chaplin) make this a *practice*, not a coincidence — the grounded heart of this concept.

## External neighbor `[~]` (scholarly, not an influence claim)
**Kristeva's intertextuality** — texts mean *through* other texts and codes, not as sealed objects — is the cleanest scholarly neighbor: it explains *why* echoes are natural and pervasive. But the vault needs something **stricter**: Kristeva says echoes are expected; **Inherited Substrate says how to *tier* them.** Held `[~]` — a frame for *us*, **not** a claim that Roy sources Kristeva.
