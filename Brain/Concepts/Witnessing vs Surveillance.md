# Witnessing vs Surveillance

Type: Concept / Doctrine

Two modes of being-seen — the distinction the [[Machine vs Human]] thread turns on. Both involve a gaze fixed on the self; they are opposites in *function*.

## The distinction

| | Witnessing (true seeing) | Surveillance (counterfeit seeing) |
|---|---|---|
| Mode | relational presence | extraction |
| Action | restores · recognizes · dignifies · integrates | predicts · categorizes · monetizes · stabilizes behavior |
| Effect on subject | **transformed** by being seen | **shaped**, not known |
| Register | El Roi / Tiferet-recognition | the Algorithm / Yesod severed from heart |

Functional test: **surveillance gathers data; witnessing transforms the witnessed.** *The algorithm does not know you — it knows how to shape you.* This is why surveillance feels spiritually wrong in this cosmology: it is a forgery of the soul's deepest need (to be seen and loved), sold back as being-watched-and-ranked.

## El Roi anchor

[W] **El Roi** — Genesis 16:13, "the God who sees me." Roy signs himself **"El Roy."** Hebrew **אל ראי** is inked on [[Sir Lucius]]'s neck/throat (confirmed — Jen transcript note + video still) → Da'at gate (see [[Not Your Homie]]).
