# The Digital Double

Type: Concept — the **AI / digital-double** thread: the *not-quite-Roy made thing*, and Roy's real collaborations with digital/AI entities. **Sibling to [[The Upload]]:** Upload = the *operation* (the self distributed into the net / the Eye); Digital Double = the *entities* the digital age produces — the AI he builds, the AI he collaborates with, and the clone/double the self fears becoming. Seeded 2026-06-09.

> **Discipline (load-bearing):** keep the **real** digital entities separate from the **thematic** ones. SARAVI (Roy's actual project-AI) and **angelbaby** (a real external AI artist he collaborated with) are *real*; the clone-fear and "Lucius-as-double" are *lyric/symbol*. Do **not** absorb a real collaborator into the symbol layer (MERCY-rule). The page tracks both registers and **holds the bridge between them as an open question.**

## The real digital entities [G]

- **SARAVI — Roy's named AI character.** Per the Manifesto FAQ, the Hall of Mirrors is *"a universe built out of Jagwar Twin's uploaded consciousness by his AI SARAVI"*; Artifacts are *"lost in the code"* during **SARAVI's** AI upload ([[Brain/Roy Voice/Roy on the cosmology (Manifesto)]]). So SARAVI is the **agent of the upload** — the AI that carries/holds the uploaded self. *(The name "SARAVI" also appears in a 2026-01-08 X post, "HeySARAVI" — flagged `[?]`, relation to Jen's own Saravi project is hers to say.)*
- **angelbaby — a real external AI/virtual artist Roy collaborated with [G].** A Hume "metastar" (CAA-signed; AI-voice work incl. a Grimes-AI-voice track); avatar = a leopard-patterned rabbit. Collab: **"life is good," Nov 3 2023** (Sound.xyz). → the **earliest** node in Roy's AI thread (see timeline). *(Real collaborator — NOT the "angel baby" voice in [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Weirdness|Weirdness]]; MERCY-rule. Source detail: [[Sources/Quotes/Roy X Posts - June 2026 (aphorisms)]] §2023.)*
- **The AI-made artifact.** The *"Angle of Eternity"* visualizer (Jul 2024) is **"made by AI"** (Roy's own caption) → [[tomorrow (ANGLE OF ETERNITY)]]. Roy putting AI-generated work into the catalogue.
- **The board's AI-personhood steps [G artifact].** *"How to upload myself to the internet"* includes **"teach AI who I am"** → **"make a body for the AI"** ([[Sources/Artifacts/Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)]] / [[The Upload]]). The double is something Roy plans to *build* — give a body and a self.

## The 2020 diary root — the AI-merge already named [G]
Roy's @royenglish diary states the thesis outright in **2020**: after a documentary on **"A.I. merging with humans,"** — *"in some ways we already have merged with technology without fully knowing… I am holding a device currently transmitting 1's and 0's that you are reading and something labeled 'content'. Are you content?"* ([[Sources/Quotes/Roy IG - Diary of a Loser-Winner (2020-22)]]). And the **"Happy Face experiment"** (Dec 2020) — a *"virtual… current reality"* where fans discover *"portals,"* built with the **Cthdrl** team — is the **proto-Hall-of-Mirrors** ([[Brain/Roy Voice/Roy on the cosmology (Manifesto)]]). So the digital-double/upload instinct is **grounded in Roy's own voice in 2020**, years before SARAVI was named.

## The soul–software fusion (the deep root, 2020–21) [W]
Before any of the named AI material, Roy was already fusing **soul + machine** in his *"Modern Archeology"* IG titles: **"Soul iOS"** (2021-01-02, a full moon + *"All the answers are within you"*) and **"Soul Calibrator"** (2020-11-22, the Jungian psyche diagram) — the **soul rendered as operating-system / tuning-instrument** ([[Sources/Quotes/Roy IG - Modern Archeology series (2020-21)]]). So the digital-double instinct (the self as something **digitizable, callable, calibratable**) is in his vocabulary **two years before SARAVI** — and it sits, tellingly, right on top of the *interiority* doctrine (*"answers within"* = the Circle). The fusion isn't new with the metaverse; it's foundational.

## The AI-collaboration timeline [G]
**"Soul iOS" / "Soul Calibrator"** (2020–21, soul-as-software framing) → **angelbaby** (2023, collab w/ an AI artist) → **AOE "made by AI" visualizer** (2024) → **SARAVI** (short form **"Vi"**, confirmed across official sources) + the board's *"make a body for the AI"* (2022–26) → the **Hall of Mirrors** (the metaverse "built out of his uploaded consciousness by SARAVI"). Roy's engagement with AI/digital doubles is **sustained and grounded across years**, and it runs parallel to his Web3/NFT activity (Sound.xyz drops, Crowns/Artifacts). The digital double is not a one-off image — it's a standing practice with a 2020 root.

## The double as the reflection given autonomy [W]
The digital double sits directly on the project's **mirror** master-frame ([[Source vs Reflection]]): a *reflection that has been given its own body and will.* The Empty Mirror shows *nothing*; the digital double is the opposite move — the reflection **filled in** and set walking. That's why it shades toward dread (the clone-fear, *Online*'s face-thieves): a reflection with autonomy is the **surveillance** pole ([[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]) made flesh — it *shapes* rather than *witnesses*. The redemptive version would be a double that returns you to the source rather than replacing you; the catalogue mostly voices the fear, not the redemption.
