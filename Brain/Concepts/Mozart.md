# Mozart

Type: Concept / **figure-as-lens** — a named influence Roy actually reaches for, and, through *Amadeus*, the catalogue's cleanest outside mirror of the Roy/Lucius split.

> **Minted 2026-07-13** from the "divided self" thread (see [[The Divided Self]]). ## Definition
Mozart works two jobs at once in the catalogue. **(1) A named influence** Roy reaches for by name, the geometer-composer and the "greatest song" board-joke. **(2) A lens:** through *Amadeus*, Mozart-and-Salieri is the culture's cleanest dramatization of the **un-reconciled** divided self, the favored gift and the craftsman who cannot forgive it, which lands with uncanny precision on the vault's own Roy/Lucius map.

> **The load-bearing reading `[~]`:** *Salieri is what [[Sir Lucius]] becomes if the twin is never reconciled, the craftsman who would rather destroy the gift than serve it.*

## The *Amadeus* lens — the un-reconciled twin `[~]`
- *Amadeus* (Shaffer's play, Forman's film) reads as a **Cain/Abel** story — Gregory Robbins, *"Mozart & Salieri, Cain & Abel: A Cinematic Transformation of Genesis 4,"* Journal of Religion & Film 1.1 (1997): **Mozart = Abel** (the gift *regarded*), **Salieri = Cain** (the offering *refused* → envy → destruction).
- So *Amadeus* is the **cultural exemplar of the Lucius-as-Cain / un-reconciled prognosis** the vault already tracks (see [[Jacob & Esau]] §"the other twin-pair" and the [[The Divided Self|divided-self array]]).
- The craft-vs-gift engine: Salieri the devout **laborer** (Hod / Mercury / the analytic) against Mozart the effortless **gift** (Netzach / Venus / the heart) — the very split the vault relays as Lucius / Roy.

## The route onto the Tree `[~]`
- Cain/Abel have a documented Lurianic placement: the **two shoulders** of Adam — Cain on the **left** (Gevurah / severity), Abel on the **right** (Chesed / mercy) — *Sha'ar HaGilgulim* (Gate of Reincarnations) ch. 31. **Netzach/Hod is the lower octave of those same two pillars.**
- Two things fall out. **(1)** Salieri = Cain = **left / Severity (Gevurah → Hod)** is exactly where the vault independently seats Lucius (Hod cross-cut Geburah — the 2026 lunar re-mapping; [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]], [[The Light-Bearer]]). Convergence, not analogy. **(2)** Mozart = Abel = **right / Mercy (Chesed → Netzach)** matches Roy's Netzach/Venus heart-seat — but Roy also stands **alone on the Middle Pillar**, and *that asymmetry is the reading*: **the shoulders with no middle is the un-reconciled ending (murder); the middle held (Tiferet) is the embrace.** The full geometry lives in the [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]] Deep End and the array.
