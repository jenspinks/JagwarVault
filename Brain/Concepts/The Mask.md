# The Mask

Type: Concept / Symbol

---

## Description

The Mask is a recurring structural element across the Jagwar Twin catalogue — the face that mediates between interior self and exterior world, the persona (Greek: *prosopon*, theatre mask; Latin: *persona*, "through-sound") worn for survival, for performance, or for concealment. The mask can be protective, necessary, and ultimately imprisoning when worn so long it becomes the face.

The project traces a progression: **Happy Face** (the minimum-viable social mask — say you're good, say you're fine), → **the jester face** (the Circus-optimized mask, fully painted, "in style," rewarded with applause), → **the face paint removed** (the potential arc toward Roy English visible beneath). The mask is not simply a lie — it begins as protection, becomes performance, and finally threatens to replace the original.

## Traditional / Esoteric Resonance

- **Jung's persona** — the social mask, the face we wear in the world; useful but dangerous when confused with the Self
- **Greek theatre** — *prosopon*, the actor's mask through which character speaks; *persona* literally means "through-sound"
- **Ash Wednesday** — face as liturgical surface (ash marks mortality); inverted by face paint (marks performance)
- **Tarot — The Magician (I)** — the performer's face; the conscious will to control appearance
- **War paint / ceremonial paint** — the face transformed for sacred or martial occasion; the transformation is the point
- **The Empty Throne / Invisible King** — mask as presence in absence; the face that persists when the person beneath it is unclear
