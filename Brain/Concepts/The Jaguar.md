# The Jaguar

Type: Concept / Symbol — the project's emblem of **seeing.** Seeded 2026-06-07 from [[Jagwar Twin]].

## What it is [G — grounded etymology · W as frame]
The name's emblem: **the jaguar = "the creature who looks into the souls of other people and then, in turn, can look back into itself"** — *"humans are mirrors for each other"* → **a mirror of consciousness** (Roy's voice, **3 public interviews**: [[Sources/Transcripts/Ones-To-Watch-2018-Debut-QA]] 2018; [[Sources/Transcripts/Highwire-2021-Power-of-Sound]] 2021; [[Sources/Transcripts/Mic-Magazine-UK-Myth-of-Music]] 2024 — the last adds *"pulls people out of the darkness… into the light"*). So "the Jaguar" names the **mirror / reflexive seeing** — looks into others *and* back into the self — which is exactly the [[Source vs Reflection]] master-frame (a reflection that returns you to the source rather than trapping you in the image). [[Jagwar Twin]] is canon's *"meta-system / container… a stage for voices,"* the title read as *"Jagwar (the mirror/seer) + Twin (the paired self) — observer and observed."* The Jaguar is the **field/frame**, not a mask in the story. *(The older "sees into all living beings / travels between worlds / inter-world traveler" gloss is **not** in these quotes — that attribute's sourcing stays `[?]`.)*
- **Both directions, no fixed start [W] (dossier 07):** across tellings the *sequence* flips — 2018 (look into others → back into self); a 2019 telling reverses it (self first → others). **Not a contradiction** — the mirror-loop has **no fixed starting point**; the reciprocity *is* the doctrine. (The Big Loud Rock bio adds a **third** public gloss — *"the speed of the jaguar… moving through technological and cultural dynamics"* — so treat no single telling as definitive.)
- **Reinforced in Roy's 2026 X posts** ([[Sources/Quotes/Roy X Posts - June 2026 (aphorisms)]]): the mirror doctrine restated — *"Relationships are fundamentally about two people reflecting to each other what they need to know to become more of who they are"* (01-12) + *"To Love is to meet the hidden part of yourself in someone else"* (02-15). The jaguar's *"humans are mirrors for each other"* is a standing, repeated Roy idea, not a one-off etymology.
- **The field sings, not only sees [W].** The etymology centers *seeing*, but the primordial Jaguar's defining act in [[Brain/Songs/33 (2022)/The Circle|The Circle]] is **teaching the song** (*"she made other Jagwars, then taught them to sing"*). So the Jaguar-origin is **voice as much as vision** — a single capacity with two faces: *sees-into-beings* **and** *sounds-the-world*. The *"song hidden inside the wings"* is that field's lost voice; the Manifesto FAQ's *"there's only one song"* ([[The Circle]] §connective-spine) is, in this register, **the Jaguar's song.** Guard against reading the seeing-field as *surveillance only* — it is also the original singer.

## NOT a fourth peer-character [W→G — the anti-drift guard]
There is **no peer "Jaguar" figure** standing alongside [[Roy]] / [[Sir Lucius]] / [[Mary]]. The four character pages are **Jagwar Twin (the container)**, Roy, Sir Lucius, Mary. So do **not** re-read the Jaguar as a claimant in a four-figure **tetramorph** (Mary=ox / Lucius=lion / Roy=man / Jaguar=eagle) — that 1:1 is **overfit**; it belongs only to the Ezekiel paratext ([[Brain/Songs/Singles & EPs/The Watchers]]), not to the JT figures. This is the line most likely to drift; it is written down here on purpose.

## OPEN `[?]` — its order vs Mary (do NOT lock)
What stays unsettled is the Jaguar's relation to [[Mary]] (the feminine source-figure / the seer who witnesses, *El Ro'i*):
- **Reading A:** the Jaguar is a **genderless capacity** — the *seeing-field* — **distinct in order** from Mary (a figure who sees *rightly inside* it); within that field the two modes of seeing are **Lucius = surveillance** vs **Mary = witnessing** ([[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]).
- **Reading B:** Roy **deliberately collapses** them — the seeing (Jaguar) and the seer (Mary/Shekinah) as **one function, two faces.** Myth/scripture are *tools, not fixed truth* here, and we have **no grounding that he keeps them apart** (CLAUDE.md §figure-distinctness; [[Jagwar Twin]] open note).

**Default to neither.** The genderless-capacity-distinct-from-Mary framing is **Reading A**, not settled fact — record it as such.

### ⚠ RETRACTED: "cat-skull = death-mask." The jaguar is a *multiple*, not a skull. `[?]`
The Fable-pass gloss *"the BMRC logo = Ra's cat rendered as a skull / death-mask"* is **retracted (Jen, 2026-07-08)** — poetic, ungrounded, and it **flattened a multiple.** Only the *performer's face-paint* (white face, blacked-out eye-sockets) is skull-*like*; the drawn jaguar is **not** a cat-skull. Correct frame: **the jaguar is a gendered / polarized MULTIPLE** — same emblem, opposed operations along **life↔death, feminine↔masculine, ankh↔skull-like.** Crucially there is a **white, feminine jaguar bearing an ANKH (= life)** that **inverts** any death reading. Leave that figure **UNPLACED `[?]`** — candidates (do not lock): an Egyptian feminine cat-goddess (**Bastet / Sekhmet** — cf. the Statue-of-Roy in **Mut**'s temple with **Hathor**'s ears, [[Roy]]); the serene **whole-self icon** (HoM room `3252cfd9`); or the **divine-feminine [[SARAVI]] / Wise One / [[Mary]]**. This is the **Reading-B axis** of the open question below: if the ankh-jaguar is the maker-feminine, the emblem itself spans the seer/source split. Needs a direct look at the specific image.

### The serpent is double `[W]/[~]`
Against the solar cat, the serpent runs **two ways at once**: **Apophis / the Genesis-3 tempter** (chaos, the fruit-tempter — [[Sir Lucius]]'s register) **and** the board's own **NChSh / Nachash = 358 = Mashiach/Messiah**, the **ascending** serpent-path up the Tree ([[The Im 33 Board - Decoded (Roy's upload cosmology)]] §Hopscotch). The transformation-hinge that turns one into the other is **Numbers 21:9 / John 3:14** — the **bronze serpent** lifted up (the poison made the cure) — with **Matthew 10:16** (*"wise as serpents, harmless as doves"*) as the ethic. So "serpent" is not simply the enemy of the cat; it is the **thing that must be transformed, not only slain.** `[~]` (scriptural correspondence; the Nachash=358 gematria is a grounded board item).
