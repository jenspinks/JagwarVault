# The Jaguar

Type: Concept / Symbol — the project's emblem of **seeing.** Seeded 2026-06-07 from [[Jagwar Twin]].

## What it is [G — grounded etymology · W as frame]
The name's emblem: **the jaguar = "the creature who looks into the souls of other people and then, in turn, can look back into itself"** — *"humans are mirrors for each other"* → **a mirror of consciousness** (Roy's voice, **3 public interviews**: [[Sources/Transcripts/Ones-To-Watch-2018-Debut-QA]] 2018; [[Sources/Transcripts/Highwire-2021-Power-of-Sound]] 2021; [[Sources/Transcripts/Mic-Magazine-UK-Myth-of-Music]] 2024 — the last adds *"pulls people out of the darkness… into the light"*). So "the Jaguar" names the **mirror / reflexive seeing** — looks into others *and* back into the self — which is exactly the [[Source vs Reflection]] master-frame (a reflection that returns you to the source rather than trapping you in the image). [[Jagwar Twin]] is canon's *"meta-system / container… a stage for voices,"* the title read as *"Jagwar (the mirror/seer) + Twin (the paired self) — observer and observed."* The Jaguar is the **field/frame**, not a mask in the story. *(The older "sees into all living beings / travels between worlds / inter-world traveler" gloss is **not** in these quotes — that attribute's sourcing stays `[?]`.)*

## NOT a fourth peer-character [W→G — the anti-drift guard]
There is **no peer "Jaguar" figure** standing alongside [[Roy]] / [[Sir Lucius]] / [[Mary]]. The four character pages are **Jagwar Twin (the container)**, Roy, Sir Lucius, Mary. So do **not** re-read the Jaguar as a claimant in a four-figure **tetramorph** (Mary=ox / Lucius=lion / Roy=man / Jaguar=eagle) — that 1:1 is **overfit**; it belongs only to the Ezekiel paratext ([[Brain/Songs/Singles & EPs/The Watchers]]), not to the JT figures. This is the line most likely to drift; it is written down here on purpose.

## OPEN `[?]` — its order vs Mary (do NOT lock)
What stays unsettled is the Jaguar's relation to [[Mary]] (the feminine source-figure / the seer who witnesses, *El Ro'i*):
- **Reading A:** the Jaguar is a **genderless capacity** — the *seeing-field* — **distinct in order** from Mary (a figure who sees *rightly inside* it); within that field the two modes of seeing are **Lucius = surveillance** vs **Mary = witnessing** ([[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]).
- **Reading B:** Roy **deliberately collapses** them — the seeing (Jaguar) and the seer (Mary/Shekinah) as **one function, two faces.** Myth/scripture are *tools, not fixed truth* here, and we have **no grounding that he keeps them apart** (CLAUDE.md §figure-distinctness; [[Jagwar Twin]] open note).

**Default to neither.** The genderless-capacity-distinct-from-Mary framing is **Reading A**, not settled fact — record it as such.
