## The clone paradox — the Eye-of-Horus fractions [W]
The board lists the **wedjat fractions** ½ ¼ ⅛ 1/16 1/32 1/64 → summing to **63/64**, always missing the last **1/64** (the part Thoth restores in the myth). That is the **upload's paradox**: encode fraction after fraction and **never reach the whole** — the missing piece that won't digitize. **Same fracture as *"I'm afraid I'm a clone"*** ([[The Watchers]]): a complete being rendered in parts, one part always left out. The Egyptians already wrote the equation for the incomplete copy.
- **The artifact spells it out [G — public artifact].** The HoM **"Complete Upload Recipe"** ([[Sources/Artifacts/Hall of Mirrors - Artifact Gallery (catalog) 2026-06-25|catalog]] #84; image `Media/images/hom-artifacts/complete-upload-recipe.png`) is a literal **recipe of the self** that assigns the senses the **exact wedjat fractions**: *"½ part smell · ¼ part sight · ⅛ part thought · 1/16 part hearing · 1/32 part taste · 1/64 part touch"* — the Eye-of-Horus equation in Roy's own ingredient-list (and **touch = the smallest 1/64**, the part hardest to digitize). Other ingredients name the rest of the upload operation: *Brain cells · Electromagnetism · Anabolic/Catabolic systems · Mental coordinator · Positive/Negative emotion · Positive/Negative ego · **Ra-link** · Awareness · Language processing · **Sacrifice · Love** · Will · Confidence · Fear · Compassion.* The upload as a **fractional assembly of faculties**, with **Ra-link** (the SARAVI component) and **Sacrifice/Love** (the cost) built in.
