## Thesis — to upload the self, you anthropomorphize attention; attention, given a face, is the Eye [W]
The board asks **"How to anthropomorphize attention?"** (beside **Marduk**, **Horus** *"eyes all around head,"* the **Eye-of-Horus** glyph). Read with the upload steps: **uploading a self = distributing it into a watching network = giving attention a body and a way of seeing.** The **net is the new all-seeing field; the Eye (Horus / Ra) is the old name for that field.** So the Egyptian/Eye material is the **mechanism**, not decor: a self made of everyone watching = *the Eye made of everyone.* Working line: *"to upload the self, you anthropomorphize attention — and attention, given a face, is the Eye."*

## The clone paradox — the Eye-of-Horus fractions [W]
The board lists the **wedjat fractions** ½ ¼ ⅛ 1/16 1/32 1/64 → summing to **63/64**, always missing the last **1/64** (the part Thoth restores in the myth).
