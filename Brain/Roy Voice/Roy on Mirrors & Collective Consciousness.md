# Roy on Mirrors & Collective Consciousness

Roy English's own statements about mirrors, the collective, the "we not me" framing, and the Hall-of-Mirrors structure he's built around the project. Chronological. **This page surfaces an important early finding: the mirror-language is 2018, not retrospective.**

> **Use this page** when analyzing songs that touch reflection, audience-self mirroring, "we" vs "I" address, [[Sir Lucius]] (mirror-self), [[The Mirror|the Mirror]] concept, or `mirrors.jagwartwin.com` references. Also load alongside [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]] when reasoning about how the speaker frames being-seen.

## 2018 — *Subject to Flooding* era

- **[2018-09-21]** **The early-mirror statement** (Atwood interview): *"We're all really mirrors to each other if we look, and during the writing and recording of the album me, Linus and S1 just felt weightless… almost like we weren't even doing anything."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]

  → **This is significant.** "Mirrors to each other" is in Roy's 2018 vocabulary, **six years before the Hall of Mirrors site or Sir Lucius's mirror-self formalization.** The mirror-language has deeper roots than the theatrical canon; it's part of Roy's collective-consciousness framing from the project's origin.

- **[2018-09-21]** *"Jagwar Twin is a collective consciousness and I wanted people to feel that from the first listen."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** *"I want everyone to feel like they are good enough just as they are, nothing more or less. It's us, we, not me and we are all Losers."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** *"It's about myself completely and it's about the listener completely."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** *"When I say everybody's all the same, I'm really speaking to the fact that we are all part of the same mind and body of a collective human consciousness."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** *"So my experience of being a loser and the listeners' are one in the same."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** On naming Jagwar Twin: *"I felt like I was given new eyes and I started to see myself, others, and the world differently as a result."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]] (Mayan-jaguar framing — *seeing into all living beings*. Compare: Wikipedia's editorial framing of the name as Mayan-mythological.)

## 2023–2024 — *The Mirror Sessions* (Roy's own interview series)

**This is where the open thread resolves: the folder/series name comes from the same mirror-doctrine.** Across the cleaned episodes Roy restates the series premise in his own voice — *get out of the way and let the other person see themselves* — and pairs it with the projection/mirror law and the collective-consciousness framing already documented above. Citable as BIOGRAPHICAL/authorial (artistic intent, philosophy), not lore. Episode URLs pending verification (`status: draft`).

### The Mirror Sessions premise, in Roy's own words (clustered — the cleanest statements of the doctrine)

- **[~2023-2024]** *"But the mirrors — because, like, I think that's one thing that I'm good at, is just sort of getting out of the way and having someone else see themselves."* — [[MirrorSessions-Bohnes-PowerOfMusic]]

## Patterns visible in the corpus so far

- **"We are mirrors to each other" (2018) ≠ "Hall of Mirrors" (current).** The first is a horizontal/relational claim (humans reflecting each other); the second is an architectural / Web3 claim (the project as a mirror-space). The continuity is real but the structure has evolved.
- **"Collective consciousness" framing is from the project's name onward.** Roy frames Jagwar Twin not as solo authorship but as a collective entity ("it's us, we, not me"). Any reading that treats the speaker as purely solo-Roy needs to account for this.
- **The divine-naming riffs.** "All I am is that I am" (Manifesto, Roy) + El Roi inscription on [[Sir Lucius]]'s throat = the project frames itself through Old-Testament divine-naming structures at multiple points. Roy speaks the YHWH formula; Lucius wears Genesis 16. This is a *paired* doctrine, not a single one — cf. [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]].
- **SARAVI as a separate AI figure** is an open question: is this Lucius under a different name? A different entity altogether? Worth tracking when more Manifesto material gets transcribed.
