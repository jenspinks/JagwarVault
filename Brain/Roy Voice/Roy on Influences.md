## Patterns visible in the corpus so far

- **Influence diffuse, eclectic, "what's inside comes out"** — Roy explicitly *does not* think about influences while writing. Limits intentional-encoding attributions for any specific influence claim.
- **Egyptian mythology / ancient cultures as childhood fascination** is sourced (2019 Knockturnal). The Egyptian statue under the table in *The Interview* EP 1 set is therefore **not retrofitted** — it draws on a long-term Roy fascination predating the theatrical canon. Useful for [[Sir Lucius]] and any reading that hinges on Egyptian / mystery-religion imagery.
- **Mother's "weird world music" upbringing** (Celtic chants, African drumming, Native American flute) is sourced — relevant to readings about cross-cultural / collective-spiritual content. Roy's musical formation is genuinely eclectic from the start.
- **Bailey is now a named, captured influence (not silent substrate).** Earlier in this corpus Roy named *no* esoteric source directly. Now, twice over: he **shared his hand-annotated copy** (Story, 2026-05-28) **and names the book + author aloud, teaching it as his own lens** on IG Live (2026, above) — *"a book by Alice Bailey called The Twelve Labours of Hercules… the soul… the initiate… all twelve signs… inside of us."* This **decisively breaks the old "Roy never names the framework" pattern for Bailey**; Bailey-dependent reads can cite Roy directly (grounded), not just resonance. **Steiner, Gurdjieff, Jung, Kabbalah, Karpathy, etc. remain unnamed** — keep at `Resonance` unless Roy is sourced directly. *Asymmetry: Bailey is grounded; the rest are not — don't let Bailey's promotion bleed onto them.*
