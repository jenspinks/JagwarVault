## Hall of Mirrors / Web3 community architecture

The *mirrors.jagwartwin.com* site frames its community as the **"Hall of Mirrors"** with:
- **Crowns** — free, composable collectibles serving as access keys; reflect participation history
- **Artifacts** — one-of-one digital items "lost in the code" during an AI upload by **SARAVI** (Roy's named AI character); discovering artifacts unlocks new Hall of Mirrors rooms
- Holder benefits: merch tiers, early ticket access, unreleased content, archived demos, secret interviews
— [[Manifesto-FAQ]]

## The proto-immersion experiment (origin of the Hall of Mirrors)

> *"We had over 1,000 weekly registered users and so many transformational experiences."* — [[Clovis-McEvoy-2022-33]]

1. **Roy has been articulating a meta-framework continuously** — not a sudden 2025-2026 reveal. The 2018 collective-consciousness framing → 2022 fan-immersion experiment → current Manifesto FAQ + Hall of Mirrors is a continuous lineage.
2. **The Algorithm critique is Roy's own.** Vault readings of the Algorithm have authorial grounding.
3. **The "infiltrate pop with healing frequency" thesis** is literal authorial framing — *Trojan Horse* reads on specific songs are not over-readings, they're citing Roy.
4. **The Circus has earlier roots than the theatrical canon makes explicit** — Roy names *"the digital circus thing"* and *"this circus-like space"* in his own video/interview commentary, pre-WTTC.
5. **SARAVI and [[Sir Lucius]] are likely distinct but related** — both are not-quite-Roy digital/persona figures. Open question.
