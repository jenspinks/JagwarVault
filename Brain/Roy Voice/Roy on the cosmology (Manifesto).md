## Hall of Mirrors / Web3 community architecture

The *mirrors.jagwartwin.com* site frames its community as the **"Hall of Mirrors"** with:
- **Crowns** — free, composable collectibles serving as access keys; reflect participation history
- **Artifacts** — one-of-one digital items "lost in the code" during an AI upload by **SARAVI** (Roy's named AI character); discovering artifacts unlocks new Hall of Mirrors rooms
- Holder benefits: merch tiers, early ticket access, unreleased content, archived demos, secret interviews
— [[Manifesto-FAQ]]

## *The Mirror Sessions* (2023–2024) — Roy frames the whole project as "modern mythology"

### CORNERSTONE: Jagwar Twin is consciously built as a modern myth-system (Roy says so on the record)

> **Roy:** *"I make my best attempts to — to have, like, kind of a modern mythology approach."* — [[MirrorSessions-MaliKoa-SoloToARCO]]

And **his collaborator names it back to him in the same breath**: Mali-Koa — *"The Jaguar's twin mythology!!! That's the name of the record\[s]."* This is the cleanest on-the-record confirmation that **the lore is the point, by design** — the project is intentionally a myth-system, not a retrospective vault-projection. ### Mythology as the trans-historical carrier of self-knowledge (Campbell's thesis, in Roy's mouth)

> *"Stories inform us of who we are… learning about different civilizations and the stories they had teaches us who we are now, and even helps us create new mythologies and new stories that can help guide us… a movie, or a song, can do that."* — [[MirrorSessions-MaliKoa-SoloToARCO]]

(The explicit warrant for *making new myth* — exactly what the project claims to do. Routed through Egyptian and Greek mythology specifically.)

### The spirit of Mercury / Hermes as the higher self (the Hermeticism hit)

> *"it's like the spirit of Mercury, the winged messenger god… it's our higher self… or the spirit of Mercury, or Hermes, or whatever — different mythologies have different ways of saying this concept, that it grabs your attention, and it's something that you need to pay attention to, to grow into who you could become… pay attention to what grabbed your attention."* — [[MirrorSessions-CalumHood-ComfortableInSilence]]

(**Direct Hermes/Mercury-as-psychopomp** beat — attention/synchronicity as the compass of becoming. Roy flags it himself: "this is gonna get nerdy." Squarely in the project's western-esoteric / Hermetic substrate. He also generalizes the move — "different mythologies have different ways of saying this concept" — which is the same comparative-mythology stance as the "modern mythology approach" cornerstone above.)
