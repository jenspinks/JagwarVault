# Roy on 33

Roy English's own statements about the *33* album (2022). **Substantial corpus now in place** — use this page when reasoning about any 33 track, the album's overall framing, or the catalogue arc between *Subject to Flooding* (2018) and *Lucius Lullaby* (2026).

---

## The album's title & theme (Ones to Watch 2022 — KEY)

> **"thirty-three is the age I wrote the album. It represents peace within and without."** — [[Ones-To-Watch-2022-33-QA]]

> **"The whole theme of the album is, in some ways, catalyzing pain."** — [[Ones-To-Watch-2022-33-QA]]

> *"The whole album is about finding peace with all those things that made this beautiful body of work out of those difficult things."* — [[Ones-To-Watch-2022-33-QA]]

**Grounded:** *33* is **the literal age**, not (only) a Christ-age / numerological reference. The vault should treat any reading that depends on age-33 mysticism as **resonance**, not grounded — Roy's own framing is biographical-and-pain-processing, not esoteric-numerological. (Resonance reads can still operate; they just shouldn't claim authorial intent.)

## Process & creative orientation in this era

- **[2022-12-21]** *"I really used that time to re-evaluate why I was making music in the first place."* — [[Clovis-McEvoy-2022-33]]
- **[2022-12-21]** *"I desire to tell stories. The mediums change through the ages, but the stories are the same."* — [[Clovis-McEvoy-2022-33]]
- **[2022]** *"I've just been trying to listen to that voice more. It was innate in me, and it's innate in everyone, but at least for me, I went through a period where I blocked it."* — [[Ones-To-Watch-2022-33-QA]]
- **[2022]** *"So it's become about creating more from the heart and a place of intuition."* — [[Ones-To-Watch-2022-33-QA]]
- **[2022]** *"Try to take things less seriously now... I'm just trying to have fun."* — [[Ones-To-Watch-2022-33-QA]]

## The fan-immersion experiment (proto-Manifesto / proto-mirror-architecture)

- **[2022-12-21]** *"I wanted to experiment with how a fan could really immerse themselves psychologically and physiologically in a song."* — [[Clovis-McEvoy-2022-33]]
- **[2022-12-21]** *"We had over 1,000 weekly registered users and so many transformational experiences."* — [[Clovis-McEvoy-2022-33]]

This is **the lineage line** between the early-period collective-consciousness framing (2018 Atwood) and the later *mirrors.jagwartwin.com* Hall-of-Mirrors / Crown / Artifact / SARAVI architecture (per [[Manifesto-FAQ]]). The fan-immersion work is in place by 2022.

## Identity-as-narrative

- **[2022-12-21]** *"We are the stories we tell, and I wonder if we have forgotten who we are."* — [[Clovis-McEvoy-2022-33]]
- **[2022-12-21]** *"To be able to share deeply personal stories and memories and connect with others, you need to be able to connect with yourself."* — [[Clovis-McEvoy-2022-33]]
- **[2022-12-21]** *"We are more connected to each other than ever, but somehow many of us feel more disconnected from ourselves."* — [[Clovis-McEvoy-2022-33]] (echoes the 2018 Atwood disconnection line)

- **[[Online]]**: *"That song is so fun and so ridiculous."* — [[Ones-To-Watch-2022-33-QA]] *(Useful counterweight to over-serious readings. The song is playful AND it diagnoses database identity / predictive profiling. Both registers are real; Roy's own framing is the playful one.)*
