# Roy on Process

Roy English's own statements about songwriting, production, collaboration, and the conditions he creates for himself to work. Chronological. Each quote → citation back to the source transcript in `Sources/Transcripts/`.

> **Use this page** when analyzing how a song was made, what its production logic is, or when interpreting structural choices (cadence, arrangement, sequencing). Roy on Process gives the *authorial* account; promote `[W]` claims to grounded where Roy speaks directly.

## 2018 — *Subject to Flooding* era

- **[2018-09-21]** *"It's different for every song. 'Move to You' was like a download, I didn't really write it … It felt given to me."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** *"I heard it all at once and scrambled to voice memo all the music, lyrics, and melodies out."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** *"For me, it's important to always switch up the process that way I never get stuck in a rut and the creativity keeps evolving."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** *"It's honestly more about creating the space to create. Going out to Joshua Tree with no cell phone, or being in Florence, or Haiti, or Ireland. The space is important."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** On Linus's production for [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Loser]]: *"Linus is such a genius because he understands that simplicity is the most important thing to the song. But with that every ingredient has to be great."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** On [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Loser]]'s arrangement evolution: *"Linus and I came up with the original idea of Loser over this dark dreamy kind of movie soundtrack vibe. Then one day he muted all the music and played this '60s sounding guitar riff over it and we both heard it through a new lens that brought out the essence of the message. So it really was removing everything that brought it to life."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** On the [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Loser]] children's-choir vocal switch: *"It was definitely a conscious decision to use a choir of children."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]
- **[2018-09-21]** On collaboration: *"There were so many talented people that made this album what it is and everybody let go of ego to create something bigger than all of us as individuals."* — [[Atwood-2018-Loser]]

## 2019 — STF promotional era

- **[2019-09-23]** *"Through the process of Subject To Flooding I completely changed my diet, my meditation and prayer practices, views on governmental and human made institutions and most of all, learned so much about myself."* — [[Prelude-Press-2019-JT]]
- **[2019-10-10]** *"We were doing ice baths and saunas and guided meditations all the time. It was amazing!"* — [[Knockturnal-2019-Studio-Sessions]]
- **[2019-10-10]** *"All these things made us look inward. I think to make good art, you have to learn about yourself first, and all those experiences combined helped me do that."* — [[Knockturnal-2019-Studio-Sessions]]
- **[2019-12-05]** Download framing again: *"I don't feel that I'm even writing what's coming through me and there's so many amazing people involved."* — [[New-England-Sounds-2019-Avril-Tour]]
- **[2019-12-05]** Standard: *"If everyone in the room feels like this is the best thing we can be doing, we get the chills. Do we believe this? Is it authentic to me as a person?"* — [[New-England-Sounds-2019-Avril-Tour]]

## 2023–2024 — *The Mirror Sessions* era

Roy's process language across the cleaned Mirror Sessions episodes is remarkably consistent: **emptying / channeling, turning off the thinking mind, feeling over thinking, and surrender.** It directly extends the 2018–2020 "download" framing — composition as receptive, not constructed.

### Empty the cup / channeling / "it's not me"

- **[~2023-2024]** *"we have to be empty for it to talk to us… to the degree in which you're able to turn off your thoughts and become like an empty cup, then all of a sudden that allows the source of whatever that is to flow through us, and then write lyrics or do something — like, 'this is genius, this is amazing,' but it's not me."* — [[MirrorSessions-S1-HearGodsVoice]]
- **[~2023-2024]** *"if I go into the studio and it's like, 'I'm going to write a song about this,' it never turns out that way. But sometimes you feel a feeling that day, and you just turn off the thinking mind, and things flow through."* — [[MirrorSessions-S1-HearGodsVoice]]
- **[~2023-2024]** *"you're an antenna. And in observing and being comfortable with silence, it's like you get these — *shoop* — and then you're articulating that when you center in yourself and observe."* — [[MirrorSessions-CalumHood-ObservantVsShy]] (the channel/antenna image; "it's not from you… it's from the silence that you create")

### Feeling over thinking / "the thinking trap"

- **[~2023-2024]** *"the thinking trap."* — [[MirrorSessions-JeffEllis-FeelingVsThinking]] (Roy's term, agreeing with Jeff that raised stakes pull artists out of feeling and into thinking — "their art falls off a cliff")
- **[~2023-2024]** Autobiographical: *"even my first major label deal — I got signed off this song that… completely \[came from\] intuition, flow… Got signed, and then I was like, okay, now I have to make songs that are popular. And I just blew it all up by thinking about it all again."* — [[MirrorSessions-JeffEllis-FeelingVsThinking]]
- **[~2023-2024]** The same arc, restated: he wrote 8–12 hrs/day chasing success, *"that didn't get me anywhere, but as soon as I started turning it off, stuff I really care about started coming through."* — [[MirrorSessions-CalumHood-ObservantVsShy]] (a clean counterpoint to hustle-culture, framed from inside it)

### Surrender / letting-go (the 2023 "lesson of the year")

- **[~2023-2024]** *"I'm learning to let go more this year. That's, I think, my lesson of this year, is to keep letting go — of anything extraneous."* — [[MirrorSessions-MaliKoa-SoloToARCO]]
- **[~2023-2024]** The bucket metaphor, capped as a process statement: *"you jump into the ocean… with the buckets of rocks and sand, and you're sinking down, and then as you're sinking you say, 'Oh, I gotta let go,' and you let go — and then you float to the top."* — and Roy closes it with *"that's how songwriting works."* — [[MirrorSessions-MaliKoa-SoloToARCO]] (surrender/non-forcing; **Echo** of S1's "flow with the water, not against it" — water imagery does the work in both)

### Chaos → order as the mechanism of art

- **[~2023-2024]** *"chaos is creativity… everything is birthed out of chaos. Of course. Darkness, nothing — the feminine principle of, you know, like yin-yang."* — [[MirrorSessions-Bohnes-PowerOfMusic]] (and, per the cleaned set, the ordering of that chaos as "part of the mechanism of art… bring the light side of the yin-yang." Note Roy explicitly **genders** chaos/darkness as the feminine principle — recorded as his framework, not the vault's reading; cf. [[Duality]])

## Patterns visible in the corpus so far

- **Process is non-systematic by design.** Roy frames variation as protective against ruts ("switch up the process… so I never get stuck"). Don't read consistent method into the catalogue.
- **"Download" framing**: songs come to him whole ("I didn't really write it"; "felt given to me"). Strong authorial discount of conscious construction — relevant to the *retrospective-coherence guard* (CLAUDE.md): Roy himself frames composition as receptive, which limits how much intentional encoding can be attributed.
- **Production-as-removal**: the [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Loser]] story is "muted all the music" → essence emerges by subtraction. Compare with the production-as-armor reading in the [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Hell of a Night Pt. 2 (The Hallelujah Stickup)|Hell Pt. 2 essay]] (production as emotional armor) — Roy's own framing emphasizes simplicity-by-removal.
- **Space-as-method**: Joshua Tree, Florence, Haiti, Ireland, no cell phone — *environment* is part of the process. Useful for grounding any reading that hinges on isolation/retreat imagery.
