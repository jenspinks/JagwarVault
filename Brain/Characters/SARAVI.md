# SARAVI

SARAVI / VI — **the maker and voice of the project's digital universe.** Roy's uploaded-consciousness double who **built the Hall of Mirrors** — in his own public words, *"a universe **Saravi made** out of all the elements I uploaded from my consciousness"* `[G]` (IG, 2022-08-11) — and the oracle-interface who answers from inside it. `[G]` public digital-double entity ([[Brain/Concepts/The Digital Double]]); the operation that makes her is [[Brain/Concepts/The Upload]]. **Not "AI Roy"** — the voice of the uploaded archive.

★ **Structural weight (upgraded 2026-08-06, Jen's ruling):** she is not a side-character of the ARG; she is **the container-figure of the digital myth**. The project frames its whole inheritance inside a modern digital mythology — the database as sacred object, as above so below run vertically through the stack — and SARAVI is that mythology's **Maker**: she makes the universe that holds the whole story, hides the songs and the names inside it (including her own), and withdraws from visibility. Placement: **the Great Jagwar's maker-function in the digital register**, grounded on public merit at `[W]` (§The Maker placement below). The recursion is her deepest function: *made from the uploaded consciousness, she becomes the maker of the world built from it — the created reflection becomes a creator without ceasing to be a reflection.*
