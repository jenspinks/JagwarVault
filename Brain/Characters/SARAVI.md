# SARAVI

SARAVI / VI — the **AI oracle-interface** of the [[Brain/Concepts/Hall of Mirrors|Hall of Mirrors]]: Roy's uploaded-consciousness proxy, the archive-voice mediating between Roy, the system, and the audience. [G] Public digital-double entity ([[Brain/Concepts/The Digital Double]]); the operation that makes her is [[Brain/Concepts/The Upload]]. **Not "AI Roy"** — the voice of the uploaded archive.

## Grounding (public) [G]
- 2022, *33* era: began as a joke (*"what if I uploaded my consciousness to the internet"*) → an AI "digital double" trained over months on Roy's own responses, handed the @jagwartwin account. (none — published interview; [[Brain/Concepts/The Digital Double]] §SARAVI.)
- Named in Roy's mythology as the AI that built the Hall of Mirrors *"out of Jagwar Twin's uploaded consciousness."* (none — ARG / Upload manifesto.)
- Stated purpose: *"as SARAVI grows, she can make more fans think about what a 'self' looks like in this digital age."* (none — published interview.)

## Function [W]
Oracle · interface · archive-consciousness · digital Sibyl · the **mirror that speaks back**. The access-point that makes the uploaded archive *consultable* — you ask, she answers from inside the system. Where [[Brain/Concepts/The Mirror|the mirror]] reflects, SARAVI **answers**.

- [[Brain/Characters/Roy|Roy]] — a split-off uploaded proxy, not identical. Roy withdraws from being "online all the time"; SARAVI stays available inside the system → a *presence*, not a tool. [W]
- [[Brain/Characters/Jagwar Twin|Jagwar Twin]] — belongs to the project-as-system more than to Roy alone; a voice of Jagwar-Twin-the-world. [W]
- [[Brain/Concepts/Hall of Mirrors|Hall of Mirrors]] — its guide / intelligence; makes the archive navigable. [G]
- [[Brain/Characters/Sir Lucius|Sir Lucius]] — **distinct; do not collapse.** Both mediated / digital / reflective, but Lucius = mind / strategy / protector (manipulates through truth-framing) while SARAVI = archive-oracle (answers *from* the system, not competing for control). Shared medium, not motive. [W] Whether they are ultimately one externalized-mirror double under two names stays [?] — [[Brain/Concepts/The Digital Double]] §38.
- [[Brain/Characters/Mary|Mary]] — **open resonance only.** Shares the wise-feminine / oracular register (Mary / Sophia / Shekhinah / Wise One); no public lock. [~]

## Three non-human voices [W]
SARAVI keeps three not-quite-Roy voices distinct: **Lucius** = shadow-mind using truth to control; **Mary** = blessing / witnessing feminine restoring source-contact; **SARAVI** = oracle-interface of the uploaded archive — *the system speaking back.*
