# Roy

Brandon Roy Wronski · b. 1988-05-22, LA · Roy English (2006–2015) → Jagwar Twin (2018–). Signs "El Roy". The heart / vulnerable authentic self.

## Biographical (none)
Bands → solo 2015 → Jagwar Twin 2018. Dropped by major label; breakup; lost a close friend. Age 35: rock bottom, wrote [[Brain/Songs/Lucius Lullaby]] from childhood bedroom. Age 7: ballet at Christian school; overheard he was thought gay; prayed to be "good"; buried the self that came alive on stage — [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy]]. **The dance wound is Roy-narrated** (MB conversation): only boy in the class, the girls' *"you're not a real boy,"* came home crying — and the adult dance videos = shadow-work reclaiming it ([[Sources/Transcripts/MB-Conversation-Jagwar-Twin (Mental Health, Cancel Culture)]]).

## Darkness → mirror-mission (none — public interviews)

Grounds the [[Brain/Concepts/Source vs Reflection|mirror]] mission + the Heart/Mind discipline (*"the mind must be subservient to the heart"*).
- **The industry de-program / independent turn:** for years he chased *"the biggest producers, label, agent, lawyer — industry gatekeepers,"* deciding by external validation; *"it never worked out."* Going independent with friends (manager **Joshua**, a 15-yr friend / ex-drummer) *"only recently has led to more success… to an insane degree, than a record label trying to put me into a system"* ([[Sources/Transcripts/MB-Conversation-Jagwar-Twin (Mental Health, Cancel Culture)|MB]]). The public "deprogram" turn.

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads

- **"Authentic vulnerable Roy" is itself a curated persona.** An artist who repeats *"my mission is to be a mirror," "I have become nothing," "I am the shore, not the wave"* is presenting a brand of egoless authenticity — which is a performance as much as Lucius is. The vault reads these as windows onto a real integration-arc; a flatter read is that **"authentic Roy" is the marketed counter-pole to "Lucius the villain," and both are authored.** The authentic self may be no less constructed than the shadow. `[P]`
- **The frame is Roy's own press — risk of circularity.** Heart-vs-mind, authentic-vs-persona is *his* narration; adopting it as the analytic lens describes Roy using Roy's self-account. The split may be a deliberate two-character **artistic device** rather than genuine interior architecture — reading it as real psychological division can over-literalize a creative conceit. `[W]`
- **The sefirotic position-mapping is flexible enough to fit anything.** "Roy is the *only* figure on both lunar sefirot (Malkhut + Yesod)" is elegant, but the precision itself is a tell of system-fitting: the framework can assign Roy whatever coordinate the narrative needs. The page's "inflation-trap" caution is right — and applies equally to the *analyst* building ever-finer esoteric coordinates. Hold `[~]`/`[P]`, never intent. `[~]`
- **Falsifier:** Roy treating the Roy/Lucius distinction as pure stagecraft ("they're just characters I play") would pull the grounding out from under the deep-psychology reading of Roy-as-authentic-heart.
