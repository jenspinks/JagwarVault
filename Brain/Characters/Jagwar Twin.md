# Jagwar Twin

Type: Character / Project

## Description
- The primary musical project of [[Roy|Brandon Roy Wronski]] since 2018 (previously: Roy English, 2006–2015).
- Per [[Context Store 1]]: the **meta-system / container** — not a single voice but a stage for voices. Contains [[Sir Lucius]] (mind / strategy / reflection), [[Roy]] (heart / meaning / interpretation), and [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy]] (origin / light).
- Name etymology: Mayan jaguar mythology — the jaguar could see into all living beings, travel between worlds. The self-knowing inter-world traveler. (source: [[Roy]])
- Signed to Big Loud Rock, under exclusive license to Mercury Records (UMG).
- Notable streaming and viral metrics: over 1 billion global streams, over 1 billion YouTube views, Happy Face (RIAA Certified Gold), Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa) (Top 40 US radio).

## Notes
- The project name itself encodes the duality: **Jagwar** (the seer, the inter-world traveler) + **Twin** (the paired / mirrored self). The project's title *is* the thesis — a self that is both one and two, both observer and observed.
- Spelling: "Jagwar" not "Jaguar" — intentional choice by Wronski.

## Open question (working — NOT locked): the seeing-field, the four figures, and collapse
**Status `[?]` — recording, not deciding.** Seeded by a reviewer thread (2026-06-07) that mapped Ezekiel's four living creatures → tetramorph → Jung's quaternity → a four-figure system (Roy / Lucius / Mary / Jaguar). Jen's hesitation flagged a real strain, so we hold it open.

- **The strain.** If the Jaguar's defining trait is *seeing into all beings / the wise self-knowing one*, that overlaps a function already given to **Mary / Shekinah** (the hidden source, the thread, *El Ro'i* = the seeing-God). Two figures can't both simply *be* "the wise seer who holds the string" — so either they are **distinct (different orders)** *or* **Roy means them as one.**
- **Reading A — distinct orders `[~]/[W]`.** Canon already calls Jagwar Twin *"the meta-system / container… a stage for voices,"* and reads the name as *"Jagwar (the seer) + Twin (the paired self)… observer and observed."* On A, the **Jaguar = the seeing-field** (Roy-sourced Mayan etymology, *"sees into all living beings"*) — **not a fourth peer** — within which the vault's two **modes of seeing** operate: **Lucius = surveillance** (wears *El Roi* on the throat, cannot witness) vs **Mary = witnessing** (*El Roi* fulfilled) — [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]. And the **thread** is *feeling* ([[The Thread]]): **Mary gives** it (source), **Roy must hold** it — so "holds the string" is not cleanly any single figure. Under A the tetramorph 1:1 (Mary=ox / Lucius=lion / Roy=man / Jaguar=eagle) is **overfit**, kept to the Ezekiel paratext ([[Brain/Songs/Singles & EPs/The Watchers]]), not mapped onto the four figures.
- **Reading B — deliberate collapse `[?]` (co-equal; do NOT rule out).** Roy plays loosely with mythology; scripture/myth are **tools, not fixed truth** here (CLAUDE.md §figure-distinctness). He may **intend** the overlaps — Jaguar ≈ Mary (the seeing and the seer), or seer ≈ string-holder — as **one function wearing two faces** (note *Jagwar **Twin*** foregrounds the *duality*; the feminine source stays the *hidden* term, as she is everywhere). **We have no grounding that he keeps these distinct.** A's tidiness is *our* heuristic, not his law.
- **What would ground it (seeking):** a Roy statement on the Jaguar↔Mary / seer↔source relationship; the **public source** for the Mayan-jaguar "sees into all beings" gloss (currently *source: Roy*, uncited); casting/credit for the Mary voice; any instance where Roy explicitly merges or separates figures. **Until then: neither lock A nor dismiss B.**
