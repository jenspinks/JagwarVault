## Description
- The primary musical project of [[Roy]] since 2018 (earlier releases as **Roy English**, which remains his songwriting credit).
- The **meta-system / container** — not a single voice but a stage for voices. Contains [[Sir Lucius]] (mind / strategy / reflection), [[Roy]] (heart / meaning / interpretation), and [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy]] (origin / light). *(Container-vs-contained is the vault's established character architecture, grounded via the name-etymology below + [[The Jaguar]]; it does not rest on the retired `Context Store 1` synthesis it once cited.)*

## Open question (working — NOT locked): the seeing-field, the four figures, and collapse
