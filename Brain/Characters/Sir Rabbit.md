# Sir Rabbit

Type: Character / **Roy's initiatory self-image** — the seeker before fixed identity.

> **Promoted from `[?]` to a filed character 2026-06-29**, now that the grounding exists: the full **[[Sources/Artifacts/HoM Room 51 - Sir Rabbit (poem)|Sir Rabbit poem]]** (HoM Room 51, code `Q763`, **2022-08-12**, on Roy's own platform `[G]`) + Roy's **"I was a rabbit"** self-identification in the [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Good Time (1-08)|Good Time (1:08)]] dream-origin (TikTok, 2023, `[G]`).

## Who he is `[W]`
Sir Rabbit is **Roy's initiatory self-image: the seeker before fixed identity** — *before* any guide hardens into a sovereign persona. Forgetful, searching, uncertain, teachable, heart-recovering; capable of a *chosen* leap. His defining line is identity-as-open-question: *"I do not know… but I must be someone"* `[G]`.

> ⚠ **Register guard:** do **not** write "Roy *is* Sir Rabbit." The grounded claim is Roy's **rabbit self-identification across two texts**; the character-register claim (Sir Rabbit = the Roy-pole seeker) is `[W]`. And he is **not a third figure outside the Roy/Lucius axis** — in the grounded dream-origin *Roy* is the rabbit and *Lucius* is the **second** rabbit who slides into the passenger seat. Roy's *"sir rabbit pre-lucius"* (Discord 2023-10-12, `[G]`) = the self **before** the split, i.e. the Roy-register — not a proto-*Lucius*. *(This corrects the older "proto-Lucius origin" framing on the Room 51 note.)*

## The connection to Ballerina Boy `[W]`
Sir Rabbit's **identity-vacancy** (*"I do not know… but I must be someone"*) is what the shame-wound *produces*. The [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy|Ballerina Boy]] is the **buried content** (the embodied child told *"you're not a real boy"*); **Sir Rabbit is the searching self that results** — directionless but intact, capable of initiation. Wound → buried child → searching rabbit.

## The poem as the authentic arc; the dream as the intercepted one `[W]`
The Sir Rabbit poem and the Good Time dream are **the same initiatory arc — one authentic, one intercepted:**
- **The poem** (`[G]`, Room 51) runs the full sequence: Cat → the **Violet Moon** (a machine) → the **Fox** + the box → the forgotten **heartbeat** → the **Dove** (lamp, emerald garden) → the **Spider** (the Divide) → through the **Mourning Sun** → **transformed return** (*"back where he begun"*).
- **The dream** (`[G]`, Good Time): Roy-as-rabbit, searching without knowing what he seeks, is joined by **Sir Lucius before any authentic guide appears** — Lucius is factually correct but emotionally wrong; Roy's heart detects the manipulation before his mind can say why.
- **The reading:** the poem shows the *authentic* initiation; the dream shows the same seeker-position **intercepted by the wrong guide at the threshold.** *(The poem ends in a chosen leap → Circle return; the dream ends in an involuntary lightning-crash → interrupted initiation.)*

## What the poem's figures establish (full cast → [[Sources/Artifacts/HoM Room 51 - Sir Rabbit (poem)]])
- **The Fox — a *redeemed* trickster `[W]`.** Manipulative in *form*, **medicinal in *result*** — his box forces Rabbit inward until the heartbeat is recovered (*"the key is hidden in yourself / just listen for the knock"*). He arrives carrying **bread** (real provision the machine can't generate). **The cosmology has a trickster mode that is NOT Lucius** — do not flatten all cunning onto Lucius. (Contrast: Fox = manipulative-form/medicinal-result; **Lucius = helpful-form/parasitic-result**.)
- **The "other mind" `[W]`** — *"Now you must use your other mind… it's been mapped,"* and the **key to that mind is the heartbeat.** This refines the heart/mind formula: **mind *severed* from heart becomes Lucius; mind *returned through* the heartbeat becomes the other mind.** Not heart *against* mind — mind *reoriented through* the forgotten pulse. ("It's been mapped" = anamnesis: the faculty was always there.)
- **The Spider** = threshold initiator (the **Divide**); poses the name/identity question (*"Who are you Rabbit?"*), names the dreaming (*"you're dreaming all the time"* → the wake-up faculty), and **preserves the seeker's sovereignty** (*"that's up to you, it's not for my to pry"*). The companion of the cracked **I'M THE SPIDER** room (`the answers in a name`) — selfhood as a *name*-problem before a code-problem.
- **The Dove** = a **[[Mary]] / Sophia-function** guide — gives light (a lamp), then **releases** (*"as much as I love company you must be on your way"*); helps without trapping in devotion. *(Guard: a Mary-register, not "the clearest Mary in the catalogue.")*
- **The Violet Moon** = mechanized reflection ([[Brain/Concepts/Source vs Reflection]]) — its *beauty* (violet sky-moon) precedes the *revelation of mechanism* (a machine on arrival), mirroring the Lucius dynamic (helpfulness precedes felt manipulation).

## The Lucius mechanism this grounds `[W]`
Lucius is the **counterfeit guide who gives true answers without the heartbeat** — he occupies the guide-seat *before* the initiation has been endured. *"Lucius does not only deceive by lying; he deceives by **arriving too early with true answers**."* (The *mechanism* beneath the vault's existing *"truth that deceives"* — complementary, not competing.) → [[Sir Lucius]].
