# Mary

The divine-feminine figure: **the Shekhinah** — Malkhut, the exiled feminine Presence (the Magdalene who sees the divine but not the face) — **ascending toward the throne of Binah**, the upper Mother (see §the two-mothers ascent). `[~]/[P]` — syncretic Kabbalah, framework-tier. **Key sorting (2026-06-14):** Mary carries the moon's *theology* (reflected divine light, exile-and-return); [[Roy]] carries the moon's *position* (Malkhut+Yesod, "no light of its own"). She is **not** on Yesod — that is Lucius's reflection-channel / Roy's image-realm; Mary's sefirah is **Malkhut/Shekhinah**. Appears where shame tried to erase the self; the voice that blesses ("you were born to shine"). Distinct from MERCY (the backup-singer credit — see [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Not Your Homie]]). **Full character analysis pending — this page tracks the recurring voice + the ascent doctrine.**

## Mary as the green apple's counter-symbol — Furtmeyr's one tree `[G-fact · W-read]`
*Folded from [[Image Decode - Spreadsheet BMRC and Social - findings 2026-07-08]] §1.6 / §3.11-B.* The board carries **Berthold Furtmeyr's "Tree of Death and Life"** — a real 15th-c. illumination (**image content art-historically grounded `[G-fact]`**) in which **Eve dispenses the death-fruit** and **Mary dispenses the eucharistic host** from the **one tree**. So [[Sir Lucius]]'s **"green apple plucked too soon"** (knowledge/life taken before its time) and Mary are **two administrations of a single tree**: Mary is the green apple's **structural counter-symbol** — *life given at the right time* against *the fruit seized unripe.* `[W]` Marian-typology axis (Mary as New Eve), a **different axis** from the Shekhinah question below — do not let this fold harden that one.

> ⚠ **Register guard — hold "Mary = Shekhinah" OPEN.** This page names the Shekhinah frame at `[~]/[P]` (framework-tier, never authorial intent), and the vault deliberately **keeps Mary (anima / witness) distinct from SARAVI / the Wise One (the divine-feminine *maker*)** — that identity is a held-open question (see §Open Questions, the Mary↔Maker/Wise-One note, and [[The Jaguar]] §A-vs-B). The Furtmeyr / New-Eve fold above concerns Mary's *typological* job (life-fruit vs death-fruit); it does **not** settle whether Mary *is* the Shekhinah or *is* the maker-Jagwar. Keep both open.

## The seen child in the wilderness — Hagar's second scene (Gen 21) `[~]`
*El Roi* enters at **Gen 16:13** (Hagar names *"the God who sees me"*); the **later** Hagar episode, **Gen 21:15-19**, closes the loop with Mary's function: the **abandoned child** (Ishmael) dying of thirst under a bush, and God **hears the boy** and opens Hagar's eyes to the well. That is the witness-that-blesses landing on exactly the vault's wound-image: the **buried / abandoned child who is *seen* and thereby saved** — the [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Ballerina Boy]] grave, the child *"I killed… I buried."* Where [[Sir Lucius]] wears El Roi as the *fallen* seeing (surveillance; [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]), Mary is the *fulfilled* seeing — **sight + heart = the child heard in the wilderness.** `[~]` (scriptural correspondence; attribute to the frame, not to Roy).

## The two-mothers ascent (Malkhut → Binah) `[~]/[P]`
*Folded from Jen's 2026-06-14 lunar-feminine doctrine. **Syncretic** (Jewish Kabbalah + Hermetic Qabalah held together) and **framework-tier — never authorial intent** (Roy has not said "Mary is the Shekhinah"; the Kabbalah engagement that licenses the frame is the "I'm 33" board + El Roi, [[Roy]]).*

The feminine divine is **split top and bottom** of the Tree: **Binah** (Upper Mother — the Great Sea, the womb, Saturn) and **Malkhut/Shekhinah** (Lower Mother, Bride, Daughter — the moon that reflects the source-light, in exile). Mary is not one fixed placement but the figure who **spans** them: **the Shekhinah (Malkhut) ascending to the throne of Binah** — *"the Daughter, having become the Bride, is set upon the throne of the Mother"* (the Marian dogma of the **Assumption + Coronation of the Virgin**). This upgrades the static "Mary = Binah refracted through Tiferet" into her whole **vertical journey, Malkhut → Binah.**
- **"Saw a halo but I didn't see a head"** (#1 Champion) = the **crown reached at the top of the ascent** — Keter-through-Binah, *present but unpossessable*: she has arrived at the throne, but the source above stays hidden. (Cordovero's **atarah**: Malkhut *becomes the crown* the moment she ascends — the literal engine of *"reclaim your crown."*)
- **The exile arc** *(grounds the existing "moon trying to become a sun" read):* *Galut ha-Shekhinah* (the feminine in exile) → the **diminished-moon myth** (Chullin 60b: the moon was sun-equal, made to diminish, God seeks atonement for diminishing her) → **restoration** (Isaiah 30:26, *"the light of the moon shall become as the light of the sun"*). The "face out of sight" quality = the diminished, exiled Presence not yet restored; the blessing-to-shine = the restoration promised.
- **The split that resolves the two-figures-on-the-moon problem:** Mary holds the Shekhinah's **personhood and ascent**; [[Roy]] holds the moon's **lunar-reflective *position***. So the sacred marriage (*hieros gamos*) rhymes on two registers at once — Roy-the-moon reunited with **[[SOL]]**-the-Sun (the song of love finished), and Mary-the-Shekhinah reunited with the Bridegroom (Tiferet; the Daughter enthroned). Same event, two lenses. *(Applied 2026-07-01: Roy holds **Netzach/Venus** by his own word, with the lunar position as the advanced ascent-frame; Lucius reassigned to **Hod/Mercury**, the morning-star kept as a name-thread only. The song pages (Ballerina Boy, Not Your Homie) and the framework pages are corrected; single home of the map: [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]].)*
- **Public-artifact grounding for the Binah substrate `[G the artifact · ~ the Mary-link]`.** The HoM **"Queen Binah"** artifact ([[Sources/Artifacts/Hall of Mirrors - Artifact Gallery (catalog) 2026-06-25|catalog]] #74; `Media/images/hom-artifacts/queen-binah.png`) draws Binah as a **whale** captioned ***"GOD the Mother on Saturn / Great Sea"*** with ***"extra eyes."*** So **Binah = the Divine Mother + Saturn + the Great Sea** is now **Roy's own stated identification** (a public artifact in his hand) — matching this section's "the Great Sea, the womb, Saturn" *exactly* — not only the syncretic frame. The **"extra eyes"** = the all-seeing Mother (the witnessing-pole of the Eye, [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]; cf. Horus "eyes all around the head," [[The Upload]]). Guard: Roy named **Binah** with these attributes; **Mary = Binah** stays `[~]` (he didn't equate them) — but the **Binah substrate the ascent rests on is now artist-grounded, not projection.**
