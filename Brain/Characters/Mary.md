# Mary

The divine-feminine figure. [P] Shekinah / Anima register; appears where shame tried to erase the self; the voice that blesses ("you were born to shine"). Distinct from MERCY (the backup-singer credit — see [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Not Your Homie]]). **Full character analysis pending — this page currently tracks the recurring voice.**
