The Lucius / Mask / Circus iconography is visible in the **original music videos years before the 2026 billing**, and it lands on the very songs Lucius verbally claims in *The Interview* EP1 (*"happy face, bad feeling… those are me"*). The arc:

([[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Happy Face]]; videos [[Master Source List]] §3.)

Kyle Vorbach): Roy bloodied inside a ring of **blank grey masks** — which Roy's *own pinned comment* names *"a beautiful group of mind/body/spirit complexes"* (the Ra / Law-of-One term; citable Roy-voice). The **official description** names the stakes — *"loss of identity… a self created hell."* The faceless congregation the song's narrator runs (*"the people are mine"*). - **2023 — *Good Time (1:08)*** (logged above): full face paint + **knight armor** + *"I'm a mastermind."* The **armor recurs in EP1's staging** (Roy *"wears metal armor from the waist down"*) — a visual rhyme three years early.

**Motif (~2020) → assembled face (2023) → named/billed/claiming (2026): the *visual* claim precedes the *verbal* one.** This is the strongest evidence yet for the doctrine above — *billing changed, not the figure* — and it's **grounded in public iconography** ([[Boundaries]] §visible-iconography), not only the written record. **Guard:** the iconography *accumulated* and was *retroactively named*; this is **not** a claim that a conscious "Sir Lucius" was authoring or claiming songs in 2020.
