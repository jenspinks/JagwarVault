## Grounded

Debut studio album. Produced with a "we, not me" collaborative ethos (Roy's public framing). Roy's stated theme: individualism, authenticity, removing masks. Breakthrough single: "Loser" (premiered Zane Lowe / Beats 1, 17.4M Spotify streams; released as a single 2019-09-13 per MusicBrainz). "Long Time Coming" (track 1; also a 2019-05-31 single) had sports sync success. "Shine" is **track 13 of the album** (none — MusicBrainz), later released as a "Shine (Live In Studio)" single (2020-01-17) — not a standalone studio single from 2018. The album was released via Atlantic Records.

## The Title — the Flood

[P] **"Subject to Flooding" names the biblical Flood (Genesis 6–9) — an extinction event.** The debut is titled for the deluge: humanity *subject to* (liable to, under threat of) annihilation and judgment. This frames the record as the opening of the **machine-vs-human** thread ([[Machine vs Human]], `CONCEPT-MachineVsHuman`): the Flood is the stake — what is at risk when the human is overwhelmed. The modern deluge is digital (screens, surveillance, optimization), and the album's anchor interlude ([[Superhuman (Interlude)]], Chaplin's *Great Dictator* speech) states the human-vs-machine tension at the source. Flood mythology is inherited substrate (Genesis, Gilgamesh); the biblical/extinction reading of the title is [P].
