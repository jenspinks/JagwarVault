## Grounded

Debut studio album. Produced with a "we, not me" collaborative ethos (Roy's public framing). Roy's stated theme: individualism, authenticity, removing masks. Breakthrough single: "Loser" (premiered Zane Lowe / Beats 1, 17.4M Spotify streams; released as a single 2019-09-13 per MusicBrainz). "Long Time Coming" (track 1; also a 2019-05-31 single) had sports sync success. "Shine" is **track 13 of the album** (none — MusicBrainz), later released as a "Shine (Live In Studio)" single (2020-01-17) — not a standalone studio single from 2018. The album was released via Atlantic Records.
