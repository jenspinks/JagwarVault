## What it is — `[G]` (observable)
A **silver flip-top (Zippo-style) lighter** standing upright on **weathered grey barn-wood planks** (vertical grain, several boards), cropped to the irregular torn-edge oval that frames every open-leaf capture, on black.

On the lighter's face is an **enamelled heraldic shield** in the USAF *organizational-emblem* style — a **blue curved-top shield with a silver/white border**, a **pale upper field**, and a **white stylized swept-wing aircraft / delta-and-star device** rising across it; beneath the shield is a **red-bordered banner scroll** carrying small institutional lettering.

**The scroll text is not legible at this resolution** — I did not read a motto off the object.
