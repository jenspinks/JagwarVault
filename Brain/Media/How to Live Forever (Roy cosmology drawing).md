## What it is — `[G]` (observable)
A single hand-drawn page, pencil, titled across the top **"How to Live Forever? ∞"** (the infinity glyph closes the title). It is a dense syncretic-cosmology sketch in Roy's hand — the same kind of page as the **"I'm 33" board**. Verified element-by-element against the local leaf image:

- **A "Hopscotch" ladder** (labeled) climbing **Earth** (boxed, bottom) → **1, 2, 3, 4 | 5, 6, 7 | 8, 9** → **"Heaven"** in a domed arch at the top. (Single squares and side-by-side pairs, exactly as a hopscotch court is drawn.)
- **"NChSh"** written to the left of the ladder.
- A **Lo Shu magic square**: `4 9 2 / 3 5 7 / 8 1 6` (every row/column/diagonal sums to 15).
- A played **tic-tac-toe** grid (X/O marks) above the square; a lone **"5"** and a small **snowflake/asterisk glyph** below it.
- **"Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil"** (text, left-center).
- A **"GOD" circle** — a circle with a center dot and a horizontal diameter, labeled *GOD*.
- A **vesica piscis** — two overlapping circles — below the GOD circle.
- Top-right cluster: **"Strange Theory"**, **"4 worlds"** (beside a small triangle/cone glyph), **"Genesis 1:26"**.
- Two physics formulas: **speed of sound ÷ (2× length of cylinder) = fundamental frequency of an open pipe**; and **1/(2× stringlength) · √(string tension / string mass) = fundamental frequency of a tensioned string**.
- **Eye-of-Horus fractions**, four little eyes: `¼+⅛=3/8`, `⅛+1/16=3/16`, `½+¼+⅛=7/8`, and the full eye `½+¼+⅛+1/16+1/32+1/64 = 63/64`.
- A **Fibonacci daisy** plant, branches/leaves counted `1 2 3 5 8 13`, labeled **"Ways of Growing."**
- **Continued-fraction identities** for **φ (golden ratio)**, **√2**, **√3**.
- Lower-right text: **"A realm close to Divine"** (a word scribbled out beside *Divine*); **"myths, symbols, dreams"**; and the **Eden four-rivers verse** — *"And a river went out of Eden to water the garden, and from thence it was parted, and became four heads"* (**Genesis 2:10**).

**The authorial content is the whole page** — a worked cosmology sketch, not a caption. But see §HoM code status: in the maze this page also functions as an **index of other rooms' codes**, which constrains how much of the *selection* of elements is "symbolic argument" vs. "puzzle scaffolding."

## The reading — tiered

### Core thesis — *forever = entering the Pattern, not never-dying* — `[W]`
The title asks **"How to Live Forever?"** and closes on **∞**; everything beneath it is a catalogue of **self-similar, recurring, or infinite structures** — a magic square that always sums to 15, fundamental frequencies, the Eye's converging fractions, Fibonacci growth, **continued fractions that literally never terminate**, the Edenic source-river that *parts into four and keeps flowing*. The page's implied answer is therefore **not** "never die" but **"align with the structures that recur"** — you persist by entering a pattern that outlasts any instance of it. The ∞ in the title and the non-terminating φ/√2/√3 fractions are the same idea twice: *forever* = *recursion*, not *duration*. This is the [[The Pattern|Pattern]] doctrine in worksheet form — "the single law that repeats at every scale" — and a direct sibling of the **"I'm 33" board** (same hand, same syncretic sources, same "how to live forever / upload" question).

### The hopscotch ladder — childhood game as ascent ladder — `[W]`
**Earth → 1–9 → Heaven**, drawn as a hopscotch court and labeled as one. A **nursery game carrying a metaphysical ladder** — very JT (the catalogue's "twisted adolescent" nursery-substrate). It rhymes with the board's own **Jacob's-Ladder / hopscotch Earth→Heaven** figure, which is likewise marked **"NChSh"** — so the motif is a *repeat across two of Roy's cosmology pages*, not a one-off. `[~]` on the Hebrew: **NChSh** = *Nachash* (serpent), whose gematria (358) equals *Mashiach* (Messiah) — the serpent/Messiah equivalence is a known Kabbalistic correspondence; cite the tradition, not Roy.
