## The reading — tiered

### The armour is a costume, and that is the whole point `[W]`
This file's Pass-1 note already routes the image to [[Brain/Concepts/The Protector]] as its *"biographical genesis"*, and that routing is right. The addition this read makes is the **tense**:

The Protector doctrine is *armour formed over the wound* — protection that outlives its necessity and becomes a prison ([[Sir Lucius]] as *"preservation operating beyond its necessary duration"*). What the photograph shows is that structure **before any of that is true**. The armour is **plastic**. It is **play**. The visor is **up**, so the face is visible, unguarded, looking away from the camera at something more interesting. The child is on a pony, being photographed by someone who loves him.

So the image is not the Protector; it is **the Protector's raw material, still innocent** — the same relation [[Ballerina Boy]] holds to the buried self. `[W]`: this rests on the photograph plus the vault's existing Protector/Ballerina-Boy architecture, not on any statement by Roy about this picture.

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
- ⚠ **The identity is asserted, not grounded.** Pass-1 and the [[Hall of Mirrors]] hub both call this *"young Roy"*. **I have found no source establishing that.** It is highly plausible (the corpus is demonstrably his family archive) but it is an inference from context, and a face is not a citation. **Held `[W]`, and do not write "Roy" into a public page from this photo alone.**
- **Knight costumes are universal.** Any reading that leans on the costume being *chosen* is weak: a parent bought it, a child wore it. The Protector read survives this because it does not require the costume to be meaningful in 1994, only for the *archivist in 2022* to have selected this photograph out of thousands.
- **Selection may not be authored either.** If the maze's photographs were bulk-scanned from a family album rather than curated one by one, "he put this in the maze" loses its force. **This is the load-bearing uncertainty for the whole corpus,** not just this page.
- **Falsifier:** evidence that the HoM photo set was ingested wholesale (a scanned album, a shared family drive) rather than selected would demote every "why this artifact" reading here to `[~]`.
