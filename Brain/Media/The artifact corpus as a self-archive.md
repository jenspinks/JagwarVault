# The artifact corpus as a self-archive

> **Jen, 2026-07-29, setting the frame for all artifact work:**
> *"These artifacts are the source code to the whole project. Every one of them should be considered a direct statement and invaluable in the interpretation of the project. It is an attempt to download his own consciousness, the same one that built the project, wrote the songs, and created the JT cosmos. We are not looking for codes. We are using the artifacts to understand the project as the keys that they are."*

This page exists because the vault's artifact work had been organised around **crackability**, and that organisation was destroying interpretive value. It is the synthesis layer: what the corpus *is*, taken as a whole. Per-artifact reads live beside it in `Brain/Media/`; codes and provenance stay in `Sources/Artifacts/`.

**The claim `[W]`:** this is not a themed art collection and not a puzzle inventory. It is **a life inventoried in the categories a person would use to reconstruct themselves** — where I come from, who I was, who my people are, what I made before, what I was working out, what I made, what I said out loud, what I never released. The corpus has the shape of **a self, indexed**.

**Why `[W]` and not `[G]`:** the strata are observable, but "organised as a person" is my reading of the arrangement. *(The curation premise itself is settled — §5.)*

### ★ Roy's own candidate taxonomies are on the board (added 2026-07-29)

The eight strata above are **my** analytic grid. The **"I'm 33" board carries Roy's own organising schemata**, and two of them map onto the archive directly (→ [[Board - The Artifact Overlap (crops, rooms, subjects)]]):
- **The Hero's-Journey wheel** (board §13, typed by Roy): **Ordinary World · Rabbit Hole · Guardian at The Gate · Belly of Whale · Rescue Father · Dragon · Water · Treasure · Ancestors · The Way Back · Master of Twin Worlds…** The archive populates these stations *in objects*: Ancestors (the aviation heirlooms), Rescue Father (Father Mirror, Dad's Clock), Dragon (the [[Brain/Media/The child knight (armour and pony)|child knight]]'s crowned-dragon armour), Rabbit Hole (Sir Rabbit), Water (the Flood set). **The wheel is the corpus's myth-index in Roy's own hand.** `[W]` — and precisely because it fits well, hold per-object assignments loosely ([[Brain/Concepts/The Pattern]]).
- **The Body-Part Stages ladder** (board §12): the ascent mapped somatically, 1 feet → 5 **heart** → 8–9 **forehead** → 10 **crown**. The corpus's marked-body images (the `+`-figure, the ROMA chest-pendant sketch, Roy's own tattoo placements) sit on these rungs.

**The honest formulation:** my strata answer *"what kind of thing is each object?"*; Roy's wheel answers *"what station of the journey does each object serve?"* They are compatible grids over one archive, and his is the one with authorial standing.

## 2. ★ The corpus is the upload doctrine performed, not described `[W]`

The vault already holds the upload cosmology as **grounded**: the Hall of Mirrors is publicly framed as a metaverse *"built out of Jagwar Twin's uploaded consciousness by SARAVI"*; the mint is **by body part**; there is an artifact literally titled **`COMPLETE UPLOAD RECIPE`** (code `INTERNAL GUIDE`) which assigns the senses the *wedjat* fractions — ½ smell, ¼ sight, ⅛ thought, 1/16 hearing, 1/32 taste, 1/64 touch — summing to **63/64**, a self that totals *less than one*, with **touch** as the missing part ([[Brain/Concepts/The Upload]], [[Sources/Artifacts/Board - The Source-Code]]).

Set that beside §1 and the two halves close on each other. The ARG **says** it is an uploaded consciousness; the artifact set **is** the contents of one, sorted into strata. **The maze is not a container for the upload metaphor. It is the upload, carried out with real objects.** `[W]`

Two consequences worth holding:
- **The missing 1/64 is touch**, and a corpus of photographs, scans and audio files is precisely a self with everything *but* touch. The upload recipe names its own deficiency, and the archive embodies it. `[~]` — elegant, and therefore to be watched (see [[Brain/Concepts/The Pattern]] on clean fits).
- The gating is thematically consistent rather than incidental: retrieval requires an inward search, which is the same instruction as **VITRIOL** (*"visit the interior of the earth… you will find the hidden stone"*, [[Brain/Concepts/The Rose Cross]] §VITRIOL). `[~]`

**Two structural facts fall out of this, both new:**

1. ★ **Some open leaves are *alias leaves*: the same artwork, a second code-string, still listed open.** Already flagged as a *code* hazard by Jen on 2026-06-28 (*"a cracked leaf is only a NEW room if its ARTIFACT isn't already in the catalog — verify per-artifact, not per-code-string"*). What is new is that it is also an **interpretive** hazard: an alias leaf reads as an unknown object and gets re-analysed from scratch, or worse, mis-named and lost.
2. ★ **The board is the artifact quarry at cell level.** That the board is the HoM's source-quarry was established; what these two leaves show is that **individual spreadsheet cells were exported as artifact artwork**. So when an artifact looks like an isolated esoteric fragment, **check the board first.**

**Method rule this earns:** before writing a new artifact read, check (a) the 149-artifact catalog by *subject*, not by leaf hash, (b) **the board**, and (c) **the drafts/findings files** — "not minted" does not mean "unanalysed" (the `+`-figure had a full 2026-07-08 colour-session reading this page's first draft missed). *(Negative results from 2026-07-29, recorded so they are not re-run: `3252cfd9` is **not** `ENLIGHTENED BEING` #60, `EVERYONE LOVES THE INTERNET` #109, or `THE EMPTY ROOM` #13 — not an alias of any minted artifact. The rooster `f112c376` and the child knight `99996623` have no minted counterpart by name — but the rooster carries a **double board-subject tie**: the EYE ALASKA poster's rooster/phoenix art + the egg→chicken aphorism. ## 4. What the strata say about the project `[W]`/`[~]`

- **The ancestry stratum is martial and mechanical; the family stratum is domestic and caring.** Aviation, war-planes, a test-pilot's lighter, masks and clocks on one side; a garden, a grandmother's song, a cow, a hospital photograph on the other. The catalogue's **heart/mind** split has a **genealogical shape** in the archive before it is ever a character. `[W]` — two well-populated strata, not a single artifact.
- **The juvenilia are preserved under a name that means undeliverable.** A *dead letter* is mail that can be neither delivered nor returned. The first band was called **Dead Letter Diaries**, and the archivist chose to scan that CD and nail it to a fence. Whatever else the corpus is doing, it is **keeping what did not arrive.** `[W]`
- **The working-notes stratum shows the cosmology being built, not presented.** Handwriting, arithmetic, crossings-out, a hopscotch ladder. This is the strongest available counter to the "retrospective coherence" hazard in [[CLAUDE]]: the esoterica are datable working documents, not back-fitted doctrine. `[G]` that the pages exist in his hand; `[W]` the inference.
- **The child appears twice, in two costumes.** [[Ballerina Boy]] is the buried expressive self; [[Brain/Media/The child knight (armour and pony)|the child knight]] is the armoured one. Both are in the archive. [[Brain/Concepts/The Protector]] is the relation between them. `[W]`

## 5. Counterarguments / limits
- ★ ~~**Curation is assumed, not demonstrated.**~~ ✅ **RESOLVED BY RULING (Jen, 2026-07-29): "it is curated. every picture is placed for a reason."** `[P]` — the project lead's stated position, which is exactly what the `[P]` tier exists for. The default flips: **"why this artifact" is a legitimate question for every object in the corpus**, and §1–§2 stand at `[W]` without the bulk-ingest discount. *(The old falsifier — evidence of wholesale ingestion — would still bite if it ever surfaced; it is retained below as a falsifier rather than as a live doubt.)*
- **Eight strata is my taxonomy.** The gallery's own realms (PHANTOMS, MONOLOGUE, VIDEO CONNECTIONS, …) are the author-side grouping. Mine is imposed for analysis; do not present it as his structure.
- **"A self, indexed" fits too cleanly.** [[Brain/Concepts/The Pattern]] warns precisely about frames that fit without friction. The honest position: the *upload framing is grounded* (the ARG says it outright), the *stratification is observable*, and only the **join between them** is mine.
- **Much of the content is inherited, not authored** ([[Inherited Substrate]]): a rooster oil painting, a Kush painting, a Coronation of the Virgin, heraldic dragons on a costume. A consciousness-archive containing other people's objects is still a real thing, but it weakens any "everything here is a statement by Roy" reading to "everything here was kept by Roy."
- **Falsifier:** evidence of wholesale ingestion (a scanned album, a bulk import), or a Roy statement that the artifact set was assembled by the CTHDRL team rather than by him, would demote §1–§2 from `[W]` to `[~]`.
