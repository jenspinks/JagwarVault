**No text anywhere on the image.** The authorial content is the figure and, specifically, **where its two marks sit.**

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
- **A cross on a forehead is heavily overdetermined.** Ash Wednesday ashes, a bindi position, a plus sign, a doodle. The `+`-as-Roy's-mark read leans on the tattoo gloss, and the gloss is about **his chest**, not a forehead. Moving a mark to a different body position may simply be a different mark. `[P]`
- **Authorship is unestablished `[?]`.** Nothing shows Roy drew this. The HoM contains inherited and third-party objects (an oil painting of a rooster; a Kush painting; a Coronation of the Virgin). This could be commissioned, fan-made, or bought art folded into the archive, in which case the "swap" is not authored at all. **This is the single question that most changes the page.**
- **The heart/mind swap may be a drawing convention, not a statement.** Foreheads take small marks well; chests take medallions well. Composition can produce the same arrangement with no doctrine behind it.
- **Falsifier:** any evidence that this is a purchased/fan illustration, or that the `+` here is a religious convention unconnected to Roy's tattoo, collapses the whole §find to a coincidence of placement.
