## What it is — `[G]` (observable)
A single-weight **white line drawing** (tattoo-flash / folk-icon idiom) set inside a dark panel over a field of **mirror-symmetric roses** (peach-orange + mauve-purple blooms, bilaterally doubled). Verified against the local leaf asset. Depicted, exactly:
- A **bald ovoid head** with two small ornament dots at the crown.
- **Three eyes:** two in normal position plus a larger almond **third eye centered on the forehead**, round pupil.
- A simple **nose**; **three short whisker / scarification strokes on each cheek**; a small **zig-zag (lightning-like) squiggle** running down the figure's right cheek (viewer's left).
- An open **mouth showing a row of teeth** — a jagged, faintly uneasy grin (not a serene/closed devotional mouth).
- Shoulders and upper torso reduced to bare outline, a few **dot-marks** at the collarbone / shoulders.
- A **chest emblem:** the **Jagwar Twin heart-monogram** (the heart-with-Λ "AØ" mark) inside a **disc/orb set on a small stand or base**, the disc half-ringed by a **beaded/dashed dial** (radiating ticks on its right side).

**The authorial content is the illustration itself** — no text/caption is written on the image. The only unambiguously JT-specific element is the **heart-monogram on the chest**; the face-style and rose field are a general mystical-flash idiom (see §Counterarguments).

### Core thesis — the watcher-mask *between* Mary and Lucius `[W]`
The drawing sits squarely in the JT **seeing / witnessing complex** (El Roi "the God who sees," the Watchers, the Jaguar's eye, the mirror-mission). But the third eye here does **not** read as a serene Ajna of pure higher sight: the mouth bares **teeth**, the cheeks carry **mask-marks**, the grin is **off** — closer to a **watcher-*mask*** than to a contemplative spiritual eye. `[W]` (a read of the actual marks, not a gloss).

That tension is the [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]] axis made into a single face. Being-seen can **heal** (true witnessing — being known as you are) or **consume** (spectacle / the algorithm / surveillance — being watched as content). The image holds both valences at once: it looks like the thing **doing the seeing** *and* a thing **braced against being seen**. So I read it as standing **between the two poles the vault already names** — `[W]`:
- **Mary / the Shekhinah pole — seeing-without-consuming** ([[Mary]]): the gaze that witnesses and restores. `[~]`
- **[[Sir Lucius]] pole — attention-as-control / surveillance**: the gaze that watches to capture. `[~]`

The **roses** soften the face toward **devotion / beauty / the heart**, but they do **not** disarm the watcher's edge — the teeth interrupt the garden. This is the recurrent JT move of **love and fear sharing one face**: an ancient seeing-mask inside a field of beauty. `[W]/[~]`

## Counterarguments / Alternate Reads
- **Third-eye + roses + mask-face is near-universal iconography.** The forehead third eye (Ajna), the rose tattoo, the white line-flash "mystic face," the heart-in-a-disc — these are **stock mystical-flash idiom**, not bespoke JT encoding. Per [[Inherited Substrate]] this is *participation in an ancient image*. The **only** demonstrably JT-specific element is the **chest heart-monogram**; strip it and this is a generic mystical sticker. So the *watcher-mask* and Mary/Lucius readings rest substantially on **inherited substrate + the vault's frame**, not on JT-unique signal. `[P]`
- **"Watcher-mask, not serene eye" leans on reading the teeth as menace.** An open mouth with teeth in folk line-art can be **neutral or even friendly**; the "uneasy grin / strange smile" is partly the viewer's projection. A flatter read: a benign mystic-face icon, no threat encoded. `[W]/[P]`
- **The Mary↔Lucius "between" is the vault's triangulation, imposed.** The image names neither figure; placing it *between* two poles is our cosmology applied to a single drawing. Held as a frame, not a claim about the artist. `[P]`
- **It's reveal-art in an emergent maze position.** Don't read "why this artifact / why here" as authored clue-structure; placement and the seeded-elsewhere code are not interpretive evidence.
- **Falsifier:** a Roy statement that this is a stock tattoo design / fan art / generic third-eye icon (chosen for look, not meaning) collapses the watcher-mask and Witnessing-vs-Surveillance apparatus to one inherited symbol + the JT logo.
