## Tiers

- `[G]` **"That said."** The disputed word (machines split I/That/It/They) is ear-resolved: the relative pronoun attaches the shame-verdict to the *something* — **the possessor speaks**, not the bare self, not the social chorus.
- `[W]` The song is the **confession of the compromise**, written from 33 and looking back: the external-sounding voice ("something got a hold of me"), the verdict ("never good enough on your own"), the persona-compliance instructions (talk / act / face / name / dress "like that"), the bargain ("I closed my eyes and compromised"), the death. The board's biographical cell is the prose twin: *"dropped by my record label... I think it shook my confidence. IDK / Maybe I shook my own confidence"* ([[Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)]] §14).
- **Register guard:** "the death of me" is the song's own metaphor for the death of the authentic self at the compromise threshold. **No biographical death claim**; the same guard applies to Soul Is A Star's "He took his life" (the Son of the released lyric, an archetype).
- `[?]` **Album naming.** The tempting extension ("the album *33* is named for this song") does not fold: the grounded naming source is Roy's age (*"Thirty-three is the age I wrote the album,"* the manifesto, `[G]`). A maze-only song, the album, and the board share the title-number because all three share the age referent; direction of naming undetermined.
