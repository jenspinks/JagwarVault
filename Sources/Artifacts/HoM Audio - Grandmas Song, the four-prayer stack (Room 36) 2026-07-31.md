# HoM Audio — Grandma's Song, the four-prayer stack (Room 36)

Room 36, code **`THE LIGHTNING FLASH`**, realm **"Unseen Things / Concealed Generations."** A collage of four voices, each a prayer in a different register, stacked around an answering-machine message.

## The four layers (as sung/heard, ear-reviewed)

**1 · The Great Invocation clause (0:00–0:03).**
> Restore the plan on Earth

- `[G]` **The text is the exact closing clause of Alice Bailey's Great Invocation** ("…Let Light and Love and Power restore the Plan on Earth") — materially present, not a thematic resemblance.
- `[G]` **It is performed by a woman and intentionally placed** at the artifact's opening, among the collage's other found/inserted voices. Not a Roy performance.
- `[W]` **The recording likely derives from or closely follows the Lucis Trust's own published performance** ("read by Chris", lucistrust.org). Jen's voice/cadence match + timing analysis: the **Adapted** recording is the closer candidate — its "restore the plan on earth" ≈ **2.1 s** vs Room 36's **2.62 s**, word-by-word pacing especially close after Room 36's longer-held "restore." (The Trust's page also carries a separately credited musical composition by Dora Stavrou.)
- `[?]` **The exact source recording and transformation** (slowed, pitched, processed, resung) remain open until a stronger sample-level match. Both Trust recordings are archived beside the working transcript.

**2 · A child's voice (~0:03):** *"That was so fun!"* — words confirmed; whose voice, open `[?]`.

**3 · Grandma's voicemail (0:11–0:30), ear-resolved:**
> This is Grandma. Are you okay?
> [Likely:] Lightning hit Venice.
> Venice looks good on the map.
> Got upgraded.
> Give me a call. Love you, sweetie. Hope everything's going good. Bye. Bye.

- `[P]` **Referent (Jen's identification, historically checkable):** the **July 27, 2014 Venice Beach lightning strike** — a rare summer storm; multiple strikes; one fatality. The middle lines read as Grandma relaying the news and the forecast map ("got upgraded" = a later watch/warning or forecast state).
- ⚠ **The lines do not date the call.** Grandma may have called that day, or during later regional weather while referring back to the deadly event. **Do not date the voicemail from these lines alone.**
- The event stays context for her call; **the victim is kept out of the mythology.**
- Roy's Venice geography is corroborated in his own voice: *"Hanging in Venice"* (the 2022-08-16 Listening Room Space, captured transcript).
- Machine note: the Parakeet cross-architecture check independently returned all three middle lines on the clean channel; Jen's ear is the resolving authority.

**4 · Roy's own plea (1:00–1:09), ear-recovered in full:**
> Floating in space
> Out there, if you're out there
> Just send me a message
> Just one little sign
> Did you see the light?
> Are you out there?

**5 · The chant (~1:09–1:44), ending "Amen. Amen."**
- ✅ **Confirmed by ear (Jen, 2026-07-31): the Lord's Prayer**, chanted in Aramaic ("Abwoon d'bwashmaya…" = *Our Father*). The transliteration in the working transcript is a normalized standard-text reading, not syllable-perfect.
