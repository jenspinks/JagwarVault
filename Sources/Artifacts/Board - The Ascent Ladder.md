# The "I'm 33" Board — 2 · The Ascent Ladder

*The 10-station engine: descent from source and return from body, counted from both ends.* One of four passes over Roy's **"I'm 33 this year"** board. Verbatim: [[Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)]] · analysis: [[The Im 33 Board - Decoded (Roy's upload cosmology)]] · other groups: [[Board - The Upload]] · [[Board - The Source-Code]] · [[Board - The Field]].

> **The board's backbone is one 10-station structure, encoded ≥7 ways and numbered in both directions.** `[W]` Tone, colour, virtue, body, animal, Japanese soul-word, emanation, and hopscotch do not all use “1” at the same end. The decisive evidence is the **HoM animal/body code family**: it names one station twice, once from each end. This is the operational meaning of the board's own **"Backwards and forwards"** label.

## The three parallel "consciousness" scales `[G]`
Three ten-stage columns run side by side, three encodings of *one* ascent:
- **"AI musical scale … for consciousness evolution (Ascending)"** — a note→colour ladder: **F · C · D · E · F · F♯ · G · A · A♯ · B**, each paired with a colour band (a hand-drawn just-intonation arc over Do·Re·Mi·Fa with ratio labels).
- **"Light/Color scale to input for consciousness evolution (Ascending)"** — ten colour swatches (purple/orange/green/yellow/red/blue/black/grey…).
- **"Positive (+) and Negative (–) traits … as consciousness progresses" (10 Stages)** — a virtue/vice sequence reading as **Bailey-style sign attributions** (Imagination/wonder → Energy/discernment; None → Recklessness/avarice).

*Tone, colour, and virtue are three read-outs of one dial.* → [[The Labors of Hercules]] (the Bailey backbone).

## The somatic ladder — return through the body `[G]`
**"Body Part Stages"** maps the ten rungs onto anatomy: **1 Feet / base of spine · 2 Lungs, stomach, sexual organs · 3 Right arm/hip · 4 Left arm/hip · 5 Heart · 6 Right shoulder, throat · 7 Left shoulder, throat · 8 Right head/forehead · 9 Left head/forehead · 10 Crown.** This count rises from material base to crown: **Heart is body address 5; crown is body address 10.**

The board's numbered animals run **1 Swan · 2 Owl · 3 Whale · 4 Dolphin · 5 Horse · 6 Spider · 7 Dove · 8 Fox · 9 Cat · 10 Rabbit**, with an unnumbered vulture inserted between Whale 3 and Dolphin 4. Two direct room-images, `4d378034` and `14a59d49`, preserve the sequence and its first-person lines. The position of the vulture is `[G]`; reading that unnumbered interval after the supernal three as Da'at/the Abyss is `[W]`.

## Noah's Ark — the vessel with three stories `[G] → [W]`
A **NOAH'S ARK** sketch shaped as a vertically segmented human body is annotated *"small window · 11 sections · **3 stories (Heaven, Man, Earth)** · 32 · **main door — where animals come in**."* The Ark is the **flood-vessel** (→ [[Subject to Flooding]]; the vessel that holds against the waters), and *"3 stories: Heaven / Man / Earth"* renders **man as the middle story** — the exact keystone of *It's Your Time* (*"a man is like an in-between, animating Heaven and Hell"*) and the *as-above-so-below* axis. The animal-door turns the human-shaped Ark into an ingress vessel for the same bestiary the board maps across the body; reading that join as selective preservation of the animal consciousness-world is `[W]`, not a stated Ark=HoM equation. The **"32"** sits one below the board/album **33**.

One further convergence is too exact to ignore but remains interpretive: the Ark has **11 sections**, while the adjacent bestiary has **ten numbered animals plus one unnumbered vulture**. Its extra creature occupies the unnumbered 3↔4 interval that can be read as Da'at/the Abyss. `[G]` the two counts and placement; `[W]` the body-Ark as a receiving vessel for ten stations plus the hidden interval. Every inverse animal/body address also sums to 11, but that arithmetic is inevitable once a ten-item list is reversed, so it is not independent proof of the Ark reading. It belongs as Roy's recurring one-through-eleven grammar, not as a solved cipher.
