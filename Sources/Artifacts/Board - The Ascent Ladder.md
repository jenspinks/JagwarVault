## The somatic ladder — the body *is* the Tree `[G]`

## Structural reading — the two ladders disagree on where the Heart sits `[~]`
The board encodes the ascent ≥6 ways, but two of them **don't line up.** The **somatic ladder** puts *"5 Heart"* dead-centre with the throat (utterance-gate) *above* it at 6–7. The **0–10 emanation list** speaks the word early (*"2. I speak the word"*) and only reaches the heart at *"6. mirror of the Soul and the heart."* So one ladder puts the mouth *after* the Heart; the other puts speech *before* it. Read structurally, this internal mismatch **is** the *"my mind must serve my heart"* tension drawn twice, two ways — the board arguing with itself about whether speech serves the heart or precedes it. *(Board content `[G]`; the Heart-Mind reading of the mismatch is `[~]` — the board never flags the ladders as conflicting.)* → [[Roy on the Heart-Mind Split]].
