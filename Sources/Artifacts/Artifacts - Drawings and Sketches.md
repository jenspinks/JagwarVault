# Artifacts — Drawings & Sketches

Roy's hand-drawn artifacts from the [[Hall of Mirrors - Artifact Gallery (catalog) 2026-06-25|discovered-artifact set]] (codes known): sketches, cosmological diagrams, portraits, and the coded creature-drawings. Grouped here rather than one page each; **tags overlap** — many of these are also `#cipher` or `#concept-code`, so they surface in those views too. Files live in `Media/images/hom-artifacts/complete/`. Hub: [[Media Evaluation - Artifacts hub]].

> **Reading note.** The angle / creature sketches were long flagged for the `[P]` angle-vs-angel "consonant–vowel" method — but on inspection they read as **childlike creature / angel drawings, not that method**, so the privacy flag eases. See [[The Angels and the Angles]].
