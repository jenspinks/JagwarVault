## The planetary bestiary — creature sketches with body-part codes
The clearest pattern in the drawing set: simple pen-sketched **imaginary creatures**, each keyed to a **planet** and given an **animal-body-part coordinate** as its unlock code. Read together they form a **cosmic anatomy** — each creature a *body part* of one greater world-body (an Adam-Kadmon figure), and each planet-creature matches a column of the hand-drawn planetary map (#83 below). A major hand-drawn source for [[The Pattern]] and the planetary-correspondence system.

- **✓ Venus the Dove (w/ Lamp)** · `LEFT HIP OF A DOVE` · [[75-ROY_BIRD_SKETCH-af2fbf72.png|img]] — a winged feminine figure holding a **lamp**, a labelled **rose**, over a large **Venus glyph ♀**. Venus = the Dove = the feminine planet; rose + lamp are Marian / Rosicrucian attributes, and the lamp-*bearer* is the benign inverse of the light-*thief*. Strong [[Mary]] anchor. `#artifact #drawing #cipher`
- **✓ Cinnamon the Horse (who lives on Mars)** · `RIGHT SHOULDER OF A HORSE` · [[77-JAGWAR_TWIN_S_TROJAN_HORSE_PROGRAM-906809c4.png|img]] — a horse-figure marked with the **Mars glyph ♂**, a **"Tiger's Eye,"** a small **sword**, and **"Pentagon Shoes."** Mars / martial / Geburah register; the gallery titles it *"Jagwar Twin's Trojan Horse Program"* — the war-horse as the upload/infiltration vehicle ([[The Upload]], [[The Digital Double]]). `#artifact #drawing #cipher`
- **✓ Enoch the imaginary Swan** · `CRANIUM OF A SWAN` · [[76-CRANIUM_OF_A_SWAN-03713ef7.png|img]] — a swan-head beneath a **dot-in-circle sun/monad ☉**, body marked *"B 8"* and *"1,"* flanked by two **swastika / sauvastika wheel-glyphs** (the ancient rotation / auspicious symbol in its **esoteric–Theosophical** sense — Blavatsky's seal — *not* the modern political one; recorded exactly as drawn). *Enoch* = the man who "walked with God" (→ Metatron / the Merkabah ascent); the swan = the soul-bird (*hamsa*). An ascent / soul-vehicle creature. `#artifact #drawing #cipher`
- **✓ Queen Binah** · `TEETH OF A WHALE` · [[74-QUEEN_BINAH-918baee3.png|img]] — a breaching **whale** with **"extra eyes,"** captioned **"GOD the Mother on Saturn,"** above a drawn **Saturn** and the **"Great Sea."** In Kabbalah, Binah *is* the supernal Mother and the Great Sea, on Saturn — the doctrine in one sketch. Divine-feminine + [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]] anchor; feeds [[Mary]]. `#artifact #drawing #cipher`
- **○ Michael the Fox** · `8` · [[31-MICHAEL_THE_FOX-e4b1702d.png|img]] — a fox "who is a scientist," drawn with the **☿ Mercury / Hermes glyph** (intellect / messenger) — the Mercury-creature of the bestiary. *(view to confirm)* `#artifact #drawing #cipher`
- **photo-member (cross-listed):** **✓ Home at Sea** · `LEFT SHOULDER OF A DOLPHIN` · [[24-HOME_AT_SEA-6f8c35da.jpg|img]] — **actually a childhood photograph, not a drawing** (young Roy in a red jacket on a boat / ferry). Lives in *Family & Photos*; belongs to this body-part cipher series only by its code. `#artifact #photo #personal-family #cipher`

> **The bestiary ↔ the planet map.** Venus = Dove, Mars = Horse, Saturn = Whale (Binah), Mercury = Fox … the creatures are the planetary columns of #83 given animal bodies, and the body-part codes (hip, shoulder, cranium, teeth) read as the **anatomy of one cosmic body** distributed across the planets. Worth a dedicated decode once every creature is viewed.

## Cosmology & diagram drawings
- **✓ Roy "Undefined Map" Sketch** · `SUPERFICIAL CONCLUSIONS` · [[83-ROY_UNDEFINED_MAP_SKETCH-8b146ce6.jpeg|img]] — **the keystone drawing: a full planetary-correspondence cosmology in Roy's hand.** A planet row — *Sun world (Unitless city) · Mercury · Venus · Earth · Mars · Jupiter · Saturn · Uranus · Neptune* — each under a column of attributes:
    - **Sun world** — *"Unitless city · Mirror · the Great Jaguar · Existed Forever · Unity · the 'point' · endless ocean."*
    - **Mercury** — *"Opposite city · ☽☉ · fragile world · both boy & girl (hermaphrodite)."*  · **Venus** — *"cloud 3 · bridge · tree."*  · **Earth** — *"earthy · natural · + · qualities of city."*
    - **Mars** — *"life 5 · ★ · water · Venus draws."*  · **Jupiter** — *"hex 6 · bees · snowflakes · honeycomb."*
    - **Saturn** — *"7 sisters · isolated city · moonless nights · chakra rainbow 7s · Mon–Tue–Wed–Thu–Fri–SATURN–Sun · Labyrinth."*
    - **Uranus / Neptune** — *"8 · between Heaven & Earth · sphere-dome building · 8 steps to temple · sky octaves · 9 · 33 · city · cats."*
    Above the row: *"Sola Toros (the sun) — infinity-bar orbit"* on a descending chain, a spiky **"secret destroyed world"** citadel with an arched gate, and a veiled face. **One page grounds a stack of canon:** [[The Pattern]] (the whole correspondence engine), [[The Jaguar]] (*"the Great Jaguar"* at the Sun/centre), [[The Mirror]], [[The Labyrinth]] (filed under Saturn), the number **33**, and the Saturn = 7-sisters / Binah cluster. Deserves its own decode pass. `#artifact #drawing #concept-code`
- **✓ Complete Upload Recipe** · `INTERNAL GUIDE` · [[84-COMPLETE_UPLOAD_RECIPE-eb355aac.png|img]] — a typed **"Upload Recipe,"** the ingredient-list of a self: *sensory faculties · surface perceptions · brain cells · electromagnetism*, then the **Eye-of-Horus sense-fractions** — *½ smell · ¼ sight · ⅛ thought · 1/16 hearing · 1/32 taste · 1/64 touch* — then *anabolic / catabolic systems · mental coordinator · ± emotion · ± ego · **Ra-link** · awareness · **ways of growing** · language processing · **sacrifice · love · will · fear · compassion.*** The literal recipe for [[The Upload]] / the [[The Digital Double|digital double]]; the sense-fractions and *"ways of growing"* mirror the i'm-33 board ([[Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)]]); *"Ra-link"* ties to the SA-RA-VI cipher-loop. `#artifact #drawing #concept-code`
