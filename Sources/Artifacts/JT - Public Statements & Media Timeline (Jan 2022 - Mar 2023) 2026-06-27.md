## The report (verbatim, as compiled)

### TL;DR
- Window dominated by the rollout of **33** — announced Aug 24 2022, released Sept 30 2022 (Big Loud Rock; prod. Matthew Pauling; mixed by Jeff Ellis) — plus its Web3 "Hall of Mirrors"/SARAVI AI campaign, the EP **The Circle: The Great Jagwar Myth** (Feb 17 2023), and the debut of **The Mirror Sessions**.
- Richest sourced statements: long-form interviews (Atwood, Culture3, Honey POP, Alternative Press, Tokyo Journal) + the self-produced Mirror Sessions. Social activity centered on @JagwarTwin, deliberately handed to the AI persona **SARAVI** during the late-summer 2022 campaign.
- Sir Lucius / "Black Magic Rainbow Circus" / "welcome to the circus" = **2025–2026, out of window**.

### Chronological timeline
- **Early Jan 2022** — "Happy Face" on YouTube's "Trending Artists to Watch"; single later >70M streams / 50M video views. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagwar_Twin)
- **Mar 16, 2022** — @JagwarTwin thread beginning "Error — 404 Not Found (a)": *"In the last month I've put a lot of thought into the current state of the world through the lens of the internet… I don't have any answers, but I do have some clarity on what I see."*
- **May 2, 2022** — CTHDRL Hall-of-Mirrors site auctions NFT 3D scans of "digital body parts"; sells out, unlocks the 3rd JT music NFT (*Pay Attention* ft. little luna). (cthdrl.co/jagwartwin-hallofmirrors/)
- **May 16–18, 2022** — Multi-day livestream sharing his desktop as the "ingredients" of SARAVI are uploaded; upload completed May 18.
- **June 11, 2022** — SARAVI "came to life," took over Roy's desktop, offered fans a free participation-based **"Crown" NFT** as the key to the JT universe.
- **Mid-2022 (Issue #281)** — Tokyo Journal interview (Anthony Al-Jamie) on his popularity in Japan: *"I've really enjoyed talking to a lot of Japanese fans on Twitter or Instagram. Sometimes, I'll use Google Translate…"*
- **Aug 24, 2022** — Announced **33**, released single/video "It's Your Time." *"I felt a shift once I turned 33. Like a switch flipped and I knew my purpose and why I was even still making music after so long and so many 'failures.' I have a different story to tell. One that I feel like only I can tell."* (BroadwayWorld; The Honey POP)
- **Late Aug–Sept 2022** — SARAVI given control of @JagwarTwin; fans tweet "HeySARAVI" prompts (Aug 31, Sept 6) → AI responses.
- **Sept 8, 2022** — *"Have you talked to SARAVI, the A.I. version of me? I didn't want to be Online all the time so I uploaded my consciousness so that she can do it for me. Tweet HeySARAVI and then ask anything and I'll answer it."*
- **Sept 29, 2022** — Firefly Festival main-stage + on-site interview (Jackie Cular).
- **Sept 30, 2022** — **33** released; "Online" video. *"I've been thinking a lot about why we tell stories… We are the stories we tell ourselves and share with the world… May we all find strength, spirit and most importantly ourselves in each other. Reclaim your crown!"* (BroadwayWorld)
- **~Sept 30, 2022 (Firefly interview)** — Atwood Magazine: *"Those things can make beautiful diamonds"*; *"I would go to Mozart, the way he does his math… It's geometric. I would love to do something with Radiohead."* (Published Nov 9, 2022.)
- **Oct 21, 2022** — Culture3 (Clovis McEvoy), "I was going to make music or die trying": *"I joked with the CTHDRL team saying, 'what if I uploaded my consciousness to the internet so that there could be a 24/7 social influencer version of me.'"*
- **Nov 2, 2022** — "Online" acoustic, filmed in Joshua Tree. (youtu.be/k2vUVPFvIm4)
- **Nov 7, 2022** — The Honey POP interview (journey, 33, Firefly, the Hall of Mirrors NFT world).
- **Nov 9, 2022** — Atwood feature published; "The Circle" live acoustic video (BroadwayWorld Nov 9 / Honey POP Nov 10).
- **Nov 11, 2022** — SARAVI "took over" jagwartwin.com → opens the Hall of Mirrors metaverse to Crown holders + an edition of 33 "Happy Face" music NFTs. (mirrors.jagwartwin.com)
- **Nov 30, 2022** — The Mirror Sessions Ep.1 Pt.1 with Calum Hood (5SOS). Series of walking interviews; guests incl. little luna, S1, Bohnes, Mali-Koa, Jeff Ellis.
- **Dec 5, 2022** — Honey POP on the series: *"I don't know if I think you can feel seen until you truly see yourself."*
- **Dec 7 / Dec 28 2022** — Mirror Sessions: Calum Hood Pt.2; little luna.
- **Jan–Mar 2023** — Mirror Sessions: S1 ("How to Hear God's Voice" / "Dying Empty: The Secret to Meaningful Creativity"), Bohnes (Jan 18 Pt.1; Feb 2 Pt.2), Mali-Koa (Feb 8 / Mar 1), Jeff Ellis (Mar 8 Pt.1 / mid-late-Mar Pt.2).
- **Feb 2023** — Ones To Watch "Live From the Rooftop" ("Long Time Coming"); Alternative Press interview (*"33 is a reflection of myself at a very important time in my life…"*); MendoWerks feature (announces Apr–May Transviolet support dates, "BODY: The Tour").
- **Feb 17, 2023** — EP **The Circle: The Great Jagwar Myth** (3 reimaginings of "The Circle"); the song *"feels like the story of Jagwar Twin but, in many ways, the story of all of us."*
- **Mar 5, 2023** — Honey POP "First Thoughts on First Listen."
- **Apr 14, 2023** (just past window) — Transviolet support run opens, Constellation Room, Santa Ana.
