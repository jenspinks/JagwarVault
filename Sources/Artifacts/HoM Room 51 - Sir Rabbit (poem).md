# Sir Rabbit (the poem) — HoM Room 51

The full text of the **Sir Rabbit** artifact ([[Sources/Artifacts/Hall of Mirrors - Artifact Gallery (catalog) 2026-06-25|HoM]] #48, *IMAGE GENESIS*, Room 51, unlock code `Q763`). Roy: *"this looks **sir rabbit pre-lucius**"* (Discord 2023-10-12). **★ Sir Rabbit is now a filed character: [[Brain/Characters/Sir Rabbit]]** — and his register is **the Roy-pole *seeker before the split*, NOT a proto-*Lucius*** (corrected 2026-06-29): in the grounded [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Good Time (1-08)|Good Time]] dream *Roy* is the rabbit and *Lucius* is the **second** rabbit who intercepts him, so *"pre-lucius"* = the self **before** Lucius. This poem is the single richest primary source for **both** the seeker-pole *and* the [[Sir Lucius]] origin.

## Text (verbatim, 4 pages)
> Once upon a time ago / A way away from now / There was a Rabbit with a home / In a garden full of sound
>
> Sir Rabbit took a walk one night / To count the stars he knew / The stars that once were you and I / Way back before they flew
>
> The moon was violet in the sky / Then something made a crack / A very strange Cat came bounding by / And said get on my back
>
> I'll take you to the moon for lunch / But time as got me running / So we must go let's leave at once / Sir Rabbit are you coming?
>
> Sir Rabbit climbed aboard the Cat to meet the Violet Moon / They flew out East forward and back / Till it was almost noon
>
> The world Sir Rabbit saw below / Was now a little dot / The Cat said you've been here you know / It seems that you forgot
>
> I don't remember any sight like this explained Sir Rabbit / The stardust falling up and down, the angels in the abbot
>
> They came upon The Violet Moon but it was a machine / With whirling gears and funny ears that always kept it clean
>
> The Cat said welcome to the moon / The wheels inside were turning / And they had lunch till half past two / With Seals and candles burning
>
> Just after they had finished lunch / A Fox lifted his head / He held his breath without a crunch / And came to them with bread
>
> I've heard of places far away / With knowledge of great kings / And gentlemen I know The Game / The Game of Many Things
>
> I wish to know this game dear Fox / Said Rabbit, can you teach me? / Indeed I can step in this box / You'll find the thing that beats Thee

> The Rabbit slightly hesitant then stepped into the box / And when he did the lid slammed shut and he yelled Mr Fox!
>
> The Fox explained that all was well / There's just one simple lock / The key is hidden in yourself / Just listen for the knock
>
> The Rabbit thought he had been tricked / The Fox was ever cunning / And so he sat. That's all he did / Till something he heard drumming
>
> A voice from on the other side / Said Rabbit you've been trapped / Now you must use your other mind / Dear Rabbit, it's been mapped
>
> If quietly you listen for the beat behind the wall / Then surely you wont miss it if you listen to its call
>
> The Rabbit waited silent in the center of the box / And then he heard it faintly like a heartbeat he forgot
>
> Over the spot he heard the sound / Sir Rabbit gently knocked / And then a door appeared and when he checked it was unlocked
>
> A Dove was waiting for him in an emerald garden tree / He held a lamp before him so Sir Rabbit he could see
>
> As much as I love company you must be on your way / Sir Rabbit said but I am lost I have no place to stay

> The Dove said there's no need to fear / A friend is on his way / But you must go Sir Rabbit dear / A shadow is at play
>
> Sir Rabbit went along his way then saw a shadow spinning / A Spider with a golden cape appeared and he was grinning
>
> Who are you Rabbit? Spider said / And where does You come from? / I do not know Sir Rabbit said / But I must be someone
>
> You are someone The Spider said, a twinkle in his eye / You are someone asleep in bed you're dreaming all the time
>
> I must wake up Sir Rabbit said! / I must wake up tonight! / The Spider laughed and said well good! / You're here at The Divide / I'm here to be your guide
>
> The Spider led him to an edge below the Great Divide / Jump off the edge the Spider said and through the sun you fly
>
> Sir Rabbit cried is this a test? I'm not ready to fly! / The Spider said thats up to you it's not for my to pry

> Now the Sun was glowing and the Door was open wide / Sir Rabbit said I'm going and I will not change my mind
>
> Sir Rabbit took a moment looked about the constellations / And felt his heartbeat growing looking down upon the nations
>
> With one great leap Sir Rabbit soared straight through the Mourning Sun / And in a flash of lighting he was back where he begun

## Why it matters (canon hooks — for folding)
- **The seeker-pole origin (recentered 2026-06-29).** Roy-confirmed "pre-lucius" — but **Sir Rabbit = the Roy-register *seeker*** (the searching, drifting self), **not** a proto-*Lucius*: in the grounded dream-origin Roy is the rabbit and Lucius is the *second* rabbit who intercepts him. The poem is the *authentic* initiation; the [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Good Time (1-08)|Good Time]] dream is the same arc **intercepted** by the wrong guide at the threshold. See [[Brain/Characters/Sir Rabbit]]. *(cf. the 2023 dream-rabbit, the March Hare.)*
- **The mind/heart split, pre-named.** *"use your **other mind**"* + *"the thing that beats Thee"* / *"a **heartbeat** he forgot"* = the heart/mind division that becomes [[Sir Lucius]] (mind divorced from heart). The trap's solution is the **heartbeat**, not the mind.
- **"The key is hidden in yourself / Just listen for the knock"** = Roy's *"u are the key"* / *"you are it"* (the MAGIK password, [[The Labyrinth|"you are the maze"]]). The maze-doctrine in seed form.
- **"it's been mapped"** — the Hall-of-Mirrors map named inside the origin poem.
- **The awakening.** *"you're **dreaming** all the time / I must **wake up**"* = the [[The Aquarium]] / waking-from-the-dream arc; *"The Divide" / "the Great Divide"* = the threshold/[[Duality]].
- **The Mourning/Morning Sun.** *"soared straight through the **Mourning Sun**"* — the mourning/**morning** pun → the **Morning Star → Lucifer → Lucius** as a *name/figure resonance* (the fallen light-bearer, the herald mistaken for the dawn). **Name-echo only, NOT the planet:** Venus/Netzach is Roy's by Roy's own word (2026-07-01), so the astronomical morning star is held strictly apart — see [[Kabbalah - The Tree of Life]] §emanation map. The Rabbit passes *through* the morning sun and returns "where he begun" (the [[The Circle|Circle]]/return).
- **The character menagerie = the cast in seed:** the **Fox** ("Mr Fox" — cf. the *Michael the Fox* artifact + Michael Morgan), the **Cat**, the **Dove** (lamp-bearer, an emerald garden — a Light-Bearer foil), the **Spider** (golden cape, grinning — a second trickster/guide). The Dove-with-a-lamp vs the later "shadow" sets up the light/shadow pair.
- **"The Game / The Game of Many Things"** — the ARG/maze named as *the Game* (cf. Roy *"the game is to reveal the game"*, HoM code for *Hopscotch*).
