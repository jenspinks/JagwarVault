## The Room 73 variant (ear-verified; absent from every other state)

Wise-one verse as sung in the memo:

> Till a wise one from a small place heard a sound under her wing
> On the inside of the inside, the song of the ring

*(The release reads: "'Til a wise one found a small cave where the Jagwars used to sing / And the wise one remembered she once held the string.")*

Coda, sung only in the memo:

> Oh, the circle will grow
> May we always remember the song that we sing
> Oh, the circle will grow
> May we always remember the song of the ring
> May we always remember the song of the ring

Ear-check note: the coda repeats **"ring"** throughout; the recording **never says "rain"** (a large-v3 model artifact, rejected by ear 2026-07-30). The memo's sung verse order is verse 1 → kings/wings verse → earth-changed/wise-one verse (timestamp-verified).

- `[G]` **The ring / O / circle title-axis**: the memo is titled *Song of the Ring*; Room 4 is titled *O* under code `ENSO` (the drawn circle); the release is *The Circle*. Three names for one object, one circle drawn three ways.
- `[G]` **Room 4 holds the released recording** (Jen's ear). The title/content tension is held `[?]`: either the room mislabels the master as a demo, or the released Circle **is** the 444-tuned demo take (444 = Roy's tuning practice, not a date).

Another Way II Heaven's own refrain, *"I see it different every time."*
