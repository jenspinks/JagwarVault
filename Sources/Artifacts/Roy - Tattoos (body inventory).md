## 0. The structural fact (the most important datum)

[G] **Every tattoo is inked on the right side of the body, then mirrored onto the left. One `+` sits in the center and spans both sides.** *(His own description.)*

The body is composed as a **mirror diagram with a central axis** — bilateral symmetry (right/left pillars) resolved by a single central mark. Read structurally:
- [W] A **Tree of Life / two-pillars** figure: right + left pillars, the `+` as the **middle pillar / Tiferet**.
- [W] An **"as above, so below / as within, so without"** object (the Kybalion Correspondence principle he transcribes on the [[Sources/Artifacts/Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)|"I'm 33" board]]) — the doctrine enacted on the flesh.
- [W] A **mirror** — tying the body to the Empty Mirror, "we are all mirrors," Paul's "glass darkly." The self built as a reflection around a center.

The tattoos are not a list — they are a **symmetrical map.**

## II. Readings (tiered)

### The cross-and-rose center → Rosicrucianism + the 4 Worlds + Tiferet
- [G] He states the `+` = Christ + the 4 worlds + the center.
- [W] The 4 worlds = the Kabbalistic Atziluth / Beriah / Yetzirah / Assiah. The center = **Tiferet** (the Sun/heart/Christ-center, the integration point of the Tree). Consistent with his VI/6 self-marking on the [[Sources/Artifacts/Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)|board]].
- [G/W] **ROSE ESOR = the Rosy Cross**, rendered as a mirror-word *(rose|esor)* wrapped around the `+`. Confirms **Rosicrucianism** from his own explanation, built as an inversion/palindrome (see [[Inversion]]). The rose gloss elsewhere: "the heart revealed, the union of opposites."

### "Fear functions like Prayer in Reverse" → the inversion engine
- [W] **The single cleanest grounding of the [[Inversion]] principle, and it is on his body.** Fear = inverted prayer = inverted faith — the mechanism the vault attributes to [[Sir Lucius]] (operate by inversion). Anchors to the inverted cross on Lucius's cheek. Fear is the reversed prayer; Lucius runs on it.

### The mirror / emptiness cluster → anatta, the Empty Mirror, the seeing-self
- [G] "The Empty Mirror" + "looking at yourself and seeing nothing."
- [W] No-self (anatta) / the Zen empty mirror / Paul's "glass darkly." Ties to "we are all mirrors," "ME" crossed out. The self seen in the mirror is *nothing* — the doctrine, not a mood. → [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]], mirror thread.

### The ego-erasure cluster → "it's not about me"
- [G] "ME" crossed out ("because it's not about me"); "In service to all, in service to the one"; "I'm not here / but I'm also here."
- [W] Ego-dissolution / the self in service to the One — matches "the instruments play me," the vessel doctrine, the collective-consciousness framing in the interviews.

### "KILL YOUR SELF" — handle with care
- [W, flagged unexplained] Within his stated system the coherent reading is **ego-death**: kill the *constructed/false* self (consistent with "ME" crossed out, "it's not about me," the empty mirror that shows *nothing*) — "die before you die." **He did not explain it**, and the refusal is itself notable. Log the ego-death reading as contextually-coherent; **build nothing heavier on it.** Do not read literally; do not sensationalize.

### Apophatic theology → the crossed-out God line
- [G/W] "What we say about god is always incomplete," then crossed out. The *via negativa*: even the statement about God's incompleteness is struck through — the crossing-out *performs* the apophasis.

### The wound → "I CAN'T FEEL"
- [W] The heart-numbness named plainly — the very condition the vault assigns to Lucius (mind divorced from heart). The wound is on the body; the catalogue is the attempt to feel again. → [[Roy on the Heart-Mind Split]].

### Gurdjieff on the body → "WAKE UP"
- [G/W] "WAKE UP" (back of hand) = Gurdjieff's "Man is asleep. He must wake up." The Loop is the sleep; breaking it is waking. → [[The Circle]] (remembrance/anamnesis), [[Sir Lucius]] (the loop). *(Gurdjieff now Roy's most-named public influence — [[Brain/Roy Voice/Roy on Influences]] §2025.)*

### The Loop on the body → the ouroboros
- [W] The simplified ouroboros (snake eating itself) = the Circle / Loop / eternal return, inked. Got "with Little Luna" (relational context, not load-bearing).

### Cosmic-origin → "Spaceship Eden"
- [~] Eden as a vehicle/craft — ties to the descent/cosmic-origin thread and the consciousness-upload frame ([[The Upload]]). Resonance; don't overbuild.

### Other anchors
- [G] "///" = Power of Three (his gloss). [~] the threefold — the Roy/Lucius/Ballerina-Boy tripartite; the trinity.
- [G] "Y" = "you've gotta ask the question" (Y / why).
- [G] "In Process," "Be," "Remember you're alive," "Trust your guts," "LISTEN" — the present-tense, embodied, anti-conceptual instructions; consistent with the meditation/somatic practice he describes.
