## Outgoing-code check — leaf, but the decode lands on real codes (validated 2026-06-26)
**~108 candidate codes** drawn from this artifact tested against `getMintProof` across every register — the full caption + subtitle (`ON THE MOUNTAIN OF THE SOUL` + variants), the `⊙`/`⊕` symbols, the archangel/Mercury/Hermes read, the Christmas-spider weaver/web/gold folklore, the SOL-demo lines, and (after the full decode) short thematic distillations of the deciphered *meaning* (`RESURRECTION`, `ASCENSION`, `MERCURY RISING`, `THE SUMMIT`, `THE WINGED MESSENGER`, `THOTH`, `MERCURIUS`, `SILVER AND GOLD`, etc.).

**Result — the artifact is a leaf, but the decode is sound.** The *only* two candidates that validate are **`REBIRTH`** and **`THE LIGHTNING FLASH`** — and they validate because they are **already-discovered codes** ([[REBIRTH]] = Family Portrait #149; `THE LIGHTNING FLASH` = Grandma's Song). Both are *exactly* the decoded meaning: **rebirth** (death→reborn) and **the lightning flash** (the mountaintop where man prays to lightning). So the meaning-decode method **works** — distill an artifact's meaning to its short thematic core and you land on the maze's real answer-vocabulary — it's just that this artifact's meaning-codes were already in the discovered set, and the **long literal phrases never hit** (the register is short distilled concept, e.g. `REBIRTH`, not the whole lyric). No *new* outgoing chain-link exists here.

**Methodological takeaway (folds to [[Sources/Artifacts/HoM - Master Brute-Force Candidates (2026-06-26)]]):** decipher meaning → distill to a **short thematic noun/phrase** → validate. This is the productive form of "use the vault to decipher," and it is distinct from the dead word-permutation approach. Confirms the broader pattern: codes are seeded in **artifact names (chain-links) + the board's hidden-text labels + short thematic distillations of meaning**, NOT in long captions/lyrics/aphorisms.
