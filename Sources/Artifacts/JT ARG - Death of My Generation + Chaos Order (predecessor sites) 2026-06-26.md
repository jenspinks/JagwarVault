# JT ARG — Death of My Generation + The Chaos Order (predecessor sites)

Two interactive Jagwar Twin microsites that **predate the Hall of Mirrors** and tie to specific singles. Both dead; recovered from Wayback. **Important scope note:** these are **NOT HoM clue-carriers** — their path slugs (`/happy-faces/<id>`, `/s/<code>`) are **user-generated share-states**, not seeded unlock codes. But they are **early instances of the same lore** (the Circus/consume satire; the you-are-it and concept-of-self doctrines), so they ground how far back those threads run.

## A. `deathofmygeneration.fun` — the Happy Face Experiment (Dec 2020) `[G]`
Tied to the **Happy Face** single. CDX: 2020-11-13 → 2025. Title: *"Jagwar Twin - Happy Face Experiment 🙃"*; meta: *"Are you content or are you content? Join the Happy Face Experiment and smile to hear the single before it hits DSPs. World gone mad, let's start the show!"*

**Verbatim copy:**
- *"Turn your speakers up / Enable your camera / Put on a happy Face / Start the Show"*
- *"Hey! Put on a Happy Face! Turn up the volume on your device, face the camera, smile, and let the show begin! There's something waiting for you on the other side!"*
- *"KEEP SMILING / THE SHOW IS ABOUT TO BEGIN / Are you content?"*
- *"Oh No! YOU WERE ONLY HAPPY FOR …"* (the smile-gate fail state)
- *"YOU ARE CONTENT! Congratulations! You were happy for the whole song and have unlocked the chance to acquire limited edition Happy Face merchandise…"*
- *"SOMEONE IS CONTENT! If you landed on this page, someone you know was, and is, content…"*
- *"BUY! BUY! BUY!"* · *"WHAT TO WEAR! WHAT TO LIKE! HOW TO BE!"*
- phone gag: *"(313)-251-4932" / "ARE YOU CONTENT? CALL NOW!"*

**⚑ Embedded manifesto (verbatim):** *"We each have the power of the universe within us, and the power to change the world within us. But first we must each know and change ourselves. We are burning the old ways down to the ground, and that is a painful process, but out of the fire something new is being birthed. This is the deat[h of my generation]…"*

**Canon ties:** grounds [[The Manifesto Cards]] / [[The Circus]] / [[Brain/Songs/Singles & EPs/Happy Face]] back to **Dec 2020** — the "Are you content?" triple pun, the consume-satire ("WHAT TO WEAR / LIKE / HOW TO BE" = the exact copy Roy paraphrases in the culture3 interview), and the **"burn the old ways down… something new is birthed"** manifesto (the death/rebirth engine). No HoM codes; asset host `happy-face.cdn.prismic.io`; outbound only to merch / `jgwrtwn.lnk.to/HappyFaceAW`.

## B. `thechaosorder.academy` — Down to You (2021) `[G]`
Tied to the **Down to You** single. CDX: 2021-04-21 → 2025. Title: *"Jagwar Twin - Down to You"*; meta: *"What is it that you seek?"* (= the HoM `DOWN TO YOU → "WHAT IS IT THAT YOU SEEK?"` code, here in its native home).

**Verbatim lore:**
- *"To change the world one must first change the concept of the world. To do that one must change the concept of one's self which makes one see the world as they do."*
- *"This is the path you chose but there are others you could have taken."*
- *"Any way that you go / It all comes down to you."*
- *"YOUR JOURNEY HAS ENDED BUT IT HAS ALSO JUST BEGUN / LISTEN NOW"*
- *"The universe is ever expanding and we as humans can have any experience we desire on this earth so long as we first imagine it and second take action to achieve it. No one else can be IT, you must be IT for your own unique journey."*
- *"STREAM DOWN TO YOU"*

**⚑ Canon FLAGS (folds pending Jen):**
1. **"You must be IT" (2021) PREDATES the MAGIK "you are it" (2025)** — the you-are-it doctrine is now grounded **four years earlier** than the MAGIK ARG. → [[Brain/Concepts/The Circle]] / the you-are-it thread ([[Sources/Quotes/JT MAGIK ARG - middle-of-the-maze (you are it) 2025-05]]).
2. **"change the concept of one's self"** = **Neville Goddard's "concept of self"** doctrine — grounds the open **Neville Goddard lead** from the visual-canon pass to a 2021 primary anchor. → [[The Pattern]] / [[Roy on Influences]].
3. The branching *"the path you chose but there are others"* = an early instance of the **two-paths / left-right** maze logic that HoM later formalizes.
- Path slugs `/s/<20-char>` = saved individual journey share-states (not codes). Imagery: `person.png`, `person-flame.png`.
