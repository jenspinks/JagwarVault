## Canon hooks
- **"I'm always with you / No matter what you choose"** — the shadow that never leaves → [[Sir Lucius]] / [[The Dweller on the Threshold]] (the "always with you" register).
- **"turn back the stone / From the inside"** — resurrection (the rolled-back stone) done *from the inside* = the Circle's **"inside of the inside"** (literally this artifact's unlock code) → [[The Circle]].
- **"Now it's all symbols"** — the esoteric turn (the maze, [[The Pattern]]); life rewritten as cipher.
- **"The edge of a knife"** — the knife-edge between living and dying (*"I wana live / I wana die"*); the precarious path. Cf. the [[Sources/Artifacts/Roy - I'm 33 this year (spreadsheet)|board]]'s tightrope/threshold motifs.
- **"Call you my mistress / wife / witness"** — the feminine addressed three ways (muse / [[Mary]]-class? · cf. *witness* = [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]]).
- **The millefleurs tapestry** (verdure with rabbits) — medieval *Unicorn-Tapestry* aesthetic; the **rabbit** foliage rhymes with [[Sir Lucius|Sir Rabbit]].
