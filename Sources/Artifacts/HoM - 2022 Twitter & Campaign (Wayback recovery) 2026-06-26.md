The live record is **gone** — Roy deleted most 2022 @jagwartwin posts and the campaign sites are dead — but the **2022 social/campaign layer survives on the Wayback Machine.** Method (reusable, §E): the Wayback `id_` endpoint over legacy (pre-React) Twitter snapshots returns the **full tweet text inside the `<title>` tag**; ~982 unique 2022 @JagwarTwin tweets were recovered this way, dated by decoding the snowflake ID.
