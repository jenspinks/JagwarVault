# Hall of Mirrors — Clue Origins (provenance index)

**The alignment Jen has wanted from the start:** the codes are the *answers*; this index records **where Roy seeded each one.** The source is the community **Deep Dive board**, whose author annotated many codes with the medium they came from — `(Tweet)`, `(Lyrics)`, `(Spreadsheet)`, `(NFT name)`, `(Tattoo)`, `(Waste bin)`, `(Song)`. Extracting that taxonomy + the gallery's discoverer/date data gives a first **clue→origin map.**

**Tier discipline:** the provenance tag is the board author's attribution = **community finding-aid `[W]`** (not Roy-confirmed). The code, discoverer, and unlock date are **gallery `[G]`**. **Recovering the actual artifact (e.g. pulling the real archived tweet) promotes that origin `[W]→[G]`** — that's what the web-recovery queue (§6) is for.
