# Roy / @JagwarTwin — YouTube Short caption: "I became a hammer"

A **Roy-voice aphorism** on the official Jagwar Twin YouTube channel, inverting a Japanese proverb: the world hammers down whatever sticks out, so *become the hammer.* Read within the work, this is a **compact origin-story for the Lucius formation** — the vulnerable self hardening into the instrument of force. The caption sits under the title **"Your weirdness is the sunshine,"** which is the album's *blessing* register — so the one artifact carries the Roy/Lucius split on its face. Surfaced from the feed mirror (where the text had sat un-mined) and re-verified live, 2026-07-15.

  - *Reference (framework, not a JT claim):* the source proverb is **出る杭は打たれる** (*deru kui wa utareru*), "the stake/nail that sticks out gets hammered down." Roy keeps the proverb intact and adds the **turn**: not the nail, the hammer.

## The reading — the Lucius formation in one line `[W]`

The nail→hammer turn is **Roy's own stated Lucius doctrine as a formation story**. Roy: *"what we fight we become, and we become what we fight"* ([[Brain/Characters/Sir Lucius]] §Integration, Discord 2024-11-15 `[G]`). The nail, threatened with being hammered down, **becomes the hammer** — the thing it feared. That maps directly onto the brain's `[P]` nature-of-Lucius: *"action spurred by fear and pain,"* *"ego trying to protect what is vulnerable, unknowingly destroying the very vulnerability he holds dear."* The hammer is a **Geburah/Mars** instrument (severity, force — "his cutting edge"); the wound grows the armor, the armor becomes the weapon ([[The Protector]]).

**Tier:** the *text* is `[G]` (Roy said it, verified twice). The *Lucius reading* is `[W]` — Roy tags it `#weirdness` and titles it with the blessing line, so he does **not** say "this is Lucius." Held as a strong read, not a verdict.

## The superposition — blessing-title over shadow-body `[W]`

The title **"Your weirdness is the sunshine"** is, in the brain, the *integration* voice: the angel/blessing line Roy speaks **back to Lucius** in the EP4 Spoon exchange (Lucius: *"I'm your weirdness"* → Roy: *"Your weirdness is the sunshine"*), and the seed of the [[Brain/Songs/Lucius Lullaby (2026)/Weirdness|Weirdness]] song's pronoun turn (*"your* weirdness…" → *"my* weirdness is my sunshine"). So this short stacks the two poles on one artifact:

- **Roy's road** — *"my weirdness is my sunshine"*: own the light, integrate the odd thing.
- **Lucius's road** — *"I became a hammer"*: weaponize the odd thing, hammer first so as not to be hammered.

Same wound (being the one who sticks out), two exits. The caption's force is that it names the **shadow exit** in Roy's own cheerful promo voice — empowerment and its curdled twin in one 17-second post. **Guard (do not flatten):** the empowerment reading is genuinely present and is Roy's surface frame; the Lucius reading is the underside, not a replacement. Both held (cf. [[feedback_lucius_register]]).
