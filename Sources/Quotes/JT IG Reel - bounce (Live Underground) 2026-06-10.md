### Beth = house ↔ "tear the roof off" (Jen 2026-06-27; tiered after adversarial review)

1. **Beth (ב) literally means "house."**

Read through **Beth = house = heart**, it states the project's foundational tenet outright: **"HE MADE THE LETTER BETH KING OVER WISDOM"** = the **house-letter (the heart) enthroned as *king over* Wisdom (the mind)** → **[[Roy on the Heart-Mind Split|the heart must rule the mind]]**. **`[W]` Strong interpretation — the lyric reactivates Beth, turning the house from *container* into *opening*:**
