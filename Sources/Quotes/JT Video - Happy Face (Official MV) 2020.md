### Textual notes (not interpretation)
- **"BROUGHT TO YOU BUY"** — the **misspelling is on the card** ("BUY," not "BY"); transcribe verbatim. This is the **consumerist origin** of the 2026 *Live Underground* cards' *"BROUGHT TO YOU BY JAGWAR TWIN"* ([[Sources/Quotes/JT IG Reel - bounce (Live Underground) 2026-06-10]], [[Sources/Quotes/JT IG Reel - welcome to the circus (Live Underground) 2026-06-26]]).
- *(TICK)…(TOCK)* reproduces the catalogue's parenthetical background-echo device in typography (as Bounce did with *(bounce)…(bounce)*, WTTC with *(welcome)…(welcome)*).
- The **®** in the seal and **"CALL NOW"** frame the release as a **TV-infomercial product**.
