# AltPress 2023 — Jagwar Twin on *33* (Source)

Verbatim quote extracts (not the full article). **No interpretation here** — analysis lives in Brain/ and Essays/.

## Source
- AltPress, ~April 2023. "Jagwar Twin on '33': 'I still find myself in these songs, and I hope others do, too.'" altpress.com.

## Quotes
- On *The Circle*: *"[It] seemed to write itself… The mind had very little to do with it, and I just got out of the way."*
- On the reimagined versions of *The Circle*: *"I hope with the reimagined 'angles' of the song that everyone can find their own 'home' in one of the spaces."*

## Notes
- The "download / it wrote itself / got out of the way" framing **persists into the *33* era** — continuous with the 2018 Atwood "download" account ([[Roy on Process]]).
- Roy's own use of **"angles"** = perspectives/spaces a song is heard in — the public anchor for the *angles* half of [[The Angels and the Angles]]. (The *angles-as-spaces* sense is his and public; the angel/angle *method* remains unconfirmed in his words.)
