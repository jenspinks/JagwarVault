# Roy English / Jagwar Twin — culture3 interview (2022)

**The most complete artist-statement we hold on the 33-era project.** Clovis McEvoy, *culture3.xyz*, ~Oct 2022. Live page dead (404); recovered in full from Wayback ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20221021182348id_/https://www.culture3.xyz/posts/jagwar-twin)). Title: *"There was never a backup plan, I was going to make music or die trying."* Roy's words quoted verbatim; journalist context paraphrased.

## Biography
- Orange County; self-described "intense" child: **"I had so many thoughts and feelings as a young kid. I often didn't know how to express myself in language."**
- Drawn to the **folk and Celtic music his mother played around the house** (→ [[Roy on Influences]] mother-channel). At **13**, found the venue **Chain Reaction** (punk shows): **"I knew I was going to make music forever. There was never a backup plan. I felt like I was going to make music or die trying."** *(`CHAIN REACTION` is also a HoM code — Yellow & Elephant 2, room 29.)*
- Producer/songwriter credits for **Lana Del Rey and Alesso**; debut LP **Subject To Flooding (2018)**.

## The COVID re-evaluation
- **"I really used that time to re-evaluate why I was making music in the first place. Some of that childhood intensity was manifesting in the kind of music I was making. There was a part of me that was making music out of a need to survive and a deep desire for external validation."**
- Daily meditation + reading; ran a **free weekly guided-meditation space** for fans — **"over 1,000 weekly registered users and so many transformational experiences"** — which he credits as the seed of **Happy Face** (Dec 2020, 100M+ plays).

## Happy Face — the interactive
- **"I wanted to experiment with how a fan could really immerse themselves psychologically and physiologically in a song."** The facial-recognition smile-gate against the satirical consume-commands (Hollywood "telling you what to wear and what to like and how to be"): **"creates a very interesting effect on the mind. It really gets to the heart of the question the song asks."**
- On the studio CTHDRL: **"I desire to tell stories. The mediums change through the ages, but the stories are the same. It all felt naturally aligned."**

## SARAVI — the digital double ⚑
- Origin = a **joke** to CTHDRL: **"I joked with the CTHDRL team saying, 'what if I uploaded my consciousness to the internet so that there could be a 24/7 social influencer version of me.'"**
- McEvoy: SARAVI = *"the digital doppelganger of Jagwar Twin,"* a *"creative take on an AI 'chatbot,'"* **trained over months on Roy's own responses**, and **given control of the official @jagwartwin Twitter account in August** [2022] as part of the 33 campaign.
- Roy's intent: **"Hopefully, as SARAVI grows, she can make more fans think about what a 'self' looks like in this digital age."**

## Web3 / FWB / the Hall of Mirrors
- **Friends With Benefits (FWB)** = the on-ramp; first skeptical ("naively utopian"), then: **"the idea of decentralisation and onchain records felt like it opened the door for rich storytelling in ways that could bring fans much deeper into the lore of the music — and allow fans to participate in a community where they could win financially by doing what they would do anyway."**
- HoM (McEvoy's description): enter by connecting a wallet + receiving a **crown NFT**; a "rabbit hole" of red, 1/1 NFT artifacts, grainy VHS, **unreleased demos**, 3D wireframe scans of objects and bodies; solving puzzles unlocks rooms; the first unlocker is recorded **on-chain**, but the room then opens **to all** (matches Jen's emergent-map mechanic).

## 33 — the album thesis ⚑
- **"We are more connected to each other than ever, but somehow many of us feel more disconnected from ourselves. We are the stories we tell, and I wonder if we have forgotten who we are. On 33 I am asking: what will our human story be?"**
- **"Every civilisation has its myths and tales. Those stories shape the way humans of that age live their lives and orient themselves in the world. What are the stories we tell in the current digital age?"**

## "The Song of The Sun & The Moon" — Sound.xyz genesis drop ⚑ (flag → SOL?)
- A previously-unreleased track, **genesis drop on Sound.xyz** (sold out in under a minute). Written in **solitude in the woods of Ithaca, New York**, just after being **dropped from a major label**: **"I had just been dropped from a major record label and had been largely unsuccessful in terms of making a living as an artist, but I felt a peace about it all in that moment. I was proud of myself."**
- ⚑ **Possible [[SOL]] grounding** — the vault's SOL (the Sun-and-Moon / Song-of-Love keystone) may BE this released 2022 track. **Flagged, not folded** — SOL is load-bearing; needs Jen's call before equating.

## The duality — the keystone line
- **"To be able to share deeply personal stories and memories and connect with others, you need to be able to connect with yourself. The only way I know to do that is to disconnect and be with myself. Disconnecting allows you to zoom out and get perspective on what is really going on."**
- McEvoy frames a **"warring desire… to connect with fans and share intimacy, but also to stay one step removed"** — *"a natural duality which he embraced long ago."* → [[Source vs Reflection]] / [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]].
- Closing (Roy): **"Whatever technologies we create will be created in our own image. It is up to us how we will use it — but I do have faith in humanity to use it, unselfishly, to build a better world."**
