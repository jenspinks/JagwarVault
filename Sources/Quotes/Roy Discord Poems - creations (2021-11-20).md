## Poem 1 — "Myself at 33 saying…" `[G]`
**Permalink (cite):** https://discord.com/channels/705391219915882516/709809067090116608/911723457807515648

```text
Myself at 33 saying
Don’t give up
Don’t give up
Don’t give up the hope
Don’t give up
Don’t give up
Don’t give up, you’re close 
My eyes were closed for so long
Had a strong veil upon me
A Darkness from my fathers Side
From long before he earthed 
You’re never gunna make it
Spoke the Shadow to the Light
You’re never gunna make it
Make it in this world alive
Many men have died for this
And you will not survive 
But if you’re gunna try for this
Then ask and you will find
Knock knock on the door
Is anybody home?
Knock knock knock on the door
It’s someone that you know
Knock knock on the door
Is anybody home?
Knock knock knock on the door
Its me you wanna know 
33 and in a dream I made a long the way
The only one of many 
In the universal Game
The patterns in the pattern
Pay attention, look around 
It’s never you, it’s always you.
Which difference have you found?
People, places, tiny things
Like Alice in the book
People always show you who you are 
But will you look?
It’s time the code was taught to you 
The people need a chance
But maybe you’re not ready just to give it up and dance
It’s They it’s Them, it’s all pretend
Like chem trails in my city 
The government the Gala Ball
The ones who keep on winning 
A stars someone that tells a truth 
For better or for worse 
It’s gravity it’s radiance 
Like betting on your horse
MJ knew that JFK 
Was giving up the game
So what’s the game you wanna play?
I’ll meet you on the way
```

**Load-bearing lines (annotation targets):**
- *"A Darkness from my fathers Side / From long before he earthed"* — the **inherited father-shadow** `[W]`. Root for the *Soul Is A Star* father/son transmission, the jeans/genes inheritance bridge, and **Lucius-as-inherited-shadow** → [[Soul Is A Star]], [[Sir Lucius]].
- *"You're never gunna make it / Spoke the Shadow to the Light"* — the **shadow-voice as a speaking adversary**, 3 years before the named figure. `[G]` text · `[W]` reading. Pairs with the 2025-08-16 Lucius monologue (see [[Sir Lucius]] §Grounding) → [[Sir Lucius]].
- *"It's never you, it's always you. / Which difference have you found?"* + *"People always show you who you are / But will you look?"* — the **mirror doctrine** `[G]` text → [[The Empty Mirror]].
- *"A stars someone that tells a truth / For better or for worse"* — the **star/truth** complex → [[Soul Is A Star]] + the truth-motif.
- *"The patterns in the pattern / Pay attention, look around"* — → [[Pay Attention (feat. little luna)]] (the *33* attention-disease arc).
- *"33 and in a dream… / In the universal Game"* — the **Game** + the *33* frame, pre-album.

**Recorded present-in-text, no interpretive load `[?]`:** the poem also contains conspiracy-adjacent lines — *"Like chem trails in my city,"* *"The government the Gala Ball,"* and *"MJ knew that JFK / Was giving up the game."* Logged for completeness only; **do not elevate or build readings on these.**

## Poem 2 — "Sunlight / Shining down…" `[G]`
**Permalink (cite):** https://discord.com/channels/705391219915882516/709809067090116608/911723480297402438

```text
Sunlight
Shining down
On my face
Through the night
Sunlight
Shunting down
What’s my name?
Who am I?
I’m the spider
I’m the spider
I put my name on that headstone
When David marked my grave
And Solomon and Krishna knew the nature of my game
The clue I give is “nature”
There’s answers in a name
But nothing that you call a thing will help you find it’s way
The Way, 
The Way Is
Through your head
A river 
Let it flow
The Way,
The Way Is
In your bed
Just listen 
Let it go
Let it go
Let it go
Let me know
When you find it
Let it go
Let it go
Then you’ll know
WHO designed it
You’re the architect 
Imagination comes
In many forms
You’re the architect 
So keep on thinking up 
There’s so much more
```

**Load-bearing lines (annotation targets):**
- *"I'm the spider / I put my name on that headstone / When David marked my grave / And Solomon and Krishna knew the nature of my game"* — the **trans-incarnational "I AM" / single identity across figures** `[W]`. Connects to the "Jenny is Brandon" cipher (Open Questions) and the El Roi name-doctrine.
- *"The clue I give is 'nature' / There's answers in a name … nothing that you call a thing will help you find it's way"* — **name-as-cipher** `[G]` text. Same engine as the Hall-of-Mirrors *"name vs. what something is called"* thread (Roy, 2022-04-19) and "Jenny is Brandon" → El Roi / name-doctrine (see [[Sir Lucius]] §El Roi cluster).
- *"The Way, The Way Is / Through your head / A river / Let it flow … Let it go"* — river / let-go → Heart/Mind material.
- *"You're the architect / Imagination comes / In many forms … So keep on thinking up"* — **creation-by-imagination / Neville-Goddard register** `[W]`. Corroborated by Roy's repeated Neville Goddard shares (2021-12-20 *"MIND to MATTER"*; 2023-11-01 *"The Art of Dying"*) → influences/reading.
