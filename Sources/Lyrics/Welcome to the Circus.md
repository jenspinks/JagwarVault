## Structural Notes

(Form-level observations only. No meaning claims.)

- **Form:** Intro – Verse 1 – Pre-Chorus – Chorus 1 – Chorus 2 – Verse 2 / Bridge – Pre-Chorus – Chorus 1 – Chorus 2 – Outro
- **Length:** 2:30 — short by current pop standards
- **Verse 1 vs. Verse 2:** sharply different in voice. Verse 1 is *experiential / dissociative* (first-person describing what's happening to me — teeth falling, screaming with no sound, lost in a maze). Verse 2 is *operational / performative* (first-person describing what I'm doing as the operator — juggling, painting face, running the show, asking after the show).
- **Refrain:** *"Welcome to the circus"* repeats 11 times across the song — including 4 times in the Outro alone. The single most repeated phrase by a wide margin.
- **Chorus structure:** Two-part chorus. Chorus 1 (Step right up / Chew you up) is the imperative-and-threat. Chorus 2 (Take your seat / I'm the one) is the audience-instruction-and-self-designation.
- **Pre-Chorus shift:** *"'round and 'round, 'round"* in the first instance becomes *"'round and 'round"* in the second (one less *'round*). Possibly a transcription artifact, possibly a deliberate truncation. User to verify.
- **Bridge climax:** *"They love it when you lose control / Lose control"* — the song's emotional peak before returning to the choruses.
- **Address shifts:** the song moves between *"my"* (interior — Verse 1), *"you"* (audience — Pre-Chorus, Chorus 1, Bridge), *"I"* (operator — Chorus 2, Verse 2), *"they"* (the impersonal audience — Chorus 2, Bridge). Multiple subject positions across a short song.
