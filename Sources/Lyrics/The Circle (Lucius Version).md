## Content (Jen, by ear / listening party, 2026-06-06)
Track 1, Lucius Lullaby. **NOT instrumental** (corrects the prior "instrumental" label — same error class as [[SOL]]).

- The keystone is **reduced to sound like it is playing from a music box, with a little violin.**
- **One looped line:**

> **"It's a beautiful life, it's a beautiful life, I'm dreaming."**

- This line is **NOT** from the *33* original [[The Circle]] — it is the **refrain of [[Sources/Lyrics/Lucius Lullaby|Lucius Lullaby]] (track 2)** (Apple-Music-verified there: *"It's a beautiful life / And I'm feeling alright / It's a beautiful life / I'm dreaming"*). So the track wears the **name** "The Circle" but carries the **Lullaby's** words.
- **Seam → track 2.** The music **flows seamlessly into [[Sources/Lyrics/Lucius Lullaby|Lucius Lullaby]]**; were it not for the track split there would be no pause. **Jen experienced it live as one song**, with the Circle (Lucius Version) functioning as the **intro/overture** to Lucius Lullaby.
- Vocal is **layered (Roy + possibly a child/children) aligned to sound like one voice unless you listen closely.** `[?]` who the child voice(s) are — Charlotte/Reagan Egner are credited BG ([[Sources/Credits/Lucius Lullaby]]).
