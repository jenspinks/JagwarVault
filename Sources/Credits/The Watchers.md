## ⚠ GROUNDED INTERPOLATION — Rockwell, "Somebody's Watching Me" (1984)
**Rockwell** (Kennedy William Gordon) is a **credited writer** on "The Watchers" → this is a **cleared, credited interpolation** of **"Somebody's Watching Me"** (1984, Motown), not an allusion. The interpolation surfaces verbatim as the song's hook: *"(I always feel like somebody's watching me)"* ([[Sources/Lyrics/The Watchers]]). **Same registry standing** as Bricusse + Newley on [[Sources/Lyrics/Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa)]] (Oompa-Loompa), Joni Mitchell on [[Sources/Lyrics/Soul Is A Star]], and Mercer on [[Sources/Lyrics/Dream - Dream]]. Frame as **participation in an older song** (mythic emergence / [[Inherited Substrate]]), not invented encoding.
- The original's hook was sung by **Michael Jackson** (BG/featured on Rockwell's record) — which lifts the MJ layer off the floor; the *intent* of the in-song MJ line (*"dancing like Michael Jackson"*) stays `[W]`.
- Interpolation-log formatting to confirm: writer = **Kennedy William Gordon ("Rockwell")**; publishing for "Somebody's Watching Me."
