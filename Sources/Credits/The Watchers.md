## ⚠ GROUNDED INTERPOLATION — Rockwell, "Somebody's Watching Me" (1984)
**Rockwell** (**Kennedy William Gordy** — **Berry Gordy's son**, Motown royalty) is a **credited writer** on "The Watchers" → this is a **cleared, credited interpolation** of **"Somebody's Watching Me"** (1984, Motown), not an allusion. The interpolation surfaces verbatim as the song's hook: *"(I always feel like somebody's watching me)"* ([[Sources/Lyrics/The Watchers]]). **Same registry standing** as Bricusse + Newley on [[Sources/Lyrics/Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa)]] (Oompa-Loompa), Joni Mitchell on [[Sources/Lyrics/Soul Is A Star]], and Mercer on [[Sources/Lyrics/Dream - Dream]]. Frame as **participation in an older song** (mythic emergence / [[Inherited Substrate]]), not invented encoding.
- **Source-song context (grounded):** see [[Sources/Reference/Rockwell — Somebody's Watching Me source context (Rolling Stone)]] — Rockwell **shunned the Gordy name** (fake British accent + stage name) to escape being a *nepo-copy* of his father; **wrote the song after a literal prayer** (*"God grant me the creativity…"*) with a **vow of testimony**; **Michael Jackson sang the hook** (uncredited; organic family ties). Paranoia/voyeurism is his signature ("King of Paranoia Rock"; follow-ups "Obscene Phone Caller," "Peeping Tom").
- **Why the choice resonates** (the interpolation imports pre-loaded themes; Roy's *intent* `[W]`): the source already braids **surveillance ↔ a watching/granting God** (its prayer-genesis → the El Roi / [[Witnessing vs Surveillance]] axis), and its author **lived the clone-of-the-father fear** Roy's lyric names (*"I'm afraid I'm a clone"*). **MJ grounded** — *"dancing like Michael Jackson"* rides on MJ having literally sung this hook (`[G]`); intent `[W]`.
