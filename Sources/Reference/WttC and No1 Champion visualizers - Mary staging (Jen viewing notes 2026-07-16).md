# WttC + #1 Champion visualizers, Mary staging (Jen viewing notes, 2026-07-16)

**Primary observation record.** Jen watched both videos and captured the staging as completely as she could. Cite as (Jen, video observation, 2026-07-16). Her own rule, preserved here: **facial expressions and conveyed feelings are flagged as interpretation** (suggestions), not staging fact. Staging/action facts below are [G via Jen relay]; feeling-reads are marked *(interp.)*.

## welcome to the circus, Visualizer (2026-01-16)

- Opens with **ONLY the Mary character**, without the sacred-heart tattoo she has in the #1 Champion video. She is **sexualized, dressed as a stripper/burlesque dancer**. She **wears his face paint** (the Lucius white-face design). She sits on the **black/white circus-painted pedestal** that he is pictured on in his later video Shorts.
- She stares off *(interp.: looking unhappy)* until she seems to notice the audience (looks directly into the camera), then **smiles (overdone, unnatural)** and begins to dance.
- **The Hebrew on her neck reads the same** (as Lucius's El Roi mark) to Jen's eye; her stated caveat: "if it is different it is some subtle difference my untrained eye can not pick out." ✅ **FRAME-VERIFIED 2026-07-16** — Jen's screen captures (`~/Documents/ElRoi/`, 14 frames) confirm it: WttC-Mary's throat carries the angular Hebrew (clearest: `2.10.08`, `2.05.38`, `2.04.09`), reading ~"X77X" to an untrained eye, matching the vault's independent gloss of Lucius's mark (*אל ראי, ~"X73X" to a non-Hebrew eye*); direct comparison frames (`2.13.29`, `2.13.38`) show the same letterforms on Lucius. **[G].**
- Her dancing, though sexual, is **"weird, off, stilted... almost right but made somehow a bit grotesque/scary. It's not accidental"**, her dancing in #1 Champ does not carry this distortion (there it is regular, very sensual, not scary).
- She is **alone for the first 44 seconds**. He does not appear until the line **"everything's upside down."** He fills the frame for that one line and exits the other side; the camera follows him one more line, then jumps back to her.
- He returns at 1:05 for "welcome to the circus, YEAH!" and immediately turns and leaves; the camera does not follow, right back to her.
- Back at 1:19 for "I'm juggling your darkest thoughts, it's a spectacle, I'm the final boss..."
- **Gesture note (Jen side-note; possibly a mistake, possibly relevant):** on "when the curtains close" he mimes the **opposite, opening**, hands over his face which he OPENS, the image landing on the word CLOSE.
- At 1:37, after "they love it when you lose control," there is a scream in the song; he **mimes the scream, then looks put out** *(interp.)* and exits the scene again.
- Each time he leaves, Mary comes forward and takes the focus back.
- He comes back again on **"everything's upside down"**, and at that moment **Mary is literally upside down** (bent over backwards in her dance). This time he carries the first of his **inflatable weapons**.
- She *(interp.: seems to willingly)* **takes her place under him**, reaches up and grabs one of his arms, then around his back and waist, dancing around him while he holds the guns, stripper-style.
- They finish most of the song. She does **a LOT of rapid facial-expression shifts**, *(interp.)* like she remembers she is supposed to be wearing the smile.
- He exits again and she **returns to her starting position like it was another cycle among many complete.**
- *(interp.)* She looks bored, annoyed, sad, and angry when she doesn't remember to smile for the audience.
- He enters again as the music fades, **squats in front of her and lights a cigarette.** She shifts rapidly between the forced happy and *(interp.)* annoyed, eye-rolling, maybe resentful.

## #1 Champion (la Conquistadora) visualizer

- **Same actress**, but **without the face paint/mask**, and **with the sacred-heart tattoo now.** ✅ **FRAME-VERIFIED 2026-07-16** (Jen's 46 embedded stills + Jen 2026-07-16: *"she does not wear his mask or the hebrew in #1 champion"*): **no face paint, NO El Roi Hebrew on the throat, sacred heart present on the chest.** With the WttC verification (mask + throat-Hebrew present, no heart), this establishes the **throat → heart relocation of the eye** `[G placements]`.
- Opens on an **empty chair**; he wanders in, sits, **lights a cigarette**, looks into the distance (off-scene) and **gestures for someone to approach**. Mary enters and starts dancing **as if they are at a strip club and he had called for a dance.**
- Her dancing is **not stilted and weird like WttC**, it is normal, smooth movements. He sings the first part **as if he is narrating her**, gesturing/reacting slightly toward her on "she was scary when she walked by" and "she's got good genes."
- **The tattoo:** a **heart with a crying eye in the center, pierced by three swords from below, spikes around the sides, a flaming crown, also bleeding, a drop of blood falls. Twin stars on each side of it.** ✅ **FRAME-VERIFIED 2026-07-16** (10 focused stills, clearest `image29/image23/image15/image17`): red heart, **open eye at the center**, flame above, rays/spikes radiating, sword-forms rising from below, star flanking, drops. Jen's description confirmed exact.
- He sings "she was la conquistadora with the gun in her hand" while **miming aiming a gun at the Mary character** as she dances at his feet.
- Mary dances honestly, without any sign of resentment or anger, until **he puts his arm around her neck and tries to hold her**; that *(interp.: seems to alarm her)* and **she breaks away** but keeps dancing.
- Shortly after she *(interp.: seems to get angry at him, for not paying attention, for not being engaged)*, **shoves his head and stands up, moving away from him** *(interp.: like she is giving up)*.
- He **smiles at the camera, grabs her arm and drags her back, whispers in her ear** and gets excited. **Folds up his pants to reveal his legs** (something he does in multiple songs) and sits waiting excitedly.
- She comes back with the **giant inflatable missile launcher**, *(interp.: looking concerned)*. He sings "Mary Mary such a revolutionary" while **posing triumphantly with the weapon.**
- The song winds down, **he hands the gun back and leaves** *(interp.: looking really pleased with himself)*. **Mary is left sitting in the chair holding the weapon**, *(interp.: she looks sad, like she is losing hope)*.
