## Verified passages (verbatim)

### Labour IV — Cancer (the instinct/intellect/intuition synthesis)
> "Three words summarize the objective self-awareness or the conscious aspect of the evolving human being: **instinct, intellect, intuition**. The sign which we are now studying is predominantly the sign of instinct; but the **sublimation of instinct is the intuition**… The **intermediate stage is that of the intellect**."

> "Again and yet again must all the sons of men, who are the sons of God, **seek for the golden antlered fawn** and bear it to the holy place."

- The two advising goddesses = **Diana / Artemis**; the doe is laid before the **altar of Apollo / the Sun-God**; the doe **symbolizes the intuition itself**. (Roy personalizes the doe as *"the thing I attract / love / create"* — Venus-in-Cancer overlay; faithful application, not Bailey's literal symbol.)

### Labour IX — Sagittarius — "Two Gates, Three Constellations" (the gates doctrine)
> "Sagittarius is the little gate to Capricorn. There are two cosmic gates: Cancer, the gate into incarnation; Capricorn, the gate into the spiritual kingdom. Prior to Capricorn is Sagittarius, spoken of as 'a lesser gate'. I like to think of it as the little gate at the foot of the hill through which we pass before climbing the mountain…"
- **Verified verbatim** against the primary text (archive.org `Labours-of-Hercules_djvu.txt`, 2026-06-11, Chrome MCP — cleaned of OCR soft-hyphens). Promotes the dossier's "lesser gate" lead ([[Sources/Reference/Bailey - Labours of Hercules (AI research dossier, citable-false finding-aid)]]) **to grounded** by tracing it to primary. Doctrine: **two cosmic gates (Cancer = incarnation, Capricorn = initiation)** + **Sagittarius = the "lesser/little gate"** at the foot of the hill before the Capricorn climb. (Bonus: Bailey's "foot of the hill" image rhymes with the Capricorn failure-mode, worldly ambition at the foot of the mountain.)

**The labour's faculty (verified verbatim, same pass):**
> "Right use of thought, restraint of speech, and consequent harmlessness on the physical plane, result in liberation; for we are… imprisoned to the planet… by what we ourselves have said and done."
- The "birds" Bailey names = **gossip, critical/self-pitying thought, "copious talking about things for which people may not as yet be ready"** (casting pearls). The labour = **restraint of speech / right use of thought / harmlessness** — i.e. it **corrects Sagittarius's own besetting vice** (the blunt proclaiming tongue, the scattered arrows), not its gift. Method strips the archer's **bow** (can't shoot the birds; indirection + silence instead) → lower Sag (scattered/loud) transmuted to higher Sag (one-pointed/harmless). This is the prerequisite for the gate: *"In Capricorn he becomes the Initiate, and this stage is always impossible until illusion has been overcome and the power of silence has been achieved."*
- **⚠ Dossier correction (vs primary):** the dossier calls Sagittarius "the sign of silence" — **WRONG.** Bailey gives **silence as the motto of SCORPIO** (Fixed-Cross mottos: Taurus *power to do*, Leo *to dare*, **Scorpio *silence***, Aquarius *to know*). The Sag labour *restores* silence ("silence returned"); the epithet itself is Scorpio's. (Logged so the finding-aid's conflation does not propagate.)

### Labour X — Capricorn — the summit-through-descent paradox (verified verbatim)
> "At the top of the mountain the sacred goat sees the vision and the initiate appears… One myth puts the emphasis on the descent into hell to free humanity (in the figure of the tortured Prometheus)."
- Keywords (p.214): *"Ambition. Crystallization. Struggle overcome. Transfiguration. Initiate consciousness. 'The unicorn of God.'"* Form-keynote *"Let ambition rule and let the door stand wide."* Rulers: Saturn (exoteric + esoteric). (Verified vs primary, 2026-06-11 — confirms the dossier's ✅ Capricorn claims.)
- **The paradox (resolves the "Sag→Cap = mountain→hill?" question):** Capricorn holds the **full vertical axis** — its labour is a **descent into hell** (Cerberus/Prometheus), yet its sign is the **mountain-top/Initiate** (goat → "unicorn of God"). The summit is reached *through* the underworld; the descent **is** the ascent ("as above, so below"). The exoteric *feel* (lofty fire-Archer → earthy grind-Goat = a comedown) **inverts** the esoteric order (Sag = the **foot** still aiming/scattered → Cap = the **summit**/initiation) — i.e. the **wheel-reversal doctrine** applied. → grounds the vault's **[[Brain/Songs/Lucius Lullaby]] = Capricorn X** mapping: the bottoming-out floor *is* the threshold/gate, not its opposite.

### Labour XI — Aquarius — Cleansing the Augean Stables (verified verbatim, 2026-06-11)
> "Turn now your back upon the brightness; **reverse your steps**; go back to those for whom the light is but a transient point, and help them make it grow."

- **Keynotes (verbatim):** form *"Let desire in form be the rule"*; soul *"**Water of life am I, poured forth for thirsty men.**"* Sign: **Fixed Air**, the **water carrier** (Bailey's term), world-server / group-consciousness; part of the **Fixed Cross** (the disciple's). Rulers: Uranus/Saturn (exoteric), Jupiter (esoteric).
- **The labour (myth gist):** the land lies under **pestilence** (a cart "piled high with dead"); the filth of **King Augeas's** stables = "the **accumulated filth of ages**." Hercules **diverts two rivers — the Alpheus and the Peneus —** through them, cleansing by **redirecting a current**, not shovelling. Augeas, suspecting a trick, **refuses payment and banishes him.**
- **Faculty / failure:** the cleansing is of the **COLLECTIVE**, not the self — *world service*, selfless, expecting no reward; the failure-pole is **separateness / working for reward**, and the **king who cannot recognize selfless service.** All verified vs primary (archive.org djvu, Chrome MCP).
- **JT tie-ins:** (1) the *"turn your back on the brightness, reverse your steps, **go back** to serve"* instruction is the **same reversal** as Capricorn's *"on that light I turn my back"* and the **wheel-reversal** above — the return-to-serve motif; and Aquarius (Server) **directly precedes Pisces (Saviour)** in the sequence. (2) Grounds the vault's systemic-rot / **"redirect the river"** material — *"empires built cages and sold everything"* ([[Brain/Concepts/The Circle]]); cf. the algorithm/media songs (Online, Happy Face, Pay Attention, I Like to Party) which **depict the stables** more than perform the **cleansing** (Shine = the rare river-redirection). (3) **Arc-prediction support:** the Aquarian cleansing is a *completion*-labour the catalogue has left largely **unperformed** — see [[log]] 06-11 status (the two open completion-labours = Aquarian cleansing + Piscean return). *(Per-song JT mappings stay `[P]`/`[~]`; this entry records only Bailey's verified text.)*

## Structural doctrines (verified verbatim vs primary, 2026-06-11)

### The wheel-reversal — Bailey's source for Loop vs Circle [~ framework, [G] as Bailey's text]
> "The man who is immersed in form… follows necessarily the **path of illusion and of appearances**; but **Hercules, the soul, follows the true Way, reverses the usual procedure and, figuratively speaking, goes against the tide.**"

- **Verified verbatim** (archive.org djvu, 2026-06-11, Chrome MCP). Two directions on **one** wheel: ordinary humanity travels it **retrograde/clockwise** (mechanical = a **loop**); the disciple **reverses** and goes **anti-clockwise, zodiacal order Aries→Pisces** ("against the tide") = a deliberate, transformative **return**. → **the source-anchor for the [[Brain/Concepts/The Circle|Loop-vs-Circle / Orbit-vs-Return]] keystone**: same structure, opposite direction. Note the *soul's* path IS sequential — but that is the developmental wheel, **not** the catalogue's release order (do not conflate; cf. [[The Labors of Hercules]]).

### The three Crosses (verified verbatim)
Bailey assigns the zodiac to three Crosses (all three terms confirmed in-text): **Mutable Cross** (Gemini–Virgo–Sagittarius–Pisces) = ordinary humanity / the personality; **Fixed Cross** (Taurus–Leo–Scorpio–Aquarius) = **the disciple's cross, which Hercules "mounts"**; **Cardinal Cross** (Aries–Cancer–Libra–Capricorn) = the Spirit / Initiate. *(Roy's stated placements — Gemini Sun, Leo Moon, Libra Rising, Venus Cancer, Mars/NN Pisces — span all three Crosses.)* Fixed-Cross mottos: Taurus *power to do* · Leo *to dare* · **Scorpio *silence*** · Aquarius *to know* (this is the line that corrects the dossier's "Sagittarius = sign of silence" — see §IX above).

### Glamour / illusion / maya = the Dweller on the Threshold
From Bailey's *Glamour: A World Problem* (1950): **illusion** = mental plane · **glamour** = astral/emotional · **maya** = etheric/vital force; their **combined sum in the integrated personality = the Dweller on the Threshold** (phrase present in *Labours* too). → grounds [[Brain/Concepts/The Dweller on the Threshold]] / Lucius-as-Dweller in Bailey's own system; in *Labours*, the Gemini deceiver (Antaeus/Busiris) and the Scorpio Hydra operate in the **astral-glamour** register.
