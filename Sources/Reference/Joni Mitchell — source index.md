## 1. Ann Powers — "Joni Mitchell and the 'Me' decade" (Guardian, 2024-06-18)
- **Cite:** Ann Powers, *"'I had a lot of questions about myself': Joni Mitchell's psychological 70s quest and the 'Me' decade,"* **The Guardian**, 18 Jun 2024 — an extract from her book *Traveling: On the Path of Joni Mitchell* (2024).
- **URLs:** theguardian.com/music/article/2024/jun/18/joni-mitchell-and-the-me-decade-ann-powers-travelling · also reprinted at **jonimitchell.com/library/print.cfm?id=5745** (the Guardian blocks WebFetch; the jonimitchell.com mirror served the text).
- **What it is / says:** Frames Joni's 1970s as a **psychological/therapeutic quest** central to her artistic innovation — the shift from confessional *Blue* toward observer-based songwriting while staying psychologically self-focused; therapy (Dr. Martin Grotjahn) → mysticism/meditation; a **Chögyam Trungpa** encounter that triggered a 3-day meditative/"awakened" state (corroborates dossier Part 3 / "Refuge of the Roads").
- **JT relevance `[W]`/`[~]`:** the **ego-wrestling / "returning to myself" / ego-dissolution** arc ↔ JT's **anamnesis & Self-vs-ego** ([[Essays/Concepts/Songs/It's Your Time]] "your Self"; the board's *"it's inconvenient to live life awake… once you're awake you can't go back to sleep"*). "I can't quit analysing; I'm an artist" ↔ the JT thinker/seeker register.
- **Quotes (verify verbatim):** *"I tried to run away myself, to run away and wrestle with my ego."* · *"I am returning to myself / These things that you and I suppressed."* · *"I left his office and for three days I was in an awakened state."* · *"I can't quit analysing; I'm an artist."*

## 2. Ann Powers (interview, Robin Young) — WBUR *Here & Now* (2024-06-11)

- **Quotes (verify):** *"I'm on a lonely road, and I am traveling, traveling, traveling, looking for something, what can it be?"* · *"I am not old, I'm told, but I am not young."*

## 3. Pitchfork — "Joni Mitchell: Her Art and Life in 33 Songs" (2019)
- **Cite:** Pitchfork staff (intro essay by **Ann Powers**, *"Joni Mitchell Shifts the Weather"*), *"Joni Mitchell: Her Art and Life in 33 Songs,"* **Pitchfork**, 6 May 2019 (career-retrospective song guide). *(Pasted by Jen — domain-blocked to WebFetch.)*

- **JT relevance `[W]`/`[~]`:**

## 4. EBSCO Research Starter — "Joni Mitchell" (encyclopedia entry)
- **Cite:** *"Joni Mitchell,"* **EBSCO Research Starters** (Women's Studies & Feminism).
- **URL:** ebsco.com/research-starters/womens-studies-and-feminism/joni-mitchell
- **What it is / says:** Encyclopedic bio anchor. **Born Roberta Joan Anderson, Fort Macleod, Alberta, 7 Nov 1943**; self-taught; folk → folk-rock (*Ladies of the Canyon*, *Blue*) → commercial peak (*Court and Spark*, 1974) → jazz-experimental (*Hejira* onward). **Deliberately abandoned commercial accessibility for artistic integrity** — a role-model framing. Trail-blazer for female artists (Patti Smith, Courtney Love) + influence on Prince, Elvis Costello. 5 Grammys; RRHoF 1997; Lifetime Achievement Grammy 2002; 2024 Grammy.
- **JT relevance `[~]`:** the **"deliberately moved away from commercial accessibility"** = the artistic-integrity-vs-industry posture that rhymes with JT's anti-machine stance; bio dates for any timeline work.

## 5. Joni Mitchell — *The Millennium Project Interview* (NicheTV, 2000)
- **Cite:** Kathryn Walker, *"The Millennium Project Interview,"* **NicheTV.com**, 15 Mar 2000 (archived: jonimitchell.com/library/print.cfm?id=1389 / view.cfm?id=1389).
- **What it is / says:** Joni on **end-times / indigenous prophecy** (Aboriginal Dreamtime, Hopi, Judeo-Christian Revelation) manifesting through environmental collapse; pessimism about human self-correction (greed, war-instinct); prophetic dreams; **Jung's subconscious transcending time**; finding presence/joy in nature; becoming "more present" with age over material accumulation.
- **JT relevance `[W]`/`[~]`:** **she glosses her own "Woodstock"** — *"we've got to get ourselves back to the garden"* = "my own primitive instinct that we were rushing towards the end," a reversal of humanity's destructive trajectory. This is **Joni's own reading of the garden/return** that JT welds into [[Sources/Lyrics/Soul Is A Star]] (seeds/garden) — and the apocalyptic register ↔ JT's *I Like to Party* bridge ("Devil waking up to claim the kingdom"), the board's mushroom-cloud/Revelation imagery; the **Jung-subconscious-beyond-time** ↔ JT's Jungian Shadow/Self material + anamnesis.
- **Quotes (verify verbatim):** *"We've got to get ourselves back to the garden."* · *"We're a stupid animal, you know?"* · *"Many must die that some may live."*

And music and the genuinely gifted… being shunned from the airwaves."* · *"I'm a painter first and a musician second, as it turns out."* · *"I never wanted to be a human jukebox."*

## 8. Ethan Brown — "Influences: Joni Mitchell" (New York Magazine, 2005)
- **Cite:** Ethan Brown, *"Influences: Joni Mitchell"* (Q&A), **New York Magazine**, 29 Apr 2005 (nymag.com/nymetro/arts/music/pop/11888/). *(Pasted by Jen — domain-blocked to WebFetch.)* **A first-person Q&A — her direct words.**
- **What it is:** Joni on her formative influences — childhood (polio, Saskatchewan), art, literature, jazz, the industry.
- **JT relevance `[W]` (high — two big anchors):**
  - **`[W]` NIETZSCHE / *Thus Spoke Zarathustra*.** *"Nietzsche was a hero, especially with Thus Spoke Zarathustra… He's a maker of individuals, and he was a teacher of teachers."* She also quotes his line on poets ("the vainest of the vain, the peacock of the peacocks… muddles his waters so that they might appear deep"). **This lands directly on the board's "Remember when I was the camel" DM** — Zarathustra's camel→lion→child metamorphoses — and JT's self-overcoming/individuation register. A confirmed shared source.
  - **`[W]` JUNG / synchronicity.** *"My publisher gave me Carl Jung's autobiography, Memories, Dreams, Reflections… The things that interest him are the things that interest me — particularly synchronistic events. I kind of pick up where he left off."* **Corroborates the deep Jung thread** (the board's psyche map; #7 Medicine Wheel; #9 Anima Rising) — Joni is self-avowedly Jungian.
  - **Industry-wore-me-down:** on the 2000 *Both Sides Now* standards record — *"I had to fulfill a contract — I wasn't writing. The business had just worn me down… no public recognition… Same frustration Van Gogh and Gauguin felt."* (↔ the fame-bargain thread; board "dropped by my record label.")
  - **Painter-first / "need for originals":** Van Gogh/Gauguin/Matisse/Rembrandt heroes; *"It's in my stars to invent… I have this need for originals, for innovation."* Prince anecdote (his fan-mail tossed as "lunatic fringe"). ## 13. Joni on "Wired" (TV, May 1988) — "a mirror of human nature" ✅ VERIFIED
- **Cite:** Joni Mitchell, guest on **"Wired"** (a 1988 TV program, host **Lenore Pemberton**), **May 1988**, discussing *Chalk Mark in a Rain Storm* and her writing style. **Source: video on Joni Mitchell's official (verified) Facebook page**, posted 10 May 2021 — facebook.com/watch/?v=771430250182829 ("Joni Mitchell Discussing Her Writing Style on Wired in 1988"). *(Resolved 2026-06-02: the earlier "WIRED magazine" confusion was the **1988 TV show "Wired,"** NOT WIRED the tech magazine — hence the 1988 date. Citable per Jen: the video is verified, on Joni's official FB.)*
- **The quote (verbatim, full):** *"The songs were not intended as a portrait of myself, you know, my naked self. They were intended to be a mirror of human nature. I think they can be best enjoyed and the most is derived from them if the listener sees themself in them, not if they see Joni doing something. Although it's convenient saying that's Joni going through those changes if you don't identify with those particular changes. But I think the people that get the most out of my music identify and feel that we've gone through the same thing."*
- **JT relevance `[W]` (high):** this is **the Empty-Mirror / ENSO ("Tell Us Who You Are") / witness stance** at JT's core — *"a mirror of human nature… the listener sees themself in them, not Joni doing something."* Structural mate of **[[Sources/Lyrics/Soul Is A Star|Soul Is A Star]] addressing the listener** (*"do you know who you are?"*), the board's **ENSO** ("a mirror of its painters — reflecting who you are"), and `CONCEPT-TheSpeakerProblem`. Joni — a confirmed influence — articulating the exact "songs are a mirror you see yourself in, not a self-portrait" doctrine JT runs. A strong anchor for the **mirror thread**, ready to feed Soul Is A Star / an Empty-Mirror concept.
