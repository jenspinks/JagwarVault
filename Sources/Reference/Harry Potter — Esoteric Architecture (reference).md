# Harry Potter — Esoteric Architecture (reference / context node)

> **What this is.** A bounded reference on the Western-esoteric material *in the Harry Potter books*, kept as a **context node** so the [[Jagwar Twin and Harry Potter — Shared-Source Parallels|JT × Potter parallels map]] can route through it. **This page is about Potter/Rowling/Granger — it is NOT evidence about Roy English.** The only *documented* direct JT→Potter link is the song [[dumbledore]]; everything here is the **shared-tradition substrate** both works draw on.
>
> **Tiering is the whole point here:** Rowling has confirmed *some* of this; a large body of respected fan-scholarship (esp. **John Granger**) *interprets* much more. Do not let interpretive scholarship read as Rowling-authored fact.

## [G] Rowling-confirmed
- **Alchemy is load-bearing.** Rowling has said she "learned a ridiculous amount about alchemy" to build the wizarding world's internal logic.
- **Red/white alchemical colour-naming in the father-figures.** Per Pottermore (summarized by *Time*): Rowling describes red and white in alchemy and links those colours to **Rubeus** Hagrid (red) and **Albus** Dumbledore (white) as two sides of Harry's ideal father figure.
- **Nicolas Flamel / the Philosopher's Stone** is literal book-1 canon (the historical alchemist; the Stone as the Magnum Opus's goal).
- **House polarity** is canon: Gryffindor **lion** vs Slytherin **serpent**; Fawkes the **phoenix** (death-rebirth) is Dumbledore's familiar.

## [W] Interpretive scholarship (Granger / fandom — NOT Rowling-authored)
- **The three-stage Great Work mapped across characters/colours** (nigredo→albedo→rubedo through Sirius **Black** → **Albus** Dumbledore → **Rubeus** Hagrid / Rufus Scrimgeour, the Weasleys). Rowling confirmed alchemy + the red/white naming; she did **not** confirm this full stage-map.
- **Ron = sulphur, Hermione = mercury**; **Bill/Fleur as the alchemical wedding** — known critical readings, interpretive, not settled.
- **Ring-composition / chiasmus** — Granger's thesis that the series is structured as a mirror-symmetric ring (book 1 ↔ book 7, etc.). Interpretive, but a *structural* reading, not just thematic.

## [G] Other Potter-side esoterica (canon facts, usable as context)
- **Black-family astronomy names** — **Sirius** (the Dog Star; also a dog animagus, surname *Black*), Regulus, Bellatrix, Andromeda, Arcturus: a house named in stars.
- **Deathly Hallows geometry** — the triangle-circle-line sigil (canon symbol).
- **Sevenfold recurrence** — seven books, seven Horcruxes, seven-year arc.
