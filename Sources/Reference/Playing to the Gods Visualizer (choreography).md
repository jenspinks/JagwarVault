# Playing to the Gods → City of Angels — Visualizer (choreography, observed)

Observable blocking from the **official visualizer** (covers the seamed pair 9→10). **No interpretation here** — analysis lives in Brain/ and Essays/. Evidence class = **visual** (cf. the #1 Champion visualizer; the Ballerina Boy outro audio).

## Source
- youtube.com/watch?v=zK-EKx5djas — "Jagwar Twin, sir lucius — playing to the gods (Visualizer)" (official, Vevo).

## Setting
- Two figures at the head of a **red-carpet rope-line** (velvet stanchions). Lighting moves from deep underwater **teal-green** to a blown-out **white**.
- **Sir Lucius** — white face paint, joker smile, cheek-cross marks, throat tattoos, and a **spiked headpiece covered in brand-text / logos.**
- A **woman in a full white rabbit mask** and black lace lingerie — **faceless, still, passive** throughout.

## Blocking — Playing to the Gods
- ~First 40 s: the two stand still, **holding hands.**
- When the **singing starts**, Lucius **lets go of her hand**, steps forward, and **waves / bows to the cameras** (plays to the paparazzi).
- As he steps back and **re-takes her hand**, a **heart-monitor tone + flatline** (the solid stop-beep) enters and continues to the song's end.

## Blocking — City of Angels (continuous)
- Opens still **holding hands** (as PTTG left off). He **stares at the approaching camera** (reads as annoyed/angry), looks up, **waits**; at the **singing** he **lets go and steps forward** again.
- He sings the **first verse toward the rabbit-woman**, the **chorus to the camera.**
- At the end he **reaches back for her and pulls her one step** to his side — **her only movement in either song.** They end **shoulder to shoulder**, as they began.
- When the music stops he **turns and leads her off** (screen right).

The **flatline under *"never gonna die"*** and the **hand-drop-to-perform** are the load-bearing beats. Readings: [[Brain/Songs/Lucius Lullaby (2026)/Playing to the Gods]] · [[Brain/Songs/Lucius Lullaby (2026)/City of Angels]].
