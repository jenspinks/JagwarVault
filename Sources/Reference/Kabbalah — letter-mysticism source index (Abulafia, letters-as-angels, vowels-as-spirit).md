# Kabbalah — letter-mysticism source index (Source)

Public, citable sources for the **angles/angels — consonants/vowels — letters/spirit** framework, so the catalogue's correspondence to it can be grounded **without** any private source. Cite the **tradition**, not the man: there is **no public Jagwar Twin source** stating this as Roy's method (external hunt, Jen 2026-06-07), so in public canon it is **tradition-correspondence `[~]`**, attributed to these. ## 1. Sefer Yetzirah — creation through the letters
The oldest Hebrew cosmological text: the cosmos derives from the **22 Hebrew letters** + 10 sefirot ("32 paths of secret wisdom"). The letters are the **formative powers of existence** — "He traced, cut, multiplied, weighed and inverted the twenty-two letters" to create. Letters split into 3 *mothers* (א מ ש), 7 *doubles*, 12 *simples*.
- *Sefer Yetzira* — Britannica: https://www.britannica.com/topic/Sefer-Yetzira
- Sefer Yetzirah — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sefer_Yetzirah
- "Letters as the Building Blocks of the World" — National Library of Israel: https://blog.nli.org.il/en/sefer-yetzirah/

## 2. Abraham Abulafia — ecstatic / prophetic Kabbalah (the method)
Abulafia (1240–~1291) studied *Sefer Yetzirah* and built a practice of **permuting the letters** (*tzeruf*) **with added vowel sounds**; in his school **"vowels and musical accents took on spiritual significance, as the combination of sound and letter was thought to produce a direct encounter with the Divine Word."** The letters in rotation become **"a ladder for the mind to climb to prophetic vision."** The standard scholar is **Moshe Idel**.
- Abraham Abulafia — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Abulafia
- "Abraham Abulafia & Ecstatic Kabbalah" — My Jewish Learning: https://www.myjewishlearning.com/article/abraham-abulafia-ecstatic-kabbalah/
- Moshe Idel, *The Mystical Experience in Abraham Abulafia* (SUNY, 1988) / *Ecstatic Kabbalah* — the scholarly anchor for letter-and-vowel recitation as ecstatic technique.
- "Letter Permutations (Tzerufim) in Medieval Hebrew Literature" — Ezra Brand: https://www.ezrabrand.com/p/letter-permutations-tzerufim-in-medieval

## 3. Vowels = spirit, consonants = body (the cleanest match)
Explicit medieval-Kabbalah metaphor: **"the vowels function among the consonants like the spirit in the body"** — attributed to 13th-c. kabbalist **Ezra ben Solomon of Gerona**. Vowels (the *niqqud*, written as marks around/within the consonants, unseen in an unpointed text) are **"the soul of the letters"** — they give light and movement to the consonant-bodies. This is, almost verbatim, the framework's *vowels-as-spirit / consonants-as-structure.*
- "Soul of the Letters — The Vowels of the Hebrew Alphabet" — Chabad.org: https://www.chabad.org/multimedia/audio_cdo/aid/2567066/jewish/Soul-of-the-Letters.htm
- Giqatilla's *Ginnat Egoz* / *Sefer ha-Niqqud* (philosophical poems on the vowels) — *Religions* 12(7):554: https://www.mdpi.com/2077-1444/12/7/554
- Hebrew is an **abjad** (consonants written; vowel-points added later by the Masoretes) — the plain-linguistic basis of "structure first, spirit added": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niqqud

## 4. Letters = angels (the 72-Name tradition)
In Kabbalah the Hebrew letters are treated as **living / angelic**; the **Shem HaMephorash** ("the explicit Name") is derived from **Exodus 14:19–21** — three verses of **72 letters each**; written in the boustrophedon arrangement they yield **72 three-letter Names**, each made an angel by the suffix **-el / -iah**. So *letters → angels* is native, not imposed.
- Shem HaMephorash — Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shem_HaMephorash
- Shemhamphorash — Encyclopedia.com: https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/shemhamphorash
