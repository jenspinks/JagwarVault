## The Happy Face descent (the melt sequence)
- By this point in the set the **white paint was already degrading from sweat**, skin showing through `[P]`, ✅ visible in barrier footage (IMG_2361: patchy around the eyes, crosses smeared).
- He **walked down the stage steps to the barrier** and leaned into the crowd `[P]` ✅ (steps visible behind him, IMG_2361, 2363).
- At the barrier: **clasped hands tightly with one man while singing into his eyes, shared the mic with him; looked out over the crowd; then embraced a girl and sang with her, faces touching; continued through the song** `[P]`, ✅ corroborated (IMG_2360 hand-clasp with mic between; IMG_2363 fan singing up into the shared mic; IMG_2366 a fan's face pressed close; IMG_2369 phone-length close-up in the crowd).
- **Fans' touch visibly wiped paint; each contact removed more** `[P]`. He finished the song with much of his skin showing through `[P]` *(the degree, and "much of himself showing through," = interp.)*.
- **"The musicality was lost as they screamed into the mic together"** `[P]` (Jen's phrasing, kept verbatim as an observation of the audio).
- *(interp., Jen)*: no Lucius-register inauthenticity, none of the City of Angels press-annoyance; he read as a participant in a shared experience, "just as desperate for the crowd's embrace as they were for his."
- **A stabilized-camera operator was positioned in the pit filming the barrier sequence at close range** ✅ (IMG_2361). Whose crew = TO VERIFY.
- Confetti fell during the barrier sequence ✅ (IMG_2361, 2363).
