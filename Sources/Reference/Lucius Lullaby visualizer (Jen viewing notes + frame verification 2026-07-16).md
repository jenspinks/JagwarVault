- A single room. **Black-and-white checkerboard floor** ✅. Heavy teal-green wash and smoke throughout.

## The table ✅
- **Five-light candelabrum, lit, with the centre candle clearly elevated on a raised central shaft, two arms each side** ✅ (frame-verified 2026-07-16; Jen's count exact). *Form note for future readers: a graduated arc with a raised centre is the standard antique 5-light form. Do not treat the shape as a marked choice without a second anchor.*
- **Fruit:** pale grapes (large bunch), dark grapes, an orange, and a **green apple** — prominent, front-lit, centre of the fruit group ✅. The green apple is Lucius's mark `[G]` ([[Sir Lucius]] §Keys to Instagram).
- **Silver:** an ornate lidded **ewer/pot** ✅, and an ornate oval **silver tray carrying vessels** ✅.
- **★ THREE CUPS ✅ CONFIRMED (Jen HD frame, 2026-07-16).** The ornate oval silver tray carries the lidded ewer **plus three footed cups, each set with red stones** — matching the small gold red-stoned cup he drinks from. **Three cups, one drinker; he is alone all 2:35.** `[G]`
  - *Claude could not resolve the count at 360p and took Jen's count; her HD frame then confirmed it. The vessels are visible in the 360p crops at 0:19–0:21 once you know what you are looking at.*

## Recurring gestures (Jen, 2026-07-16)
- **The snake hand-puppet.** He makes his hand into a **talking snake**, several times. **Jen, exact: "He does have the puppet snake talk back at him, all the while placing himself as the snake."** Both directions at once — the lyric has him being the snake; the gesture has the snake addressing him.
- **The toast.** He **drinks to something offstage and up**, and **looks up and to his left**. ✅ Not the paper sun (behind him). **What he looks at is never shown in frame.**
- **Register:** *(interp., Jen)* "nothing about it seems joking. he seems very serious during this song." Noted because it contrasts with his register elsewhere; kept as interpretation, not fact.
