# Rockwell — "Somebody's Watching Me" source context (Source)

Public context for the song **[[Brain/Songs/Singles & EPs/The Watchers|The Watchers]]** interpolates (Rockwell credited). **No JT interpretation here** — this is the documented backstory of the *interpolated source.* Cite the Rolling Stone feature / Rockwell's own words; analysis lives in the song pages.

## The facts (grounded — Rolling Stone / Rockwell's own words)
- **Rockwell = Kennedy William Gordy**, son of **Berry Gordy** (Motown founder). Music royalty. *(Confirms the writer-credit name for the interpolation log.)*
- **Shunned the nepotism / the name.** He submitted his demo under "Rockwell," **not** the Gordy name, "to avoid charges of nepotism" — and adopted a **fake British accent** (claimed to be from Portsmouth) on record and in interviews to distance himself from his father and from Michael Jackson's involvement. *"I didn't want anybody to… say, 'If it wasn't because of Berry Gordy or Michael Jackson, you'd be nothing.'"* He wanted to be judged on **his own music.**
- **Born from a prayer.** Frustrated after "mediocre" early songs, he **dropped to his knees and prayed:** *"God grant me the creativity to write a song that'll go to the top of the charts and tickle the taste buds of the music connoisseur."* "Everything came to me so easily after that prayer." When it was done he prayed again, promising: *"I will tell everyone about you… how you did this for me and how it really happened."* — **the song was framed by its author as God-granted, with a vow of testimony.**
- **Michael Jackson sang the hook** (uncredited — "the worst-kept secret in music"); **Jermaine Jackson** also contributed. Organic, not label maneuvering: Rockwell grew up at the Jacksons' house (his sister Hazel married Jermaine). MJ: *"Who are you going to get to sing background vocals?"* — Rockwell: *"Why don't you do it?"*
- **Surveillance/voyeurism is his signature, not incidental.** "The King of Paranoia Rock." The shower line — *"When I'm in the shower / I'm afraid to wash my hair / 'cause I might open my eyes / and find someone standing there"* — came from a real prank (pressing his face to the shower glass). Follow-up singles double down: **"Obscene Phone Caller," "Peeping Tom."**
- **King/prince + disappearance + "Second Coming."** *"I came from a king, Berry Gordy, and I'm like a prince."* Retired fast ("it wasn't fun anymore… I needed to just be a regular guy"); planned a comeback album titled ***The Second Coming.***
- The 1984 single peaked **No. 2** (behind "Footloose"), went gold; haunted-house video (dir. Francis Delia).
