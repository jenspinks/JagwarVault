# The Interview / The Spoon — Roy vs Sir Lucius (theatrical series, EP1–7)

**Primary source — THEATRICAL CANON.** The narrative backbone of the *Lucius Lullaby* rollout: a scripted video series on the Jagwar Twin YouTube channel in which **Roy** and his shadow-self **Sir Lucius** argue across a table. This is the consolidated home for the dialogue that dozens of vault essays cite by episode ("In Interview EP3, Lucius says X").

> [!warning] How to cite / fidelity
> - **THEATRICAL, not autobiographical** (CLAUDE.md): *Lucius speaking is NOT automatically Roy speaking.* Correct: "In Interview EP3, Lucius says X." Incorrect: "Roy admits X."
> - **Cite the YouTube episode** (URL per section), never this file or the Google Doc. The Doc is a working reference (per [[Boundaries]]).
> - **`status: draft`** — transcribed from Jen's working Doc; **listen-through against the videos still pending** for exact wording + in-video timestamps (EP1–4 lack timestamps; EP6–7 carry partial ones from the Doc). Proper nouns normalized to canonical vault spellings (Jagwar Twin, Sir Lucius, Joey Moi, Not Your Homie, Happy Face); obvious Doc-entry typos cleaned in stage directions only; genuinely uncertain spots flagged `[?]`.
> - Registered in [[Master Source List]] §2. Do **not** confuse with the Mirror Sessions (separate, biographical; `MirrorSessions-*.md`).

**Recurring visual/character notes:** Lucius wears an "Anonymous Club" bunny/Lambchop hat (reads as bunny ears / could double as horns or moth antennae) + white goth-clown/mime makeup, almost always smoking. He has Hebrew on his neck reading **"El Roi"** (אל ראי, "the God who sees me," Gen 16). Recurring set: underground parking garage, small table/altar, fruit bowl (green apples + bananas), two red candles, an **Egyptian statue under the table**, **Tree-of-Life pillars**, **moon-like set-lights** over each shoulder. The DJ/interviewer is a voice, never shown.

## EP 1 — The War Begins
**Released 2025-07-31** · https://youtu.be/H1lGRYY6204

*Set: underground parking garage, greenish light. Roy shirtless, single large chain necklace, metal armor from the waist down. Two folding chairs, small table between them: bowl of fruit (green apples + bananas), two red candles in white holders, Egyptian statue underneath. Tree-of-Life motifs on the pillars; a moon-like set light over each shoulder. Lucius enters late — bunny/Lambchop hat, white clown makeup, black sweater/pants/shoes, smoking.*

> **DJ:** Thanks for being here today, Jagwar Twin. I love your music. The range is wide, to say the least. How would you describe your music to someone who's never heard it before?
>
> **Roy:** Well, my music is, uh… what's *he* doing here?
>
> **Lucius:** I wouldn't miss this for the world. Shall we continue?
>
> **Roy:** Let's just get this over with.
>
> **Lucius:** What was the first question again?
>
> **Roy:** He asked me how I would describe my music.
>
> **Lucius:** *Your* music? You mean *our* music?
>
> **Roy:** I made it. You were just a tool. A tool.
>
> **Lucius:** Without me, you'd still be singing sweet little lullabies that don't stream very well on the internet. By the way — **happy face, bad feeling, hundreds of millions of streams.** Those are me. *You need me.*
>
> **Roy:** It is true that light needs shadow to be seen. And I used to need you, but **I'm done now.** Look, I just want to answer these questions about my music.
>
> **Lucius:** *Our* music?
>
> **Roy:** Fine. Our music is an exploration of light and dark, the beauty and the chaos of being a human — a mirror for those seeking to remember who they are. Oh—
>
> **Lucius:** Jesus, there you go again. Talking about some spiritual mumbo jumbo, like you're on some prophetic enlightenment mission. News flash: the world is burning. People want the truth. The raw truth. The kind of truth that *I* offer. Your truth is one-sided.
>
> **Roy:** It's not that simple.
>
> **Lucius:** *[~2:01]* It is, though. And trust me, your fans are confused. One minute your music is a twisted circus, the next it's Dumbledore. **You don't stand for anything. The darkness or the light?**
>
> **Roy:** I guess I'm somewhere in the middle.
>
> **Lucius:** You naive little god. Without me, you'll drown. Your whole career is teetering on the edge of the abyss.
>
> **Roy:** You walk my own path, not yours. Lucius, there is no path without me. **I made you.**
>
> **Lucius:** Every song they love — it was me. If you leave me, you will fail.
>
> **Roy:** Maybe. Or maybe **this is where I'm finally free.**

## EP 2 — The Rise of sir lucius (THE BARGAIN)

> **DJ:** So, we're excited to have you here today, Jagwar Twin.
>
> **Roy:** Yes. Thank you for having me.
>
> **DJ:** And we hear you're going to play a little something for us. *[Doc labels this line "ROY"; reads as the DJ — `[?]` speaker]*

>
> **Lucius:** Congratulations on *Loop*. How's it streaming?
>
> **Roy:** It's not about that, Lucius.
>
> **Lucius:** Right. Well then, what do you have next for us, Roy?
>
> **Roy:** I have probably my favorite song I've ever written. It's called **The Song of Love.** *(singing)* "Love… love is a question. Everyone's guessing. Everyone knows…"
>
> **Lucius:** **STOP!!!** *(lights flicker)* No one wants this love stuff. You know your label doesn't want to put this out, right?
>
> **Roy:** Joey said that—
>
> **Lucius:** Joey lied to you to make you feel better. He thinks this is another flop. You know how many hits Joey Moi has had? Enough to have his own jet. Morgan Wallen, Nickelback, Florida Georgia Line. Okay — maybe not the coolest artist in the world, but you know what *is* cool? Having a mansion, a house — even a studio apartment at this point would be a start. Get you out of your parents' house. You're 37, right?
>
> **Roy:** *The Song of Love* is a masterpiece.
>
> **Lucius:** Just give me one, Roy. One hit. I promise I won't lead you astray. You can make enough money to take care of everyone you love. What about all the people you employ? The ones who believe in you. Michael's about to have his first kid. You wouldn't want him out of a job, would you?
>
> **Roy:** What do you have in mind?
>
> **Lucius:** I'm glad you asked. I prepared a little something.
> *[Grabs a browning banana from the fruit bowl, presses it like a button → plays **Not Your Homie**]*
> *♫ "You're not my homie. You don't know me. Let's not pretend… I'm ring-around-the-rosie, my pocket full of posies…" ♫*
>
> **Lucius:** This is what the people want.
>
> **Roy:** It doesn't mean anything.
>
> **Lucius:** Exactly. **It's nonsense. It's all nonsense.**
> *[Cross-ref: the public "It's nonsense" line ties to the Angle/Angel framework (Roy's public "angels and angles" / consonants-vowels theme). — A prior cross-ref note here was **removed 2026-06-07**: it cited **private phone-call** material (quarantined per [[Boundaries]]). The private call content must not re-enter the vault; the public anchor for the "nonsense" theme is the on-camera EP2 line above.]*
>
> **Roy:** Fine, **I'll give you this one.**
>
> **Lucius:** I knew you were a reasonable man, Roy. You won't be disappointed.

## EP 3 — welcome to the circus
**Released 2026-01-05** · https://youtu.be/HqsTH99QcPk

*Roy in a rockstar suit + red leather gloves, many chains, sunglasses. Small altar center: two red candles, fruit bowl, Egyptian statue beneath. An unsheathed longsword on the floor.*

> **DJ:** Jagwar Twin! *Not Your Homie* is just blowing up — I hear it's getting a major movie placement. How do you feel about all the success?
>
> *(Roy begins to answer. Lucius enters from the side — classic ears, black sweater and pants. Roy pushes his sunglasses up onto his head, uncovering his eyes.)*
>
> **Lucius:** *(interrupting)* Marvelous, just marvelous! You know, it really came down to Roy's leadership on this one. I always knew that when it came down to it, he would make the right decision. Now look at us! Roy can afford to dress like a… yeti…
>
> *(childish laughter in background)*
>
> **Roy:** *(laughing)* It did work out for us. *(sincere)* Thank you.
>
> **Lucius:** *(sincere)* You're welcome.
>
> **Roy:** While Lucius was singing songs about sticking pencils in people's eyes *(Lucius laughs)*, I was recording **the song of love**.
>
> **Lucius:** No, no, no. You're going to mess it all up again. You do this every time. **You're in a loop and you don't even realize it.** You had *Loser* — it was on the radio everywhere — and then *you* decided to drop a ballad, "Move To You." Does anyone remember "Move To You"? *(echoes, crow calling)* Then you tap me in, I give you **Happy Face.**
>
> **Together:** Hundreds of millions of streams.
>
> **Roy:** We know the speech, Lucius. But music is supposed to come from your soul. It's supposed to be vulnerable.
>
> **Lucius:** Let's talk about being vulnerable. Do you know what your lowest-performing song of all time is? *(mimes ballet dancing)* **Ballerina Boy. When you're vulnerable you LOSE!**
>
> **Roy:** It's not about winning. What are we winning?
>
> **Lucius:** *(almost pleading)* A good LIFE, Roy. For you and everyone around you. You want a world tour? You can't tour the world if you're broke and can't afford rent and you live at your parents' house at 37 years old.
>
> **Roy:** I know that when I follow my own compass — not the numbers, not what the record label says, not your well-meaning advice — everything works out, eventually.
>
> **Lucius:** What if eventually never comes?
>
> **Roy:** It always comes. And not because of luck, or some scheme, but because this whole game was **designed to reward truth. And love is the ultimate truth.** And when I move from that place, nothing can stop me.
>
> **Sir Lucius:** …Speaking of not stopping — what if, for now, we keep this train rolling. It looks good on you, by the way… success. The fuzziness of it looks good on you. *(Roy looks pleased and amused.)* We finally have the momentum back. *Not Your Homie* is flying. Let me give you another hit.
>
> **Roy:** I assume you already have something in mind.
>
> **Lucius:** *(grabs a banana from the bowl, presses play → plays **welcome to the circus**)*
>
> **Roy:** I mean… this is exactly what I think *you* would make.
>
> **Lucius:** And the people will LOVE IT.
>
> **Roy:** What happened to music that was created from the soul?
>
> **Lucius:** **The algorithm, baby.**
>
> **Roy:** I feel like I was born in the wrong time.
>
> **Lucius:** Maybe you were.
>
> **Roy:** Okay. *(puts sunglasses back on)* Welcome to the circus. *(turns toward camera)*
>
> **Lucius:** Welcome to the circus. *(turns toward camera)*

## EP 4 — The Spoon (weirdness)
**Released 2026-02-17** · https://youtu.be/DKD44ULpy3o

*Roy alone in a folding chair, cement wall behind him. Shirtless, black pants, heavy chains, sunglasses, bulky wired headphones. He stares into a **spoon** held up in front of him. Lucius walks up holding an apple — childlike, like a kid speaking to an adult.*

> **Lucius:** You're very strange.
>
> **Roy:** *(moves a headphone off one ear without looking away from the spoon)* What?
>
> **Lucius:** You're very strange.
>
> **Roy:** Says the rabbit clown. What even *are* you?
>
> **Lucius:** **I'm your weirdness.** *(bites the apple, chews)*
>
> **Roy:** **Your weirdness is the sunshine.**
>
> *(music from **Weirdness** begins to play)*
>
> **Lucius:** I'll take that. *(walks off screen)*

## EP 5 — sir lucius takes over the "lucius lullaby" premiere (solo speech)
**Video posted 2026-03-23** · listening party **2026-03-03**, Santa Monica · https://youtu.be/aCftmfU_YpA

*At the album's industry listening party (Roy had appeared earlier, announced the album, thanked fans). Lucius comes out, supposedly unexpectedly — **Leif's first appearance**, standing in front as a bodyguard. Lighting goes red; a dark heartbeat rhythm plays. Lucius in full makeup + ringmaster jacket: cold, hard, unsmiling, emotionless. Solo address to the room of label heads:*

> Every system believes that it's permanent. We all know that's not the case. **Systems don't fail because they get attacked; they fail when people stop believing in them.** That's how every empire falls. Not with fire, not with war. With an idea, a story — a better story than the one that was being told.
>
> We're sitting in a room built on stories, incentives. We're sitting in a room full of people who get paid to be careful. But **the future doesn't ask for permission to arrive; it just arrives.** And when it arrives, it's not in the form that you approved. **It's wearing a mask** — a mask that you thought was a bad idea. **It's speaking in a voice that you tried to eliminate [with fear].** This is a game. This is all a game! This is all a game. I understand the game. And when you understand the game, that changes your leverage. You stop asking for a seat at the table and **you build your own table.** And when you build your own table, everybody in the room has to decide where to sit. This is the one, the only, instruction that I offer you tonight. **Get out of the way. You're welcome.**
