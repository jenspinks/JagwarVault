# Digital Journal — Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa) era interview

Tied to Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa). **Surfaces Roy's "circus-like space" reference in his own video commentary** + a *third* explicit mirror statement ("It's a mirror. It's a mirror for you.").

## Verbatim excerpts

### On Bad Feeling (Oompa Loompa) — origins
> "The song came about a little over a year ago. I've been exploring my ancestors and my German, Polish, and Slavic roots, and so I was using a lot of accordions and tubas."

> "We were in the studio jamming on this accordion sort of vibe, and I kept saying, 'We need some oompa shit. We need German oompa music.'"

### KEY — Roy names the "circus-like space"
> **"The music video was sort of just expanding on that world. We wanted to bring it into this circus-like space..."**

(This is Roy himself referencing a *circus-like space* in his Bad Feeling video — pre-*Lucius Lullaby* / pre-WTTC. Worth tracking: the visual-circus motif is in Roy's directing vocabulary 2023-ish, predating the theatrical Circus / *Welcome to the Circus* release by ~2 years. Soft-grounded support for tracing the Circus thread further back than the theatrical canon makes explicit.)

### On songwriting / inspiration (Buddhist-ish framing)
> "I am inspired by nothing… because nothing creates everything. So, getting into that place of nothing is what sparks things to come through..."

### On the digital age
> "I think being an artist now is the same as it always was. You know, it's always different but we're always going to paint with the canvas of the day."

> "...TikTok and social media is similar – it depends on how you use it."

### On career / centering
> "...you need to run it all through your own filter. That's the biggest thing: coming to your own center and knowing what's true..."

> "Being able to walk into any place and be centered and fully present in oneself."

### KEY — third explicit mirror statement
> **"It's a mirror. It's a mirror for you."**

(Mirror-doctrine now sourced at **2018 / 2020 / 2023-ish** — three independent press contexts. The "song / artist / project as mirror" framing is Roy's stable lifelong self-description. Fully grounds the Mirrors thread.)
