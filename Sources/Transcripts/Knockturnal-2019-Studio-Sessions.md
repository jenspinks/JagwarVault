# Knockturnal — 2019-10-10 "Studio Sessions Trippier Than Hippy Circles"

October 2019 piece on the recording process for *Subject to Flooding*. **Surfaces Egyptian-mythology fascination and the ice-bath / meditation studio practice** — useful biographical/process grounding for the EP1 Egyptian-statue set and for [[Roy on Process]].

## Verbatim excerpts

### On childhood interests (KEY — Egyptian mythology anchor)
> "I've been fascinated with Egyptian mythology and other ancient cultures since I was a kid."

(Important context for: *The Interview EP 1* set, which includes an Egyptian statue under the table. Roy's fascination is pre-Lucius and pre-theatrical — it's a long-term influence, not retrofitted for the EPs.)

### On the studio process for STF
> "We were doing ice baths and saunas and guided meditations all the time. It was amazing!"

> "The crazy part was the change that happened within us. The process of making the music transformed after these sessions."

> "All these things made us look inward. I think to make good art, you have to learn about yourself first, and all those experiences combined helped me do that."

### On the outcast / misfit self (proto-Loser framing)
> "For a large majority of my life, I was a misfit, an outcast, really thinking negativity... once I got older, my perceptive changed."

> "Those people who bullied me… I have no anger against them. They were just projecting their insecurities."

### On message
> "I just want people to feel good... Creating happiness is all I want to do. Who doesn't deserve to be happy?!"

### Collaborators / context
- Producers / engineers: **S1** and **Linus** (cf. [[Sources/Credits/Subject to Flooding]])
- Album: *Subject to Flooding*
- Tour: scheduled with Avril Lavigne (fall 2019)
