But for me, I'm like looking at it like, well, now we know what we can fix and solve. Or maybe in the past there was the illusion that it was all good, but we just didn't even know what was really going on under the hood. And it's kind of like — if you know the "darkest before dawn" saying — but I really, that feels to be true, you know, in my heart, that we're just now seeing the darkness more than ever. But it's for the purpose of getting that out of the way and confronting those dark things. But I think it's important, like, for us as artists too — help those people, and ourselves, you know, all humans, to feel seen and heard.
