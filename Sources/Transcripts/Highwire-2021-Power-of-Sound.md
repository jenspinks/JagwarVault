# Highwire (2021) — "Jagwar Twin and the Power of Sound" (Source)

Verbatim/near-verbatim ingest of a 2021 profile. **No interpretation here** — analysis lives in Brain/ and Essays/. (Profile prose, third-person + quoted; cite as published.)

## The heart/mind origin of the split — verbatim Roy [G]
> *"I was creating music from my mind, thinking about what would be successful and what other people would like. I really lost my way for a long time without really knowing. **I think evil works a lot more subversively than we think, and it creeps in very slowly.**"*

Context (profile): *"There was a time when Jagwar Twin was yet to be born; it was just Roy English, and **not all of his music came from the heart.**"*

→ **Grounds the Heart/Mind split origin AND the Sir Lucius origin in a named public interview:** pre-Jagwar Roy made music **from the mind** (success-calculation), not the heart; **"evil… creeps in very slowly"** = the Dweller-accumulates doctrine. Pairs with the Mirror-Sessions "mind = Lucifer archetype" beat on [[Brain/Roy Voice/Roy on the Heart-Mind Split]]; underwrites [[Sir Lucius]] = the mind divorced from the heart.

## The name — mirror of consciousness [G]
Profile: *"Roy's artistic name was inspired by the jaguar in Mayan mythology — a creature who **looks within others as within itself. A mirror of consciousness.**"* And: the "twin" partly from **Gemini**, and from "worldly forces such as **light and dark and yin and yang.**"

→ Third independent source for **jaguar = mirror-of-consciousness** + **twin = duality/Gemini** (with [[Sources/Transcripts/Ones-To-Watch-2018-Debut-QA]] and [[Sources/Transcripts/Mic-Magazine-UK-Myth-of-Music]]).

## Ego-dissolution origin [G]
Profile: *"Jagwar Twin became a collaborative effort in which everyone involved, including Roy, and producers **Matt Pauling and S1**, had to **forgo their egos to create something bigger than themselves.**"*

→ Corroborates the vessel/ego-dissolution origin (2018 OTW). Producers: **Matt Pauling** (= Matthew Pauling, cf. [[Sources/Lyrics/Dream - Dream]]) + **S1**.
