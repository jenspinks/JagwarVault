## Key content (citable substance)

### On "Move To You" [G — Roy's own words]
> "It is really special to me… I felt so lost when I wrote 'Move To You.' I felt like I was never going to 'make it' in music."
> "I recorded the vocals in Florence, Italy and Joshua Tree, CA. It's a prayer to and from the universe."

He names *Move To You* his **personal favorite** on the album.

### The Circle — earliest known naming (2019) [G — Roy's own words]
In the *Move To You* answer, on realizing he was never actually lost:
> "Then I realized I was never lost, I was learning what I needed to learn on the exact timeline I needed to be on… **There is no beginning and no end it's just a circle.**"

- **"There is no beginning and no end it's just a circle"** is the rock-solid verbatim (both reads agree). Jen's read also captured a following sentence — *"I feel the circle every time we play it live and hear the crowd singing it back"* — which the WebFetch pass didn't surface; treat that one sentence as `[verify]`, but the **circle-doctrine naming itself is grounded.**
- **This is the earliest documented naming of the Circle doctrine — 2019, three years before *33* (2022) and the entire [[Sir Lucius]] / Black-Magic-Rainbow-Circus mythology** — spoken in the *Subject to Flooding* press cycle, on the album's most vulnerable song. Full anchor: [[Brain/Concepts/The Circle]] §Provenance. See also [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/The Circle]] (the 2022 keystone song) and [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Move To You]].

### On "Loser" [G — Roy's own words]
> "I was bullied growing up and always felt like a loser in the old sense of the word… At a certain point, I realized that we all feel like losers sometimes. We're all weird and unique in our own ways and it's time we embrace that."

- **Origin:** bullying → **resolution into solidarity** (*"we all feel like losers sometimes… embrace that"*). A **third independent outlet** corroborating the masks-off / shared-humanity reading of *Loser*, alongside **Prelude Press 2019-09-23** (the "masks" line) and **Knockturnal 2019** (misfit/outcast). *(Jen's read specified the bullying took the form of older kids running a blog mocking his singing — `[verify]` that exact detail against the article; the "bullied growing up… loser in the old sense" framing is confirmed verbatim.)*
