# Strife Mag — 2024 Bad Feeling / Oompa Loompa interview

Post-*33*, pre-*Lucius Lullaby* era. Roy on tour in Europe; commercial reflection.

## Verbatim excerpts

### On state & Europe
> "I'm doing great, really good. It's beautiful to be here."

> "Europe is now my favourite place to play. I've never toured in Europe before. And now I don't want to leave. It's amazing."

### On the artist name & philosophy
> "That's a good question. I think it captures a bit of who I am at the core, playing in light and dark."

(On "Jagwar Twin" as name — **"playing in light and dark"** = duality acknowledged in Roy's own voice.)

### On creative process
> "I never thought about genres, even when I was a kid, I never thought about them. I would just feel a feeling or an emotion."

### On commercial trajectory
> "They said the song sucked... it was too experimental for that time, got dropped from the label, but then it found its way."

> "I think that it's moving quicker, now, the waves are moving faster. Life is moving faster."

### Dr. Dre studio session anecdote
> "Oh, I don't like drums, it doesn't need drums... You're a fucking idiot. Dr. Dre was really just ready to bless you with the track."

(Self-deprecating story about a Dr. Dre session encounter.)

### Lightning round
- Favorite European city: **Prague**
- Favorite live song: **The Circle**
- Road listening: **Kendrick Lamar**
- Ice cream: **Mint chocolate chip**
