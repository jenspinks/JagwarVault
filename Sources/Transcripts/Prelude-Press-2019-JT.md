# Prelude Press — 2019-09-23 Jagwar Twin interview

STF promotional interview. **Contains the verbatim "masks" framing** that the [[Essays/Concepts/Songs/Loser]] Brain page had previously cited as Roy's stated framing without a citable source. Now grounded here.

## Verbatim excerpts

### On the album's transformation
> "This album changed me and everyone involved."

### On "Loser" — the masks line (HIGH-VALUE FOR LOSER PAGE)
> "Loser is about how we all walk around with these masks on and try to be what we think other people want us to be. But if we remove those masks we find that we're all human underneath and we're all losers. There is only perceived separation."

### On personal growth via the album
> "Through the process of Subject To Flooding I completely changed my diet, my meditation and prayer practices, views on governmental and human made institutions and most of all, learned so much about myself."

### On what he hopes fans take away
> "That everything is possible and we are all one. More now than ever, we need to come together as a human race."

### On pride in the album
> "I'm most proud of the fact that we pulled it together at all. The odds were completely against us."
