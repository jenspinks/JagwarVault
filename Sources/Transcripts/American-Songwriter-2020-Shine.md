# American Songwriter — 2020-04-27 Shine / quarantine

Tied to the Shine quarantine video. **Contains two important explicit mirror statements** — Roy declaring himself a mirror and the song a mirror.

## Verbatim excerpts

### On his artistic philosophy (KEY — mirror grounding)
> "I just want to be a mirror."

### On Shine — the inspiration
> "I wrote 'Shine' a couple of years ago when the collective thought was 'this couldn't possibly get any worse!'"

> "I was hanging out watching 'Troy' with Brad Pitt. A lot of war and killing people in that movie, so I wanted to write a chorus that felt happy, like the sun rising."

### On Shine's intent
> "My thought was that even in dark times, there is always light if you want to find it. There's love in every moment if you know where to look."

### On the quarantine video project (KEY — second mirror statement)
> "I hope that people see themselves. More and more I realize that **a song is a mirror.**"

### On his songwriting process
> "I'm at my best as a songwriter when I'm alone and centered into myself."

### On daily practice
> "I like to wake up, grab a guitar first-thing and follow whatever comes out for five or ten minutes and then leave it. Don't judge it."

### On meditation and songwriting
> "I have found that my best songwriting comes from being alone, centered and present in myself and what I'm feeling."
