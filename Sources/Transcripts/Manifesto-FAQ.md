# Manifesto FAQ — mirrors.jagwartwin.com

Roy English's own self-framing on the `mirrors.jagwartwin.com` site. Tied to the "Hall of Mirrors" / Web3 community structure. **This is Roy speaking in his own platform, undated.** Highest-tier source for current authorial framing.

## Verbatim excerpts (per WebFetch — content summary; full text on the live page)

### Core manifesto themes — the substitution table

Roy frames the project around a critique of internet culture's transformation of artistic values:

- **"Immersion"** has been replaced with **"Volume of content"**
- **"Purpose"** has been replaced with **"Reach"**
- **"Feeling"** has been replaced with **"Algorithm"**

### On influence and followers
> "I don't desire more 'followers'. I desire to see more leaders, more free thinkers, more truth seekers."

### Core philosophical statement
> "We are not defined by what we do, but by who we are. All I am is that I am."

(Riff on YHWH / Exodus 3:14 — "I am that I am" — divine self-naming.)

### On music and necessity
> "The world needs music, but the world doesn't need more songs. There's only one song."

### Web3 / Hall of Mirrors structure

The Manifesto frames a community-and-collectibles structure:
- **Crowns** — free, composable collectibles serving as access keys; unique to each holder; reflect participation history.
- **Artifacts** — one-of-one digital items described as "lost in the code" during an AI upload by **SARAVI** (described as "his AI"). Discovering artifacts unlocks new Hall of Mirrors rooms accessible to all community members; ownership remains exclusive.

### Holder benefits / value propositions
- Exclusive merch drops and discount tiers
- Early concert ticket access
- Exclusive/early access to unreleased content, music videos, listening parties
- Access to archived demos, secret interviews

### On Web3 philosophy
Roy emphasizes blockchain technology enabling "verifiable proof" of early community participation with tangible benefits — moving beyond social bragging rights to monetizable ownership stakes for engaged fans.
