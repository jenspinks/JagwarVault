The first thing we did — because that was another thing, when we was talking about overthinking — sometimes we try to overthink, and it's like, no, they wanting this, give them this. So we went through our sessions that we made for the Yeezus pack, and the same chain that we created for those sessions — when we got the demos, we ran everything through our chain, put it back in the Pro Tools, and then we started creating from that, sent it back, and she was like, "Loved it, send us another one." Did the same thing, sent it back, she was like, "Loved it. Okay, paperwork." Dude. **S1:** That's true. You know, I can also be very introverted and want to stay to myself, but then I know there are times when I have to put myself out more, because it will be helpful to people. What's crazy is — and I don't even ask you if I should tell this, because we can edit —
