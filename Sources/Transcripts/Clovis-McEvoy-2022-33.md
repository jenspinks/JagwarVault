# Clovis McEvoy — 2022-12-21 long-form interview

Long-form personal-site interview after *33* release. Surfaces an early **subscription/immersion experiment** with 1000+ weekly users — possibly proto-Mirror-Sessions or proto-Manifesto-community structure.

## Verbatim excerpts

### Early life / musical commitment
> "I had so many thoughts and feelings as a young kid. I often didn't know how to express myself in language."

> "I knew I was going to make music forever. There was never a backup plan."

> "I felt like I was going to make music or die trying."

### On the post-STF period / *33* gestation
> "I really used that time to re-evaluate why I was making music in the first place."

### KEY — the immersion experiment (proto-community work)
> "I wanted to experiment with how a fan could really immerse themselves psychologically and physiologically in a song."

> "We had over 1,000 weekly registered users and so many transformational experiences."

(This is significant for understanding the lineage of the later *mirrors.jagwartwin.com* Hall of Mirrors / SARAVI / Web3 community structure. The fan-immersion experiment is pre-*33*-release setup. **Worth cross-checking with the Manifesto FAQ ingestion — likely this is what later becomes the Manifesto FAQ's Crown/Artifact architecture.**)

### Storytelling as core
> "I desire to tell stories. The mediums change through the ages, but the stories are the same."

### Connection vs disconnection
> "We are more connected to each other than ever, but somehow many of us feel more disconnected from ourselves."

(Direct echo of [[Atwood-2018-Loser]] *"The world has never felt more disconnected in a lot of ways. But we have all the tools at our disposal to connect, at all times."* Stable thread across 2018→2022.)

### Identity-as-narrative (KEY)
> "We are the stories we tell, and I wonder if we have forgotten who we are."

> "To be able to share deeply personal stories and memories and connect with others, you need to be able to connect with yourself."
