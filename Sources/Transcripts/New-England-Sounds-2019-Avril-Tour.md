# New England Sounds — 2019-12-05 Foundations / Avril Lavigne tour interview

End-of-2019 promotional interview tied to the Avril Lavigne tour. **Contains the verbatim "twin = dark and light" explanation** — Roy's own framing of his project name's duality. Grounds [[Duality]] concept page.

## Verbatim excerpts

### On the early creative process
> "I was just making music as I do, solo music. Got into the mix of things."

> "I feel like there's been an energetic shift in the world where the world has gotten so crazy right now."

> "It's a very collaborative process."

> "I don't feel that I'm even writing what's coming through me and there's so many amazing people involved."

### On the Jagwar Twin name (KEY — duality grounding)
> "Jagwar in my mythology is this creature who kind of looks first into itself really deeply, but that self realization allows it to look and see into others."

> **"The twin is just the nature of everything we've been talking about. The dark and the light."**

### On the Avril Lavigne tour
> "Avril's fans have been so warm. She's very intuitive and just has so much good advice."

> "This was our second tour ever."

### On creative standards
> "If everyone in the room feels like this is the best thing we can be doing, we get the chills."

> "Do we believe this? Is it authentic to me as a person?"
