Roy: I'm Jagwar Twin and you're watching my most requested live Ask Anything chat. My new song is called Bad Feeling. Thanks to Romeo and iHeartRadio for having me on the show tonight.

Host: This one is from Tiffany from Des Moines, Iowa. I'm a new fan. Can you tell us a little bit about yourself?

Roy: Well, I'm Jagwar Twin. Some people call me Roy. And I make music that explores polarity. Light and dark and good and evil. The name Jagwar Twin comes from my love of mythology. And so the jaguar in my mythology is the creature who looks deeply into itself, into the dark shadow places and then is therefore able to look and see into others and pull others out of the darkness. So that's really what everything I do with Jagwar Twin is all about.

Host: Gael Dario from Mexico. What was your inspiration to release Bad Feeling Oompa Loompa?

Roy: Bad Feeling almost didn't come out. I wrote it almost a year ago, a little longer than a year ago, with no idea that there was a Wonka movie being made. I've always loved Willy Wonka and the one with Gene Wilder really spoke to me as a kid. I would watch it at my grandma's house over and over and over. And even as an adult, I would watch it because there's so many beautiful messages and codes in that movie. So I was inspired by that when writing Bad Feeling, but it uses the original Oompa Loompa song and so we had to clear that interpolation of the sample. And I had my lawyers, my record label's lawyers, my record label's team, my management. So many people reached out to clear this sample and we got no's from every single person. All the doors closed. And eventually nine months went by and I came back from this beautiful trip in London and I thought to myself, there's a way for this to happen. If the universe wants this song to be in the world, it will happen. I have nothing to worry about. And so I thought, how can this happen? And this person came to my mind and it was a friend of a friend of a friend and long story short, I reached out to this friend who knew someone who knew someone and I was able to get in contact with the lawyer and I ended up clearing the sample for Oompa Loompa myself. And it was such a beautiful sort of testament to not giving up on something. And sometimes when you get the no's, it's just the universe testing you and like, do you want this really bad enough? Are you willing to put your neck out and to do this work? So it was quite a process, but it's out in the world now.

Host: Lynn from Louvre, France. Are there any arts other than song music that you practice or would like to practice one day?

Roy: Yes, I paint and I make sculptures. I just love storytelling. I love mythology. I've written a couple children's books. I don't even really consider myself a musician. I enjoy using music as a way to tell stories, but whatever medium serves the story, I'm all about.
