# Ones to Watch — 2022 *33* Q&A ("existential sophomore album")

Major per-track and per-album Q&A. The most-cited Roy framings for [[33 (album)|*33*]] and individual tracks (Soul Is A Star, Online, The Circle).

## Verbatim excerpts

### On the album's title & theme (KEY)
> **"thirty-three is the age I wrote the album. It represents peace within and without."**

> **"The whole theme of the album is, in some ways, catalyzing pain."**

> *"The whole album is about finding peace with all those things that made this beautiful body of work out of those difficult things."*

### On creative process / voice
> "I've just been trying to listen to that voice more."

> "It was innate in me, and it's innate in everyone, but at least for me, I went through a period where I blocked it."

> "So it's become about creating more from the heart and a place of intuition."

### Per-track quotes (HIGH-VALUE)

**On Soul Is A Star:**
> "I'm so glad I listened to that voice because that's what flowed through me."

**On [[Online]]:**
> **"That song is so fun and so ridiculous."**

(Important counterweight to over-serious / over-doctrinal readings — Roy's own framing of Online is *playful*, even as the lyrics name database identity / "infiltrate my brain" / predictive profiling. Both registers are real — the song is fun *and* the diagnosis is sharp. Useful for the [[Online]] Brain page.)

**On The Circle:**
> **"It's my favorite song that has ever come through me."**

(Roy's strongest personal-favorite statement in the corpus. Reinforces in the catalogue Roy puts The Circle at the top.)

### On authenticity & being human
> "Being an artist is about knowing yourself. Being a human is about knowing yourself."

> "Try to take things less seriously now... I'm just trying to have fun."
