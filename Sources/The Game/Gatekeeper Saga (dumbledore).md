## The double-bind — the engine of the pathos `[W]`
The Circle is **mechanically an algorithm-gaming machine**, and Roy is both the Circus's critic and its operator — which is what makes *"Lucius was right again"* land as **tragedy, not triumph** (see [[Sir Lucius]] §Grounding).
- **Bad Feeling radio war** (2024-01): organized request campaign for *Most Requested Live* — *"it really is time to go to war. becuase we're at war with the forces of darkness in pop music"* (Roy, [2024-01-13](https://discord.com/channels/705391219915882516/709750776284708955/1195551748119609415)); the call-to-arms tactics ([2024-01-13](https://discord.com/channels/705391219915882516/709750776284708955/1195547291172483112)).
- **The Spotify mission** (2025-11-16): Roy asks the Circle to loop *not your homie* "171 or more time" overnight, noting *"having a few other songs besides not your homie in there makes spotify think that its a real playlist too"* ([2025-11-16](https://discord.com/channels/705391219915882516/709750776284708955/1439505459462672406); the mission post [2025-11-16](https://discord.com/channels/705391219915882516/709750776284708955/1439499504381329419)).
- **Reading `[W]`:** to *escape* the Circus, the Circle *runs* the Circus. The artist who hates the spectacle still has to win inside it. (Mechanics `[G]`; the irony-reading `[W]`.)
